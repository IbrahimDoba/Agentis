To send a WhatsApp broadcast with the least risk to your number, message only people who already chat with you, send in small batches spread over hours and not minutes, personalise each message, and give people an easy way to opt out. No method removes the risk completely. WhatsApp restricts numbers mostly because recipients block or report them, so who you send to matters more than how many.

### What actually gets a broadcast number restricted

WhatsApp does not publish a safe number of messages a day. What is consistent is the pattern:

- **Recipients block or report you.** This is the biggest cause.
- **You message people who never contacted you**, such as bought lists or numbers copied from groups.
- **A new number sends to many people at once.**
- **The same text, often with a link, goes to everyone in one burst.**
- **You use a modified app** such as GB WhatsApp.

The full list and what to do after a restriction are in [how to stop WhatsApp banning your business number](/blog/how-to-stop-whatsapp-banning-your-business-number).

### How to broadcast by hand in WhatsApp

WhatsApp and WhatsApp Business both have broadcast lists.

1. Open WhatsApp, tap the menu and choose **New broadcast**.
2. Pick the contacts. A list holds up to 256 people.
3. Type the message and send. Each person gets it as a private chat from you.

There is a built-in safety rule here that works in your favour: a broadcast list message is only delivered to people who have saved your number. Strangers do not get it.

To keep manual broadcasts safe:

- Split customers into lists by interest, so each message is relevant.
- Send one list, wait, then send the next. Do not fire ten lists in ten minutes.
- Start with "Hi" and the kind of content customers asked for: restocks, price drops, delivery updates.
- End with "Reply STOP and I will remove you", and actually remove them.
- Do not broadcast daily. Once or twice a week is plenty for most shops.

### How to send a paced broadcast in Dailzero

Dailzero links to the number you already use and sends through it at a human pace.

1. **Link your number.** Broadcasts stay locked until WhatsApp is connected.
2. **Open Broadcasts in the sidebar.**
3. **Choose recipients.** The list shows only people who have already chatted with your agent. Search by name or phone, tick the people you want, or use "Select visible". You can select up to 2,000 contacts per broadcast. There is no option to upload a list of numbers.
4. **Write the message under Compose.** Put `{name}` where the customer's name should go, for example "Hi {name}, we just restocked your most requested item". Personalising reduces the identical-message pattern.
5. **Set "Send over".** Choose 24 hours, 48 hours, 72 hours or a custom window. Messages are spread evenly across the window so they go out gradually. "As soon as possible" is offered only when you have selected 10 people or fewer.
6. **Leave "Pause overnight" on** if you do not want messages going out between 11 PM and 6 AM. The run picks up again at 6 AM.
7. **Click "Queue Broadcast".**
8. **Watch it under "Recent Campaigns"**, where each run shows live progress and can be stopped if something looks off.

### The honest part

A linked-device connection is not the official WhatsApp API. WhatsApp does not officially support automating an account this way, so it carries more risk than the official route. Dailzero's pacing lowers that risk. It does not remove it, and no tool can promise a number will never be restricted.

If losing the number would badly damage your business, you have two better options:

- **Use the official WhatsApp Business Platform.** You verify with Meta, use approved message templates and pay Meta's message fees. Dailzero supports this route too, set up with the team on request, and a number connected that way can send a broadcast as an approved template. Read [official WhatsApp API vs linked device](/blog/official-whatsapp-api-vs-linked-device-which-should-you-use).
- **Use a second number** for broadcasts and keep your main line for replies.

Also, a new number should be used normally for about two weeks before you broadcast from it at all.

### Frequently asked questions

#### How many people can I broadcast to on WhatsApp in a day?

WhatsApp does not publish a number. It depends on the age and history of your number and how recipients react. Blocks and reports matter more than the count.

#### Can I send a broadcast to numbers that have never messaged me?

In WhatsApp's own broadcast lists, people who have not saved your number will not receive it. In Dailzero you can only pick people who have already chatted with your agent. Messaging strangers is the fastest way to lose a number.

#### Is a broadcast list the same as a WhatsApp group?

No. In a group everyone sees everyone. A broadcast arrives as a private message, and recipients cannot see who else received it.

#### Why does Dailzero take 24 hours to send my broadcast?

Because a burst of identical messages is what gets numbers flagged. Spreading the sends over a day makes them go out gradually. Only lists of 10 or fewer can go out straight away.

#### Will the AI answer people who reply to a broadcast?

Yes. Replies arrive as normal chats and the agent answers them, or hands over to you when a person is needed.
