To handle late night client messages automatically, you have two options: the free away message in the WhatsApp Business app, which sends one fixed reply, or an AI agent linked to your number, which actually answers the question, records the request and leaves it ready for you in the morning. Which one you need depends on what your clients send at night.

### What clients really send after 10pm

Night messages to an agency or freelancer usually fall into four types:

- **A new enquiry.** Someone scrolling Instagram at midnight finally decides to ask about your service.
- **A change request.** A client remembers an edit and sends it before they forget, often as a voice note.
- **A status check.** "How far with the design?"
- **A real emergency.** The campaign is live with the wrong price on it.

A fixed away message treats all four the same. That is fine for the second and third. It is costly for the first, because a new lead who gets "we are closed" may message someone else.

### One night, hour by hour

Here is how a night could go for a small Lagos design studio with an AI agent on its number. This is an illustration.

**10:47pm.** A new lead writes:

> Good evening, how much is your social media management package? I run a skincare brand in Abuja.

The agent replies within seconds with the package prices the studio gave it, sends the service brochure from the media library, and asks how many platforms she wants managed and when she wants to start. She answers. The agent marks her as a lead.

**11:30pm.** An existing client sends a voice note asking for the flyer text to be changed. The agent understands voice notes, so it replies: "Noted. I have recorded the change: replace 'Grand Opening' with 'Now Open' and move the date to the 14th. The team will send the updated flyer in the morning."

**1:15am.** A client asks if his logo is ready. The agent cannot see the studio's project files, so it says so honestly, tells him the team is back at 9am, and hands the chat over.

**2:05am.** Another client writes that an ad is running with the wrong phone number. The agent cannot fix this, so it hands off at once and tells the client the account owner will see it first thing.

**8:30am.** The studio owner opens the dashboard. One new lead with her answers attached, one clear change request, two handed-off chats waiting.

### Rules to give the agent for night messages

The agent follows the instructions you write. These four keep it out of trouble:

1. **State your real hours.** "The team is available 9am to 6pm, Monday to Friday."
2. **No promises you have not set.** It must never agree a deadline, a discount or a scope change by itself.
3. **Work-in-progress questions go to a human.** It cannot see your files or task board, so status checks become a handoff with a clear time for a reply.
4. **Define an emergency.** List what counts, such as a live campaign error, and tell it to hand over immediately.

### What it cannot do at 2am

- **It will not wake you.** A handoff puts the chat in your queue. If true emergencies are part of your work, give those clients a phone number to call. Dailzero does not make voice calls.
- **It cannot fix the problem.** It cannot pause an ad or edit a design. It can only record, reassure and escalate.
- **It does not confirm payments.** If a client sends a transfer receipt at midnight, the agent can acknowledge it and tell them you will confirm in the morning.
- **It runs all day, not only at night.** During the day, if you reply to a chat yourself, the AI pauses on that chat until you switch it back on.

Your phone does not have to stay awake through the night for the agent to work. Like WhatsApp Web, it only needs to come online regularly.

### When the free away message is enough

If you get one or two night messages a week and they are all from existing clients who know you, set an away message in the WhatsApp Business app and stop there. An AI agent earns its cost when night messages include new enquiries.

### Frequently asked questions

#### Can I set the AI to reply only at night?

Plan for it to answer at all hours. In the daytime you can take over any chat by replying yourself, and the AI pauses on that chat.

#### Will clients feel ignored if a bot replies?

Less than if nobody replies. A reply that records their request correctly and says when a person will respond is better than silence until morning.

#### Does my phone need to be on overnight?

No. It should come online regularly, as with WhatsApp Web, but it does not need to be awake all night.
