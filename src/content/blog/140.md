### What "built for Nigeria first" means

Dailzero describes its market as Nigeria first, usable by any business that sells or supports customers on WhatsApp. In practice, three things are Nigerian by design:

- **Prices are in Naira.** Plans are ₦20,000, ₦40,000 and ₦75,000 a month, and pay as you go starts from ₦1,000.

The product itself is not tied to a country. The agent works on WhatsApp, and WhatsApp works the same way in Accra, Nairobi, London or Houston as it does in Lagos.

### When another tool is the better choice

Be honest with yourself about these cases:

- **You need invoices and billing in dollars, pounds or euros.** A provider that bills in your currency will be simpler for your accounts.
- **You need support in your own time zone or language.** Dailzero's support is in English, on West Africa Time.
- **Your country's rules require a local data or compliance arrangement.** Dailzero's privacy policy is written around Nigeria's data protection regulation. Check what your own regulator expects.
- **You need the official WhatsApp API with a local provider.** Dailzero does offer the official WhatsApp Business Platform on request, set up with its team, but a provider in your own market may be more convenient.

The two connection routes are explained in [how Dailzero connects to WhatsApp](/blog/how-dailzero-connects-to-whatsapp).

### Number safety applies everywhere

WhatsApp's rules are the same in every country. On the linked-device route, Dailzero queues every message, sends at a human pace, warms up new numbers and slows down automatically when WhatsApp signals a limit. No tool can promise a number will never be restricted. If your number is new, use it normally for about two weeks before you connect it.
