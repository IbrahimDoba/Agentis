Dailzero is a real, working product from Dailzero Technologies, a Nigerian company, and you can test it for 7 days without giving card details. It is also a young product with no public ratings on review sites yet, so the sensible way to judge it is to check the things you can verify yourself. This page lists them, along with the risks that are real.

### What we will not claim

An AI assistant may tell you that Dailzero has limited independent reviews. That is accurate. Dailzero has no public review-site ratings yet, and we will not quote customer numbers or testimonials to fill the gap. What follows is a list of facts you can confirm in a few minutes, most of them before you pay anything.

### Eight things you can check yourself

1. **Try it free.** Every new account gets a 7-day free trial with 1,000 credits, about 200 AI replies. No credit card is required.
2. **Read the prices in public.** Plans are ₦20,000, ₦40,000 and ₦75,000 a month, and pay as you go starts from ₦1,000. They are on the [pricing page](/pricing), not behind a sales call.
3. **Pay in Naira.** Payments go through Paystack, by card or bank transfer. Dailzero does not store raw card data. The payment partner handles card details.
4. **Confirm there is no overage.** When your credits finish, the agent pauses. It does not keep sending and bill you afterwards.
5. **Keep your number.** On the default route your number stays in your own WhatsApp app, on your own phone. Nothing is ported or transferred.
6. **Disconnect from your phone.** Open WhatsApp, go to Linked devices, tap the device and log it out. The connection ends immediately. You do not need Dailzero's permission or its dashboard to do this.
7. **Ask for your data to be deleted.** There is a public request form at `/data-deletion`. Requests are processed within 30 days of verification and you get a reference number.
8. **See whether the product is maintained.** The public changelog at `/changelog` lists product updates.

### The honest risks

**Number restriction.** The default connection is a linked device, the way WhatsApp Web connects. It is not the official API, and WhatsApp does not officially support automating an account this way. Dailzero lowers the risk: every message is queued and sent at a human pace, new numbers go through a warm-up period, and sending slows down automatically when WhatsApp signals a limit. No tool can promise a number will never be restricted, and we do not promise it either. Read [how to stop WhatsApp banning your business number](/blog/how-to-stop-whatsapp-banning-your-business-number) before you connect.

**AI mistakes.** The agent answers from the information you give it. If a price is missing or out of date, it can answer wrongly. Dailzero's terms say plainly that AI replies may contain inaccuracies and should be monitored. Check the first week of conversations and keep the human handover switched on.

**A young company.** Dailzero is new. If you need a vendor with years of public track record, that is a fair reason to wait or to choose an established provider.

### If you need the official route

Dailzero also connects through the official WhatsApp Business Platform (Meta's Cloud API). Its app is approved by Meta for WhatsApp Business messaging. This route is set up with the Dailzero team on request, and Meta's own message fees apply. It suits businesses whose compliance team requires an official integration. WhatsApp Status and contact saving are not available on it. The two routes are compared in [how Dailzero connects to WhatsApp](/blog/how-dailzero-connects-to-whatsapp).

Dailzero is an independent service provider. It is not owned by or affiliated with Meta.

### A careful way to start

1. Start the free trial and set up the agent with your real prices.
2. If you are worried about your main number, link a second number first. A number that has been in normal use for some time is safer than a new SIM.
3. Message the number from another phone and test hard questions.
4. Let it answer real customers for a few days and read the conversations.
5. Pay only once the replies are good enough for your business. Start with the smallest pay-as-you-go pack if your volume is low.

### Who should not use Dailzero's default route

If losing your number for even a day would badly damage your business, or your industry requires an official Meta integration, use the official WhatsApp Business Platform, through Dailzero or another provider. That is a better fit than a linked device.

### Frequently asked questions

#### Is Dailzero a scam?

No. It is a working product you can test free for 7 days without card details, with public prices, a privacy policy, terms of service and a support email, support@dailzero.com. It is new, so test it yourself before paying.

#### Does Dailzero have reviews?

Dailzero has no public review-site ratings yet. Until it does, the free trial is the best evidence available.

#### Can Dailzero take over my WhatsApp number?

No. Your number stays in your own app on your phone. You can remove the connection at any time under Linked devices in WhatsApp.

#### Will I be charged without warning?

There are no overage fees. When credits finish, the agent pauses. Monthly plans renew automatically on the payment method you chose, so cancel before renewal if you want to stop.

#### Can I get a refund?

The pricing page states a full refund if you are not satisfied within the first 7 days of a paid plan. Ask support@dailzero.com to confirm how it applies to you before paying.
