WhatsApp does not save the people who message you. Each new chat stays an unsaved number until you add it to your phone's contacts yourself, and unsaved numbers cannot see your Status. You can save them one at a time by hand, or let a tool do it. Dailzero's Contacts tab saves every chat on your number in one run, and can auto-save each new chat, for 1 credit per contact saved.

### Why saving contacts matters

Your WhatsApp Status is shown only to people saved in your phone. A business with 3,000 chats and 200 saved contacts is posting to 200 people. The other 2,800 have already messaged you, already know you, and never see a single update.

Saving also helps in other ways. Broadcast lists only work with saved contacts, a saved name is easier to find than a number, and a conversation between two saved contacts looks like a wanted conversation.

One honest note: for your Status to show, the customer should have your number saved too. So ask for it in your greeting: "Save our number to see new stock on our Status."

### How to save contacts by hand

1. Open the chat with the unsaved number.
2. Tap the number at the top of the chat.
3. Tap **Save** or **Add to contacts**.
4. Type a name. Add a word such as "Customer" or your shop name after it, so you can find all your customers later by searching that word.
5. Save, and go to the next chat.

For a handful of new chats a day this is fine. Make it a closing habit: before you sleep, save everyone who messaged that day.

It breaks down when there is a backlog. At about 20 seconds each, 1,000 chats is more than five hours of tapping, and most owners never finish.

### Where this does not apply

Contact saving works on the linked-device connection only. It is not available if your number runs on the official WhatsApp Business Platform. It also saves people who have chatted with you. It does not import a list of numbers, and you should not add strangers to your contacts in the hope they will see your Status. They will not, unless they have saved you.

### Frequently asked questions

#### Does WhatsApp Business save contacts automatically?

No. Neither WhatsApp nor WhatsApp Business saves a new chat to your contacts for you. You add each one by hand unless a tool does it.

#### Where are the contacts saved?

In your own phone's contacts, through your WhatsApp account. They are yours and stay there if you stop using Dailzero.

#### Will it rename contacts I already saved?

No. Contacts already in your phone are never renamed. The name tag applies only to contacts saved from Dailzero.

#### How long does it take to save 1,000 contacts?

A few seconds each, so roughly an hour and a half. Up to 1,000 are saved in a day and anything above that continues the next day.

#### Can I do this on the free trial?

Yes, up to 200 contacts in total on a free account. To save more, subscribe to a plan or top up credits.
