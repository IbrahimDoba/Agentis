Most of a dropshipper's customer chat can be handed to a WhatsApp AI agent: product questions, prices, delivery timelines, order details and payment instructions. Two things it cannot do alone are confirm that a transfer has landed and tell a customer where a specific parcel is, because it cannot see your bank or your supplier's tracking.

Dropshipping and mini importation follow a cycle: an ad brings a rush of enquiries, customers pay before you have the goods, then everyone waits. Here is what to automate at each stage.

### Stage 1: the enquiry rush

When an ad or a Status post goes out, forty people may ask the same thing within an hour. This is where AI helps most. Load the agent with:

- Each product's price, colours, sizes and what is in the box.
- Photos and short videos in the media library.
- Whether it is ready stock or pre-order, and the timeline for each.
- Delivery fees by location: Lagos, Abuja, Port Harcourt and other states.
- Your payment terms, for example full payment before order, or pay on delivery for ready stock only.

If the customer came from a Facebook or Instagram click-to-WhatsApp ad, Dailzero records which ad it was. If you are testing several products, that shows which one is pulling buyers.

### Stage 2: taking the order

Once a customer decides, the agent collects name, phone number, full address, colour, size and quantity. It then sends the bank account or Paystack link you provided.

Here is the first handoff. The AI does not verify payment. It passes the chat to you, you check the alert, and you confirm. Do not instruct it to treat a screenshot as proof. Fake receipts are a known problem, and only your bank app settles it.

### Stage 3: the wait

This is the stage that damages dropshippers' reputations. The goods are coming from a supplier, the customer has paid, and the messages start:

> "It's been 10 days since I paid for the smartwatch. When is it coming??"

The agent cannot see your supplier's shipment. What it can do is repeat the timeline you set, calmly, at any hour, and escalate:

> "I understand, and thank you for your patience. Pre-order items take 14 to 21 days from payment to arrive in Nigeria, then 1 to 3 days for delivery. I am passing your chat to the team so they can check your exact order and update you."

### Be honest about timelines in the setup

The agent says what you told it. If you write "delivery in 7 days" because it sells better, and the real figure is three weeks, you have automated a false promise and it will be repeated to every customer.

Write the range you actually achieve. State clearly which items are pre-order. Say what happens if a batch is delayed at the port or by the supplier. Customers accept a long wait they were warned about far better than a short one that was missed.

### Stage 4: when something goes wrong

Wrong colour, damaged item, supplier out of stock after payment. These are not AI conversations. Instruct the agent to apologise briefly, collect a photo of the problem if relevant, and hand the chat to you. You decide on the refund or replacement.

### Keeping customers updated in bulk

When a batch lands, you can broadcast one message to the customers waiting on it, for example "Your order has arrived in Lagos and dispatch starts tomorrow." A text broadcast costs 5 credits a person, so updating 80 customers costs 400 credits. Sends are paced to protect your number. Broadcasts are for people who have already messaged you, not for bought contact lists.

### Frequently asked questions

#### Can the AI place the order with my supplier?

No. It collects the customer's order details and hands them to you. Ordering from the supplier remains your job.

#### Can it confirm a customer's payment?

No. It shares the payment details you gave it and passes the chat to you to confirm the transfer in your bank app.

#### I am just starting with a new SIM. Can I connect it immediately?

Use a brand-new number normally for about two weeks before connecting it. New numbers also go through a warm-up period, and no tool can promise a number will never be restricted.
