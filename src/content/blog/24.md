A Nigerian property developer has three ways to run WhatsApp: the free WhatsApp Business app, the official WhatsApp Business Platform (the API), or the Business app with an AI agent linked to it. Most developers selling one to five estates do well on the third option. The API only makes sense at very high volume or when you need a verified badge.

This guide covers what each option gives you and how to set up the app properly for off-plan and estate sales.

### The three options side by side

| | WhatsApp Business app | Official API | App plus linked AI agent |
|---|---|---|---|
| Who replies | You and your staff | Software you build or buy | AI first, your team when needed |
| Cost | Free | Meta fees plus a provider | The AI tool's fee only |
| Verified badge | No | Possible | No |
| Setup | Minutes | Needs a provider or developer | Scan a QR code |
| Keeps your current number and chats | Yes | Number usually moves to the API | Yes |

### Setting up the Business app for a development

Start here whatever you choose later. The free app has features most developers never switch on.

- **Business profile.** Put the company name as registered, your office address, hours, website and a short description that names your estates.
- **Catalogue.** Add each unit type as an item: 2 bed apartment, 4 bed terrace, 500 sqm plot. Include the price and the payment plan in the description.
- **Greeting and away messages.** A greeting that states which estates you sell saves one round of "which property do you mean?".
- **Labels.** Tag chats by stage: new enquiry, inspected, deposit paid, fully paid, allocated.

### What buyers and subscribers actually ask a developer

You get two crowds on one number.

**Prospects** ask about price, title, location, payment plans and inspection:

> Good afternoon. Pls what is the title on the Epe estate and can I pay in 12 months?

A good reply is direct and complete:

> Good afternoon. The estate has a registered survey and a Certificate of Occupancy. A 500 sqm plot is ₦6 million outright, or ₦6.6 million spread over 12 months with a ₦1 million initial deposit. Free site inspections hold every Saturday at 10am from our Ajah office. Would you like the brochure?

Those figures are an illustration. Use your own.

**Existing subscribers** ask when allocation will happen, how much they have left to pay, when the road will be done, and where their deed of assignment is. These chats are where developers lose trust, because the answers are slow and the questions are repeated by hundreds of people.

### Where the app alone stops working

The app is fine until the volume arrives. After a billboard goes up or a click-to-WhatsApp ad runs, three sales staff cannot answer two hundred "how much?" messages the same afternoon, and nobody is awake for buyers in the diaspora.

### Adding an AI agent to the number

An AI agent such as Dailzero links to your Business app the way WhatsApp Web does. You scan a QR code or enter a pairing code, your number and chat history stay where they are, and the AI starts replying using the estates, prices, payment plans and tone you gave it.

For a developer that covers:

- Answering prospects in seconds, day and night, and sending brochures, layout plans, price lists and site videos from your media library.
- Collecting name, preferred plot size, budget and payment plan.
- Booking site inspections and sending reminders.
- Listing people with buying intent as leads in the dashboard.
- Posting scheduled WhatsApp Status updates, which suits construction progress photos. Paid plans reach 2,000 to 10,000 contacts per post depending on the plan.
- Handing the chat to your team for negotiation, payment confirmation and anything it cannot answer.

Plans start at ₦20,000 a month, and there are no per-conversation fees from Meta. Details are on the [pricing page](/pricing).

### Limits a developer should know

Dailzero does not process or verify payments. It shares the account details or payment link you give it and passes the chat to you to confirm.

It also cannot see a subscriber's balance or allocation status on its own. It can answer general questions ("allocation for Phase 2 begins in March") from what you typed in. For account-specific answers ("how much do I have left?"), either your team responds, or a developer connects your records through webhook tools in the [developer API](/developers).

And it is not the official API. If you need a green verified badge or many staff answering on one number at enterprise scale, the API route fits you better.

### Frequently asked questions

#### Do I need the WhatsApp Business API to automate replies?

No. A linked AI agent automates replies on the ordinary Business app. The API is a separate, heavier route.

#### Can the AI send my estate brochure and layout plan?

Yes. Upload them to the media library and it sends them when a buyer asks.

#### Can our sales team still reply themselves?

Yes. Team members can have their own logins and reply from the dashboard or the phone. The AI pauses on a chat when a person steps in.
