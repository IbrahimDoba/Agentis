To automate your agency's WhatsApp communication, sort the messages you receive into four streams (new enquiries, routine client questions, scheduling and announcements), let an AI agent send the first reply on each, and keep scope, money and complaints with a person. With Dailzero this runs on the number your agency already uses, linked by QR code, with no code to write.

### Start with a one-week inbox audit

Scroll back through seven days of chats on the agency line and tally each one against the four streams below. The questions that repeat are the ones an AI can answer from information you type in once.

| Stream | Typical message | First reply | Who finishes it |
|---|---|---|---|
| New enquiries | "How much for social media management?" | AI, with your packages and a few questions | You, on a discovery call |
| Routine client questions | "Please resend your account details" | AI, from the policies you gave it | AI, unless it is specific to one account |
| Scheduling | "Can we move Thursday's review?" | AI offers a slot and sends a reminder | AI |
| Announcements | Christmas closing dates, a new service | Broadcast or WhatsApp Status | AI answers the replies |

Anything that fits no row, such as a client unhappy with a design, is a handover, not a candidate for automation.

### What a good automated first reply looks like

Say a prospect in Port Harcourt writes at 8:40pm, after your team has gone home:

> Good evening. Please how much do you charge to run Instagram ads for a skincare brand?

A weak auto-reply says "Thanks for your message, we will get back to you." A good one answers and moves the conversation forward, using the figures you gave it:

> Good evening. Ads management starts from our Growth package, and your ad budget is separate. So I can point you to the right package: are you selling online already, and roughly what do you plan to spend on ads each month?

By morning you have a prospect who has shared their budget and goals, and the chat is flagged as a lead in the dashboard.

### Set it up in five steps

1. **Write down your packages and prices** exactly as you would say them to a prospect, including what is not included (ad spend, printing, influencer fees).
2. **Add your working rules:** office hours, turnaround times, how many revision rounds a package covers, when invoices go out and the bank details clients should pay into.
3. **Upload your documents** to the media library: rate card, credentials deck, sample report. The agent sends them when someone asks.
4. **Give it your meeting slots** so it can book discovery calls and client reviews, and remind people beforehand.
5. **Link the number, test it yourself, then go live.** Message the agent from another phone with your ten most common questions and fix any answer you would not send yourself.

### Decide the handover rules before day one

The agent passes a chat to a person when the customer asks for one or when it cannot help. Tell it clearly which topics are yours:

- Discounts and custom quotes outside the published packages.
- Complaints about work already delivered.
- Anything about a specific client's campaign results or unpaid invoice.
- Confirming that a payment has landed. The agent can share your account details but cannot see your bank.

When you reply from your phone or the dashboard, the AI pauses on that chat until you switch it back on, so the client never gets two voices at once.

### The limits you should plan around

The agent only knows what you typed in. It can say that monthly reports go out on the 5th. It cannot open your project board to check whether one client's report is ready. For that, either hand over to the account manager or have a developer connect your system through [webhook tools in the developer API](/developers).

It also covers one WhatsApp number per agent. If your account managers talk to clients on their personal lines, those chats are not automated. Move client communication to the agency number first.

### Frequently asked questions

#### Will clients know they are talking to an AI?

That depends on the greeting you set. Saying so in the first message and offering a person on request avoids awkwardness later.

#### Can the AI send our rate card as a PDF?

Yes. Upload it to the media library and the agent sends it when a prospect asks for prices. A document costs 8 credits to send.

#### Can each team member see the chats?

Yes. Team members can have their own logins to the dashboard and take over a chat when it is handed to a person.
