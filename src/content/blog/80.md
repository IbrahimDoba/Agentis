An event planner can use WhatsApp AI to answer the same six questions every enquiry starts with: whether you are free on the date, what you charge, what a package includes, whether you cover the location, what your past work looks like, and how to book. The AI replies in seconds with your answers and photos, collects the event details, and hands the chat to you when it is time to quote and take a deposit.

That matters on Saturdays, when enquiries arrive while you are on site at someone else's wedding.

### The six questions, and how to prepare each answer

#### 1. "Are you available on 14th December?"

The agent cannot see your diary. It works from what you tell it, so give it a list of dates that are already taken and update that list every time you confirm a booking. Tell it to say a date "looks open, subject to confirmation" and never to promise one. For peak December weekends, have it hand over at once.

#### 2. "How much is your package?"

Decide what you will publish: usually a starting price per package, with the final quote depending on guest count, venue and decor. The agent gives the starting figure, then asks the questions that determine the real one.

#### 3. "What does it include?"

Write each package as a plain list: vendor sourcing, on-the-day coordination, ushers, decor or no decor. Upload your brochure as a PDF for the agent to send.

#### 4. "Do you do events in Ibadan?"

List the cities you cover and how travel and accommodation are charged outside your base.

#### 5. "Can I see your work?"

Upload photos and short videos to the media library, labelled by type: traditional wedding, white wedding, birthday, corporate dinner, burial reception. The agent sends the ones that match what the person asked about. An image uses 8 credits and a video 12.

#### 6. "How do I book?"

State your deposit terms and bank details. The agent shares them, and when the client sends a transfer receipt it passes the chat to you. It cannot confirm that money has arrived.

### What a good first exchange looks like

> Hello. I am planning my mum's 60th birthday for March, about 300 guests in Lagos. How much do you charge?

> Hello, and happy birthday to your mum in advance. For a 300-guest birthday in Lagos, our full planning package starts from the price in our brochure, which I am sending now. The final quote depends on the venue and how much decor you want. Do you have a venue already, and is it one day or are you adding a thanksgiving service?

### The details the agent should collect before you step in

By the time you open the chat, you want these already in it:

- Type of event and date, with a second date if the first is flexible.
- City and venue, or "no venue yet".
- Expected guest count.
- Budget range, marked optional.
- Whether they need full planning, coordination only, or decor.
- Colours or aso ebi theme, if chosen.
- Name and best time for a call.

The agent understands the inspiration pictures and voice notes clients send, and it can [book a call or venue visit](/solutions/appointment-booking) into the slots you give it, with a reminder.

### When to take the chat yourself

Set these as handover points:

- Any request for a custom quote or a discount.
- A date in peak season.
- A client ready to pay a deposit.
- Complaints or changes from a client who has already booked.
- Vendor chats with caterers, rental companies and venue managers.

When you reply from your phone or the dashboard, the AI pauses on that chat until you turn it back on. Leave it off for clients who have booked.

### Staying visible between events

Contacts who message you are saved so they can view your WhatsApp Status, and you can schedule Status posts of finished halls after every event. Before peak season, a text broadcast to past enquirers costs 5 credits per person, so 400 people is 2,000 credits. Broadcasts go only to people who have messaged you before. ### Frequently asked questions

#### Can the AI check my calendar for free dates?

Not by itself. It uses the list of booked dates you give it. Connecting a real calendar needs a developer and the webhook tools in the API.

#### Can it send a custom quotation?

It can send your standard brochure and starting prices. A quote built for one client's event should come from you.

#### Will it talk to my vendors?

It replies to whoever messages the linked number, so either tell it how to treat vendor messages or keep vendors on a separate line.
