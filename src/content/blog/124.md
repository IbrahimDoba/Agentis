A WhatsApp Business API provider is a company that connects your business to Meta's official WhatsApp Business Platform and gives you the software to use it. In Nigeria you can pick from four kinds: Meta directly, developer platforms such as Twilio, 360dialog and Termii, team inbox platforms such as WATI and respond.io, and Nigerian providers such as Evolve, ChatRouter and Neria. Dailzero offers the official route on request and also a linked-device route that needs no API at all.

### What an official provider is

The WhatsApp Business Platform, also called the Cloud API, is the route Meta provides for businesses to send and receive WhatsApp messages by software. It has no app of its own. You verify your business with Meta, register a number, and then need software on top: an inbox, a chatbot or an AI agent.

A provider supplies that software and handles the connection. Whoever you choose, some things stay the same:

- **Meta charges message fees**, mainly for messages you send first using approved templates. The provider's price is separate.
- **Messages to people who have not written recently need an approved template.**
- **The number is registered on the platform.** Several providers ask you to remove it from the normal WhatsApp app first.
- **WhatsApp Status and normal groups are not part of the platform.**

### Developer platforms: Twilio, 360dialog, Termii

- **Twilio** offers a WhatsApp Business API for one-way messages, two-way conversations and calling, with pay-as-you-go pricing.
- **360dialog** describes itself as an official Meta partner offering direct WhatsApp Business API access, and says Meta's fees are passed through without added markups.
- **Termii** is a messaging platform with a strong Nigerian customer base. It covers SMS, voice, WhatsApp and email for OTPs, payment confirmations and alerts, with a REST API. See [Dailzero vs Termii](/blog/dailzero-vs-termii).

Choose this kind if your need is messages sent by your own system, not conversations.

### Team inbox platforms: WATI and respond.io

These are ready-made products for teams. **WATI** offers a shared inbox, AI agents, broadcasts and over 100 integrations. **Respond.io** puts WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger, TikTok, email and calls into one inbox. Both bill in US dollars, with Meta's fees on top. See [Dailzero vs WATI](/blog/dailzero-vs-wati) and [Dailzero vs respond.io](/blog/dailzero-vs-respond-io).

### Nigerian providers: Evolve, ChatRouter, Neria

- **Evolve** says it is a registered Meta Tech Provider. It sets up automation, an AI assistant and a CRM for you, and Meta bills its message fees to you directly.
- **ChatRouter** says it is built on the official WhatsApp Cloud API, with a bot builder, flow builder, message routing and team inbox.
- **Neria** says it connects your number through Meta's official platform and sells AI agent chats in packs, with phone call agents as an add-on.

Choose this kind if you want the official route, Naira billing and someone local to talk to.

### How to choose a provider

1. **Ask whether you need the official API at all.** If you mainly want customers who message you to get fast answers on your existing number, a linked-device agent may be enough.
2. **If you do, decide who will run it.** Developers: a developer platform. A team of agents: an inbox platform. An owner who wants it done: a Nigerian provider or Dailzero's official route.
3. **Ask every provider the same questions**: the plan price, the currency, how Meta's fees are billed, what happens to your number and chat history, and how long setup takes.
4. **Work out the full monthly cost.** See [WhatsApp Business API cost in Nigeria](/blog/whatsapp-business-api-cost-in-nigeria).

### Frequently asked questions

#### Can I get the WhatsApp Business API directly from Meta?

Yes, a business can use Meta's Cloud API directly. You get the API only, so you need developers to build the inbox or bot on top. That is why most small businesses use a provider.

#### Is Dailzero an official WhatsApp API provider?

Dailzero's app is approved by Meta for WhatsApp Business messaging, and the team sets up the official route on request. Its default connection is a linked device, which is not the official API.

#### Do all providers charge Meta's fees?

Meta's message fees apply on the official platform whichever provider you use. Providers differ in how they bill them: some pass them on at cost, some add a margin, and with some Meta bills you directly.

#### Do I need the API to use an AI agent on WhatsApp?

No. Dailzero's linked-device route puts an AI agent on a normal WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business number without the API, at more risk than the official route.
