When someone replies to your WhatsApp Status, the reply arrives as a normal message in your chat with that person, with the Status quoted above it. To have an AI answer it, you need an AI agent connected to your number that can see which Status was replied to. In Dailzero you post the Status from the Posts tab and fill in "Tell the AI about this post". When a reply comes in, the agent reads that note first and answers with the right price and details.

### Why Status replies get lost

A Status reply is the warmest lead a small business gets. The customer saw a product and cared enough to type "how much?". But these replies come at any hour, often dozens at once after a good post, and each is a separate chat. Reply three hours later and the customer has bought elsewhere.

Also, the message is usually just "price?". Anything that answers has to know which post the customer was looking at.

### How to handle Status replies by hand

1. **Put the answer in the Status.** Add the price, sizes and "Reply to order" to the caption. Fewer people need to ask, and those who reply are closer to buying.
2. **Save quick replies in WhatsApp Business** for delivery fees, payment details and "which size do you want?". Type `/` in a chat to use one.
3. **Check your chats soon after posting.** Most replies come in the first hours.
4. **Look at the quoted Status** above each reply before answering, so you quote the right price.

### How a Status reply reaches the AI in Dailzero

Dailzero links to the number you already use, the way WhatsApp Web does. A reply to your Status is not a special kind of message. It lands as an ordinary chat, and the agent receives it with two extra pieces of information attached:

- **Which Status it was**: whether it was a text post, an image or a video, and its caption.
- **Your note about that post**, if you wrote one.

The agent then answers in the same chat, like any other conversation, and the conversation shows in your dashboard under Conversations. The reply is also counted under "Replied" in the Status Analytics tab.

### Setting it up, step by step

1. **Set up your agent and link your number** by QR code or pairing code.
2. **Open Status in the sidebar** and stay on the **Posts** tab.
3. **Build the post**: choose image, video or text, add the media or the words, and add a caption.
4. **Fill in "Tell the AI about this post".** Write what a salesperson would need to know: "Red ankara gown, ₦45,000, sizes 8-16, delivery in Lagos 1-2 days." You have up to 1,000 characters.
5. **Click "Post now"**, or tick "Schedule for later" and click "Schedule post".
6. **Reply to your own Status from another phone** to test it, and read the agent's answer.

The note is required when you upload a new image or video, because a picture alone tells the AI nothing about price. It is optional for text posts and for media picked from your library.

### What to put in the note

| Include | Example |
|---|---|
| What the item is | "Red ankara gown, fully lined" |
| Price | "₦45,000" |
| Options | "Sizes 8 to 16, also in blue" |
| Stock | "Only 6 left" |
| Delivery | "Lagos 1 to 2 days, other states 3 to 5 days" |
| How to order | "Collect name, address and size, then send payment details" |

Customers do not see the note. It is for the AI only.

### Posts you reuse keep their note

Everything you post is kept in the **Gallery** tab. "Post again" brings a post back with its caption and AI note, without uploading it again. Autopilot posts from the same gallery, so replies to an Autopilot post are answered with the note you wrote the first time. See [how to put your WhatsApp Status on autopilot](/blog/how-to-put-your-whatsapp-status-on-autopilot).

### What about a Status posted from my phone?

The agent still sees that the message is a reply to a Status, and it sees the caption if there was one. It has no note for that post, because notes are attached to posts made from Dailzero. So a phone-posted picture with no caption gives the AI very little to work with. Either post from the dashboard, or always write a clear caption with the price.

### When the AI should step back

The agent hands the chat to you when a customer asks for a person, is upset, or raises something sensitive such as a refund. You can also take over yourself: reply from the dashboard or your phone and the AI pauses for that conversation. More in [how to hand over from AI to a human on WhatsApp](/blog/how-to-hand-over-from-ai-to-a-human-on-whatsapp).

### Frequently asked questions

#### Where do replies to my WhatsApp Status go?

Into your one-to-one chat with the person who replied. The Status they replied to is quoted above their message. Other viewers cannot see the reply.

#### Can WhatsApp Business auto-reply to Status replies?

Only with a greeting or away message, which sends the same text to everyone and does not know which Status was replied to. It cannot answer "how much is this one?".

#### Does the AI know which Status the customer replied to?

Yes. It is told the type of post and its caption, plus the note you wrote under "Tell the AI about this post" if the Status was posted from Dailzero.

#### Do customers see the note I write for the AI?

No. Only the AI reads it. Customers see the Status and its caption, nothing else.

#### What if the customer replies with a voice note or a picture?

The agent reads images and voice notes that customers send, so it can still respond. If it cannot help, it hands the chat to you.
