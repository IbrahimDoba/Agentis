A WhatsApp chatbot in Nigeria costs anything from ₦0 to several hundred thousand Naira, because "chatbot" covers five very different products. Free keyword tools inside the WhatsApp Business app cost nothing. A monthly AI agent such as Dailzero costs ₦20,000 to ₦75,000 a month, or from ₦1,000 pay as you go. Official API platforms and custom builds cost more and are usually quoted in dollars or per project.

### The five price bands you will meet

| Type of chatbot | How you are charged | What to expect |
|---|---|---|
| Free tools in the WhatsApp Business app | Nothing | Greeting message, away message and quick replies. No AI. |
| Keyword or menu bots | Free tier or a low monthly fee | Replies only when the customer types the right word or number |
| Monthly AI agents | Subscription or credits, often in Naira | An AI that reads the message and answers from your prices |
| Official API platforms | Monthly fee, often in US dollars, plus Meta's message fees | Shared team inbox, approved templates, verified badge option |
| Custom builds by an agency or developer | One-off project fee plus hosting and maintenance | Built to your exact process, quoted case by case |

Prices in the last two bands change often and differ by provider, so we do not quote other companies' figures here. Ask each one for a written quote that includes every fee.

### Band 1: free tools

The WhatsApp Business app has a greeting message, an away message and quick replies. They cost nothing and take ten minutes to set up. They do not read what the customer wrote, so they cannot quote a price or take an order. See [is a free WhatsApp chatbot enough for your business](/blog/is-a-free-whatsapp-chatbot-enough-for-your-business) for who can stop here.

### Band 2: keyword and menu bots

These send "Reply 1 for prices, 2 for delivery". They are cheap because there is no AI behind them. They work when customers ask the same five things in the same words. They fail when a customer writes in Pidgin, sends a voice note or asks two things at once.

### Band 4: official API platforms

Here you pay twice. The platform charges its own subscription, and Meta charges for messages on the official WhatsApp Business Platform. Many of these platforms bill in US dollars, so your Naira cost moves with the exchange rate. This band suits banks, large brands and teams that need a verified badge or approved templates. Read [WhatsApp Business API cost in Nigeria](/blog/whatsapp-business-api-cost-in-nigeria) for how Meta's side of the bill works.

### Band 5: custom builds

An agency or freelance developer builds a bot just for you. You pay for the build, then for hosting, AI usage and changes. This makes sense when you need the bot wired into your own software in a way no ready-made tool offers. For most shops it is the most expensive route to the same result.

### How to compare quotes fairly

1. Work out your monthly volume: chats a day, times replies per chat, times 30.
2. Ask each provider what that volume costs in total, in Naira.
3. Ask what is charged on top: Meta fees, extra users, extra numbers, setup.
4. Ask what happens when you pass your limit. A pause is safer than a surprise bill.
5. Divide the total by your number of chats. Compare cost per chat, not headline price.

**Worked example on Dailzero:** a shop with 40 chats a day at about 6 AI replies each sends 240 replies a day. At 5 credits each that is 1,200 credits a day, or 36,000 credits in a 30-day month. That fits the Starter plan at ₦40,000. Spread over 1,200 chats, the cost is about ₦33 a chat.

### Where another option beats a monthly AI agent

- **You get five chats a day.** The free tools in the WhatsApp Business app are enough.
- **You need a verified badge or bulk alerts to people who never messaged you.** That needs an official API platform.
- **You need deep links into your own systems.** A custom build, or a developer using an API, fits better.

Many official API platforms are international companies that bill in US dollars. Meta's own message fees are also billed in a currency chosen at setup. Your Naira cost then depends on the exchange rate.
