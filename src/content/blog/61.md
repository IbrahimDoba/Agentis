### What one customer chat costs

Dailzero charges in credits. A text reply from the AI uses about 5 credits, an image 8, a video 12 and a document 8. A typical chat about price, size and delivery needs around six replies, so about 30 credits.

- On the Basic plan, ₦20,000 buys 25,000 credits. That is ₦0.80 a credit, or about ₦24 for a six-reply chat.
- On the ₦1,000 pack you get 600 credits. That is about ₦1.67 a credit, or ₦50 a chat.

Bigger packs sit in between.

### Pick your level by chats per day

**Under 5 chats a day.** Use the free trial, then packs. Three chats a day is about 2,700 credits a month, which one ₦5,000 pack covers. Pack credits stay valid for 12 months, so a slow month wastes nothing.

**5 to 13 chats a day.** Packs are still cheaper. Ten chats a day is 9,000 credits; a ₦10,000 pack plus a ₦5,000 pack gives 9,700 for ₦15,000.

**14 to 27 chats a day.** Basic fits. Its 25,000 credits cover about 27 six-reply chats a day.

**More than that.** Look at Starter at ₦40,000 for 60,000 credits. The full table is on the [Dailzero pricing page](/pricing).

### The margin test

Say you sell children's clothes from home in Ibadan and make about ₦2,500 profit on an average order. Put in your own figure. A ₦5,000 pack handles about 106 chats. If answering at 10pm, when you are asleep or NEPA has taken light and your phone is dead, saves just two orders out of those 106 chats, the pack has paid for itself.

A chat like this is where small stores usually lose the sale:

> "Good evening, how much is the 3-in-1 baby set and do you deliver to Ibadan?"

If the reply comes the next morning, the customer has often bought elsewhere. A good AI reply arrives in seconds and moves the order forward:

> "Good evening. The 3-in-1 set is ₦8,500. Delivery within Ibadan is ₦1,500 and takes 1 to 2 days. Which size do you need, newborn or 3 to 6 months?"

### Five ways to keep the bill small

1. Send photos only when the customer asks. A picture costs 8 credits against 5 for text.
2. Put delivery fees by area, sizes and your return policy in the agent's setup, so one reply answers what would have taken three.
3. Buy packs if your sales are seasonal, such as Christmas and back to school.
4. On a monthly plan, unused credits carry into the next month, up to 25% of the plan allowance.
5. If you are sure you will stay, pay yearly. You are charged 10 months for 12, which makes Basic ₦200,000 a year, about ₦16,667 a month.

### What you still handle yourself

The AI does not confirm payments. It can send the account details or Paystack link you give it, then hand the chat to you to check your bank alert. It also cannot see your stock, so update your product list when something sells out. Complaints and refunds come to you as well.

If your credits finish, there are no overage charges. The AI pauses and customer messages keep arriving in WhatsApp as normal. To see the setup from start to finish, read [how Dailzero works](/how-it-works).

### Frequently asked questions

#### What is the cheapest way to try AI customer service?

The 7-day free trial costs nothing and includes 1,000 credits, about 200 AI messages. After that, the smallest pay as you go pack is ₦1,000.

#### Do I need a new phone number or the WhatsApp API?

No. You link the WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business number you already use by scanning a QR code or entering a pairing code, the same way you link WhatsApp Web.

#### Will I be charged extra if I get a busy week?

No. There are no overage fees. When credits run out the AI pauses until you top up or your plan renews.

#### Is pay as you go or a monthly plan cheaper?

Per credit, a monthly plan is cheaper. Pay as you go is cheaper overall only when you use fewer than about 13,000 credits a month, which is roughly 14 chats a day.
