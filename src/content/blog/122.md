Dailzero and Zella are both AI sales assistants built in Nigeria for businesses that sell in chat. Zella covers WhatsApp and Instagram DMs, sends automatic receipts and has low monthly plans with a fixed number of messages. Dailzero focuses on WhatsApp, with larger message volumes, WhatsApp Status scheduling, contact saving and paced broadcasts, and it pauses when credits finish instead of charging extra. Choose Zella if Instagram DMs and receipts matter and your volume is low. Choose Dailzero if WhatsApp is your main channel and you want Status marketing in the same tool.

### Where Zella is the better choice

- **You sell on Instagram as well.** Zella answers Instagram DMs along with WhatsApp. Dailzero does not cover Instagram DMs.
- **You want payment and receipts handled in the chat.** Zella's site says it collects payments and sends receipts automatically. Dailzero takes the order and hands over to you, but receipts are not a listed feature.
- **Your message volume is low and steady.** At ₦4,000 a month for 300 messages as of November 2026, Zella has a lower entry subscription than Dailzero's ₦20,000 Basic plan.
- **You want a longer trial.** Zella lists 14 days against Dailzero's 7.
- **You want sales analytics in one dashboard.** Zella lists a dashboard for orders, conversations, analytics and receipts.

### Where Dailzero is the better choice

- **Your volume is higher.** A typical AI text reply uses about 5 credits, so Basic's 25,000 credits cover about 5,000 AI messages a month. A busy shop passes a few hundred messages quickly.
- **You want a hard stop, not extra charges.** When Dailzero credits finish the agent pauses until you renew or top up. There are no overage fees.
- **You market with WhatsApp Status.** Dailzero posts text, image or video Status on a schedule, runs Autopilot from a gallery (up to 6 posts a day, at least 2 hours apart), shows who viewed and replied, and answers people who reply to a Status.
- **You want your Status audience to grow.** It saves the contacts who message you so they can see your Status, at 1 credit per contact.
- **You want to pay only when you use it.** Pay as you go starts at ₦1,000 with no subscription, and credits last 12 months.
- **You want a clear answer on the connection.** Dailzero links your existing number as a linked device by default, and its app is approved by Meta for the official WhatsApp Business Platform, which the team sets up on request.
- **You want to build on it.** Dailzero has a developer API and a website chat widget that uses the same agent.

### Comparing the cost fairly

The two tools count differently. Zella counts messages and Dailzero counts credits, so convert before you compare.

1. Count how many AI replies you expect in a month.
2. On Zella, pick the plan that covers that number and add the per-message charge for anything above it.
3. On Dailzero, multiply replies by about 5 credits. Images cost 8 credits and videos 12. Add 5 credits for each text Status post and 1 credit for each contact saved.
4. Compare the two totals for your real volume, not the starting price.

A business with 200 replies a month will likely pay less on Zella. A business with several thousand will likely pay less on Dailzero.

### What you give up with Dailzero

You give up Instagram DMs, automatic receipts and the lowest entry subscription. The trial is shorter.

On the default route Dailzero is a linked device, not the official API. WhatsApp does not officially support automating an account this way, so it carries more risk than the official platform. Dailzero queues every message, sends at a human pace, warms up new numbers and slows down when WhatsApp signals a limit. No tool can promise a number will never be restricted, and we do not promise it. ### A quick way to decide

1. Do many of your orders come through Instagram DMs? Choose Zella.
2. Do you need receipts sent automatically after payment? Choose Zella.
3. Is WhatsApp your main channel, with Status posts driving most enquiries? Choose Dailzero.
4. Do you get thousands of messages a month, or want no charges above your plan? Choose Dailzero.

### Frequently asked questions

#### Is Dailzero or Zella cheaper?

Zella has the lower starting price, ₦4,000 a month for 300 messages as of November 2026. Dailzero gives far more volume per plan, about 5,000 AI messages on the ₦20,000 Basic plan. The cheaper one depends on how many messages you handle.

#### Does Zella work with Instagram?

Yes. Its site lists Instagram DMs alongside WhatsApp. Dailzero works on WhatsApp and a website chat widget.

#### Does Dailzero collect payments?

Dailzero's agent takes orders, captures leads and books appointments, then hands over to you where a person is needed. Automatic payment collection and receipts are not listed Dailzero features.

#### Which one schedules WhatsApp Status?

Dailzero does, with Autopilot and Status analytics. Zella's site does not list Status scheduling.

#### Can I try both for free?

Yes. Dailzero has a 7-day trial with 1,000 credits, and Zella lists a 14-day trial with 100 messages. Test each with real customer questions.
