### Where an online store's service money really goes

Salary is only the visible part. For most Nigerian stores the cost of answering customers is spread across three places:

- **Paid hours spent on the same ten questions.** "How much?", "Do you deliver to Benin?", "Is it available in black?", "Send account number."
- **Chats nobody answered.** Messages that arrive after closing time, on Sunday, or while your one attendant is busy with someone else. No invoice shows this cost, but it is real.
- **Your own time.** If you are the owner replying at midnight, that is time not spent on sourcing, content or sleep.

AI attacks all three. It answers the repeated questions in seconds, it is awake at 2am, and it handles many chats at once.

### Compare it with your current cost per chat

Do this with your own figures:

1. Add up what you spend each month on the people who answer customers: salary, data, airtime, and any commission.
2. Estimate how many chats they fully handle in a month.
3. Divide the first number by the second.

That is your current cost per chat. Put it beside ₦20 to ₦24. Then count the chats that got no answer at all last month. Those are not in your cost per chat, but they are in your lost sales.

### The costs that stay

Be realistic about what does not go away:

- **A person to take handoffs.** The AI passes a chat to you when the customer asks for a human or when it cannot help. Someone must pick those up.
- **Payment confirmation.** The AI does not verify transfers. It shares your account details or Paystack link, and a person confirms the alert.
- **Order status.** It cannot see where a dispatch rider is. Unless a developer connects your system through webhook tools, "where is my order?" goes to staff.
- **Keeping the information current.** Prices and stock change. Someone has to update the agent.

A sample handoff, so you know what to expect:

> "I received the wrong size and I want a refund."

The right behaviour is not an AI negotiation. It is a short, polite reply and a transfer:

> "I am sorry about that. I am passing this chat to a member of our team now so they can sort out the exchange or refund with you."

So the saving is in how many people you need on chat, and what they do all day.

### How stores usually make the switch

**Week one.** Run the 7-day free trial on your real number. It includes 1,000 credits, about 200 AI messages. Read every conversation.

**Weeks two to four.** Let the AI answer first on every chat. Your attendant handles only handoffs, payment confirmation and dispatch.

**After a month.** Look at how many chats needed a person. That number tells you how much human cover you really need, and whether to give your attendant other work such as packing, content or following up leads.

### Frequently asked questions

#### Can WhatsApp AI replace my customer service staff completely?

No. It handles the repeated questions and order-taking, but refunds, complaints and payment confirmation still need a person. Most stores end up needing fewer chat hours, not zero.

#### Are there hidden fees on top of the plan?

No setup fee, no overage fees and no per-conversation fees from Meta, because Dailzero links to your existing number like WhatsApp Web and is not the official WhatsApp Business Platform.

#### What if my chat volume drops in a slow month?

Unused monthly credits carry into the next month, up to 25% of your plan allowance. If your volume is very uneven, pay as you go packs last 12 months.
