### What you are actually paying for

Most WhatsApp AI tools charge in one of three ways:

- **Per message or per conversation.** Common with tools built on the official WhatsApp Business Platform, where Meta also charges its own fee on top.
- **A flat monthly subscription** with a message limit.
- **Credits**, where each reply uses a few credits and you buy them monthly or as you need them.

Dailzero uses credits. A text reply from the AI costs about 5 credits, an image 8, a video 12 and a document 8. There is no setup fee and no separate charge from Meta, because the agent runs on the WhatsApp number you already use.

### How to estimate your monthly cost

1. Count how many customers message you on a normal day.
2. Multiply by the number of replies a typical chat needs. Six is a fair average for a shop answering price and delivery questions.
3. Multiply by 5 credits, then by 30 days.

**Example:** 30 chats a day × 6 replies × 5 credits × 30 days = 27,000 credits. That is slightly above Basic, so you would either take Basic and add a ₦5,000 top-up, or move to Starter if you are growing.

**A smaller example:** 8 chats a day × 6 replies × 5 credits × 30 days = 7,200 credits. Pay as you go covers that for about ₦12,000, with no subscription.

### What makes the bill go up

- **Sending many photos or videos.** An image costs 8 credits and a video 12.
- **Broadcasts.** Messaging 1,000 customers with a text costs 5,000 credits.
- **Long back-and-forth chats**, such as negotiating a price or collecting delivery details.

### How this compares with hiring someone

A customer service assistant in Lagos typically costs ₦80,000 to ₦150,000 a month in salary, works about eight hours a day and handles one chat at a time. An AI agent on the Pro plan costs ₦75,000, replies at 2am and handles every chat at once.

It does not replace a person completely. Complaints, refunds and unusual requests still need a human, which is why the agent hands those chats to you.

### Frequently asked questions

#### Is there a free way to try it?

Yes. Every new Dailzero account gets a 7-day free trial with 1,000 credits, enough for about 200 AI replies.

#### Do unused credits roll over?

On a monthly plan, unused credits carry into the next month, up to 25% of your plan's allowance. Pay-as-you-go credits stay valid for 12 months.

#### Do I pay Meta separately?

No. Dailzero links to your existing WhatsApp number the same way WhatsApp Web does, so there are no per-conversation fees from Meta.

#### What happens when my credits run out?

The AI pauses and your customers' messages still arrive in WhatsApp as normal. You can reply yourself, top up, or wait for your plan to renew.

#### Which plan should a new business start on?

Start with the free trial, then pay as you go. Move to a monthly plan once you are spending more than ₦20,000 a month on top-ups.
