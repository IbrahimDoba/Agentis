Use the official WhatsApp API if you need compliance approval, approved message templates, a verified badge, or the lowest possible risk to your number. Use a linked device if you are a small business that wants to automate replies on the number you already use, start the same day and avoid Meta's message fees. The official API is safer and more formal. A linked device is faster and cheaper but is not officially supported for automation.

### What each one is

**The official WhatsApp API** is properly called the WhatsApp Business Platform. Its hosted version is Meta's Cloud API. A business registers a number on the platform, usually through a provider, and software sends and receives messages for it. Meta charges message fees, and messaging customers first uses templates that Meta approves.

**A linked device** uses the feature behind WhatsApp Web and WhatsApp Desktop. Your phone stays the main device and a tool connects as an extra device, by QR code or pairing code. The number stays in your normal WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business app.

### The differences that matter

| | Official WhatsApp API | Linked device |
|---|---|---|
| Supported by WhatsApp for automation | Yes | No |
| Risk to the number | Lowest, if you follow Meta's rules | Higher, and it depends on how the tool sends |
| Setup | Business verification and onboarding | Scan a QR code, minutes |
| Meta message fees | Yes | None |
| Your normal WhatsApp app | The number runs on the platform, not in the usual app | Keeps working as before |
| Messaging customers first | Approved templates | To your existing contacts only, paced |
| Verified badge | Can be applied for through Meta | Not available |
| WhatsApp Status posting | Not available | Available |
| Groups and personal chats on the number | Not part of the platform | Stay on your phone as before |
| Best for | Banks, large brands, regulated firms, high-volume notifications | Owners and small teams selling from their own number |

Meta changes its fees and rules from time to time, so check Meta's own pages or ask your provider before you budget.

### Where the official API wins

- **It is the allowed route.** Automation is what the platform is for. You can still be restricted for breaking messaging rules, but not for automating.
- **Compliance.** If a bank, regulator or parent company asks how you connect to WhatsApp, "the official Business Platform" is the answer they expect.
- **Templates.** You can message opted-in customers first with approved templates, such as delivery alerts and payment reminders.
- **Trust signals.** A verified badge is only possible through Meta.
- **Scale.** It is designed for large volumes and several support staff.

### Where a linked device wins

- **Speed.** You can be live the same day, with no business verification.
- **Cost.** There are no Meta fees. You pay only the tool.
- **Nothing moves.** Your number, chats, groups and app stay as they are, and you can still answer from your phone.
- **Status.** Posting and scheduling WhatsApp Status is only possible on a number in the normal app.
- **Low commitment.** If you stop, you log the device out from your phone and everything is as it was.

### The honest downside of each

**The official API** costs more, takes longer to set up and is less flexible for a one-person business. Templates need approval. The number no longer behaves like a normal WhatsApp account in your app, so features such as Status are not part of it.

**A linked device** is not officially supported for automation. A tool that sends in bursts, or messages strangers, can get a number restricted. A careful tool reduces that with human-paced sending and a warm-up period, but no tool can promise a number will never be restricted. Your phone also has to come online regularly, or WhatsApp signs linked devices out.

### A decision list

Work down the list and stop at the first "yes".

1. Does a regulator, bank or compliance team require an official Meta integration? **Use the official API.**
2. Do you need to send notifications to customers who have not messaged you, such as OTPs or delivery alerts? **Use the official API.**
3. Would losing the number for even a day badly damage the business? **Use the official API**, or automate a second number.
4. Do you need a verified badge? **Use the official API.**
5. Do you sell from your own number and mainly want customers answered quickly? **A linked device fits.**
6. Do you want to post WhatsApp Status on a schedule? **A linked device is the only route.**
7. Is your budget small and in Naira, with no room for per-message fees? **A linked device fits.**

### Frequently asked questions

#### Is using a linked device against WhatsApp's rules?

Linking a device is a normal WhatsApp feature. Automating an account through a linked device is not officially supported by WhatsApp, which is why it carries more risk than the official API.

#### Is the official WhatsApp API free?

No. Meta charges message fees, and most providers charge a platform fee as well. Check current rates with Meta or your provider.

#### Which is better for a small Nigerian business?

Usually a linked device, because it is quick, has no Meta fees and keeps the number you already use. If compliance, templates or the importance of the number say otherwise, choose the official API.

#### Can a number on the official API still be restricted?

Yes. Meta can limit or suspend a business that breaks its messaging policies. The risk is lower because automation itself is permitted.
