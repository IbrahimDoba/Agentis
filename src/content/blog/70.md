Qualifying a lead means finding out four things before you invest time: what the job is, the budget, the deadline, and who makes the decision. A WhatsApp AI agent can ask those four questions of every new enquiry, at any hour, and on Dailzero the people who show real buying intent are listed as leads in your dashboard. You then spend your time on the few chats that can become paid work.

### Why freelancers need this more than companies

A company has someone whose job is to reply. A freelancer has one pair of hands, and time spent explaining rates to someone who cannot afford them is time you cannot bill.

The usual enquiry gives almost nothing to go on:

> "Hi, how much for logo?"

You need at least four more messages to learn whether this is a serious client. That back and forth is what the agent takes over.

### The four qualifying questions

**1. What exactly do you need?** "A logo" might mean one mark, or a full identity with colours, fonts and social media templates. Have the agent ask what the deliverables are and what the business does.

**2. What is your budget?** Many Nigerian clients will not name a figure first. So state yours: give the agent your starting price for each service and let the client react to it. Their reaction is the answer.

**3. When do you need it?** "Yesterday" tells you about the client. A launch date three weeks away tells you it is a real project.

**4. Are you the one deciding?** For business work, ask whether they are the owner or are collecting quotes for a boss. It changes how you follow up.

### What a qualified chat looks like

Continuing the logo example, a well-set-up agent replies:

> "Hello. Logo design starts from ₦60,000, and a full brand identity from ₦150,000. What does your business do, and do you need only the logo or the complete identity?"

The client answers, and two or three messages later you have this in the chat: a skincare brand in Enugu, wants the full identity, launching next month, the owner is the one chatting, and ₦150,000 is within range. At that point the agent offers to book a call or hands the chat to you.

The other outcome is just as useful: the person says their budget is ₦10,000, the agent politely repeats your starting price, and the chat ends without costing you a minute.

### Setting it up in an afternoon

1. Sign up, describe what you do, and add each service with a starting price and typical turnaround.
2. Write the four questions as instructions, and tell the agent to ask them one or two at a time, not as a form.
3. Set your floor: "If the budget is below my starting price, say so kindly and do not negotiate."
4. Upload samples of your work so the agent can send them when asked. An image costs 8 credits, a document 8.
5. Give the times you are free for calls. The agent books appointments and sends reminders.
6. Link your number by QR code or pairing code and test it with a friend's phone.

### Use a work number, not your personal one

The agent replies on the number you link. If your clients, your mother and your church group all message the same WhatsApp, separate them first. Get a second SIM for work and put that number on your portfolio, Instagram bio and invoices.

One caution: a brand-new number should be used normally for about two weeks before you connect it.

### What to keep in your own hands

- **The final quote.** The agent gives starting prices. The actual figure for a specific scope should come from you.
- **Payment.** The agent can send the account details you gave it for a deposit, but it does not confirm transfers. You check the alert.
- **The relationship.** Once a lead is qualified, reply personally from your phone. The AI pauses on that chat when you do, until you switch it back on.

### Frequently asked questions

#### Can the AI negotiate my price?

It works from the prices and rules you give it. The safer instruction is to state your starting price, not negotiate, and hand the chat to you when a client asks for a discount.

#### What if I only get a few enquiries a week?

Then you may not need it. If you can answer every enquiry within the hour yourself, do that. The agent earns its cost when messages arrive while you are working, asleep or offline.

#### Will it reply to my friends and family on the same number?

It replies on the number you link. You can put a WhatsApp label on personal chats and set that label to AI off, so the agent stays silent on them, but a separate work number is simpler.
