A keyword chatbot replies when a customer's message contains a word you listed, such as "price" or "location". A WhatsApp AI agent reads the whole message, understands what the customer wants and writes a reply from your own business details. Keyword bots are cheap and predictable but break when customers type naturally. AI agents handle real conversation but cost more per reply and need good information from you.

### How each one works

**A keyword chatbot** is a list of rules. If the message contains "price", send the price list. If it contains "1", send the menu for option 1. The quick replies and away messages in the WhatsApp Business app work on the same idea. Nothing is understood. A match is found or it is not.

**An AI agent** uses a language model. You give it your products, prices, hours and policies. It reads each message, works out the intent, and answers in normal sentences. It can ask a follow-up question, remember what was said earlier in the chat, and hand over to a person.

### The same customer messages, two results

| Customer writes | Keyword chatbot | AI agent |
|---|---|---|
| "Price" | Sends the full price list | Asks which item, or sends the list |
| "How much be that red gown wey you post yesterday?" | No keyword match, or sends the whole list | Finds the red gown and gives its price |
| "Do you deliver to Abeokuta and how long?" | Sends the delivery message, may miss the town | Answers for Abeokuta if you gave it the fee and time |
| "I paid since morning, nobody has replied me" | Matches "paid" and sends account details again | Sees a complaint and hands over to a person |
| "Is the size 42 still available in black?" | No match | Answers from your stock details, or says it will check |
| A voice note | Cannot read it | Can transcribe it and answer, if the tool supports voice notes |
| "2" | Sends menu option 2 | Works too, but menus are not needed |

The fourth row is the one that costs businesses money. A keyword bot that replies to an angry customer with your account number makes things worse.

### Where a keyword chatbot is enough

Be honest about your needs. A keyword bot, or even the free tools in the WhatsApp Business app, is enough when:

- **Customers ask three or four fixed questions.** Opening hours, location, account number, price of one product.
- **You sell one thing at one price.** There is nothing to work out.
- **You only need an away message** for nights and Sundays.
- **Your volume is low.** If you get ten chats a day, you can answer them yourself.
- **You want every reply word for word.** A keyword bot never says anything you did not write.

Read [is a free WhatsApp chatbot enough for your business](/blog/is-a-free-whatsapp-chatbot-enough-for-your-business) before you pay for anything.

### Where an AI agent earns its cost

- **You have many products, sizes or prices.** Customers ask about them in a hundred different ways.
- **Customers write in a mix of English and Pidgin**, with typos and short forms.
- **Chats have more than one step.** Choosing an item, giving an address, booking a time.
- **You lose sales at night** because nobody answers.
- **You want leads and orders captured**, not only questions answered.

### The trade-offs of an AI agent

An AI agent is not better in every way.

- **It costs more per reply.** On Dailzero a typical AI reply uses about 5 credits. A keyword reply on most tools costs close to nothing.
- **It is only as good as what you give it.** With no price list it has nothing to answer from.
- **It can be wrong.** A well set up agent says "let me check with the team" when it does not know. You still need to test it and read chats in the first week.
- **It needs a handover plan.** Decide who replies when the AI steps back.

### Questions to help you choose

1. Do most of your customers ask the same few questions in the same few words? A keyword bot will do.
2. Do customers describe what they want in their own words, or send voice notes and photos? You need an AI agent.
3. Does a wrong automated reply cost you a sale or an angry customer? You need an AI agent with human handover.
4. Is your budget close to zero? Start with the free WhatsApp Business app tools and upgrade when chats outgrow them.

### Frequently asked questions

#### Is an AI agent the same as a chatbot?

Not quite. "Chatbot" covers both kinds. A keyword or menu chatbot follows fixed rules. An AI agent understands free text and can take actions such as recording an order or handing over to a person.

#### Are the quick replies in WhatsApp Business a keyword chatbot?

They are the simplest form of one. Greeting messages, away messages and quick replies send fixed text. They do not read what the customer asked.

#### Can a keyword chatbot understand Pidgin?

Only the exact words you list. If you add "how much" and the customer types "how much be dis", it may match. "Wetin be the last price" will not, unless you added it.

#### Can an AI agent give a wrong price?

It can if your prices are missing or out of date. Give it a complete price list and tell it never to guess. Then test it with a product you did not add.

#### Which is cheaper?

A keyword chatbot. An AI agent costs more per reply, so it is worth it only when it answers chats a keyword bot would miss.
