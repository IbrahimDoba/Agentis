Dailzero cannot give you a verified badge or approved message templates on its default linked-device route, it does not answer Instagram DMs, and each agent runs one WhatsApp number. Your phone has to come online regularly, the AI can be wrong, and no tool can promise your number will never be restricted. This page lists each limit plainly and says what to use instead.

### No verified badge or approved templates on the linked-device route

The default way to connect Dailzero is as a linked device, like WhatsApp Web. That route is not the official API. A verified badge and Meta-approved message templates exist only on the official WhatsApp Business Platform.

**Use instead:** the official platform. Dailzero offers it as a second route, set up with its team on request, and Meta's own message fees apply. If you need a large shared inbox for many support staff, an established official-API provider may suit you better. See [Dailzero vs WATI](/blog/dailzero-vs-wati) for one such comparison.

### No Status features on the official route

The reverse is also true. WhatsApp Status posting, Autopilot, Status analytics and bulk contact saving work only on the linked-device route. A number on the official platform cannot use them.

**Use instead:** the linked-device route, or keep a second number in the normal app for Status.

### WhatsApp only, no Instagram DMs

Dailzero answers customers on WhatsApp, and through a chat widget you can put on your own website. It does not reply to Instagram DMs, Facebook Messenger or TikTok messages.

It does work with click-to-WhatsApp ads. When a customer taps a Facebook or Instagram ad and lands in your WhatsApp, the agent knows which ad they came from.

**Use instead:** put your WhatsApp link in your Instagram bio and replies so buyers move to WhatsApp, or use a tool built for Instagram DMs alongside Dailzero.

### One number per agent

Each agent runs one WhatsApp number. Basic and Starter include one agent. Pro includes two.

**Use instead:** Pro if you have two numbers or two brands, or an Enterprise plan for more. Ask through the [contact page](/contact).

### Your phone must come online regularly

On the linked-device route your phone is still the main device. The agent keeps replying while your phone is locked or briefly offline, but if the phone stays off or without data for a long time, WhatsApp signs linked devices out and the agent stops until you link again.

**Use instead:** keep the business phone charged and connected. If that is not possible, the official platform does not depend on a phone.

### The AI can be wrong

The agent answers from the products, prices and policies you give it. If something is missing or out of date, it can give a wrong answer, and it can misread an unusual message. Dailzero's own terms say AI replies may contain inaccuracies and should be monitored.

**Use instead:** nothing replaces checking. Do these four things:

1. Keep prices and stock information current.
2. Tell the agent what it must never promise.
3. Leave human handover on, so a customer who asks for a person reaches you.
4. Read conversations in the first week and fix what you find.

### No promise that your number will never be restricted

On the linked-device route Dailzero queues every message, sends at a human pace, warms up new numbers and slows down automatically when WhatsApp signals a limit. That lowers the risk. It does not remove it, and no tool can promise a number will never be restricted.

**Use instead:** a second number for automation if losing your main line would badly damage your business, or the official platform. The habits that protect a number are in [how to stop WhatsApp banning your business number](/blog/how-to-stop-whatsapp-banning-your-business-number).

### Other limits worth knowing

- **Status reach is capped per post:** 2,000 contacts on Basic, 5,000 on Starter and 10,000 on Pro.
- **Autopilot posts up to 6 Status updates a day**, at least 2 hours apart, on a plan you approve.
- **Status views undercount.** People who have read receipts off do not show as viewers. That is how WhatsApp works.
- **Free accounts save up to 200 contacts in total.**
- **When credits finish, the agent pauses.** There are no overage fees, but customers get no AI reply until you renew or top up. Keep a small pay-as-you-go balance as cover. See the [pricing page](/pricing).
- **Dailzero is a young product** with no public review-site ratings yet. The 7-day free trial is the way to judge it.

### Frequently asked questions

#### Can Dailzero reply to Instagram DMs?

No. Dailzero works on WhatsApp and on a website chat widget. It does not answer Instagram, Messenger or TikTok messages.

#### Can I get a green tick with Dailzero?

Not on the linked-device route. A verified badge is applied for through Meta on the official WhatsApp Business Platform, which Dailzero offers on request.

#### Can one agent handle two WhatsApp numbers?

No. Each agent runs one number. The Pro plan includes two agents.

#### Does Dailzero work when my phone is off?

For a short time, yes. If the phone stays offline for a long stretch, WhatsApp signs linked devices out and you must link again.

#### Does Dailzero guarantee my number will not be banned?

No. It lowers the risk with human-paced sending, a warm-up period and automatic slow-down, but no tool can promise a number will never be restricted.
