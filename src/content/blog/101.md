Dailzero and WATI solve the same problem, answering customers on WhatsApp, in two different ways. WATI runs on Meta's official WhatsApp Business Platform, is priced in US dollars and is built for teams sharing one number. Dailzero links to the WhatsApp number you already use, is priced in Naira and puts an AI agent on it with no Meta fees. A small Nigerian business usually starts faster and cheaper on Dailzero. A larger team that needs the official API should choose WATI.

WATI's plans and Meta's fees change, so check WATI's own pricing page before you budget. We do not quote their prices here because third-party sources disagree on them.

### Where WATI is the better choice

- **You need the official API.** Banks, large brands and anyone whose compliance team asks for an official Meta integration should be on the official platform. WATI is an established provider of it.
- **You have a support team.** WATI's shared inbox lets several agents assign, tag and follow up chats on one number.
- **You want a verified badge.** That comes only through Meta's platform.
- **You need ready-made integrations**, such as a Shopify app or a CRM connection.
- **You send bulk notifications to people who have not messaged you**, such as OTPs or delivery alerts, using approved templates.

### Where Dailzero is the better choice

- **You sell from your own WhatsApp number and want to keep it.** Nothing moves. Your chats, your app and your customers' saved contact stay as they are.
- **You want the AI to do the selling.** The agent answers from your products and prices, sends photos, takes orders, books appointments and hands over to you when a person is needed.
- **You want to pay in Naira with no surprise fees.** There is no Meta per-message charge and no overage. When credits finish, the agent pauses.
- **You want to start today.** There is no business verification and no template approval.
- **Your volume is small or irregular.** Pay as you go from ₦1,000 has no subscription.

### What you give up with Dailzero

Dailzero is not the official API. That means no verified badge, no approved templates and no WhatsApp Flows. Your phone stays the main device and should come online regularly. Each agent runs one number.

It also means number safety matters. Dailzero queues every message and sends at a human pace, and new numbers go through a warm-up period. No tool can promise a number will never be restricted. If that risk is unacceptable for your business, the official platform is the right route. Read [how to stop WhatsApp banning your business number](/blog/how-to-stop-whatsapp-banning-your-business-number) for the detail.

### What it costs to try each

Dailzero has a 7-day free trial with 1,000 credits, about 200 AI replies, and you can link your number in minutes. See the [pricing page](/pricing).

With WATI, budget for three things: the monthly plan, any add-ons for extra users or numbers, and Meta's own message fees.

### A quick way to decide

1. Do you need a verified badge, or does compliance require the official API? Choose WATI or another official provider.
2. Do more than a handful of staff need to work one number from a shared inbox? Choose WATI.
3. Are you an owner or small team who wants an AI to answer and sell on your existing number, paid in Naira? Choose Dailzero.

### Frequently asked questions

#### Is Dailzero a WATI alternative?

For a small or medium Nigerian business that wants AI replies on its existing number, yes. For a business that needs the official WhatsApp Business Platform, no. WATI and Dailzero connect to WhatsApp in different ways.

#### Is WATI available in Nigeria?

Yes, WATI serves businesses in many countries, including Nigeria. Its plans are billed in US dollars, and Meta's message fees for Nigeria apply on top.

#### Do I pay Meta when I use Dailzero?

No. Dailzero links to your number as a linked device, so there are no Meta per-message or per-conversation fees. You pay only for Dailzero credits.

#### Can I move from WATI to Dailzero, or the other way?

Yes, but they use your number differently. A number registered on the official platform has to be set up for normal app use before Dailzero can link to it, and the reverse applies too. Ask your provider how the switch works for your number before you start.
