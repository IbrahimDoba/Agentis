To see who viewed your WhatsApp Status, open the Updates tab, tap "My status" and tap the eye icon at the bottom of the post. WhatsApp lists each viewer and the time they watched. The list is only there while the Status is live, 24 hours, and it leaves out anyone who has read receipts turned off, so the real number of views is usually higher than the count you see.

### How to check viewers in WhatsApp, step by step

1. Open WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business.
2. Go to **Updates** (called **Status** on some versions).
3. Tap **My status**.
4. Tap the post you want to check.
5. Tap the **eye icon** with a number at the bottom, or swipe up.

You will see names and view times. Likes show as a heart beside the person's name. Replies do not appear here. A reply to a Status arrives as a normal message in your chat with that person.

### Why the count is lower than the truth

- **Read receipts.** If a contact has turned off read receipts, they can watch your Status without appearing in your list. If you have turned off your own read receipts, you cannot see who viewed your Status at all. Check under Settings, Privacy.
- **Only saved contacts count.** Your Status is shown to people saved in your phone. Customers who are unsaved chats never see it, so they can never be viewers. See [how to save all your WhatsApp contacts automatically](/blog/how-to-save-all-your-whatsapp-contacts-automatically).
- **Muted statuses.** A contact who muted you will not see new posts.
- **Privacy settings.** If your Status privacy is set to "Only share with" or "My contacts except", some people are excluded by your own setting.

So treat the viewer count as a minimum, not a full figure.

### What WhatsApp does not give you

The built-in list is useful but thin for a business.

- It disappears with the Status after 24 hours.
- There are no totals across posts, so you cannot easily compare Monday with Friday.
- It does not tell you how many people the post was delivered to.
- Replies are scattered across your chats and not counted anywhere.

The manual fix is a notebook or a spreadsheet. Before each Status expires, write down the post, the time it went up, the number of views and the number of replies you received. After two or three weeks you will see which products and which times pull the most views.

### Frequently asked questions

#### Can I see who viewed my WhatsApp Status after 24 hours?

Not in WhatsApp. The viewer list goes when the Status expires. Write the numbers down before then, or post through a tool that records them.

#### Why can I not see any viewers on my Status?

The usual cause is that your own read receipts are off. Turn them on under Settings, Privacy, and viewers will show on your next posts.

#### Can someone view my Status without me knowing?

Yes. A contact with read receipts turned off can view it without appearing in your list. This is a WhatsApp privacy feature and no tool can get around it.

#### Can I see how many times one person viewed my Status?

No. WhatsApp shows that a person viewed it and when, not how many times.

#### Does WhatsApp Business show more Status statistics than normal WhatsApp?

No. The Status viewer list is the same in both apps.
