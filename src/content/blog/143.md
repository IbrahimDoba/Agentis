WhatsApp and WhatsApp Business have no built-in way to schedule a Status post. To post at a set time you either do it by hand with a reminder on your phone, or you use a tool that links to your number and posts for you. In Dailzero you open Status, go to the Posts tab, tick "Schedule for later", pick a date and time, and click "Schedule post".

### Why scheduling matters

A Status lasts 24 hours, and most views come in the first few hours after it goes up. If you post only when you remember, you miss the times your customers are actually on their phones: early morning, lunch break and evening. A schedule also keeps your Status alive on days when you are in the market, at the shop or travelling.

### How to do it by hand, with no tool

WhatsApp will not hold a post for later, so the manual way is about preparing in advance.

1. **Prepare a week of posts in one sitting.** Take your photos and videos, and write the captions in your notes app.
2. **Put the media in one album** on your phone so you are not searching your gallery each time.
3. **Set alarms or calendar reminders** for your posting times, for example 9am, 1pm and 7pm.
4. **When the alarm goes, open WhatsApp**, go to Updates, tap the camera or pencil, pick the media, paste the caption and post.
5. **Check the viewer list later** to learn which times work for your customers.

This costs nothing and it works. Its weakness is that it depends on you being free at the exact time, every day.

Avoid modified apps such as GB WhatsApp that advertise a Status scheduler. They break WhatsApp's terms and put your number at risk. See [how to stop WhatsApp banning your business number](/blog/how-to-stop-whatsapp-banning-your-business-number).

### How to schedule a Status in Dailzero

Dailzero links to the WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business number you already use, the same way WhatsApp Web does, and posts the Status from that number.

1. **Link your number.** Scan the QR code or enter the pairing code in the dashboard.
2. **Open Status in the sidebar** and stay on the **Posts** tab.
3. **Choose the post type**: image, video or text.
4. **Add the content.** For text, write under "Your status" and pick a background colour. For an image or video, upload the file or pick one from your media library, then add a caption if you want one.
5. **Fill in "Tell the AI about this post".** Put the price, sizes and delivery details here. When someone replies to the Status, the AI reads this note first and answers them.
6. **Tick "Schedule for later"** and choose the date and time. You can schedule up to 30 days ahead.
7. **Click "Schedule post".** The post appears under "Your posts" as Scheduled.

To post immediately, leave the box unticked and click "Post now". To keep a post for another day without posting it, click "Save to gallery".

### Changing or cancelling a scheduled post

A scheduled post has a **Cancel** button under "Your posts". To change it, cancel it and schedule it again. A post that has already gone out has **Delete**, which removes it from WhatsApp for everyone who has not seen it yet, and **Post again**, which reloads the same content into the composer with its caption and AI note.

### Two things to get right first

**Save your contacts.** A Status only reaches people saved in your phone. If your customers are unsaved chats, a perfectly timed post still reaches very few people. Read [how to save all your WhatsApp contacts automatically](/blog/how-to-save-all-your-whatsapp-contacts-automatically).

**Do not flood.** Three good posts a day beat ten. People mute accounts that post too much, and a muted Status is never seen again.

### Where Dailzero does not fit

Status scheduling works on the linked-device connection only. If your number runs on the official WhatsApp Business Platform, Status is not available through that route. And as with any linked-device tool, no one can promise a number will never be restricted, so a new number should be used normally for about two weeks before you connect it.

### Frequently asked questions

#### Can I schedule a WhatsApp Status inside WhatsApp itself?

No. Neither WhatsApp nor WhatsApp Business has a scheduling option for Status. You post by hand at the time, or use a tool that links to your number.

#### Does my phone need to be online when the scheduled Status goes out?

The post is sent from Dailzero's linked connection, not from your phone screen. Your phone stays the main device and should come online regularly so the link stays active. If the number is disconnected, the post waits until it reconnects.

#### How many Status posts can I schedule in a day?

Up to 10 posts a day across everything you post from Dailzero. For most businesses two to four a day is enough.

#### Do I pay for each person who views the Status?

No. A Status post costs a flat 5, 8 or 12 credits depending on whether it is text, an image or a video. There is no per-recipient charge.

#### Can I schedule a Status on the free trial?

Yes. The 7-day trial comes with 1,000 credits, and Status posts are paid from the same credits as AI replies.
