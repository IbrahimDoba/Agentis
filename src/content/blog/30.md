An AI agent for land and property sales is software that answers buyers on your WhatsApp number using the facts you give it: plot sizes, prices, titles, payment plans, location and inspection days. It can send your survey plan and brochure, collect a buyer's details, book a site inspection and flag serious buyers. It cannot verify a title, give legal advice or confirm a payment, and in land sales those limits matter as much as the features.

This guide covers what to load into it, where to draw the lines, what it costs and who should not use one.

### Why land sales suit an AI agent

Land buyers in Nigeria ask more questions before paying than almost any other customer, and with good reason. Stories of double allocation, fake documents and omo onile demands have made people careful. The same dozen questions come from every prospect:

- What is the title? C of O, Governor's Consent, Gazette, excision in process?
- What size is a plot, and is it dry land?
- Is there a survey plan and a deed of assignment?
- What other fees are there: survey, legal, development levy?
- Can I pay in instalments, and for how long?
- When is the next inspection, and is it free?

### What to load before going live

The agent only knows what you tell it. For each estate or parcel, write down:

1. **Location in buyer's terms.** Not only "Epe" but the nearest known landmark and the travel time from a place people know.
2. **Plot sizes and prices** for each size, outright and on instalment.
3. **The title, stated exactly.** If the excision is in process and not yet granted, say that. Do not let the agent round it up.
4. **Every extra fee** with its amount, and when each is due.
5. **Documents the buyer receives** and at what stage: receipt, contract of sale, survey plan, deed of assignment, allocation letter.
6. **Inspection schedule,** meeting point and whether transport is provided.
7. **Company details:** registered name, office address, the account name payments go to.

Then upload the brochure, layout, survey plan sample, site photos and drone or walkthrough videos to the media library so the agent can send them.

### A sample conversation

> Good day. How much is your land at Ibeju Lekki and what's the document?

> Good day, and thanks for your interest. Our estate at Eleko, Ibeju-Lekki has 300 sqm plots at ₦8 million and 600 sqm at ₦15 million. The title is a registered survey with Governor's Consent. You can pay outright or spread it over 6 months. May I send the brochure and a video of the site?

The prices and title in that reply are placeholders.

### Lines the agent must not cross

Set these as explicit instructions.

- **No legal advice.** It can explain in general terms what a C of O or a Governor's Consent is. Whether a specific title is good is a question for the buyer's lawyer and a search at the registry.
- **No promises you did not write.** No "it will appreciate by next year", no "government will never disturb it". If you did not load it, the agent should not say it.
- **No payment confirmation.** Dailzero does not process or verify payments. It shares your company account details and hands the chat to you to confirm the transfer and issue a receipt.
- **Company account only.** Tell buyers through the agent that payments go only to the company account name, never to an individual. This protects them and you.
- **Negotiation goes to a person.**

### What it costs

Take a land company that gets 20 WhatsApp enquiries a day. Each chat needs about 7 text replies, 2 photos and the brochure.

- Text: 7 × 5 credits = 35
- Photos: 2 × 8 credits = 16
- Brochure: 1 document × 8 credits = 8

### Who should not use one

- A seller whose titles are unclear. An AI that answers fast will only expose the gaps faster. Fix the paperwork first.
- A firm that needs the official WhatsApp API, a verified badge or enterprise-scale teams on one number.

### Frequently asked questions

#### Can the AI agent confirm that a land title is genuine?

No. It repeats the title information you supplied. Verification is done by the buyer's lawyer or surveyor through the proper registry.

#### Can it send the survey plan and deed sample?

Yes, if you upload them to the media library. Decide which documents are safe to share before payment and load only those.
