A Nigerian law firm can use AI on WhatsApp for client intake, not for legal advice. The AI answers questions about your practice areas, office hours and the consultation fee you publish, collects the basic facts of an enquiry, books a consultation and then hands the chat to a lawyer. It must never tell someone what their legal position is or what they should do.

### What the AI can safely handle

- **Practice areas.** "Do you handle land matters?" "Do you do company registration with CAC?"
- **Logistics.** Office address in Lagos or Abuja, opening hours, parking, whether you take virtual consultations.
- **Published fees.** The first consultation fee and how to pay it, if you choose to publish it. It can share your firm's account details.
- **Document checklists.** What to bring to a consultation, as a general list you wrote.
- **Booking.** Offering your available consultation slots and sending a reminder before the appointment.
- **Intake details.** Name, phone number, the type of matter, the other party's name, and any deadline the person mentions, such as a court date.

### What must go to a lawyer

- Any question that asks "what should I do?", "is this legal?" or "can I win?"
- Whether a time limit has passed on a claim.
- Anything about an existing client's matter, its status or its documents.
- Fee quotes for actual work, which depend on the facts.
- Anyone in urgent trouble: an arrest, an eviction happening today, a threat of violence.

### The same message, handled wrongly and rightly

A typical enquiry reads like this:

> Good evening. My landlord gave me seven days to pack out and I paid my rent till December. Can he do that?

The wrong reply tries to answer: "No, he must give you proper notice." That is legal advice given without knowing the agreement, the state or the facts.

The right reply acknowledges, declines to advise, and moves the person to a lawyer quickly:

> Good evening, and sorry you are dealing with this. I am the firm's assistant, so I cannot advise on your rights, but one of our lawyers can. A first consultation costs the fee shown on our price list. Could you tell me which state the property is in and whether you have a written tenancy agreement? I can book you for tomorrow morning.

Your lawyer starts the consultation knowing the basics, and nobody has relied on a chatbot's opinion.

### Set it up with the guardrails first

1. **Write the refusal before anything else.** In the agent's instructions, state that it does not give legal advice, opinions or predictions, and must offer a consultation or hand over instead. Dailzero is not a regulated-advice tool: on legal topics it answers general questions and passes case-specific ones to a person.
2. **Add the firm's facts:** practice areas, lawyers' availability, the consultation fee, payment details, office locations.
3. **Add intake questions per practice area.** Property, family, employment, corporate and debt recovery each need three or four different questions.
4. **Add urgency rules.** Tell the agent that words such as "arrested", "police", "court tomorrow" or "bailiff" mean an immediate handover.
5. **Test it with hard questions.** Try to push it into giving advice, and tighten the instructions wherever it slips.
6. **Link the firm's number** by QR or pairing code and go live. The steps are on the [how it works page](/how-it-works).

### Three things specific to legal practice

**Conflict checks.** Because the agent collects the other party's name at intake, you can check for a conflict of interest before the consultation.

**Confidentiality.** Chats pass through a third-party tool. Have the agent ask for a short summary only, and tell enquirers not to send sensitive documents until a lawyer asks. Existing clients' privileged discussions stay with their lawyer.

**Advertising rules.** Lawyers work under professional conduct rules on advertising and soliciting. Check them before you use broadcasts or scheduled Status posts.

### What happens at handover

A lawyer or the practice manager replies from the dashboard or the phone. The AI pauses on that chat until you switch it back on. For an ongoing client, leave it off.

The agent shares your account details for the consultation fee but cannot verify a payment. A person confirms the transfer.

### Frequently asked questions

#### Can the AI give simple legal information, like how long a process takes?

Only general statements you have written and are comfortable publishing. Anything that depends on a person's facts should go to a lawyer.

#### What if someone messages at 2am with an emergency?

The agent replies at once, collects the key facts and flags the chat for handover. Decide in advance who checks those out of hours, because the AI cannot phone anyone.

#### Does it work on our existing office WhatsApp number?

Yes. It links to the number you already use, as a linked device. Your phone remains the main device and should come online regularly.
