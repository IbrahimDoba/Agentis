Dailzero and respond.io are aimed at different sizes of business. Respond.io is a global customer conversation platform that puts WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger, TikTok, email and calls into one team inbox, priced in US dollars with WhatsApp fees on top. Dailzero is an AI WhatsApp agent for Nigerian businesses that links to the number you already use and is priced in Naira. Choose respond.io if you run a sales or support team across several channels. Choose Dailzero if you are an owner or small team selling mainly on WhatsApp.

### Where respond.io is the better choice

- **Your customers reach you on many channels.** Respond.io unifies WhatsApp, TikTok, Instagram and Messenger in one inbox, with email and calls in the same thread. Dailzero covers WhatsApp and website chat only.
- **You run a team of agents.** It offers smart routing, team collaboration, custom inboxes and reports that show which ads, agents and channels bring results.
- **You need calls and chat together.** It lists WhatsApp Business Calls, Messenger Calls and VoIP alongside messages.
- **You need enterprise controls.** Its higher plans list single sign-on, multiple workspaces, webhooks and custom channels.
- **You want deep workflow automation.** Workflows, Zapier and Make integrations and a developer API are listed from the Growth plan.
- **You must be on the official WhatsApp API.** Its WhatsApp connection is through the WhatsApp Business API.

### Where Dailzero is the better choice

- **You pay and earn in Naira.** Dailzero's plans run from ₦20,000 to ₦75,000 a month, paid through Paystack by card or bank transfer. A dollar subscription moves with the exchange rate.
- **You want no fees on top.** On the linked-device route there are no Meta message fees, and there is no overage. When credits finish, the agent pauses.
- **You want to keep your number in your own app.** Dailzero links like WhatsApp Web. You can still reply from your phone, and the AI pauses on that chat when you do.
- **You want the AI to do the selling without a team behind it.** The agent answers from your prices, sends product photos, takes orders, books appointments and hands over when a person is needed.
- **You market with WhatsApp Status.** Dailzero schedules Status, runs Autopilot, saves the contacts who message you and answers Status replies.
- **Your volume is small or irregular.** Pay as you go from ₦1,000 has no subscription, and credits last 12 months.

### What you give up with Dailzero

You give up the multi-channel inbox. Dailzero does not handle Instagram, Messenger, TikTok, email or calls. Its team features are simpler too: team logins and human takeover, not routing rules and agent performance reports. Each agent runs one number.

On the default route Dailzero is a linked device, not the official API. There are no approved templates and no verified badge, and WhatsApp does not officially support automating an account this way, so it carries more risk than the official platform. Dailzero queues every message, sends at a human pace, warms up new numbers and slows down when WhatsApp signals a limit. No tool can promise a number will never be restricted. Read [how to stop WhatsApp banning your business number](/blog/how-to-stop-whatsapp-banning-your-business-number).

If you need the official route, Dailzero's app is approved by Meta and the team sets it up on request. Meta's fees apply on that route.

### What to budget for each

With respond.io, plan for three costs: the monthly plan in dollars, extra charges if your monthly active contacts or AI credits go over the plan, and WhatsApp fees that Meta charges by usage.

With Dailzero on the linked-device route, plan for one cost: credits. A typical AI text reply uses about 5 credits, an image 8 and a video 12. Basic gives 25,000 credits a month, Starter 60,000 and Pro 100,000.

### A quick way to decide

1. Do you have a support or sales team working chats from several channels? Choose respond.io.
2. Do you need calls, routing rules and reports per agent? Choose respond.io.
3. Do most of your sales happen on one WhatsApp number that you or a few staff manage? Choose Dailzero.
4. Is a dollar bill with usage fees hard to plan for? Choose Dailzero.

### Frequently asked questions

#### Is Dailzero a respond.io alternative?

For a Nigerian business that sells mainly on WhatsApp and wants an AI agent paid in Naira, yes. For a company that needs one inbox for many channels and a large team, respond.io does more.

#### Is respond.io available in Nigeria?

Respond.io is a global platform and its site does not limit it by country. Its plans are in US dollars, and WhatsApp fees are charged separately by Meta.

#### How much does respond.io cost?

As of November 2026 its pricing page lists Starter at $79, Growth at $159 and Advanced at $279 a month when billed yearly, with WhatsApp fees not included. Check its pricing page for current figures.

#### Does Dailzero work with Instagram or Messenger?

No. Dailzero works on WhatsApp and through a website chat widget that uses the same agent.

#### Can I start on Dailzero and move to a bigger platform later?

Yes. Many businesses start with an AI agent on their own number and move to an official-API platform when the team and channels grow. Dailzero can also set up the official route for you on request.
