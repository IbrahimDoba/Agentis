The WhatsApp Business API, now officially called the WhatsApp Business Platform, is Meta's programmatic way for a company to send and receive WhatsApp messages from its own software instead of from a phone. A Nigerian payment company needs it for one-time passwords, transaction alerts and very high volumes. It does not need it just to answer customer questions or handle sales inquiries on WhatsApp, and Dailzero is not the official API.

### What the official API actually is

There are three ways a business can be on WhatsApp:

- **The WhatsApp Business app.** Free, runs on a phone, built for a person typing replies.
- **The official WhatsApp Business Platform (Cloud API).** Hosted by Meta. Your systems talk to it directly or through a Business Solution Provider, a company that resells access with its own dashboard on top.
- **A linked-device tool.** Software that connects to your existing number the same way WhatsApp Web does, by QR code or pairing code. Dailzero works this way.

### How the official API works for a payment company

Four things define it:

1. **Business verification.** You apply through Meta with your company documents, and the number is registered to the platform.
2. **Templates.** If you message a customer first, or after the 24-hour window since their last message has closed, you must use a message template that Meta has approved in advance. Templates fall into categories such as authentication, utility and marketing.
3. **Meta's charges.** Meta bills for template messages, with rates that vary by category and by the recipient's country. Check Meta's current rate card and your provider's quote.
4. **Scale and badge.** It is built for large volumes and many support agents on one number, and it is the route to applying for a verified badge. The badge is Meta's decision and is not guaranteed.

### How the linked-device approach differs

| | Official API | Linked device (Dailzero) |
|---|---|---|
| Connection | Meta approval and registration | QR code or pairing code, like WhatsApp Web |
| Messaging customers first | Approved templates only | Broadcasts to existing contacts, paced |
| Meta fees | Yes, on template messages | None |
| Verified badge | Can apply | Not available |
| OTPs and instant alerts | Yes | Not suitable |
| Your phone | Not the sending device | Stays the main device, must come online regularly |

With Dailzero, every message is queued and sent at a human pace to protect the number. That is good for conversations and wrong for an OTP that has to arrive in three seconds.

### When a payment company needs the official API

Use it if any of these describe you:

- You send OTPs or login codes over WhatsApp.
- You send debit, credit or settlement alerts to large numbers of customers or merchants.
- You message people who have not messaged you first, at scale.
- You need a verified badge so customers can tell you apart from impersonators.
- You have a large support floor with many agents sharing one number.

### When a linked-device AI agent is enough

Use it when the job is conversation, and the volume is what a small or mid-sized team handles:

- A merchant asks about your POS terminal: price, caution fee, settlement time, how to apply.
- A customer asks how chargebacks work or which documents a business account needs.

> Good evening, I need POS for my shop in Aba. How much and how do I get it?

A good reply gives the price and requirements you set, asks for the business name, location and expected daily volume, and passes the lead to your sales team with those details already collected.

### Can you use both?

Yes, and many payment companies should. Keep the official API on your main, verified number for OTPs and alerts. Put a linked-device AI agent on a separate sales or merchant-support number that people message from ads and your website. One WhatsApp number can only do one of these jobs, so plan for two numbers.

### What Dailzero's own API is, and is not

Dailzero has an HTTPS API for developers: send messages and media from your code, run the AI agent through a chat endpoint, and connect webhook tools so the agent can look something up in your backend mid-chat. It still sends through your linked number at a safe pace, so it is not a substitute for Meta's platform for authentication or bulk alerts. Details are in the [developer documentation](/developers).

### Frequently asked questions

#### Is Dailzero the official WhatsApp Business API?

No. Dailzero links to your existing number as a linked device, like WhatsApp Web. There is no template approval, no Meta fee and no verified badge through Dailzero.

#### Can I send OTPs through a linked-device tool?

You should not. Messages are deliberately paced to protect the number, so they are not built for time-critical codes. Use the official API or SMS for authentication.

#### How much does the official API cost in Nigeria?

It depends on Meta's rate for each template category and what your provider adds. Ask for a written quote based on your expected monthly volume in each category.
