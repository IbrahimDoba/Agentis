### What a fashion vendor needs from a chatbot

Fashion sells on pictures and quick answers. A useful tool should be able to:

- Answer "how much", "do you have size 42" and "do you deliver to Abuja" without you typing.
- Send photos and videos of the item the customer asked about.
- Understand a screenshot or voice note a customer sends.
- Take the order details and pass the chat to you for payment or special requests.
- Help you post new arrivals on WhatsApp Status, where many repeat sales start.

### How to test one before you pay

1. Write down the ten questions customers ask you most.
2. Start a free trial and add your real prices and photos.
3. Ask the ten questions from another phone, including one in Pidgin and one as a voice note.
4. Check that the right photo comes back and the price is correct.
5. Check how you take over the chat when a customer wants to negotiate.

### Frequently asked questions

#### Can a chatbot send pictures of my clothes?

Yes. With Dailzero you upload photos, videos and documents to a media library, and the agent sends the ones that match what the customer asked for.

#### Will it understand a customer who sends a screenshot?

Dailzero reads images and voice notes customers send. Test it with your own stock, because results depend on how well your products are described.

#### Does Dailzero reply to Instagram DMs?

No. Dailzero works on WhatsApp. If a customer taps a click-to-WhatsApp ad on Instagram, the agent knows which ad they came from, but it does not answer inside Instagram.
