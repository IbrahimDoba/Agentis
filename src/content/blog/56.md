If you sell through WhatsApp, your abandoned cart is the customer who asked "how much?" and never replied. You reduce it in three ways: reply within seconds so the buyer is still paying attention, remove the reasons people hesitate before they have to ask, and send one or two polite follow-ups. One thing to know first: Dailzero does not connect to a website shopping cart, so this guide is about the WhatsApp version of the problem.

### What abandonment looks like on WhatsApp

On WhatsApp there is no cart, only a chat that stops. Say you sell wigs and hair extensions in Lagos:

> Hi, how much is the 14 inch bone straight?

You reply with the price forty minutes later. Two blue ticks. Nothing after that.

That customer did not necessarily decide against you. Something got in the way. Your job is to find out what.

### Five reasons buyers go quiet, and the fix for each

#### 1. The reply came too late

She asked three vendors at the same time. By the time you answered, another had already sent a price, a video and an account number. An AI agent replies in seconds at any hour, which keeps you in the comparison.

#### 2. The price arrived alone

A bare "₦185,000" invites silence. A price with the reason for it does better: the grade of hair, the density, what is included, a clear photo or short video from your media library. Load these into the agent so every price goes out with its proof.

#### 3. The delivery cost was a surprise

Many buyers disappear at the moment they hear the delivery fee. Give it early, with the timeline: "Delivery to Ajah is ₦3,500 and arrives tomorrow." A fee the customer already expects does not end a chat.

#### 4. She is not sure you are real

Paying a stranger by transfer is a risk, and buyers know it. Tell the agent to share what builds trust without being asked: your shop address or pickup point, your return policy, whether you allow pay on delivery in certain areas, and your business name as it appears on the bank account.

#### 5. She meant to come back and forgot

Salary has not landed. NEPA took light and the phone died. A meeting started. This buyer is not lost at all. She needs a reminder.

### The follow-up that brings them back

On the Dailzero Pro plan, the agent sends automated follow-ups to customers who went quiet.

A follow-up should add something, not just poke:

> Hello again. The 14 inch bone straight you asked about is still available in natural black. If the budget is the issue, the 12 inch is ₦160,000. Would you like me to hold one for you till tomorrow?

Some rules that keep follow-ups welcome:

- **Send few.** One or two. A third and fourth start to feel like harassment, and customers who block or report you put your number at risk.
- **Give a reason to reply.** A lower-priced option, a note that stock is running low if it truly is, or an answer to the objection you suspect.
- **Make it easy to say no.** A line such as "no problem if you have changed your mind" removes the pressure.

Pro costs ₦75,000 a month. If you are on Basic or Starter, or on pay as you go, you can still do this by hand. Dailzero spots people showing buying intent and lists them as leads in the dashboard on every plan. Open that list each evening and message the ones who went quiet.

### If you do have a website with a cart

Be clear about what is possible. Dailzero has no native integration with Shopify, WooCommerce or any other cart, so it cannot see who abandoned a checkout on your site.

You have two honest options:

1. **Move the conversation to WhatsApp earlier.** Put a WhatsApp button or the Dailzero website chat widget on product pages. A customer who asks a question before checkout is in a chat you can follow up. The widget uses the same agent.
2. **Have a developer connect it.** Dailzero has an HTTPS API that can send WhatsApp messages from your own code. A developer can use it to message a customer who already chats with you when your site records an unfinished checkout. The [developer page](/developers) has the details. This is custom work, not a switch you turn on.

To check your progress, count for one week how many people asked for a price and how many paid, then count again after the fixes. To see the follow-up feature and the leads list, look at the [lead generation page](/solutions/lead-generation) or the [features page](/features).

### Frequently asked questions

#### Can Dailzero recover abandoned carts from my Shopify or WooCommerce store?

Not directly. There is no native integration with website carts. It follows up with customers who went quiet in a WhatsApp chat, and a developer can connect your site through the API.

#### Is automated follow-up available on every plan?

No. Automated follow-ups are a Pro feature at ₦75,000 a month. The leads list, which shows who was interested, is on every plan, so you can follow up manually.

#### Will follow-ups get my number banned?

Messaging someone who already contacted you is normal WhatsApp behaviour. Dailzero paces every send at a human speed, but no tool can promise a number will never be restricted. Keep follow-ups few and relevant.
