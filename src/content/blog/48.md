An AI agent can answer "do you have a room?" on WhatsApp within seconds, but its answer is only as accurate as the availability information it has. Dailzero cannot see your booking system by itself. So there are three honest ways to set it up, depending on how often you sell out and whether you have a developer.

### The three levels at a glance

| Level | How the agent knows what is free | Who it suits | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Collect and confirm | It does not. It quotes rates, takes dates and hands over to staff | Any hotel, from day one | An hour of setup |
| 2. Daily update | Staff tell it which room types are full and for which dates | Hotels that sell out on known dates | A few minutes each day |
| 3. Live lookup | A developer connects your booking system through a webhook tool | Hotels with a system that has an API, and a developer | A development project |

### Level 1: collect and confirm

This is where most hotels should start. The agent holds your room types, rates, check-in times and policies. When a guest asks about availability, it does everything except give the final yes.

The guest writes:

> Pls do you have 2 rooms available from 14th to 17th? We are coming for a burial in Enugu.

The agent replies:

> Sorry for your loss. For 14th to 17th, that is 3 nights. Our Standard rooms are ₦32,000 a night and Executive rooms are ₦45,000. I am checking those dates with the front desk now and will confirm shortly. Which room type do you prefer, and what name should the booking be under?

Then it hands the chat to a person, who checks the room rack and confirms. The guest got an instant, useful answer, your staff got a complete enquiry, and nothing false was promised.

The weakness is the gap before a human confirms, which at 2am can be hours. Tell the agent to say so plainly: "Our reservations desk will confirm by 8am."

### Level 2: tell the agent what is full

The trouble comes on the dates you sell out: Detty December in Lagos, a convention weekend in Abuja, graduation week near a university.

For those, update the agent's information. A note such as "Executive rooms are fully booked from 20 to 27 December. Standard rooms are available. Do not offer Executive for those dates" is enough. The agent then steers guests to what you can still sell.

This only works if someone owns the job. Make it part of the morning handover. An out-of-date note is worse than none, because the agent will turn guests away from rooms that have since been cancelled. Keep the instruction to confirm with staff even at this level.

### Level 3: connect your booking system

Dailzero has no built-in integration with property or hotel management systems. What it does offer is webhook tools: a developer can connect up to 50 of them so the agent calls your own backend in the middle of a conversation. One of those can be "check availability", which takes the dates and room type and returns what your system says is free.

With that in place, the agent's answer is real:

> Yes, we have 2 Standard rooms free from 14th to 17th at ₦32,000 a night each.

This needs a booking system that software can query and a developer to build the connection. The agent still does not take payment, so it collects the guest's details and passes the booking to staff, or the developer adds a second tool that places a hold in your system. The details are in the [developer documentation](/developers).

If you keep reservations in a paper ledger, Level 3 is not available to you yet. Dailzero has no native Google Sheets integration either.

### How to choose

- You have under 30 rooms and rarely sell out: Level 1.
- You sell out on predictable dates: Level 1 plus Level 2 for those dates.
- You run a booking system, have a developer, and lose bookings in the confirmation gap: Level 3.
- You need live two-way sync with online travel agencies and several staff on one number at scale: you likely need a full channel manager and the official WhatsApp API, which Dailzero is not.

At every level, decide who picks up handed-over chats on each shift. The agent pauses on a chat while a person is replying, and you switch it back on afterwards.

### Frequently asked questions

#### Can the AI see which rooms are free in real time?

Only if a developer connects your booking system through a webhook tool. Without that, it works from what you typed in and should hand over for confirmation.

#### Will it double-book a room?

It cannot book a room in your system by itself, so the final check stays with your staff or your connected system. That check is what prevents double bookings.

#### How fast does it reply to an availability question?

Within seconds, at any hour. The human confirmation that follows depends on your staff.
