Dailzero and Termii are not really competitors. Termii is messaging infrastructure for developers: APIs to send SMS, voice, email and WhatsApp messages and one-time passwords from your own software. Dailzero is a finished product for business owners: an AI agent that replies to customers on your WhatsApp number and sells for you. If you need to send OTPs and alerts from an app, use Termii. If you need customer chats answered, use Dailzero. Some businesses use both.

### What each one is

**Termii** is a Nigerian communications platform. A developer connects your app or website to its APIs so your system can send messages and verify phone numbers. It is known for SMS and OTP delivery in Nigeria, including sender IDs and delivery routes for local networks, and it also offers WhatsApp, voice and email channels. You fund a wallet and pay for what you send.

**Dailzero** is an AI WhatsApp agent. You link the WhatsApp number you already use, describe your business, add your products and prices, and the agent replies to customers day and night. It sends photos, takes orders, books appointments and hands the chat to you when a person is needed. No developer is required.

### When Termii is the right tool

- **One-time passwords and login codes.** You need them delivered in seconds, to people who have never messaged you, with SMS as the main route. That is what Termii is built for.
- **Transaction alerts and notifications** sent automatically from a banking, logistics or e-commerce system.
- **Messages across several channels** from one API: SMS first, with WhatsApp, voice or email as alternatives.
- **Sender IDs and compliance** for SMS on Nigerian networks.

Dailzero is the wrong tool for these jobs. It does not send SMS, and it is not designed to deliver a login code to a stranger with a guarantee of speed.

### When Dailzero is the right tool

- **Customers message you on WhatsApp and wait too long for a reply.** The agent answers in seconds from your own prices and policies.
- **You want sales handled, not just messages sent.** It asks the right questions, sends product photos, takes the order details and passes hot leads to you.
- **You have no developer.** Setup is a few steps in the dashboard.
- **You want your WhatsApp Status and broadcasts handled too.** Dailzero posts Status on a schedule and sends paced broadcasts to your existing customers.

### Dailzero also has an API, with a different purpose

Developers can use Dailzero's API to send WhatsApp text, images, video and documents from their own code, to run the AI agent from an app, and to let the agent call their backend during a chat. It sends through your linked WhatsApp number, at a human pace, to people who are your customers.

That makes it useful for order confirmations and follow-up messages on WhatsApp. It is not a replacement for an OTP service: sends are queued and paced to protect your number, so do not rely on it for codes that must arrive within seconds. See the [developers page](/developers) and [how to send WhatsApp messages from code without the official API](/blog/how-to-send-whatsapp-messages-from-code-without-the-official-api).

### Using both together

A common setup for a fintech, a school or a delivery company:

1. **Termii** sends OTPs, receipts and alerts from the app.
2. **Dailzero** answers the questions customers send on the support or sales WhatsApp number.

They do different jobs and do not conflict, as long as they run on different numbers.

### Frequently asked questions

#### Is Dailzero a Termii alternative?

Only for one narrow case: sending WhatsApp messages to your existing customers from code. For SMS, OTPs and bulk alerts, Termii is the right kind of tool and Dailzero is not.

#### Can Dailzero send OTPs?

It can send a code as a WhatsApp message to a customer, but delivery is paced and not guaranteed to be instant. Keep SMS as your main route for login codes.

#### Does Termii have an AI agent that answers customers?

Termii is known as messaging infrastructure. Check its site for anything newer. If your need is an AI that holds a sales conversation on your own number, that is what Dailzero does.

#### Which is cheaper?

They charge for different things, so compare by job. For Dailzero, a text reply costs about 5 credits, and plans start at ₦20,000 a month for 25,000 credits. See [pricing](/pricing).
