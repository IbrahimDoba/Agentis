You handle 1,000 or more inquiries a day without more staff by doing four things in order: remove the inquiries that should never have been sent, let an AI agent answer the repeat questions, collect the details on everything else before a person touches it, and keep your people for the cases only people can solve. Hiring is what is left over after those four.

### Start with the arithmetic of your queue

Work out how many chats one officer truly closes in an hour on your line, and call it R. A team of N officers on an eight-hour shift closes N × R × 8 chats a day.

If that figure is below 1,000, a backlog builds every day and customers wait until tomorrow. The usual reaction is to hire until N × R × 8 catches up. The alternative is to shrink the 1,000.

### Move 1: stop inquiries at the source

At this volume, a large share of messages are the same question caused by the same event. A bank's network is slow on the 28th and hundreds of people ask where their transfer is.

Answer before they ask, and fix the confusing screen or receipt that causes the question:

- **Post a WhatsApp Status.** "Transfers to some banks are delayed this afternoon. Your money is safe and will reflect or reverse." Dailzero can post Status on a schedule, reaching up to 10,000 contacts per post on Pro.
- **Broadcast to the people affected.** A text broadcast costs 5 credits per person, so telling 1,000 customers costs 5,000 credits. Sends are paced to protect your number.

### Move 2: let AI answer what repeats

List your twenty most common questions and write one correct answer for each, with real figures. An AI agent replies to those in seconds, at any hour, to every customer at once. There is no queue for a question about your charges or opening hours.

For example:

> Abeg how much una dey charge for transfer above 50k?

> Transfers above ₦50,000 cost ₦X each. Transfers below ₦5,000 cost ₦Y. You will see the exact charge on the confirmation screen before you approve.

The X and Y are whatever you entered. The AI works from your information and does not invent a fee.

### Move 3: collect details before the handover

For everything the AI cannot finish, it should still do the first half of the job: ask for the name, the registered phone number, the amount, the date and a screenshot, then hand the chat to a person. Dailzero understands photos and voice notes, so a customer can send the receipt instead of typing the reference.

This changes R. An officer who opens a chat with every detail already there closes it far faster than one who spends four messages asking for them.

### Move 4: route what is left to the right person

Give team members their own logins so the fraud desk, the reversals desk and sales each pick up their own chats. When a person replies, from the dashboard or straight from the phone, the AI pauses on that chat until you switch it back on.

### What 1,000 chats a day costs in credits, and an honest limit

The maths, at about 5 credits per text reply:

- 1,000 chats × 4 AI replies × 5 credits = 20,000 credits a day
- 20,000 × 30 days = 600,000 credits a month

Dailzero's largest standard plan, Pro, includes 100,000 credits for ₦75,000. So a steady 1,000 chats a day is six times Pro, which puts you on Enterprise with custom pricing. [Talk to the team](/contact) for a quote before you plan around it.

There is a second limit to weigh. Dailzero links your existing number like WhatsApp Web, with one number per agent, and every message is queued and sent at a human pace. That protects the number, but it means a single line carrying thousands of outgoing replies a day is being worked hard. At this scale you should either spread traffic across more than one number or ask whether you need the official WhatsApp Business Platform, which is built for very high volume and many officers on one number. If you need that, Dailzero is not the right fit for you. Our comparison of the [WhatsApp Business API and the WhatsApp Business app](/blog/whatsapp-business-api-vs-whatsapp-business-app-what-nigerian-smbs-need-to-know) covers the choice.

If your 1,000 is a peak rather than a daily average, the picture is easier. A business that sees 250 chats on a normal day and 1,000 only in salary week can run on a monthly plan and buy pay-as-you-go credits for the rush.

### Frequently asked questions

#### Can one WhatsApp number really take 1,000 chats a day?

Receiving them is not the problem. Replying to all of them safely is, which is why paced sending, more than one number, or the official API matters at that level.

#### What happens to customers if the AI runs out of credits?

The AI pauses and messages still arrive in WhatsApp as normal, so your team can reply by hand. There are no overage fees.

#### How do I know which questions to automate first?

Count them. The questions that appear most often and have the same answer for everyone come first. The [customer support page](/solutions/customer-support) shows how the handover works.
