Property leads are not lost in one place. They leak at five points: the slow first reply, the hours you are offline, the "let me get back to you" that never happens, the buyer who goes quiet after seeing the price, and the chat nobody wrote down. Close those five and you stop missing leads.

Each leak below comes with a fix you can do by hand and what an AI agent on your WhatsApp does about it.

### Leak 1: the first reply takes too long

Someone scrolling listings rarely messages one agent. They message several, and the first clear answer tends to get the inspection.

**By hand:** turn on a greeting message in WhatsApp Business that asks which property they mean, and save quick replies for your most asked listings.

**With AI:** the agent replies in seconds with the actual answer, not a holding message.

> Hi, is the mini flat in Surulere still available?

> Hi. Yes, it is. Rent is ₦1.2 million a year and the total package is ₦1.55 million. It is on the first floor, with prepaid meter and tiled floors. Would you like photos or an inspection time?

(Illustrative figures. The agent uses the ones you load.)

### Leak 2: messages that arrive while you are busy or asleep

You are on an inspection in Ikorodu with no signal. You are in church. It is 1am and a buyer in Canada has just finished work. Those messages sit unread, and by the time you reply the person has moved on.

**By hand:** an away message that gives a time you will respond, then keeping that promise.

**With AI:** it answers at any hour, sends photos, and books the inspection without you. See how in [responding to property inquiries 24/7](/blog/how-to-respond-to-property-inquiries-247-without-extra-staff-in-nigeria).

### Leak 3: "let me confirm and get back to you"

This is the quiet killer. A buyer asks about the title or the service charge, you do not have it to hand, you promise to check, and the chat sinks under fifty newer ones.

**By hand:** label the chat immediately and clear that label every evening before you sleep.

**With AI:** most of these questions stop needing a callback, because the answer is already loaded. Put the title, service charge, power situation, parking, flooding history and landlord's rules for every listing into the agent. When it truly does not know, it hands the chat to you so the gap is visible instead of forgotten.

### Leak 4: the buyer who goes quiet

They asked, you answered, they said "ok I will get back". Silence. Most agents never message again because they have forgotten who it was.

**By hand:** keep a list of everyone who asked in the last 14 days and message them on a fixed day each week.

**With AI:** on Dailzero's Pro plan, automated follow-ups message customers who went quiet. The agent also remembers returning customers and earlier chats, so when that person comes back three weeks later asking "is that Surulere flat still there?", it knows which flat they mean.

### Leak 5: nobody recorded the lead

If the only record of a serious buyer is a chat somewhere in your phone, the lead depends on your memory. Change phones or lose the chat and it is gone.

**By hand:** a notebook or spreadsheet, filled in daily. It works if you are disciplined.

**With AI:** people who show buying intent are listed as leads in the dashboard automatically, on every plan, with the details the agent collected. Contacts who message you are saved too, so they can see your Status later. If the person came from a Facebook or Instagram click-to-WhatsApp ad, you can see which ad.

### A ten minute daily routine that holds it together

Tools do not close leaks if you ignore them. Do this every morning:

1. Open the leads list and call the two or three strongest.
2. Reply to every chat the AI handed over.
3. Mark any property that got taken yesterday as unavailable.
4. Add the answer to any question the AI could not handle.

Step 3 matters most. The AI cannot see your records, so it only knows a flat has gone when you tell it.

### What "never" honestly means

No system catches every lead. Your phone still has to come online regularly because the agent connects as a linked device, like WhatsApp Web. If your credits run out, the AI pauses, though messages still land in your WhatsApp. And leads that arrive by phone call, Instagram DM or a property portal's inbox are outside its reach, since Dailzero works on WhatsApp and a website chat widget only. Send people from those channels to your WhatsApp number wherever you can.
