The surest way to increase hotel bookings with WhatsApp AI is to reply to every rate inquiry in seconds with the price, room photos and a clear next step, then follow up the guests who did not book. The AI does not create demand. It stops you losing bookings you had already attracted.

Below are six levers, in the order most hotels should pull them. No one can honestly promise you a percentage increase, so the guide ends with a simple test you can run on your own numbers.

### 1. Reply while the guest is still choosing

A traveller looking for a room in Ikeja for tomorrow night messages three hotels and books the first one that gives a straight answer. If your reply comes after forty minutes, the decision is made.

An AI agent on your number replies in seconds at 2pm and at 2am. **Check it:** count how many inquiries last week waited more than ten minutes for a first reply. That is the pool you are fishing in.

### 2. Put the price and the photos in the first reply

"Kindly call our front desk for rates" loses the booking. Compare:

> Hello, how much for a night and do you have light 24hrs?

> Hello. Our Classic room is ₦28,000 a night and the Executive is ₦40,000, both with breakfast. Yes, we have 24-hour power with generator and inverter backup. I am sending photos of both rooms now. What date are you coming in?

The agent sends photos and videos from the media library you upload. An image costs 8 credits, which is very little against a room night. The rates here are examples; the agent quotes what you enter.

### 3. Make the next step small

End every reply with one easy question, such as the date or the number of nights. Then let the agent collect the name and arrival time and share your payment details. It hands the chat to your front desk to confirm the room and the transfer, because it does not verify payments or see your room chart by itself.

### 4. Follow up the ones who went quiet

Many guests ask, get the price, and disappear to compare. A polite message the next morning brings some of them back:

> Good morning. We still have the Executive room for Saturday. Would you like me to hold it for you?

On the Pro plan the agent sends these follow-ups automatically. On Basic or Starter, open the leads list in the dashboard each morning and send them yourself. **Check it:** count how many follow-ups got a reply, and how many of those booked.

### 5. Send people to WhatsApp, not to a form

Guests who find you on Instagram, Facebook or Google would rather chat than fill a booking form.

- Run click-to-WhatsApp ads on Facebook and Instagram. The agent knows which ad each guest came from, so you can see which ad brings real inquiries.
- Put your WhatsApp link in your Instagram bio and on your Google listing.
- Add the website chat widget, which uses the same agent, for people already on your site.

Direct WhatsApp bookings also avoid the commission that online travel sites charge. Plug in your own rate to see what that is worth.

### 6. Go back to past guests

Someone who has stayed with you once is the cheapest booking you will ever get.

- **Status.** Guests who messaged you are saved as contacts and can see your WhatsApp Status. Schedule posts for weekend rates, the Sunday buffet, the pool party, Valentine and Detty December packages.
- **Broadcasts.** Message your existing guests directly before peak periods. Sends are paced to protect your number.

A worked cost: broadcasting an image of your Easter weekend package to 800 past guests costs 800 × 8 = 6,400 credits. On pay as you go, the ₦10,000 pack gives 6,500 credits, so that broadcast costs just under ₦10,000. Set that against your own room rate to see how many nights it must fill.

Broadcasts only go to people who have messaged you before, never to bought lists of numbers.

### What will not increase bookings

- **AI on top of a weak offer.** If your rates are out of line for your area or your photos are dark, faster replies will not fix it. Take proper photos first.
- **Automating the confirmation carelessly.** Double bookings cost more than slow replies. Keep a person on the final yes unless a developer has connected your booking system through webhook tools.

### A simple 30-day test

1. Count last week's inquiries and how many became bookings.
2. Set up the agent with rates, policies and photos, and link your number. The [how it works page](/how-it-works) shows the steps, and the free trial gives 7 days and 1,000 credits.
3. Run levers 1 to 4 for four weeks, then count again.

If the ratio improved, add ads, Status and broadcasts. If it did not, read the chats: the reason is usually price, photos or a slow handoff at the front desk.
