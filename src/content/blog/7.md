To run 24/7 support on WhatsApp without a night shift, connect an AI agent to your support number so every message gets an answer in seconds, then define exactly what happens between 6pm and 8am to the cases the AI cannot solve. With Dailzero the first part takes an afternoon. The second part, the after-hours rules, is what decides whether customers feel supported or stonewalled.

### Why night is the hard part

During the day, an AI that cannot help hands the chat to an officer who is at a desk. At 11.40pm on a Saturday there is no officer. Yet that is when a customer's transfer hangs, when a phone is stolen at a party, when someone finally sits down to open an account.

So your setup has to answer one question honestly: when the AI hands over at night, who receives it, and when?

### Setup in six steps

1. **Pick the number.** Use the support line your customers already know. If the number is brand new, use it normally for about two weeks before connecting it.
2. **Create the agent.** [Sign up](/signup), describe your business, and add your fees, limits, requirements, FAQs and support policies. No code is needed.
3. **Upload your documents.** Fee schedule, account opening checklist, agent guide. The AI sends them when asked.
4. **Link the number.** Scan a QR code or enter a pairing code, the same way you link WhatsApp Web.
5. **Write the after-hours rules.** See the next section.
6. **Test at night.** Message the line at midnight from a personal phone with five real scenarios before you announce anything.

### The after-hours rulebook

Write these into the agent's instructions in plain language.

**General questions: answer fully.** Charges, limits, how to reset a PIN in the app, what documents an upgrade needs. These are the same at 3am as at 3pm.

**Account problems: collect, then give a true time.** The AI should gather the details and tell the customer when a person will look. Not "shortly". An actual time.

A customer writes at midnight:

> Hello, I transferred 20,000 to Access Bank at 9pm, it shows successful but my brother has not received it.

A good after-hours reply:

> Sorry about the delay. Please send the transaction reference or a screenshot, and the phone number on your account. Our support officers resume at 8am and will check this first thing. Transfers that fail are normally reversed automatically, so please do not resend the money tonight.

Only include that last sentence if it is true for your product. The AI repeats the policy you give it.

**Emergencies: give the self-service step and wake someone.** For a stolen phone or a suspected fraud, the customer cannot wait until morning. Tell the AI the exact emergency steps you offer, such as the in-app option to block a card or the USSD code to freeze an account, and decide who is on call.

### Who is on call

Someone senior should carry the duty phone on rotation. Because Dailzero works on your normal WhatsApp number, the on-call officer can reply straight from the phone, and the AI pauses on that chat when they do. Agree which words mean "wake me": fraud, stolen, hacked, unauthorised, wrong account.

If you truly have nobody at night, say so in the reply and point to the emergency self-service step. An honest "our team is back at 8am, here is how to block your card right now" beats a promise nobody keeps.

### Keeping the line up all night

Three practical points for Nigerian conditions.

- **The phone is still the main device.** Dailzero connects as a linked device, so the phone holding the support SIM should come online regularly, as with WhatsApp Web. Keep it charged and on data or Wi-Fi. A power bank and a second network's data guard against NEPA and a bad network day.
- **Watch your credits.** When credits run out the AI pauses, and messages simply arrive in WhatsApp as normal. Check the balance before a long weekend. Forty night chats of five replies each use 1,000 credits a night, and the [pricing page](/pricing) lists plans and top-ups.
- **Review every morning.** The first job of the day shift is the list of chats handed over overnight. If the AI promised 8am, 8am it is.

### What this setup will not do

It will not reverse a transaction, unfreeze an account or confirm a payment at 2am. Dailzero does not process or verify payments and cannot see customer accounts unless a developer connects your backend through webhook tools. It also has no voice calls, so a customer who phones the line at night still gets no answer.

### Frequently asked questions

#### Do I still need night staff if I use an AI agent?

Not for general questions. You do need a named on-call person for fraud and other emergencies, or a clear self-service step customers can take alone.

#### Will the AI reply if my support phone is off?

The phone should come online regularly for the link to stay healthy, as with WhatsApp Web. Keep it powered and connected.

#### How long does setup take?

Most of the work is writing your answers and night rules. Linking the number takes minutes. The steps are laid out on the [how it works page](/how-it-works).
