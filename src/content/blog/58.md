To follow up after a purchase on WhatsApp, plan four messages: an order confirmation, a delivery check, a review request and a reorder reminder. With Dailzero, the AI sends the confirmation inside the chat and answers every reply that comes back. The later messages go out as a broadcast or a scheduled Status post to your existing customers, or from your own order system through the API. There is no built-in trigger that fires "three days after delivery", so this guide shows what is automatic, what is scheduled and what needs a developer.

### The four messages and how each one is sent

| Message | When | How it is sent on Dailzero |
|---|---|---|
| Order confirmation | As soon as details are collected | Automatic, by the AI in the chat |
| Delivery check | Day of delivery or the day after | You, or your system through the API |
| Review request | A few days after delivery | Broadcast to existing customers, or the API |
| Reorder or new stock reminder | When the product would run out | Broadcast or scheduled Status |

The example used below is a baby products store in Ibadan selling diapers, wipes and formula, because repeat purchase is the whole business.

### Message 1: the confirmation

This one is fully automatic. Once the agent has collected the customer's name, address, items and quantity, it repeats the order back and sets expectations:

> Thank you, Mrs Adeyemi. Your order is 2 packs of size 3 diapers and 1 carton of wipes, total ₦41,500 including delivery to Bodija. Once payment is confirmed, the rider will call you before he arrives tomorrow. Please message us here when it reaches you.

That last sentence does a lot of work. It invites the customer to write back, which opens the door for everything after it.

### Message 2: the delivery check

This message catches problems before they become complaints, and it is the one stores skip.

Dailzero cannot see delivery status on its own, so it does not know when a parcel has arrived. You have three ways to handle that:

1. **Let the customer start it.** If your confirmation asked them to message when it arrives, many will. The AI replies, thanks them, and asks if everything is in order.
2. **Send it yourself.** At the end of each day, message that day's deliveries from your phone. Switch the AI back on for those chats and it handles the replies.
3. **Trigger it from your system.** If you have a developer and an order database, the API can send a WhatsApp message from your own code when an order is marked delivered. See the [developer page](/developers).

### Message 3: the review request

Ask a few days after delivery, and make replying easy:

> Good afternoon. We hope the diapers are working well for your little one. Would you mind telling us how the order went? A one-line reply is enough, and it helps other mums decide.

The agent understands replies sent as text, photos or voice notes, and a complaint is handed to a person.

### Message 4: the reorder reminder

For anything that runs out, this is where the money is. A broadcast near month end, when salaries land, reaches customers just as they need to restock:

> Hello from the store. New stock of size 3 and size 4 diapers has arrived, same price as last month. Reply with your size and quantity and we will deliver tomorrow.

When customers reply, the agent handles each chat at the same time. It remembers returning customers and earlier chats, so a regular does not have to repeat her address.

Broadcasts go only to people who have already messaged you, and each send is paced to protect your number. A scheduled WhatsApp Status post is the lighter option, since Dailzero saves the contacts who message you so they can see your Status.

### Frequently asked questions

#### Can Dailzero automatically message a customer a set number of days after delivery?

Not as a built-in feature. It has no view of delivery status. You can send broadcasts and schedule Status posts, or have a developer trigger messages from your order system through the API.

#### Can I send a follow-up to buyers who have never messaged my number?

No. Broadcasts are for existing contacts, meaning people who have already messaged you. Dailzero does not message bought or imported cold lists.

#### Will it collect Google reviews for me?

No. It can ask for feedback and receive replies in WhatsApp. For Google or other sites, share your review link and the customer posts it.
