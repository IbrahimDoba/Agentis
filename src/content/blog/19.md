WhatsApp automation generates real estate leads in Nigeria by doing two things: pulling inquiries into one WhatsApp number from every place buyers find you, and replying to each one fast enough to capture a name, a budget and a preferred location before they move on. Automation does not create demand. It makes sure the demand you paid for becomes a contact you can call.

Below are the five sources that feed a property business's WhatsApp, how to automate each, and what to capture.

### Source 1: click-to-WhatsApp ads

Facebook and Instagram ads that open a WhatsApp chat are the most common paid source for land and off-plan sales. An ad for plots in Epe that runs all weekend will produce chats at midnight on Saturday.

An AI agent replies immediately and, on Dailzero, knows which ad the person came from. So the buyer who tapped the Epe ad gets Epe details, not a generic greeting.

> I saw your advert. How much is one plot and what is the size?

A good reply gives the plot size in square metres, the outright price, the instalment plan, the title, and what is and is not included, such as survey and deed fees. Then it asks whether the buyer wants it for building or for investment, and offers the next site inspection date.

### Source 2: your listings and signboards

Every listing you put on a property portal, every flyer and every "To Let" board should carry the same WhatsApp number.

The automation here is simple: the agent recognises which property is being asked about, sends the photos and full cost breakdown, and collects the person's details. Put a short reference in each listing, such as "Quote LK12", so the customer's first message identifies the property.

### Source 3: WhatsApp Status

Status is free reach to people who have saved your number and whose number you have saved.

Dailzero saves the contacts who message you and can post Status on a schedule with Autopilot. Reach per post is up to 2,000 contacts on Basic, 5,000 on Starter and 10,000 on Pro. A new listing each morning and an inspection reminder on Thursday will bring replies from people who inquired months ago and are now ready.

### Source 4: your website

The same AI agent can run as an embeddable chat widget on your website, so a visitor reading about your estate in Abuja gets answers on the page.

### Source 5: the contacts you already have

Your phone holds years of past inquirers. A broadcast to them when you launch a new estate or drop a price is often the cheapest lead source you have.

Broadcasts on Dailzero go only to existing contacts, each send paced to protect your number. A text costs 5 credits per person and an image 8. So announcing a new estate with a flyer image to 2,000 past contacts uses 2,000 × 8 = 16,000 credits, a bit over a quarter of the Starter plan's 60,000 monthly allowance.

### What to capture from every inquiry

A lead is only useful if your sales team can act on it. Set the agent to collect, through normal conversation:

- Name.
- Buying, renting or investing.
- Preferred location.
- Budget range.
- Outright or instalment, for land and off-plan.
- When they can inspect.
- Whether they are in Nigeria or abroad. Diaspora buyers need a different follow-up, often a video inspection and a relative on the ground.

People who show buying intent appear as leads in the dashboard on every plan, ready for your team to call.

### Mistakes that waste property leads

- **Hiding the price.** "Kindly call for price" loses buyers who are comparing five estates at once.
- **One number per marketer.** Leads scatter across personal phones and leave with staff who resign. Use one business number and give team members their own logins.

### What automation will not do

It will not verify that a buyer has funds, check a land title, or confirm a payment. It shares the account details you provide and hands the chat to you to confirm receipt. It also has no built-in link to property portals or CRMs, so leads live in the Dailzero dashboard unless a developer connects another system through the API.

### Frequently asked questions

#### Can I use WhatsApp automation to message people who have never contacted me?

Not with Dailzero, and you should avoid it with any tool. Broadcasts are for your existing contacts; cold messages to bought lists risk your number.

#### How do I know which advert produced a lead?

For Facebook and Instagram click-to-WhatsApp ads, the agent records which ad each chat came from, so you can compare ads by the leads they bring.

#### How many contacts can see my Status posts?

Up to 2,000 per post on Basic, 5,000 on Starter and 10,000 on Pro. The free trial reaches up to 500.
