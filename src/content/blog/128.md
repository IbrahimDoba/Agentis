On the official WhatsApp Business Platform you pay two parties: Meta, which charges per delivered message, and your provider, which charges for its software. Meta's rate depends on the type of message and the country code of the person receiving it, so messages to +234 numbers follow Meta's rate card for Nigeria. Meta does not bill in Naira. Dailzero's default linked-device route has no Meta fees at all, while its official route does.

This guide explains how the charging works as of November 2026, taken from Meta's own pricing documentation. We do not quote a per-message rate for Nigeria, because Meta updates its rate cards. Download the current one before you budget.

### How Meta charges

Meta used to charge per 24-hour conversation. Its documentation says that model is gone, replaced by per-message pricing from 1 July 2025. The rules today:

- **You are charged when a message is delivered**, not when it is sent.
- **Each message falls into one category** and carries at most one charge.
- **The rate depends on the category and the recipient's country calling code.**
- **Customers are not charged** for the messages they send you.

### The message categories

| Category | What it is | When you can send it |
|---|---|---|
| Marketing | Promotions, offers, new stock | Any time, as an approved template |
| Utility | Order updates, receipts, reminders | Any time, as an approved template |
| Authentication | One-time passwords and login codes | Any time, as an approved template |
| Service | Ordinary replies that are not templates | Only inside the customer service window |

The customer service window is the 24 hours after a customer last messaged you. It resets each time they write. Outside it, you can only send templates.

### What is free, and what recently changed

This part has changed more than once, so read Meta's page yourself before you rely on it.

- **Service messages have a monthly free tier.** Meta's documentation, as read on 24 November 2026, says each business phone number gets 1,000 delivered service messages free each month, with charging from the 1,001st. Unused free messages do not roll over. It says this took effect on 1 October 2026. Before that date, service messages were free without a cap.
- **Replies after a Click to WhatsApp ad.** When a customer comes from one of these ads and you reply in time, a free entry point window opens for up to 7 days. Meta says it does not charge for marketing, utility, authentication or service messages in that window.
- **Volume tiers.** Meta gives lower rates at higher monthly volume for utility and authentication messages only.

A busy number can pass 1,000 service messages quickly. A shop with 40 chats a day at about 6 replies each sends 240 replies a day, which is 7,200 in a 30-day month. Under the rule above, 6,200 of those would be charged.

### What currency Meta bills in

Meta's documentation lists 16 billing currencies, including US dollars, euros and pounds. Naira is not on the list. You choose the currency when the messaging account is created and Meta says it cannot be changed afterwards. For a Nigerian business this means the Meta part of your bill moves with the exchange rate.

### What providers charge on top

The platform needs software, either your own code on Meta's Cloud API or a provider's dashboard. Providers usually charge some mix of:

- A monthly subscription, often in US dollars
- Fees per user, per number or per extra feature
- A markup on Meta's message rates, or a pass-through at Meta's price
- A setup or onboarding fee

So the real monthly cost is: provider subscription + add-ons + Meta's charge for each delivered message. Ask every provider for all three in writing. For a comparison of providers, see [WhatsApp Business API providers in Nigeria compared](/blog/whatsapp-business-api-providers-in-nigeria-compared).

### How to estimate your bill

1. Count the messages you send in a month, split into marketing, utility, authentication and service.
2. Take 1,000 off the service count for each business number, if Meta's free tier still applies when you read this.
3. Look up Meta's current rate for Nigeria for each category in its rate card.
4. Multiply each count by its rate and add them up. Convert to Naira at the day's rate.
5. Add your provider's subscription and add-ons.

### Where Dailzero fits

Dailzero connects to WhatsApp in two ways.

**Linked device, the default.** You link the WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business number you already use by QR code or pairing code, like WhatsApp Web. There is no Meta verification and there are no Meta message fees. You pay only Dailzero credits, in Naira: ₦20,000 to ₦75,000 a month, or pay as you go from ₦1,000. This is not the official API. Dailzero protects the number with queued, human-paced sending, a warm-up period for new numbers and automatic slow-down when WhatsApp signals a limit, but no tool can promise a number will never be restricted.

**The official WhatsApp Business Platform.** Dailzero's app is approved by Meta for WhatsApp Business messaging. This route is set up with the Dailzero team on request, and Meta's own message fees apply on it. WhatsApp Status, contact saving and the other linked-device features are not available on this route.

Choose the official route if compliance requires it, if you need approved templates, or if losing the number is a risk you cannot take. [Official WhatsApp API vs linked device](/blog/official-whatsapp-api-vs-linked-device-which-should-you-use) goes through the choice.

### Frequently asked questions

#### Is the WhatsApp Business API free in Nigeria?

No. Meta charges per delivered message on the official platform, with some free allowances, and your provider charges for its software on top. The WhatsApp Business app on your phone is free.

#### How much does Meta charge per WhatsApp message in Nigeria?

It depends on the message category, and the rate changes. Meta publishes current rates for Nigeria in the rate cards on its pricing page. Check there on the day you budget.

#### Does Meta charge in Naira?

No. Meta's documentation lists 16 billing currencies as of November 2026 and Naira is not one of them.

#### Do I pay Meta fees on Dailzero?

Not on the default linked-device route. On Dailzero's official WhatsApp Business Platform route, Meta's own message fees apply. See [pricing](/pricing) for Dailzero's side.

#### Are replies to customers still free on the official API?

Not without limit. Meta's documentation says that from 1 October 2026 each business number gets 1,000 free service messages a month and is charged beyond that. Confirm on Meta's page, because this rule is recent.
