The WhatsApp Business app is the free phone app a small team uses by hand. The WhatsApp Business API, which Meta now calls the WhatsApp Business Platform, has no app at all: it is a paid connection that lets software send and receive messages at large scale. Most Nigerian SMBs do not need the API, and should move to it only when they hit one of the specific limits listed below.

### The two side by side

| Feature | WhatsApp Business app | WhatsApp Business API |
|---|---|---|
| What it is | A free app on your phone | A connection for software, no app |
| Who it suits | Owners and small teams | Larger companies with high volume |
| Cost | Free | Meta charges for messages, plus your provider's fee |
| Messaging people first | Type or broadcast by hand | Needs message templates approved by Meta |
| Automation | Greeting, away message, quick replies | Whatever your software or provider builds |
| Verified badge | Not included as standard | You can apply for one |
| Team access | Your phone plus a few linked devices | Many agents through a shared inbox tool |

Meta's fees and rules change, so check the current rate card with Meta or your provider before you budget.

### What the app does well

The app gives you a business profile, a catalogue, labels, quick replies, and greeting and away messages. You can link a few extra devices.

Its weakness is that a human has to type nearly everything. An away message that says "we will get back to you" does not tell the customer the price of the wig she asked about.

### What the API does that the app cannot

- Sends very large volumes of notifications, such as OTPs, receipts and delivery updates, straight from a company's system.
- Supports a verified badge next to the business name.
- Lets a large support team work one number through a helpdesk tool.
- Offers official features such as approved templates and in-chat forms.

The price is paperwork and fees. You verify your business with Meta, get templates approved before messaging customers outside the reply window, and pay under Meta's pricing. Moving a number to the API has traditionally meant it stops working in the phone app. Meta has been changing this, so confirm with a provider.

### A third option: AI on the app through a linked device

There is a middle path, and it is how Dailzero works. You keep your WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business number on your phone. Dailzero connects by scanning a QR code or entering a pairing code, the same way you link WhatsApp Web, and an AI agent then replies to customers on that number.

To be plain about it: **Dailzero is not the official API.** That has consequences in both directions.

What you gain:

- No Meta per-conversation fees and no template approval.
- You keep your number, your chats and your phone app.
- No business verification to wait for, and no code.

What you do not get:

- No verified badge through Dailzero.
- No approved templates or WhatsApp Flows.
- Your phone remains the main device and should come online regularly, as with WhatsApp Web.
- One number per agent.

Dailzero queues every message and sends at a human pace, but no tool can promise a number will never be restricted. Plans start at ₦20,000 a month, with pay as you go from ₦1,000, on the [pricing page](/pricing).

### Which one fits you: a quick decision guide

**Stay on the app alone if** you get a handful of chats a day and can answer each one yourself without customers waiting.

**Add an AI agent to the app if** enquiries wait for hours, come in at night, or repeat the same ten questions. Say a boutique in Wuse gets this:

> Do you have this in size 42 and how much to deliver to Kubwa?

The customer gets the price, the delivery fee and a photo back in seconds.

**Move to the official API if** any of these are true:

- You must send bulk transactional messages, such as OTPs or bank alerts, to customers who have not messaged you first.
- You need the verified badge for trust, as banks and big brands often do.
- You have a large support team that must share one number at enterprise scale.
- Your compliance team requires an official Meta integration.

If that is you, Dailzero is not the right tool. An official WhatsApp solution provider is.

### Frequently asked questions

#### Is the WhatsApp Business API free?

No. The app is free. On the API, Meta charges for messages under its own pricing, and most businesses also pay a provider for the software around it.

#### Can I use an AI agent without the API?

Yes. Dailzero links to the number on your existing app as a linked device, so you get AI replies without applying for the API. See [how the connection works](/how-it-works).

#### Does Dailzero give me a verified tick?

No. Verified badges come from Meta through the official platform. If the badge is a must for your business, you need the API.

#### Do developers need the API to send WhatsApp messages from code?

Not necessarily. Dailzero has its own HTTPS API for sending text, images, video and documents from your code. See the [developers page](/developers). It is not Meta's Cloud API.
