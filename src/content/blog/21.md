There is no single best AI tool for a Nigerian real estate agency. The useful ones each do one job: an AI WhatsApp agent answers enquiries, a general AI assistant writes your listings, a design tool makes your flyers, and a video editor captions your property tours. Pick by the job that is costing you the most time, and start with one.

For most agencies that job is WhatsApp, because that is where buyers and tenants ask their questions.

### Answering enquiries: an AI WhatsApp agent

This is the tool that touches revenue directly. A listing for a 2 bedroom flat in Yaba can bring dozens of messages that all ask the same things: is it still available, how much is the total package, where exactly is it, can I come and see it.

An AI WhatsApp agent answers those in seconds from the details you loaded. With Dailzero you link the WhatsApp number you already use by scanning a QR code, upload your listings, prices, photos and brochures, and the agent replies on that number day and night. It sends the photos and the price list, collects the person's budget and preferred location, books an inspection, and flags serious buyers as leads in your dashboard. When someone wants to negotiate or pay, it hands the chat to you.

A typical exchange looks like this:

> Good evening, is the 2 bed in Yaba still available? What is the total package?

> Good evening. Yes, it is available. Rent is ₦2.8 million a year, and the total package with agency, legal and caution comes to ₦3.5 million. I can send photos now. Would you like to inspect this Saturday?

The figures in that reply are only an example. The agent uses whatever numbers you give it. You can see what else it handles on the [real estate solution page](/solutions/real-estate).

### Writing listings: a general AI assistant

ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini are good at turning rough notes into a clean listing. Type the facts (3 bed terrace, Chevron, all rooms en suite, 24 hour power, ₦120 million) and ask for a listing description, an Instagram caption and a short WhatsApp Status version.

Two cautions. Check every fact in the output, because these assistants will sometimes add features you never mentioned. And do not paste clients' personal details or documents into them.

### Flyers and graphics: a design tool

Canva has templates and AI features that speed up "just listed" flyers, price list cards and open house graphics.

### Tour videos: a video editor with captions

Short walkthrough videos sell property in Nigeria. CapCut and similar editors add captions automatically and trim clips on your phone. Once the video is ready, upload it to your AI agent's media library so it can send it to anyone who asks for it.

### Ads: the platform's own automation

If you run click-to-WhatsApp ads on Facebook or Instagram, Meta's ad tools already automate a lot of the targeting. Your part is making sure every click gets an answer. Dailzero records which ad a customer came from, so you can see which listing or campaign is producing real conversations.

### How to choose where to start

1. Count how many WhatsApp enquiries went unanswered for more than an hour last week. If it is more than a handful, start with the WhatsApp agent.
2. If enquiries are low, your problem is reach. Start with better listings, photos and video.

### Where Dailzero is not the right fit

Dailzero works on WhatsApp and a website chat widget only. It does not answer Instagram DMs, emails or phone calls. It has no built-in connection to property management software or a CRM, so it works from the listings you type in unless a developer connects your system through its [developer API](/developers). And it is not the official WhatsApp Business API, so it does not give you a green verified badge. A large firm that needs those things should look at an official API provider instead.

An AI WhatsApp agent, because a small team loses the most to slow replies. Use a free general assistant for listing copy alongside it.

No. It handles first replies, repeated questions and scheduling. Inspections, negotiation, document checks and closing still need a person.
