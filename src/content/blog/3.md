The way to cut support costs with AI is to lower your cost per resolved chat, not your headcount. On Dailzero, an AI-handled WhatsApp chat of six replies costs about ₦20 to ₦25 on a monthly plan. Compare that with what the same chat costs you today, and you know exactly how much there is to save.

Below is the calculation, done step by step so you can redo it with your own figures.

### First, find your current cost per chat

Add up what your WhatsApp support costs in a month: salaries of the officers who handle it, their data and airtime, their devices, their supervisor's time, and any overtime for night or weekend cover. Divide by the number of chats they close in that month.

We will not guess that number for you. Salaries differ too much between a Yaba startup and a microfinance bank in Kano. Call your result **H**, your human cost per chat.

### Next, the AI cost per chat

Dailzero charges in credits. A text reply uses about 5 credits. Take a hypothetical lending app that gets 100 WhatsApp chats a day, with about six AI replies per chat.

- 100 chats × 6 replies × 5 credits = 3,000 credits a day
- 3,000 × 30 days = 90,000 credits a month

That fits the Pro plan: ₦75,000 a month for 100,000 credits. Over 3,000 chats a month, that is ₦25 per chat.

### Then, be honest about the share AI can close

The AI will not close every chat. In a fintech it should close the general ones (fees, limits, requirements, how to reset something, where the office is) and hand over the rest: failed transfers, disputes, fraud, anything tied to one customer's account.

Tag a week of chats to find your split. Call the share the AI can finish alone **S**.

Your new monthly cost is roughly:

1. AI cost for all chats, since the AI answers first on every one.
2. Plus H × the chats that still reach a person, which is (1 minus S) of the total.

If S is half, your officers handle half the chats they used to. You can use that time to shorten your reversal queue, cover fewer shifts, or delay the next hire. If S is small because most of your chats are account problems, AI will save you less until a developer connects your backend. Better to know that before you pay.

### Three ways to push the cost down further

- **Keep replies short.** Every reply is about 5 credits. An answer that covers fee, limit and timing in one message costs a third of three separate ones.
- **Pay yearly if your volume is steady.** Yearly billing charges 10 months for 12. Pro becomes ₦750,000 for the year, which works out to ₦62,500 a month.
- **Use carry-over.** Unused monthly credits roll into the next month, up to 25% of the plan allowance, which helps when a quiet month is followed by a salary-week rush.

### Where the saving is not real

Two warnings. First, do not count a chat as saved if the customer had to come back twice. A vague AI answer that produces a second chat and an angry phone call costs more than a person would have. Read the handed-over chats weekly.

Second, the AI replaces typing, not judgement. A customer who writes this still needs a human within minutes:

> Somebody just called me say na your office, I give am code. My money don go.

The right AI reply is short and does not try to solve it:

> I am sorry, this is urgent. I am passing you to our fraud team right now. Please do not share any other code with anyone.

Cutting the people who handle that message is a false saving.
