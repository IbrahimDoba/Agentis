Dailzero is the best fit for a Nigerian small or medium business that sells and supports customers on its existing WhatsApp number and wants AI replies without Meta fees, paperwork or code. It is not the best choice for every business. A company that needs the official WhatsApp API, a verified badge or deep system integration without a developer should choose something else, and this page says so plainly.

### Seven reasons it fits Nigerian businesses

#### 1. It works on the number your customers already have

You link your current WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business number by QR code or pairing code, the way you link WhatsApp Web. Customers keep messaging the number they have saved.

#### 2. There are no Meta fees and no approval queue

Because Dailzero connects as a linked device and not through the official API, there are no per-conversation charges from Meta and no template approval.

#### 3. Prices are in Naira and you can start small

You pay through Paystack by card or bank transfer. Plans are ₦20,000, ₦40,000 and ₦75,000 a month, or you can skip the subscription and buy credits from ₦1,000, valid for 12 months. There is no setup fee and there are no overage fees. A 7-day free trial gives you 1,000 credits, about 200 AI replies.

At Basic, ₦20,000 buys 25,000 credits, so a 5-credit text reply works out at about ₦4.

#### 4. It handles how Nigerian customers actually message

Customers do not write formal English to a shop. They write:

> Abeg hw much last for the 2 burner gas cooker, and can I pay on delivery for Benin?

The agent copes with everyday Nigerian English and Pidgin phrasing, and understands voice notes and photos. It replies in English, with multi-language replies on Pro. Its answer here would give the price you set and your pay-on-delivery rule for Benin as you wrote it, without inventing a discount.

#### 5. It sells, it does not only answer

The agent sends product photos, videos and documents from your media library, takes order details, books appointments with reminders, remembers returning customers and lists people showing buying intent in a leads list on every plan. It also knows which Facebook or Instagram click-to-WhatsApp advert a customer came from.

#### 6. A human is always one step away

When a customer asks for a person, or the agent cannot help, it hands the chat over. You reply from the dashboard or your phone, and the AI pauses on that chat.

#### 7. It is built to protect your number

Every message is queued and sent at a human pace. New numbers go through a warm-up period, and sending slows automatically when WhatsApp signals a limit. No tool can promise a number will never be restricted.

Extras include scheduled WhatsApp Status, a developer API, a website chat widget and white-label accounts for agencies. The [features page](/features) lists them all.

### Who Dailzero is not the right choice for

Choose a different tool if any of these describe you.

- **You need the official WhatsApp Business API.** For example, you send OTPs or alerts in very high volume, or compliance requires an official Meta integration.
- **You need a verified badge.** Dailzero cannot give you one.
- **You need many agents on one number at enterprise scale.** Dailzero is one number per agent, with two agents on Pro.
- **You need ready-made integrations.** There is no native connection to Shopify, WooCommerce, Jumia, Konga, Google Sheets, CRMs or hotel and property systems. A developer can connect your own backend through webhook tools. Without a developer, the agent works only from what you type in.
- **You want it to take or confirm payments.** It shares the payment details or link you provide and hands the chat to you to confirm.
- **You want one inbox for every channel.** It covers WhatsApp and a website widget, not Instagram DMs, Messenger, SMS, email or voice calls.
- **You plan to message bought lists, or want regulated advice from a bot.** It does neither. On banking, health or legal topics it answers general questions and passes sensitive cases to a human.

### How to check the claim yourself

During the free trial, run five tests from a second phone. [Create a free account](/signup) to start.

1. Ask for a price in Pidgin and see whether the answer is correct.
2. Send a voice note with a question.
3. Send a photo of a product and ask if you sell it.
4. Ask something you never taught it, and confirm it hands over instead of guessing.
5. Type "I want to speak to someone", then reply from your own phone and check that the AI pauses.
### Frequently asked questions

#### Is Dailzero an official WhatsApp product?

No. It is an independent service that connects to your number as a linked device, like WhatsApp Web. It is not the WhatsApp Business Platform.

#### What happens if I run out of credits mid-month?

The AI pauses and customer messages still arrive in WhatsApp as normal. You can top up or wait for renewal. Unused monthly credits roll over, up to 25% of your plan's allowance.

#### Is there a contract?

Plans are monthly, and pay as you go has no subscription at all. See [pricing](/pricing).
