A Nigerian fintech can automate the general half of its WhatsApp support, such as fees, limits, how-to steps and requirements, and should keep account-specific and fraud cases with people. The work is mostly sorting: decide which questions an AI may answer alone, which it may answer only with data from your system, and which it must pass to a human immediately.

Here is a rollout that a small support team can finish in about two weeks.

### Step 1: tag one week of real chats

Before you automate anything, open your support WhatsApp and label every chat from the last seven days with one of three tags.

- **General.** The answer is the same for every customer. "How much is the transfer fee?", "What do I need to upgrade my account?", "Do you work on Sundays?"
- **Account-specific.** The answer depends on that customer's record. "Where is my ₦15,000?", "Why was my card declined?"
- **Sensitive.** Fraud reports, chargebacks, account freezes, complaints about staff, anything a regulator might later ask you about.

Count each pile. That count tells you how much automation is worth to you.

### Step 2: write the answers down once

An AI agent replies from what you give it. For the general pile, write each answer the way your best support officer would say it, with the actual figures: fees in Naira, daily limits per account tier, the documents needed for each upgrade, your support hours, and how long reversals normally take.

On Dailzero you type this in when you describe your business, and you can upload documents such as a fee schedule or an agent onboarding guide so the AI can send them in the chat.

### Step 3: decide what happens to account questions

This is where most fintech automation goes wrong. An AI agent cannot see your ledger. Dailzero has no access to customer accounts, balances or transaction status on its own.

You have two honest options:

1. **Collect and hand over.** The AI asks for the details your team needs (registered phone number, amount, date, the transaction reference) and passes the chat to a person with everything already gathered.
2. **Connect your backend.** If you have a developer, Dailzero's [webhook tools](/developers) let the agent call your own system in the middle of a conversation, for example to look up a transaction status. You control what that endpoint returns and to whom.

A customer writes:

> I sent 15k to my GTB since morning, e never enter. Una don debit me o.

With option 1, a good reply looks like this:

> Sorry about that. Please send the transaction reference and the phone number on your account, and I will pass this to our support team now. Do not share your PIN or any OTP with anyone, including us.

### Step 4: set the rules the AI must never break

Put these in your instructions in plain words.

- Never ask for a PIN, password, OTP or full card number.
- Never promise a reversal date or confirm that money has moved.
- Never give investment, loan or tax advice beyond your published terms.
- Hand over at once when a customer mentions fraud, a stolen phone, a hacked account or a wrong transfer.

Dailzero hands a chat to a person when the customer asks or when the AI cannot help. Your officer can reply from the dashboard or from the phone, the AI pauses on that chat, and you switch it back on afterwards.

### Step 5: test with staff, then go live on one line

Have three colleagues attack the agent for a day with the hardest questions from your tagged week. Fix every wrong or vague answer in your written knowledge. Then go live on a single support number and read the handed-over chats each morning for the first fortnight.

### Frequently asked questions

#### Can an AI agent check a customer's balance or transaction on WhatsApp?

Not by itself. It needs a developer to connect your system through webhook tools. Without that, it collects the details and hands the chat to your team.

#### What should a fintech never automate?

Fraud reports, disputes, account restrictions and anything that needs identity verification. The AI should gather the facts and pass these to a trained person.

#### Does the AI understand Pidgin and voice notes?

Dailzero copes with everyday Nigerian English and Pidgin in customer messages and understands voice notes and photos, such as a screenshot of a failed transfer.
