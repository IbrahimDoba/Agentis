A WhatsApp AI agent usually costs less per chat than a virtual assistant, but the two do different jobs. On Dailzero the agent costs ₦20,000 to ₦75,000 a month depending on volume, replies in seconds at any hour and handles every chat at once. A virtual assistant costs whatever salary you agree, works set hours and handles judgement, complaints and tasks outside WhatsApp. Many businesses end up using both.

Salaries for virtual assistants in Nigeria vary widely with experience, hours and location, so this guide does not quote a figure. It gives you a formula and you put in your own number.

### What you pay for with each

| | WhatsApp AI agent | Virtual assistant |
|---|---|---|
| How it is priced | Credits: about 5 per text reply | Monthly salary or hourly rate |
| Hours | Day and night, every day | The hours you agree |
| Chats at once | All of them | One or a few at a time |
| Cost when volume doubles | Rises with replies sent | Stays flat until you need a second person |
| Other costs | None on the default route. No Meta fees, no overage | Data, airtime, a phone or laptop, training, your supervision time |
| Judgement and empathy | Follows your instructions, hands over when unsure | Strong |
| Work outside WhatsApp | No | Yes: calls, invoices, suppliers, social media |

### Step 1: work out the AI agent's cost

1. Count chats on a normal day.
2. Multiply by AI replies per chat. Six is a fair average.
3. Multiply by 5 credits, then by 30 days.
4. Match the total to a plan.

**Worked example:** a shop gets 40 chats a day at about 6 AI replies each.

- 40 × 6 = 240 replies a day
- 240 × 5 credits = 1,200 credits a day
- 1,200 × 30 days = 36,000 credits a month

That fits the Starter plan: ₦40,000 a month for 60,000 credits. Over the month the shop has 40 × 30 = 1,200 chats, so the cost is ₦40,000 ÷ 1,200 = about ₦33 a chat.

A smaller shop with 15 chats a day uses 15 × 6 × 5 × 30 = 13,500 credits. That fits Basic at ₦20,000. With 450 chats in the month, the cost is ₦20,000 ÷ 450 = about ₦44 a chat.

### Step 2: work out the virtual assistant's cost

Use your own figures:

**Monthly cost of an assistant = salary + data and airtime + any tools or equipment + the value of the time you spend supervising**

**Cost per chat = monthly cost ÷ chats the assistant actually handles in the month**

Two things to be honest about when you fill it in:

- **Count only chats handled.** Messages that arrive after closing time, on days off or while the assistant is busy with another customer are not handled until later, and some of those customers will have gone.
- **Count the other work too.** If your assistant spends half the day on invoices and suppliers, only half the salary belongs to WhatsApp.

### Step 3: compare

Take the 40 chats a day shop again. The AI agent costs ₦40,000 a month there. So an assistant doing only WhatsApp replies is cheaper on paper only if your full monthly cost for that person is below ₦40,000, and only if they catch every chat. Put your own salary figure in and see which side of the line you land on.

Then look at the hours. Check your WhatsApp for one week and count the messages that arrived outside working hours. If that number is high, the comparison is no longer about price. Nobody is answering those customers.

### Where a virtual assistant wins

- **Complaints and refunds.** An upset customer wants a person with the authority to fix things.
- **Negotiation and relationships.** Big orders, long-standing customers and bulk buyers.
- **Work that is not chat.** Phone calls, chasing suppliers, bookkeeping, posting content, arranging dispatch.
- **Unusual requests.** Anything your written instructions did not foresee.
- **Very low volume.** If you get a few chats a day and already have someone, the agent adds little.

### Where the AI agent wins

- **Speed at any hour.** Replies come in seconds, including at 2am and on Sunday.
- **Busy periods.** Fifty chats at once after an ad goes live are all answered.
- **Repetitive questions.** Prices, sizes, delivery fees and opening hours, answered the same way every time from your own details.
- **Predictable cost.** There are no overage fees. When credits finish, the agent pauses.

### Using both

The common setup is the agent in front and a person behind. The agent answers first, sends product photos, takes the order details and books the appointment. It hands the chat over when a customer asks for a person or when it cannot help. Your assistant, or you, replies from the dashboard or straight from the phone, and the AI pauses on that chat.

### Frequently asked questions

#### Is an AI agent cheaper than a virtual assistant in Nigeria?

For answering WhatsApp chats, usually yes on a cost per chat basis. On Dailzero, 40 chats a day costs about ₦40,000 a month. Compare that with the full monthly cost of your own assistant using the formula above.

#### Can an AI agent replace my virtual assistant?

It can take over the repetitive chat replies. It cannot take over complaints, calls, supplier work or judgement. Most businesses keep a person for those.

#### How much does a virtual assistant cost in Nigeria?

It varies widely with experience, hours and whether the role is remote or on site. Use the salary you are actually paying or being quoted, then add data, tools and your supervision time.

#### What if the AI cannot answer a customer?

It hands the chat to a person. You or your assistant reply from the dashboard or your phone and the AI pauses on that chat.
