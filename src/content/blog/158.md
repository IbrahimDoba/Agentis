A WhatsApp AI agent helps a logistics or dispatch company by answering the questions that take up most of the day: "how much to send this to Ajah?", "where is my rider?" and "when will it arrive?". It can quote from your rate card, take pickup requests and hand urgent problems to a dispatcher. To give a live tracking answer it must be connected to your own tracking system through an API. Without that link it can only tell customers what you have written down.

### What customers ask a dispatch company

- "How much from Ikeja to Lekki Phase 1?"
- "Do you do same day delivery?"
- "Can you pick up from Surulere by 2pm?"
- "Where is my package?"
- "The rider is not picking calls"
- "Do you deliver to Ibadan? How many days?"

The first three are sales questions. The next two are tracking and support. An AI agent handles them differently, so set it up for each.

### What the agent can do, and what it needs

| Job | What the agent needs from you |
|---|---|
| Quote a delivery price | Your rate card by zone or route, written clearly |
| Explain service areas and delivery times | Your coverage list and usual timelines |
| Take a pickup request | The details you want collected: addresses, item, phone numbers, time |
| Answer "where is my order?" with live status | Your tracking system connected through an API |
| Hand a problem to a dispatcher | Handover rules, and someone available to reply |
| Reply at night and on weekends | Nothing extra |

### Live tracking needs your own system

This is the point most vendors skip. An AI agent does not know where your rider is. It can only look it up.

Dailzero has a feature called live API tools. You connect an endpoint from your own backend, and the agent calls it during a chat when a customer asks about an order. If your system can return a status for a tracking number, the agent can read it back to the customer in plain words.

That needs two things: a tracking or order system that has an API, and a developer to connect it. See the [developer docs](/developers).

If your tracking lives in a notebook, a spreadsheet or the riders' own WhatsApp chats, the agent cannot give live status. In that case set it to collect the order details and pass the chat to a dispatcher, which is still faster than a customer waiting unanswered.

### Proactive delivery updates

Many logistics companies want to message customers first: "your rider is on the way". Think about how you send these, because it affects your number.

- **On the linked-device route**, messages go from your normal WhatsApp number. Sending to people who are expecting a delivery is lower risk than cold messaging, but high volumes of near-identical alerts to people who have not saved your number can still draw reports. Dailzero queues and paces every send, and developers can trigger messages from their own code through the Dailzero API.
- **On the official WhatsApp Business Platform**, you send approved message templates, which is the route built for high-volume notifications. Meta charges its own message fees. Dailzero sets this route up with its team on request.

If you send thousands of delivery alerts a day, the official route is usually the right one. Compare them in [official WhatsApp API vs linked device](/blog/official-whatsapp-api-vs-linked-device-which-should-you-use). No tool can promise a number will never be restricted.

### How to set it up

1. **Write your rate card as text.** List zones or routes with prices. "Mainland to Island: ₦3,500" is easier for an agent than a picture of a table.
2. **List what you do not carry.** Cash, liquids, fragile items, anything above a certain weight.
3. **Define a pickup request.** Decide the fields the agent must collect before it confirms anything.
4. **Set the handover rules.** Missing parcels, damaged items, refund requests and angry customers go straight to a person.
5. **Link your number.** Scan a QR code or use a pairing code.
6. **Connect tracking if you have an API.** Otherwise skip this and route tracking questions to dispatch.
7. **Test it.** Ask for a route you do not cover and a price that is not on your card. The agent should say it will confirm, not invent a fee.

### Where a different tool is the better choice

- **You have a large support team on one number.** A shared team inbox on the official API, with ticket assignment, may suit you better than an AI-first tool.
- **Your main need is bulk alerts, not conversations.** A messaging API provider with approved templates is built for that.
- **You only get a handful of bookings a day.** The free WhatsApp Business app, with a saved rate card and quick replies, may be enough.

### Frequently asked questions

#### Can the AI tell a customer exactly where the rider is?

Only if it is connected to a system that knows. With your tracking API connected, it can read out the status your system returns. Without it, the agent should collect the order details and hand over to dispatch.

#### Can it calculate a delivery price?

It can quote from a rate card you give it by zone or route. Custom quotes for unusual items or long distances are better handed to a person.

#### Can it assign riders?

No. It takes the request and records the details. Assigning a rider stays with your dispatcher or your dispatch software.

#### What happens when a customer is angry about a late delivery?

The agent is set to hand over when a customer is frustrated or asks for a person. The chat is flagged in the dashboard, and the AI pauses on it once you reply.

#### Do I need a developer?

Not for quotes, pickup requests and general questions. You need one only to connect your tracking system or to send automatic updates from your own software.
