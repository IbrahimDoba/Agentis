For most small Nigerian businesses whose WhatsApp chats are mainly repeated questions about price, availability and delivery, an AI agent makes more sense first, and a customer service rep makes sense second. If most of your chats are complaints, negotiations or need someone to check a system, hire the person. Many businesses end up with both: the AI answers the routine questions and a human takes what is left.

Decide on your own numbers, not someone else's.

### Work out the real cost of a rep

Do not use a salary figure from the internet. Use yours. Add up:

- The monthly salary you would actually pay in your city.
- Data, airtime and a work phone if you provide one.
- Pension, tax and any other statutory costs that apply to you.
- Your own time recruiting and training, and doing it again if they leave.

Then divide by the hours they will really be answering chats. That is your cost per covered hour. One shift still leaves nights, Sundays and public holidays uncovered.

### Work out the real cost of the AI

The AI's cost follows volume. On Dailzero a text reply uses about 5 credits. Say your shop gets 60 chats a day and each needs around 6 replies:

60 × 6 × 5 credits × 30 days = 54,000 credits a month.

That fits the Starter plan at ₦40,000, which includes 60,000 credits. At 20 chats a day the same sum gives 18,000 credits, inside Basic at ₦20,000.

Then add the cost people skip: someone must still handle the chats the AI hands over. If a third of your chats need a human, the AI removes only the other two thirds.

### What the AI is plainly better at

- **The 11pm "is this available?" message.** A rep is asleep. The AI replies.
- **Twenty people asking the same thing during a promo.** It answers all of them at once, with the same correct price.
- **Never forgetting to ask for the address.** It collects name, address, size and quantity every time.

### What a person is plainly better at

Consider this message:

> I paid 45k since morning and your rider has not come. If I don't get my order today, refund me.

The AI cannot see your bank account or where the rider is. The right automated reply is an apology, a line saying the issue is being passed to a staff member now, and a handoff. Everything after that needs a human who can check the alert, call the dispatch rider and decide whether to refund.

People are also better at calming an angry customer, bargaining with a bulk buyer and spotting fraud.

### A simple way to decide

Look at your last 100 WhatsApp chats and sort them into routine (the answer is the same for everyone) and non-routine.

- **Mostly routine, and you are the one answering.** Start with the AI. It frees your own time and you hire later when handoffs pile up.
- **Mostly routine, and you already have a rep.** Add the AI. Your rep stops typing prices and concentrates on complaints, payment confirmation and follow-up.
- **Mostly non-routine.** Hire a person. An AI that hands off most chats is not saving much.
- **Chats come heavily at night or on weekends.** The AI covers hours that would otherwise need a second salary.
- **Very low volume.** You may need neither.

### How the two work together

With Dailzero, team members can have their own logins. The agent answers first. When a customer asks for a person, or the question is beyond what it knows, the chat is handed over. Your rep replies from the dashboard or the phone, the AI pauses on that chat, and it is switched back on when the issue is closed.

### Where the AI is the wrong choice

Be cautious if your customers mainly want phone calls or use Instagram DMs, since Dailzero covers neither, or if every answer depends on live stock or order data and you have no developer to connect your system.

### Frequently asked questions

#### Will an AI agent replace my customer service staff?

Not completely. It takes over repeated questions, while complaints, refunds and payment confirmation still need a person.

#### Which is cheaper?

Compare a Dailzero plan, ₦20,000 to ₦75,000 a month, with your own all-in cost for a rep, remembering that each covers work the other cannot.

#### Can the AI and my rep use the same WhatsApp number?

Yes. The agent runs on your existing number and staff reply from the dashboard with their own logins or from the phone.
