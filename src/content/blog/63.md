WhatsApp AI cannot work inside your Jumia or Konga store. Dailzero has no integration with either marketplace, so it cannot see marketplace orders, answer marketplace messages or update your listings. Where it helps is the second channel most marketplace sellers already run: the direct orders that come to your own WhatsApp number.

### What the AI can and cannot do for a marketplace seller

| On your own WhatsApp number | On Jumia or Konga |
|---|---|
| Reply to enquiries in seconds, day and night | Cannot read or reply to marketplace messages |
| Send product photos and your price list | Cannot edit listings or prices |
| Take orders and collect name, address and quantity | Cannot see or process marketplace orders |
| List likely buyers as leads in your dashboard | Cannot sync stock between channels |

### Where direct WhatsApp orders come from

Most sellers who list on a marketplace also sell directly. The enquiries usually come from:

- Your Instagram or Facebook page, with a WhatsApp link in the bio.
- Click-to-WhatsApp ads. Dailzero records which Facebook or Instagram ad each customer came from.
- Repeat customers who saved your number after an earlier direct purchase.
- People who view your WhatsApp Status.

One caution. Each marketplace has its own seller rules about contacting buyers and what you may put inside a parcel. Read them and follow them. Nothing here is about pulling marketplace buyers off the platform; it is about customers who come to you directly.

### Why direct orders slip away

A marketplace has a checkout button. WhatsApp has you. If you are packing orders, at the logistics hub or asleep, the direct customer waits, and a customer who is waiting is also comparing prices.

Take a typical evening message to a seller of small home appliances:

> "Is the 1.5 litre blender still available? Is it original and can I pay on delivery in Gwarinpa?"

A useful reply covers every part of that at once:

> "Yes, it is available, brand new with a 12-month warranty, at ₦38,000. Delivery to Gwarinpa is ₦2,500 and pay on delivery is available within Abuja. Can I have your name and full address to book it?"

The AI gives that answer because you typed the warranty, price, delivery fee and pay on delivery areas into its setup. It then collects the details and the order is waiting for you in the morning.

### The stock problem, and how to manage it

This is the real risk of selling on two channels. The AI cannot see your inventory. If your last blender sells on the marketplace at 3pm, the agent will still offer it on WhatsApp at 9pm unless you tell it otherwise. Three ways to handle it:

1. **Update the product list daily.** Five minutes each evening, after you reconcile the day's orders.
2. **Mark fast-moving items "confirm first".** Instruct the agent to say it will confirm availability and hand the chat to you for those items only.
3. **Connect your own system.** If you keep stock in your own software and have a developer, Dailzero's webhook tools let the agent look up availability mid-chat. That connects to your system, not to the marketplace. Details are on the [developers page](/developers).

### What a direct order costs you to handle

On a direct order there is no marketplace commission, but delivery, trust and customer service are all on you. The service part is easy to price. A text reply costs about 5 credits, so a six-reply chat is about 30 credits. On the Basic plan, ₦20,000 for 25,000 credits, that is roughly ₦24 a chat.

Put that beside the commission you pay on the same item through a marketplace, using your own figure, and you can see what a direct order is worth.

### Payment and delivery on direct orders

The AI does not process or verify payments. It shares the bank details or Paystack link you give it and hands the chat over so you can confirm the alert. It also cannot track a dispatch rider. If a customer asks where their parcel is, it passes the chat to you.

### Frequently asked questions

#### Can Dailzero reply to customer messages inside Jumia or Konga?

No. It works on your own WhatsApp number and on a website chat widget only. Marketplace messages stay in the marketplace's seller tools.

#### Can it sync my stock between the marketplace and WhatsApp?

No. There is no marketplace integration. You either update the agent's product list yourself or have a developer connect your own inventory system through webhook tools.

#### Do I need a separate WhatsApp number for the AI?

No. You link the WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business number you already use. Each agent works with one number.

#### Is it worth it if most of my sales are on the marketplace?

Only if you get, or want, direct enquiries. Start with the 7-day free trial or a small pay as you go pack and see how many WhatsApp chats turn into orders before committing to a monthly plan.
