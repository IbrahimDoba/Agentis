Dailzero and WASBOT both link to the WhatsApp number you already use, and both schedule Status posts, save contacts and send paced broadcasts. The difference is what happens when a customer writes back. WASBOT replies with keyword sequences you build yourself, and it is strong on groups and running several numbers. Dailzero puts an AI agent on the number that holds a real conversation, answers from your prices and takes the order. Choose WASBOT for marketing reach at a flat price. Choose Dailzero if you also want the chats answered.

### Where WASBOT is the better choice

- **You run WhatsApp groups or a WhatsApp TV.** WASBOT sends to selected groups, tags members, schedules group posts and handles join requests. Dailzero is built for one-to-one customer chats, not group management.
- **You want the widest Status reach for the lowest price.** As of November 2026 its ₦8,000 Basic plan lists 10,000 Status recipients, which Dailzero only reaches on Pro.
- **You manage several numbers.** WASBOT posts one Status to several WhatsApp numbers at once. Dailzero runs one number per agent.
- **You want flat monthly pricing.** WASBOT charges by plan limits. Dailzero charges credits for each reply, broadcast and Status post.
- **You already have a contact list in a file.** WASBOT builds lists from CSV, VCF and Google Contacts. Dailzero has no option to upload a list of numbers.
- **Your replies are simple and fixed.** If "PRICE" should always return the same price list, a keyword sequence does the job.

### Where Dailzero is the better choice

- **Customers ask real questions.** "Do you have this in size 42 and can you deliver to Ibadan tomorrow?" does not match a keyword. The Dailzero agent reads the message, answers from your own products and prices, and keeps the conversation going.
- **You want orders taken, not only messages sent.** The agent sends product photos, takes orders, books appointments and hands the chat to you when a person is needed.
- **You want Status replies answered.** When someone replies to a Status post, the agent knows which post they mean and answers with the price and how to order.
- **Customers send voice notes and pictures.** The agent reads both.
- **You may need the official route later.** Dailzero's app is approved by Meta for WhatsApp Business messaging, and the team can set up the official WhatsApp Business Platform on request.

### What you give up with Dailzero

You give up group tools, multi-number cross-posting and list uploads. Status reach per post is lower at each price point, and Autopilot posts at most 6 times a day, at least 2 hours apart.

Costs also work differently. A Status post costs 5 credits for text, 8 for an image and 12 for a video, with no per-recipient charge. Saving a contact costs 1 credit. A text broadcast costs 5 credits per person. When credits finish, the agent pauses. There are no overage fees.

Both tools use a linked device by default, which WhatsApp does not officially support for automation. Dailzero queues every message, sends at a human pace and warms up new numbers, but no tool can promise a number will never be restricted. ### Frequently asked questions

#### Is Dailzero a WASBOT alternative?

Yes for Status scheduling, contact saving, Status analytics and paced broadcasts on your own number. No for group management and multi-number posting, where WASBOT does more.

#### Does WASBOT have an AI agent?

Its website does not mention AI. Replies are based on keyword triggers and sequences you set up. Check its site for the current feature list.

#### Which is cheaper, Dailzero or WASBOT?

WASBOT starts lower, at ₦8,000 a month as of November 2026. Dailzero starts at ₦20,000 a month or ₦1,000 pay as you go, and that price includes the AI replies. Compare on what you need done, not only the monthly figure.

#### Can I use both on the same number?

Both connect as linked devices, so it is technically possible, but two tools sending from one number makes pacing harder to control. It is safer to pick one tool per number.

#### Do either of them use the official WhatsApp API?

WASBOT's site says it is not Meta's official API. Dailzero uses a linked device by default and offers the official WhatsApp Business Platform on request, where Status and contact saving are not available.
