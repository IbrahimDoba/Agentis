Dailzero and Neria are both WhatsApp AI agents made for Nigerian businesses. Neria connects through Meta's official WhatsApp Business Platform, sells packs of chats and also offers phone call agents and other channels. Dailzero links to the number you already use, charges credits per message and adds WhatsApp Status tools, contact saving and broadcasts. Choose Neria if you want the official platform or an AI that also answers phone calls. Choose Dailzero if you want to keep your number in your own app and market through Status as well.

### Where Neria is the better choice

- **You want the official platform by default.** Neria's site says it connects your WhatsApp Business number through Meta's official WhatsApp Business Platform. That is the route WhatsApp itself provides for automation.
- **You want an AI that answers phone calls.** Neria sells receptionist, sales and support call agents billed per minute. Dailzero does not offer phone calls.
- **You need more channels.** Neria lists Instagram and Facebook, SMS and email alongside WhatsApp. Dailzero covers WhatsApp and a website chat widget.
- **You think in conversations, not messages.** A chat pack is simple to plan around when each customer conversation is long. Up to 30 messages in a day count as one chat.
- **Nigerian languages are central to your business.** Neria lists English, Pidgin, Yoruba, Hausa and Igbo on its site.
- **Your volume is very small.** A ₦2,000 pack with no setup fee and no contract is a low-commitment start.

### Where Dailzero is the better choice

- **You want to keep your number in your own WhatsApp app.** Dailzero links like WhatsApp Web. Your chats stay on your phone and you can reply from the phone at any time. The AI pauses on that chat when you do.
- **You sell through WhatsApp Status.** Dailzero schedules Status posts, runs Autopilot from a gallery, saves the contacts who message you so they can see your Status, shows who viewed and replied, and answers Status replies with AI.
- **Most of your chats are short.** A customer who asks one question and gets one answer uses about 5 credits on Dailzero. On Basic, ₦20,000 covers about 5,000 AI messages.
- **You want credit that does not expire in 30 days.** Pay as you go credits last 12 months.
- **You want no Meta fees.** On the linked-device route there are no Meta message charges, including for broadcasts.
- **You want to start in minutes.** There is no Meta verification on the default route.

### How the costs compare in practice

The two price models reward different patterns, so work out your own.

1. Count how many customer conversations you have in a month.
2. Count the average number of AI replies in one conversation.
3. For Dailzero, multiply replies by about 5 credits and compare with a plan's credits. Images cost 8 credits and videos 12.
4. For Neria, match your number of conversations to a pack on its pricing section.

Long conversations favour a per-chat price. Many short conversations favour a per-message price.

### What you give up with Dailzero

On the default route Dailzero is a linked device, not the official API. There are no approved templates and no verified badge, and WhatsApp does not officially support automating an account this way, so the risk is higher than on the official platform. Dailzero queues every message, sends at a human pace and warms up new numbers. No tool can promise a number will never be restricted. See [is it safe to connect an AI to your WhatsApp number](/blog/is-it-safe-to-connect-an-ai-to-your-whatsapp-number).

You also give up phone call agents, Instagram, SMS and email. If you need the official route, Dailzero sets it up on request, with Meta's fees and without Status or contact saving.

### Frequently asked questions

#### Is Dailzero or Neria cheaper?

It depends on your chats. Neria charges per conversation and Dailzero per message. Many short chats usually cost less on Dailzero, while long conversations can cost less on a chat pack. Use the four steps above with your own numbers.

#### Does Neria use the official WhatsApp API?

Yes. Its site says it connects your WhatsApp Business number through Meta's official WhatsApp Business Platform.

#### Does Dailzero answer phone calls?

No. Dailzero works on WhatsApp and through a website chat widget. If you need an AI on phone calls, Neria lists that on its site.

#### Can Neria schedule WhatsApp Status?

Its site does not list it, and WhatsApp Status is not part of the official WhatsApp Business Platform. Dailzero offers Status scheduling on its linked-device route.

#### Do both hand over to a human?

Yes. Both hand the chat to you or your staff when a customer asks for a person or a question needs your decision.
