A free WhatsApp chatbot is enough if you get a few chats a day, your customers ask the same simple questions and you can reply within the hour. It is not enough once customers ask for specific prices, write in their own words, message at night or arrive faster than you can answer. The test is simple: count how many chats went unanswered or were answered late last week.

### What "free" usually means

There are three kinds of free, and they are not the same thing.

| Kind of free | What you get | The catch |
|---|---|---|
| WhatsApp Business app tools | Greeting message, away message, quick replies, labels, catalogue | Fixed text. It cannot read the question. |
| Free tier of a keyword or menu bot | "Reply 1 for prices, 2 for delivery" | Limits on messages or contacts, and no real understanding |
| Free trial of a paid AI agent | The full product for a short time | It ends. Dailzero's is 7 days with 1,000 credits. |

The first is free for good. The second is free until you grow. The third is a test drive.

### When free is enough: yes

- **You get fewer than about ten chats a day.** You can answer them yourself between other work.
- **You sell one or two products at fixed prices.** A greeting message with the price and how to order covers most chats.
- **Your customers already know you.** Regulars who message to reorder do not need an AI.
- **You are just starting.** Learn what customers ask before you automate it.
- **You only need an "I am closed" notice.** The away message does exactly that.

If this is you, set up the free tools properly and spend nothing. [The cheapest way to automate WhatsApp replies in Nigeria](/blog/cheapest-way-to-automate-whatsapp-replies-in-nigeria) walks through the setup.

### When free is not enough: no

- **Customers ask questions with many possible answers.** "How much is the blue one in size 42 delivered to Ibadan?" has no quick reply.
- **Customers write in Pidgin, with typos or by voice note.** Keyword bots need the exact word.
- **Enquiries come at night or on Sunday.** An away message tells them to wait. Some will not.
- **You run ads.** Paying for clicks and then answering three hours later wastes the ad money.
- **You want the chat to end in an order or a booking.** Free tools reply. They do not collect a delivery address or book a slot.
- **Chats arrive in bursts.** Twenty at once after a Status post is more than one person can answer quickly.

### A two-minute check

1. Open WhatsApp and look at last week's chats.
2. Count the chats that waited more than an hour for a reply.
3. Count the chats that came in while you were closed.
4. Count the customers who asked a price and then went quiet.
5. If the total is more than a handful, free is costing you sales.

### What a paid agent does not fix

Paying will not help if your prices are not written down anywhere, if you are often out of stock or if delivery is unreliable. The agent answers from what you give it. Sort those first.

It is also not free of risk. Dailzero's default route links your number like WhatsApp Web, which is not the official API. Messages are queued and sent at a human pace and new numbers go through a warm-up period, but no tool can promise a number will never be restricted. Read [is it safe to connect an AI to your WhatsApp number](/blog/is-it-safe-to-connect-an-ai-to-your-whatsapp-number) before you decide.

### A sensible order to do things in

1. Set up the free greeting, away message and quick replies today.
2. Run the two-minute check after a week.
3. If chats are slipping, take a free trial and test with real customers.
4. Start on pay as you go. Move to a monthly plan only when the volume is steady.

#### Is there a completely free WhatsApp chatbot?

The tools inside the WhatsApp Business app are free for good: greeting message, away message and quick replies. They send fixed text and are not AI.

When chats regularly wait more than an hour, arrive while you are closed, or end after the customer asked a price and got no quick answer.

#### Can a free keyword bot understand Pidgin or voice notes?

Generally no. Keyword bots match set words or menu numbers. An AI agent reads the message itself, and Dailzero's agent also reads voice notes and images customers send.
