### How the automatic Status works

- **Scheduled posts.** Text, image or video, posted now or at a time you set.
- **Autopilot.** You upload photos and videos to a gallery. You approve a posting plan, and it posts through the day.
- **Contact saving.** It bulk-saves the people who message you, so they can see your Status. Each contact saved costs 1 credit, and free accounts save up to 200 in total.
- **Analytics.** Delivered, viewed, liked and replied for each post. Views undercount, because people with read receipts off do not show.
- **AI on replies.** When someone replies to a Status, the agent answers them.

A Status post costs 5 credits for text, 8 for an image and 12 for a video, with no charge per recipient. Reach per post is 2,000 contacts on Basic, 5,000 on Starter and 10,000 on Pro. Plans are ₦20,000, ₦40,000 and ₦75,000 a month, or pay as you go from ₦1,000. ### Where the other options win

- **You only need Status and groups, not AI.** WASBOT costs less for that and adds group posting and multi-number tools.
- **You want the lowest risk to your number.** The official platform is the route WhatsApp provides for automation. Neria, ChatRouter, Sowo and Evolve are built on it. Dailzero also offers the official route on request, but Status and contact saving are not available there.
- **You need a verified badge or approved templates.** Those come only through the official platform.
- **You want Instagram DMs or phone calls answered.** Neria lists both. Dailzero works on WhatsApp.

### What you give up without the API

A linked device is not the official API. That means no verified badge and no approved message templates on this route. Your phone stays the main device and should come online regularly.

It also means number safety matters. Dailzero queues every message and sends at a human pace, puts new numbers through a warm-up period, and slows down automatically when WhatsApp signals a limit. No tool can promise a number will never be restricted, and we do not promise it either. If the number is new, use it normally for about two weeks before you connect it. ### Frequently asked questions

#### Which WhatsApp AI agent works without the Business API?

Dailzero. It links to a normal WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business number as a linked device, so no Business API account or Meta verification is needed.

#### Can it post my WhatsApp Status automatically?

Yes. You can schedule posts, or use Autopilot, which posts from your gallery on a plan you approve, up to 6 times a day and at least 2 hours apart.

#### Does it work with normal WhatsApp, not only WhatsApp Business?

Yes. Either app works. You link the number the same way you link WhatsApp Web.

#### Do I pay Meta anything?

Not on the linked-device route. You pay only for Dailzero credits, and when they finish the agent pauses. Meta's fees apply only if you choose the official API route.

#### Is it safe for my number?

It is lower risk when the agent mostly replies to customers who wrote first and sends at a human pace, as Dailzero does. It is not zero risk. Many owners start on a second number or on a number that is at least a few months old.

#### Can Dailzero also use the official API?

Yes, on request. Dailzero's app is approved by Meta for WhatsApp Business messaging. Status and contact saving are not available on that route.
