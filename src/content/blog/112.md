### Why speed matters in property

A buyer or tenant usually messages several agents about similar listings. The agent who answers first, with the price and pictures, gets the inspection. The enquiries also repeat: "Is it still available", "What is the total package", "Is there light", "Can I see it on Saturday". An AI can answer those in seconds and leave you the serious prospects.

### Setting up a property agent with Dailzero

1. Write each listing with price, location, total package, fees and what is included.
2. Upload photos, a short video and any documents you are happy to share.
3. Set the qualifying questions: budget, preferred area, move-in date, rent or buy.
4. Set your inspection days and times, and whether an inspection fee applies.
5. Link your number and test by enquiring about three different listings.

### Frequently asked questions

#### Can an AI qualify property leads on WhatsApp?

Yes. Dailzero's agent asks the questions you set, such as budget, location and timing, and flags serious enquiries as leads in your dashboard.

#### Can it send photos and videos of a listing?

Yes. You upload media for each property and the agent sends what matches the enquiry.

#### What happens when a property is taken?

The agent answers from the details you give it. Update or remove the listing when it goes, and tell the agent to suggest similar ones.

#### Will clients know they are talking to an AI?

The agent writes in the tone you choose. Whether to tell clients is your decision. You can take over any chat from your phone, and the AI pauses on it.
