Dailzero connects to WhatsApp in two ways. The default is a linked device: you link the WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business number you already use by QR code or pairing code, the same way you link WhatsApp Web. The second is the official WhatsApp Business Platform (Meta's Cloud API), which is set up with the Dailzero team on request. Most small businesses use the first. Businesses that need compliance sign-off or approved message templates use the second.

### Route 1: linked device (the default)

WhatsApp lets you use one account on several devices. Your phone is the main device, and WhatsApp Web or WhatsApp Desktop are linked devices. Dailzero connects as one of those linked devices.

In practice:

- Your number stays in your own app, on your own phone.
- You link it in minutes. There is no Meta business verification.
- There are no Meta message fees. You pay only for Dailzero credits.
- It works with both normal WhatsApp and WhatsApp Business.
- You can remove the connection at any time: open Linked devices on your phone, tap the device and log out.

This is not the official API. WhatsApp does not officially support automating an account through a linked device, so this route carries more risk to the number than the official one. Dailzero reduces that risk in three ways: every message is queued and sent at a human pace, new numbers go through a warm-up period with lower limits, and sending slows down automatically when WhatsApp signals a limit. No tool can promise a number will never be restricted, and Dailzero does not promise it.

### Route 2: the official WhatsApp Business Platform

This is the route Meta itself provides for businesses that automate messaging. Dailzero's app is approved by Meta for WhatsApp Business messaging, so a business can run its agent through the Cloud API.

In practice:

- It is set up with the Dailzero team. It is not a self-service button.
- Meta's own message fees apply, on top of Dailzero's price.
- You can use message templates approved by Meta.
- It is the route a compliance or legal team usually asks for.
- WhatsApp Status posting, contact saving and the other linked-device features are not available.

Dailzero is an independent service provider and is not owned by or affiliated with Meta.

### Which route should you pick

**Pick the linked device if:**

- You are an owner or small team selling from your own number.
- You want to start today and pay only in Naira.
- You want to post WhatsApp Status on a schedule and save the contacts who message you.
- Most of your messages are replies to customers who wrote first.
- Your number has been in normal use for a while. If it is new, use it normally for about two weeks first.

**Pick the official platform if:**

- A bank, regulator, parent company or compliance team requires an official Meta integration.
- You need approved templates to message customers first, for example delivery alerts.
- You want a verified badge.
- Losing the number for even a day would badly damage the business.

If you are unsure, [official WhatsApp API vs linked device](/blog/official-whatsapp-api-vs-linked-device-which-should-you-use) walks through the decision without reference to any one tool.

### How to link your number (linked device)

1. Sign up and set up your agent in the Dailzero dashboard.
2. Open the WhatsApp connection step. Choose QR code or pairing code.
3. On your phone, open WhatsApp, then Settings, then Linked devices, then Link a device.
4. Scan the QR code on screen. If you chose a pairing code, tap "Link with phone number instead" and type the code.
5. Wait for the dashboard to show the number as connected.
6. Send the number a message from another phone to test the reply.

For the official platform, write to support@dailzero.com or use the [contact page](/contact) and say you want the official WhatsApp Business Platform route.

### What stays the same on your phone

On the linked-device route your phone remains the main device. Your chats, groups, contacts and Status stay where they are, and you can still read and answer any chat yourself. When you reply to a customer from your phone, the AI pauses on that chat so the two of you do not answer at once.

Your phone should come online regularly. WhatsApp signs linked devices out if the main phone stays offline for a long time, and you would then need to link again.

### Frequently asked questions

#### Does Dailzero use the official WhatsApp API?

It offers both routes. The default is a linked device, which is not the official API. The official WhatsApp Business Platform is available on request and is set up with the Dailzero team.

#### Do I pay Meta when I use Dailzero?

Not on the linked-device route. On the official platform route, Meta's own message fees apply as well as Dailzero's price.

#### Do I need a new SIM or a new number?

No. On the linked-device route you link the number you already use. Many owners choose a second number anyway, to keep their main line separate from automation.

#### Can I post WhatsApp Status on the official route?

No. Status posting, Autopilot and contact saving work only on the linked-device route.

#### Is the linked-device route safe?

It is lower risk when the agent mostly answers customers who wrote first and sends at a human pace, as Dailzero does. It is not zero risk. See [is it safe to connect an AI to your WhatsApp number](/blog/is-it-safe-to-connect-an-ai-to-your-whatsapp-number).
