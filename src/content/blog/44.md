WhatsApp Business is a free app from WhatsApp that gives a restaurant a business profile, a menu catalogue, saved replies and automatic greeting and away messages on its own number. For a small kitchen it is enough to start taking orders today. It stops being enough when orders arrive faster than one person can type, and that is the point where an AI agent on the same number makes sense.

### Part 1: set up the app properly

Many restaurants install WhatsApp Business and use it like ordinary WhatsApp. Spend an hour on these settings first.

- **Business profile.** Add your address, a short description, opening hours and a link to your Instagram page.
- **Catalogue.** Add your dishes with a photo, name and price, so a customer can browse it from your profile.
- **Greeting message.** A short welcome sent to new customers. Say what you serve and where you deliver.
- **Away message.** Sent outside opening hours. Say when you reopen and whether you take pre-orders.
- **Quick replies.** Saved answers you call up with a shortcut. Make one each for the menu, delivery fees, account details and opening hours.
- **Labels.** Tag chats as new order, awaiting payment, paid, or out for delivery, so you can see where each order stands.

### Part 2: know the five questions you will be asked

Almost every restaurant chat in Nigeria is a version of one of these:

1. What is on the menu today, and how much?
2. Do you deliver to my area, and what is the delivery fee?
3. How long will it take?
4. Can I pay on delivery, or is it transfer only?
5. Is a particular dish available right now?

Write a clear answer to each, including delivery fees by area. These answers become your quick replies now and your AI agent's knowledge later.

### Part 3: take an order without confusion

A complete order has six parts: the items, the quantities, any special request, the delivery address with a landmark, a phone number the rider can call, and the payment method.

Read the order back before the kitchen starts. For transfers, wait for the alert in your own bank app before you cook. A screenshot is not proof of payment.

### Part 4: when the app stops being enough

The app has two real limits. Quick replies still need a person to pick and send them. And the away message is the same text for everyone, so the customer asking about small chops for 50 guests gets the same reply as the one asking for a plate of rice. During the lunch rush, the person holding the phone is also packing food, and messages wait.

### Part 5: add an AI agent to the same number

An AI agent such as Dailzero links to your existing WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business number by QR code or pairing code, the way WhatsApp Web does. You keep the app on your phone. The agent then replies to customers in seconds using the menu, prices, delivery fees and hours you gave it.

A customer sends:

> Abeg how much is jollof rice and turkey, and do you deliver to Gbagada?

The agent replies:

> Jollof rice with turkey is ₦4,500 per plate. Yes, we deliver to Gbagada for ₦1,500 and it takes about 45 minutes. How many plates would you like, and what address should the rider come to?

It collects the order details, sends your menu as a document or photos of dishes from a media library, understands voice notes, and remembers returning customers. It hands the chat to you when the customer asks for a person or when it cannot help.

What it does not do: it cannot verify a payment, and it cannot see your kitchen. If the ofada has finished, it will not know unless you tell it. It also has no connection to a POS system or delivery app unless a developer builds one.

### Part 6: bring customers back

Two WhatsApp habits work well for food:

- **Status.** Post the day's special every morning. Dailzero can post Status on a schedule and saves the contacts who message you so they can see it.
- **Broadcasts.** Tell past customers about the weekend menu. With an image it costs 8 credits per person, so 500 customers cost 4,000 credits.

### Frequently asked questions

#### Is WhatsApp Business free for restaurants?

Yes, the app is free to download and use. You pay only for extra tools you add on top, such as an AI agent.

#### Should I use the WhatsApp Business app or the API?

Most restaurants only need the app. The API suits large chains with high volume and a developer. The article on [WhatsApp Business API vs the WhatsApp Business app](/blog/whatsapp-business-api-vs-whatsapp-business-app-what-nigerian-smbs-need-to-know) explains the difference.

#### Can an AI agent accept payment for an order?

No. It shares the account details or payment link you give it, and you confirm the money yourself.
