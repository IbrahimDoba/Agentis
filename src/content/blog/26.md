The practical way for an estate agent in Lagos or Abuja to use AI for customer service is to put an AI agent on the office WhatsApp number, so tenants, landlords and buyers get an immediate, accurate answer to routine questions and a person only steps in for the cases that need judgment. The setup is the same in both cities. What you teach the AI is not.

This article is about service to people you are already dealing with, not chasing new leads. For that side, see [never missing a property lead on WhatsApp](/blog/how-to-never-miss-a-property-lead-on-whatsapp-again).

### What customer service means in an agency

Once a deal starts, the questions change. People stop asking "how much?" and start asking:

- Where do I pay, and has my payment been seen?
- When do I get my receipt, my tenancy agreement, my keys?
- Who do I call about the borehole, the gate, the generator?
- When is my rent due and what is the renewal amount?
- What documents do you need from me?

### In Lagos: volume and logistics

A Lagos agency managing flats in Yaba, Gbagada and Lekki deals with heavy message volume and a city where a single inspection can take half a day in traffic. Service questions lean towards logistics and the total package.

Teach the AI:

- **The full breakdown for every property:** rent, agency fee, legal fee, caution deposit, service charge, and what the caution deposit covers and when it is refunded.
- **Directions that work in Lagos:** the nearest bus stop or landmark, which gate to use, where to park.
- **Inspection rules:** days, meeting point, inspection fee if you charge one, and whether inspections are grouped to save trips.
- **Estate facts:** power arrangement, prepaid meters, flooding in rainy season, access during estate gate hours.

A typical tenant message and a good reply:

> Good morning, light has been off in Block C since last night. Who should I call?

> Good morning, sorry about that. For power issues in Block C, call the facility manager, Mr Tunde, on the number in your handover pack, and I have also passed this to our office so someone follows up this morning. Is anything else affected, like the pumping machine?

The AI answered from the escalation contacts the agency loaded, then handed the chat to a person.

### In Abuja: documents and distance

An Abuja agency working Wuse 2, Gwarinpa, Lokogoma and Lugbe sees fewer but longer conversations. Many clients are civil servants, organisations renting for staff, or owners who live outside the FCT and want updates on their property.

Teach the AI:

- **Title and documentation for each property,** in the terms Abuja clients use, and the list of papers a buyer or corporate tenant must provide.
- **What "serviced" includes** for each building: power hours, security, cleaning, water.
- **Your process for absentee landlords:** how rent is remitted, when statements go out, who approves repairs.

An owner abroad might write:

> Hello, has the tenant in my Gwarinpa flat renewed? I have not heard anything.

Here the AI should not guess. It has no access to your records, so the right behaviour is to acknowledge, collect the property details, and hand the chat to the account officer. You set that rule when you describe the business.

### What the AI handles and what it passes on

In both cities the split is similar.

**Handles alone:** payment instructions and account details, document checklists, office hours and directions, inspection booking and reminders, standard policies on caution deposits and renewals, sending tenancy forms and brochures from the media library.

**Hands to a person:** payment confirmation, complaints about repairs, disputes over caution deposit refunds, anything about a specific client's account, and anyone who asks for a human.

When you or a colleague reply, from the dashboard or straight from the phone, the AI pauses on that chat. Team members can have their own logins, so a Lagos office and an Abuja office can each handle their own handovers.

### Honest limits

Dailzero cannot verify a transfer, look up a tenant's balance, or see your property management records unless a developer connects them through webhook tools. For tenancy disputes and legal questions it should give general information and pass the matter to you.

### Frequently asked questions

#### Can one AI agent serve both my Lagos and Abuja clients?

Yes, if both branches use the same WhatsApp number. Separate numbers need separate agents.

#### Can it handle tenant complaints?

It can acknowledge the complaint, collect the flat number and details, share the right contact, and hand the chat to your team. It cannot dispatch a plumber.

#### Does it understand Pidgin or voice notes from tenants?

It copes with everyday Nigerian English and Pidgin phrasing, and it understands voice notes and photos, such as a picture of a leaking ceiling.
