For a Nigerian fintech, AI is better at speed, availability and consistency, and human agents are better at judgement, verification and anything involving lost money. The sensible setup is not one or the other: AI answers first on every chat, and people own every case where a wrong answer could cost a customer money or cost you your licence.

The useful question is which side should handle each kind of message.

### Task by task: who should handle it

| Customer message | Best handled by | Why |
|---|---|---|
| "What are your charges?" | AI | Same answer for everyone, asked all day |
| "How do I upgrade to tier 3?" | AI | A fixed list of steps and documents |
| "Do you open on Saturday?" | AI | A fact you set once |
| "I want a POS for my shop" | AI first, then sales | AI collects details, a person closes |
| "My airtime purchase failed but I was debited" | AI collects, human resolves | Needs a look at the transaction |
| "Why is my account restricted?" | Human | Needs identity checks and discretion |
| "Someone withdrew money from my account" | Human, immediately | Fraud, time-critical, regulated |
| "I want to complain about your agent" | Human | Needs empathy and authority |

### What AI does better

**It answers in seconds at 2am.** A customer whose bill payment hangs on a Sunday night gets a reply straight away instead of on Monday.

**It handles every chat at once.** Ten people or four hundred, nobody waits behind anybody.

**It says the same thing every time.** A new officer may quote last month's fee. The AI quotes the fee you entered, until you change it.

**It remembers the customer.** Dailzero remembers returning customers and earlier chats, so someone who asked about a business account last week is not treated as a stranger.

### What humans do better

**They can verify who they are talking to.** An AI on WhatsApp cannot confirm that the person typing owns the account. A trained officer can follow your verification steps.

**They can see and act on the account.** Dailzero has no access to customer accounts, balances or transaction status unless a developer connects your system through webhook tools. Your officers have the back office open.

**They can use discretion.** Waiving a fee for a loyal merchant, escalating a reversal, calming a customer who has been bounced around: none of that should be automated.

### Where AI goes wrong in fintech

Three mistakes do the damage.

1. **Letting it guess.** If the AI was never told your reversal timeline, it should say it will pass the question on, not offer a number. Give it complete information and tell it to hand over when unsure.
2. **Hiding the human.** Customers with money stuck want a person. Make the exit obvious. Dailzero hands the chat over when the customer asks or when the AI cannot help.
3. **Using it for advice.** Whether someone should take a loan or which savings plan suits them is not a job for a chat agent. It can state your published terms and nothing more.

### What the handover should look like

A customer writes:

> I bought PHCN token of 10k yesterday, I never see the token and una don debit me.

The AI should not pretend to investigate. A good reply:

> Sorry about this. Please send your meter number, the phone number on your account and a screenshot of the debit. I am passing this to a support officer who will check the transaction and get back to you here.

It gathers the details, then a person takes over. On Dailzero the officer can reply from the dashboard or straight from the phone. The AI pauses on that chat while they do, and they switch it back on when the case is closed.

### How to compare the cost fairly

Do not compare an AI subscription with one salary. Compare cost per resolved chat.

For the human side, divide your monthly support cost by chats closed. Use your own payroll figures.

For the AI side on Dailzero, a text reply is about 5 credits. A five-reply chat is 25 credits. On the Starter plan, ₦40,000 buys 60,000 credits, which is 2,400 such chats at about ₦17 each.

Then remember the AI only closes the general chats. Your people still handle the rest, so the saving is their time on the repeat questions, not their whole job.

### Frequently asked questions

#### Will customers know they are chatting with AI?

Tell them. A short line such as "I am the automated assistant, a support officer is available if you need one" builds more trust in a money business than pretending.

#### Is AI customer service safe for a fintech?

It is safe for general information when you instruct it never to ask for PINs, OTPs or passwords and to hand fraud and account cases to people. It is not a substitute for your verification process.
