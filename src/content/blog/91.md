A business qualifies leads automatically on WhatsApp by having an AI agent ask every new enquirer the same few questions the owner would ask: what they need, where they are, what they want to spend and how soon. The agent then flags the serious ones, so you call the people ready to buy instead of chatting with everybody.

### What qualifying actually means

Qualifying is sorting. Of everyone who sends "how much?", some will pay this week and some are only comparing prices. A qualified lead is one where you know enough to decide whether to pick up the phone.

For most Nigerian businesses that comes down to four things:

- **Need.** What exactly do they want?
- **Location.** Can you serve them, and what will delivery or a site visit involve?
- **Budget.** Does it match what you sell?
- **Timing.** This week, month end, or "just asking"?

### The questions change with the business

The four things stay the same, but the wording does not. Here is what different businesses might have an agent ask.

| Business | Questions that separate serious from casual |
|---|---|
| Solar and inverter installer | What do you want to power? Home or office? Which area? |
| Event caterer | What date? How many guests? Which city, and is the venue booked? |
| Car dealer | Which model and year? Outright or instalments? When can you come for inspection? |
| Private school | Which class is the child entering? Day or boarding? Which term? |
| Printing press | What item and quantity? Is the design ready? When do you need it? |

### What it looks like in a real chat

Say a solar installer in Abuja runs an Instagram advert. At 9:40pm someone taps it and sends:

> Good evening, how much is your 5kva inverter?

A weak reply is a price and nothing else. A qualifying reply answers the question and moves the chat forward:

> Good evening. I have just sent our price list, the 5kVA systems are on page 2. So I can recommend the right size, what do you want to power, and is it for a home or an office?

Three messages later the agent knows the customer has a four-bedroom house in Gwarinpa, wants to run two air conditioners and a freezer, and wants installation this month. That is someone worth calling at 8am. The person who replied "ok, just checking" is not.

### Four rules for good qualifying questions

- **Ask no more than four.** Each extra question loses people.
- **Give before you ask.** Answer the price question first. A customer who has to beg for a price usually goes to your competitor.
- **Ask about budget gently.** "Are you looking at the standard or the premium option?" works better than "What is your budget?"
- **Define a hot lead.** For example: inside your service area and buying within 30 days. Tell the agent to hand those chats to you immediately.

### What automatic qualifying will not do

It will not prove that a customer can pay, confirm a bank transfer, or close the negotiation on a ₦3 million job. Dailzero does not process or verify payments, and it only knows the prices you typed in. Closing is still yours.
