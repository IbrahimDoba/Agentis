A recruitment agency can use a WhatsApp AI agent to run the first screen: tell each applicant what the role is, ask your knockout questions, collect the answers, and book suitable candidates for an interview. It should not decide who gets hired, and it will not read and score CVs for you. A recruiter still makes every shortlist decision.

That first screen is where the hours go. Post one vacancy with a WhatsApp number on it and you may get hundreds of messages in a week, many asking the same questions.

### The first-screen script, question by question

Give the agent the role details and the questions in the order you would ask them on a phone screen. For a hypothetical customer service officer role in Ikeja, the flow could be:

1. **Confirm the role and location.** "This is a full-time, on-site role in Ikeja, Monday to Friday. Is that location workable for you?"
2. **Qualification.** "What is your highest qualification, and have you completed NYSC?"
3. **Experience.** "How many years have you worked in customer service, and where most recently?"
4. **Availability.** "If selected, how soon can you start?"
5. **Salary expectation.** "The monthly salary range for this role is the figure the client approved. Does that fit what you are looking for?"
6. **Next step.** Suitable candidates get interview slots to choose from. Others get a polite, clear message.

The agent asks one question at a time and copes with everyday Nigerian English and Pidgin phrasing, so a reply like "I fit resume next week" is understood.

### What the candidate sees

Applicants mostly open with a short message:

> Good day, I saw the vacancy for customer service officer. Is it still available and what is the salary?

A good reply answers both parts, then starts the screen:

> Good day. Yes, the role is still open. It is on-site in Ikeja, and the salary range is in the job details I can send you. May I ask a few quick questions to check the fit? First, is Ikeja a workable location for you every weekday?

It answers the salary question instead of dodging it. Candidates who learn the pay only at interview waste your time and theirs.

### Booking interviews and cutting no-shows

Once a candidate passes your knockout questions, the agent offers the interview slots you gave it and sends a reminder before the day. Have it include the address, a landmark, what to bring and who to ask for at the gate. See the [appointment booking page](/solutions/appointment-booking) for how slots and reminders work.

### How CVs fit in

Candidates will send CVs as PDF or Word files, and those arrive in your WhatsApp as normal for your recruiters to open. Do not design a process that depends on the AI reading and ranking those files. Have it ask for the key facts as typed answers, which you can read in the chat history from the dashboard.

The agent does understand voice notes, which helps candidates who prefer to speak their answers.

### Rules to set before you go live

- **No hiring decisions by the AI.** It checks stated requirements such as location, qualification and start date. Everything else goes to a recruiter.
- **Say what you do and do not charge.** Fake job offers that demand "registration fees" are common. If you never charge candidates, have the agent say so plainly in every first reply.
- **Do not collect sensitive data in chat.** No BVN, NIN or bank details at screening stage. Collect those through your proper onboarding process later.
- **Hand over anything unusual.** A candidate asking about a rejected application, a complaint about a client employer, or a request for a reference goes to a person.

### Reaching your talent pool again

People who have messaged your number are saved as contacts. When a similar role opens, you can broadcast it to them at 5 credits per person for text, so 500 past applicants costs 2,500 credits. Broadcasts go only to people who have contacted you before, not to bought lists.

### What it does not connect to

There is no built-in link to an applicant tracking system, job boards, Google Sheets or email. A developer can send screening answers to your own database through webhook tools in the [Dailzero API](/developers). Without that, your recruiters work from the dashboard, where each can have a login.

### Frequently asked questions

#### Can the AI reject candidates automatically?

It can tell a candidate they do not meet a stated requirement, such as location or a required qualification. Keep any judgement call with a recruiter, and word rejections kindly.

#### Can one agent handle several vacancies at once?

Yes, if you describe each role clearly. The agent asks which vacancy the applicant is writing about and follows that role's questions.

#### Will it call candidates?

No. Dailzero does not make voice calls. It works on WhatsApp chat and a website chat widget.
