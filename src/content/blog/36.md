WhatsApp automation for hotel customer service means an AI agent answers routine guest questions on your hotel's WhatsApp number at every stage of a stay, and passes anything involving a room problem, a refund or an unhappy guest to a member of staff. Done well, it takes the repeated questions off the front desk without making guests feel fobbed off.

The simplest way to plan it is to follow the guest: before arrival, during the stay, after checkout.

### Before arrival: directions, check-in and requests

Once a guest has booked, the questions change from "how much" to "how do I get there".

- "What time is check-in? I land at 9am."
- "Send me the location, the driver cannot find it."
- "Do you do airport pickup from Abuja airport and how much?"
- "Can I pay the balance on arrival?"

The agent answers each from the policies you entered and sends your address with landmarks. For a request it cannot grant by itself, like a 9am early check-in, it states the policy and hands the chat to reception to approve.

### During the stay: the in-house help line

This is where most hotels do not think to use WhatsApp, and where it saves the most calls to reception. Print the hotel WhatsApp number on the key card holder and the room information sheet, and guests will use it.

> Pls what is the WiFi password and what time does breakfast end?

> The Wi-Fi network is HotelGuest and the password is on your key card holder. Breakfast is served from 6:30am to 10:30am at the ground floor restaurant. Would you like the room service menu as well?

The agent can also send the restaurant or room service menu as a document, share laundry prices and pool hours, and explain how to extend a stay.

Service requests need a clear rule. When a guest writes "the AC in 214 is not cooling" or "we need extra towels", the agent should collect the room number, acknowledge the request, and hand the chat to staff immediately. It cannot dispatch housekeeping or maintenance by itself. Someone on duty must be watching the dashboard or the hotel phone, or the fast reply becomes an empty promise.

### After checkout: receipts, lost items and return visits

- **Receipts and invoices.** A guest whose company needs a receipt asks days later. The agent collects the name and dates and hands the request to accounts.
- **Lost property.** "I left my charger in room 108." The agent takes the details and hands over. It cannot check the lost property box.
- **Return visits.** The agent remembers returning guests and earlier chats, so a regular who writes "same room as last time, next Tuesday" does not have to start from zero.

### What to automate and what to keep human

| Automate | Keep with staff |
|---|---|
| Rates, facilities, opening hours | Complaints and compensation |
| Directions and check-in times | Refunds and payment confirmation |
| Wi-Fi, breakfast, laundry and pool information | Maintenance and housekeeping action |
| Menus, price lists and photos | Security or medical emergencies |
| Collecting details for requests | Rate negotiation and corporate accounts |

For emergencies, do not rely on chat at all. Tell the agent to give the duty manager's phone line straight away. Dailzero does not make or receive voice calls.

### Five rules that keep service standards up

1. **Write policies the way reception says them.** If late checkout is ₦10,000 until 3pm, type exactly that.
2. **Set the tone.** Tell the agent to stay formal, even when a guest writes in Pidgin.
3. **Name a person per shift** who owns handed-over chats. Team members can have their own logins.
4. **Update what changes.** Pool closed for maintenance, new rates in December. The agent only knows what you tell it.
5. **Read the handed-over chats weekly.** Every question the agent could not answer is a line missing from your setup.

### Limits to know before you start

The agent has no connection to hotel management or booking systems out of the box, so it cannot see who is in which room, a guest's bill or room availability. A developer can connect those through webhook tools. It also covers WhatsApp and a website chat widget only, not Instagram DMs, email or SMS. And it does not process payments: it shares your account details or payment link and your staff confirm.

### Frequently asked questions

#### Can it serve both booking inquiries and in-house guests on one number?

Yes. One agent on one number handles both. If you want a separate in-house line, that needs a second number and a second agent, which the Pro plan includes.

#### What happens to chats when the hotel phone is off?

The hotel phone is the main device and should come online regularly, the same as with WhatsApp Web. Keep it charged and on Wi-Fi at reception.

#### How do we handle guests who do not speak English?

The agent replies in English by default. Multi-language replies are a Pro feature.
