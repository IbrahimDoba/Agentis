Your WhatsApp Status is shown to people in your phone's contacts, and in practice a customer only sees it when both sides have saved each other's number. So the way to get more Status views is not better pictures first. It is saving every customer who messages you and getting them to save you. After that, post consistently at the times your customers are online.

### Why your views are low

Most business owners post to a small audience without knowing it. Three things cut the number:

- **You never saved the customer.** If someone messaged you, bought and left, and you did not save the number, your Status does not go to them.
- **The customer never saved you.** Their phone shows your number, not your business name, and your Status does not appear in their Updates tab.
- **Your privacy setting is narrow.** If Status privacy is set to "Only share with" a short list, nobody else sees it.

### Step 1: check your Status privacy

Open WhatsApp, go to Settings, then Privacy, then Status. Choose **My contacts**. This applies to Status you post from now on, not to posts already up.

### Step 2: save every customer who messages you

This is the step that grows your audience. Every new chat is a person who has shown interest. Save the number with a name you can recognise, such as "Ada Lekki wig customer".

Doing this by hand is where most businesses fall behind. On a busy day, twenty new numbers arrive and none are saved.

### Step 3: get customers to save you

Ask at the right moment, in the chat, right after you have been helpful:

> Please save our number as "Bella Shoes" so you can see new arrivals and discounts on our Status.

Give a reason. "So you see new stock first" works better than "save my number". You can also send your contact card, which lets them save it in one tap.

### Step 4: post on a rhythm

- **Post two to four times a day**, not twenty. Too many and people mute you.
- **Post when customers are on their phones**: early morning, lunch, and from about 7pm to 10pm.
- **Show the product, the price and how to order** in each post. A photo with no price gets views but no messages.
- **Mix it up**: new stock, a customer's delivery, a short video, a limited offer.
- **End with an action**: "Reply to this Status to order."

### Step 5: watch replies, not only views

A Status with 80 views and 6 replies is worth more than one with 300 views and none. Reply quickly to every Status reply. Those are warm leads.

### Mistakes that reduce views

- **Adding strangers to your contacts** from groups or bought lists. They have not saved you, so they will not see your Status, and messaging them risks your number. See [how to stop WhatsApp banning your business number](/blog/how-to-stop-whatsapp-banning-your-business-number).
- **Posting only adverts.** People mute accounts that sell in every post.
- **Posting at 2pm on a workday** and nothing in the evening.
- **Changing your number** without telling customers to save the new one.

### Frequently asked questions

#### Why can some customers not see my Status?

Usually because one side has not saved the other's number, or because your Status privacy excludes them. Check that you saved them, ask them to save you, and set privacy to "My contacts".

#### Can people who have not saved my number see my Status?

In practice, no. Status is shared with your contacts, and it shows up for people who have you saved too.

#### How many people can see one Status?

It goes to the contacts allowed by your privacy setting. With Dailzero, the reach per post is up to 2,000, 5,000 or 10,000 contacts depending on your plan.

#### Does posting Status more often get more views?

Up to a point. Two to four posts a day keeps you visible. Far more than that and customers mute you, which removes you from their feed completely.
