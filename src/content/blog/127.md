Dailzero credits are the units your AI agent spends when it does work on WhatsApp. A typical AI text reply uses about 5 credits, an image or document 8 and a video 12. So 1,000 credits is about 200 text replies, and the Basic plan's 25,000 credits is about 5,000. When credits finish the agent pauses, and nothing extra is charged.

### What each action costs

| Action | Credits |
|---|---|
| AI text reply | About 5 |
| Image sent to a customer | 8 |
| Document sent to a customer | 8 |
| Video sent to a customer | 12 |
| Text broadcast | 5 per person |
| Image broadcast | 8 per person |
| Video broadcast | 12 per person |
| Status post, text | 5 for the whole post |
| Status post, image | 8 for the whole post |
| Status post, video | 12 for the whole post |
| Saving a contact | 1 per contact |

"About 5" is an average for a normal text reply. Longer replies and voice notes use a little more.

Two rows are worth a second look. A broadcast is charged per person, because each person gets their own message. A Status post is charged once, with no per-recipient charge, whether 50 or 5,000 contacts can see it.

### Worked example 1: a small shop

A shop gets 15 chats a day. Each chat needs about 6 AI replies.

- 15 chats × 6 replies = 90 replies a day
- 90 replies × 5 credits = 450 credits a day
- 450 credits × 30 days = 13,500 credits a month

That fits Basic with 11,500 credits to spare.

### Worked example 2: a busy vendor who posts Status

A fashion vendor gets 40 chats a day at about 6 AI replies each. She also posts one image Status a day and saves 400 new contacts in the month.

- Replies: 40 × 6 = 240 a day. 240 × 5 = 1,200 credits a day. 1,200 × 30 = 36,000 credits.
- Status: 1 image post × 8 credits × 30 days = 240 credits.
- Contacts: 400 × 1 credit = 400 credits.
- Total: 36,000 + 240 + 400 = 36,640 credits a month.

That is above Basic and inside Starter, with 23,360 credits left.

### Worked example 3: a monthly broadcast

A restaurant wants to send a new menu photo to 1,000 customers.

- Image broadcast: 1,000 people × 8 credits = 8,000 credits
- The same message as plain text: 1,000 × 5 = 5,000 credits

Compare that with putting the same photo on Status for 8 credits. Status is far cheaper, but only contacts who have saved your number and whom you have saved will see it. A broadcast lands in the chat. Many businesses use Status for daily updates and keep broadcasts for the few messages that must be seen. ### What uses credits faster than you expect

- **Photos and videos in replies.** A reply with three product photos costs 24 credits, against 5 for text.
- **Broadcasts.** They are charged per person, so the cost grows with your list.
- **Long chats.** Price negotiation or collecting a delivery address can take 10 or more replies.

### What does not multiply your cost

- **Status reach.** A post costs the same whether it reaches 100 or 2,000 contacts.
- **Checking a number.** Developers using the API can check whether a number is on WhatsApp for free.

### The order credits are spent in

1. Your monthly plan credits are used first.
2. Then any pay as you go balance.
3. When both are empty, the agent pauses until the plan renews or you top up.

There are no overage fees at any point.

### Do credits expire?

Monthly plan credits belong to that month. Unused credits carry into the next cycle up to 25% of your plan's allowance, for one cycle. On Basic that is up to 6,250 credits. Pay as you go credits stay valid for 12 months.

### How to make credits go further

1. Put your prices and delivery fees in the agent's knowledge, so it answers in one reply and not three.
2. Send one clear photo and offer more, instead of five at once.
3. Use Status for daily stock updates and broadcasts for big announcements.
4. Take over chats that need a person. The AI pauses on that chat when you reply.

### Frequently asked questions

#### How many messages is 1,000 Dailzero credits?

About 200 AI text replies, at about 5 credits each. If some replies include images at 8 credits, the number is lower.

#### How much does a WhatsApp Status post cost on Dailzero?

5 credits for a text Status, 8 for an image and 12 for a video. There is no per-recipient charge.

#### How much does a broadcast cost?

5 credits per person for text, 8 for an image and 12 for a video. A text broadcast to 500 customers costs 2,500 credits.

#### What does saving contacts cost?

1 credit per contact saved. Free accounts can save up to 200 contacts in total.

#### What happens when my credits run out?

The agent pauses. Your customers' messages still arrive in WhatsApp and you can reply yourself. Top up or wait for the plan to renew. See the [pricing page](/pricing).
