Yes. Dailzero works with both WhatsApp Business and normal WhatsApp. On its default route you link the number you already use, in either app, by scanning a QR code or entering a pairing code, the same way you link WhatsApp Web. You do not need to switch apps, change your number or register with Meta.

### Why both apps work

WhatsApp and WhatsApp Business both have a feature called Linked devices. It is what lets you use your account on a computer through WhatsApp Web. Dailzero connects as a linked device, so it works with whichever of the two apps your number is in.

Dailzero does not ask you to move from one app to the other. If you sell from normal WhatsApp today, you can stay on it.

### Normal WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business: does it matter?

For Dailzero itself, no. The agent replies the same way on both. The difference is in what the app gives your customers.

| | Normal WhatsApp | WhatsApp Business app |
|---|---|---|
| Works with Dailzero | Yes | Yes |
| How you link | QR code or pairing code | QR code or pairing code |
| Business profile (address, hours, category) | No | Yes |
| Product catalogue in the app | No | Yes |
| Labels for chats | No | Yes |
| The app's own greeting and away messages | No | Yes |
| Cost of the app | Free | Free |

If you run a business, WhatsApp Business is the better home for your number, because customers can see your hours, address and catalogue. That is advice about WhatsApp, not a Dailzero requirement.

One practical point: if you use the WhatsApp Business app's own greeting or away messages, switch them off once the agent is live. Otherwise a customer can get the app's automatic message and the agent's reply together.

### What stays the same on your phone

Linking Dailzero does not change how your phone works.

- **Your number stays yours.** It is not ported, transferred or registered anywhere else.
- **Your chats stay in your app.** You can read every conversation, including the ones the agent handled.
- **You can still reply yourself.** When you answer a customer from your phone or the dashboard, the AI pauses on that chat so you do not both reply.
- **Your groups, contacts and calls** carry on as before.
- **You can still post Status by hand**, as well as scheduling Status through Dailzero.
- **You can unlink at any time** under Linked devices in WhatsApp.

### What changes

- Customers who message you get a reply in seconds, at any hour.
- Dailzero appears in your Linked devices list.
- Orders, leads and appointments appear in your Dailzero dashboard.
- If you switch on contact saving, the people who message you are saved to your contacts so they can see your Status. This costs 1 credit per contact saved.

### How to link your number

1. Sign up at Dailzero and set up your agent with your products, prices and opening hours.
2. In the dashboard, open the WhatsApp connection step and choose QR code or pairing code.
3. On your phone, open WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business. Go to Settings, then Linked devices, then Link a device.
4. Scan the QR code. For a pairing code, tap "Link with phone number instead" and type the code shown in the dashboard.
5. When the dashboard shows the number as connected, message it from another phone to test.

The [how it works page](/how-it-works) shows the whole setup.

### Things to know before you link

**Your phone remains the main device.** It should come online regularly. If the phone stays off or without data for a long time, WhatsApp signs linked devices out and you will need to link again.

**New numbers need time.** If you registered the number recently, use it normally for about two weeks first. Chat, reply and post Status before you connect any automation.

**Use the real apps.** Dailzero is for WhatsApp and WhatsApp Business from the official app stores. Modified apps such as GB WhatsApp break WhatsApp's terms and put the number at risk.

**This route is not the official API.** Dailzero protects the number with queued, human-paced sending, a warm-up period for new numbers and automatic slow-down when WhatsApp signals a limit. No tool can promise a number will never be restricted. See [is it safe to connect an AI to your WhatsApp number](/blog/is-it-safe-to-connect-an-ai-to-your-whatsapp-number).

### What about the WhatsApp Business API?

The WhatsApp Business app and the WhatsApp Business Platform (the API) are different things, though the names are close. The app is what you install on your phone. The API is Meta's platform for larger businesses, with business verification, approved templates and Meta's own message fees.

Dailzero supports the API as a second route, set up with the Dailzero team on request. On that route the number runs on Meta's platform, and features such as Status posting and contact saving are not available. Most small businesses do not need it. The two are compared in [how Dailzero connects to WhatsApp](/blog/how-dailzero-connects-to-whatsapp).

### Frequently asked questions

#### Do I need WhatsApp Business to use Dailzero?

No. On the linked-device route Dailzero works with normal WhatsApp and with WhatsApp Business. You link whichever app your number is already in.

#### Can I keep using WhatsApp on my phone after linking?

Yes. Your phone works as before. You can read chats, reply, post Status and make calls.

#### Will my customers know anything changed?

They message the same number and see the same name and photo. They simply get faster replies. Dailzero's terms ask you to tell customers they are talking to an AI agent.

#### Can I link the same number to WhatsApp Web as well?

Yes. WhatsApp allows several linked devices on one account, so WhatsApp Web or Desktop can stay linked alongside Dailzero.

#### Can I run two numbers?

Each agent runs one number. Basic and Starter include one agent and Pro includes two. Plans are on the [pricing page](/pricing).
