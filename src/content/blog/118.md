Dailzero and Evolve both give a Nigerian business an AI assistant on WhatsApp, but they are built differently. Evolve is a done-for-you service on Meta's official WhatsApp Cloud API, with a CRM and team inbox, set up by its team over one to three weeks. Dailzero is self-serve: you link the number you already use, the AI agent is live the same day, and there are no Meta fees on the default route. Choose Evolve if you want the official API with a CRM built for you. Choose Dailzero if you want to keep your number as it is and start quickly for less.

### Where Evolve is the better choice

- **You need the official API from day one.** Evolve says it is built on the Cloud API as a registered Meta Tech Provider, and you authorise through Meta's own flow. For a business whose compliance team asks for this, it is the direct route.
- **You want someone else to build it.** Evolve sets up the flows and templates and trains the assistant on your material, and you review it before it goes live. Dailzero expects you to describe your business and add your products yourself, though it takes only a few steps.
- **You want a full sales pipeline.** Its top plan includes a CRM, pipeline, automated follow-up sequences and team training.
- **You send approved templates.** Template setup and approval is included in its plans, which matters for reminders and notices to people who have not written recently.
- **You want monthly retraining and onboarding handled.** Evolve lists both as part of its service.

### Where Dailzero is the better choice

- **You want to keep your number and your chats.** Dailzero links to the number in your own app, like WhatsApp Web. Nothing is removed and no history is lost.
- **You want to be live today.** Sign up, describe the business, add products and prices, scan a QR code. There is no verification to wait for.
- **Your budget is smaller.** Dailzero's AI agent starts at ₦20,000 a month, or ₦1,000 pay as you go, and there are no Meta message fees on the linked-device route. When credits finish the agent pauses. There are no overage fees.
- **You sell with WhatsApp Status.** Dailzero schedules Status posts, runs Autopilot from a gallery, saves the contacts who message you and answers people who reply to a Status. These are not available on the official platform.
- **You want to test first.** The 7-day free trial includes 1,000 credits, about 200 AI replies.
- **You may want both routes.** Dailzero's app is approved by Meta for WhatsApp Business messaging, and the team sets up the official route on request.

### What you give up with Dailzero

On the default linked-device route you do not get approved message templates or a verified badge, and your phone should come online regularly. WhatsApp does not officially support automating an account through a linked device, so it carries more risk than the official API. Dailzero queues every message, sends at a human pace, warms up new numbers and slows down when WhatsApp signals a limit, but no tool can promise a number will never be restricted.

You also do more of the setup yourself, and Dailzero's dashboard is lighter than a full CRM with a pipeline. If you choose Dailzero's official route instead, Meta's fees apply and Status and contact saving are not available. Read [official WhatsApp API vs linked device](/blog/official-whatsapp-api-vs-linked-device-which-should-you-use) for the trade-off.

### A quick way to decide

1. Does your business require the official API and a CRM, and can you wait a few weeks for setup? Choose Evolve.
2. Is your WhatsApp number full of customer chats you cannot lose? Choose Dailzero.
3. Do you want an AI agent live this week at the lowest cost, with Status tools included? Choose Dailzero.
4. Not sure? Run the Dailzero trial on a second number while you talk to Evolve.

### Frequently asked questions

#### Is Dailzero cheaper than Evolve?

On the listed prices, yes. As of November 2026 Evolve's AI assistant plan is ₦75,000 a month plus Meta's fees, and Dailzero's plans with the AI agent start at ₦20,000 a month. Evolve's price includes setup work by its team.

#### Does Evolve use the official WhatsApp API?

Yes. Its website says it is built on the WhatsApp Business Platform (Cloud API) as a registered Meta Tech Provider.

#### Can I keep my chat history with either tool?

With Dailzero's linked-device route, yes, because the number stays in your app. Evolve's site says a number active on the WhatsApp app must be removed from it first, which deletes the chat history.

#### Does Dailzero offer the official API too?

Yes, on request. Dailzero's app is approved by Meta, and the team sets up the official WhatsApp Business Platform for businesses that need it. Meta's message fees apply on that route.

#### Which one has a free trial?

Dailzero has a 7-day free trial with 1,000 credits. Evolve's site does not list a free trial for its WhatsApp plans, so ask them directly.
