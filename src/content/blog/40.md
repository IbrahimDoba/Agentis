For a hotel or resort, WhatsApp lead generation means getting people who are planning an event, a group trip, a corporate stay or a holiday to message your number, capturing what they need in the chat, and following up until they book. The leads worth chasing are rarely single room nights. They are the wedding party, the company retreat and the family booking four rooms for December.

This guide covers which leads matter, where they come from, what to capture, and the mistakes that waste them.

### Which inquiries count as leads

A one-night walk-in rate request is a sale you close in five minutes or lose. A lead is bigger and slower. For hotels and resorts, these are the main kinds:

- **Events.** Wedding receptions, birthdays, conferences and training sessions that need a hall, catering and sometimes rooms.
- **Groups.** A church retreat, a wedding party needing 15 rooms, a school excursion.
- **Corporate accounts.** A company that sends staff to Lagos or Port Harcourt every month and wants a fixed rate and invoicing.
- **Long stays.** Expatriates, contractors and returnees staying weeks, not nights.
- **Leisure packages.** Couples and families planning a beach weekend, a honeymoon or a festive season getaway.

Each of these takes several conversations and often a site visit.

### Where the leads come from

#### Click-to-WhatsApp ads

Run Facebook and Instagram ads that open a chat with your number. A resort might run one ad for weddings, one for corporate retreats and one for a weekend couples package. Dailzero's agent knows which ad each person came from, so you can compare ads by real inquiries.

#### Your own pages and premises

Put your WhatsApp link in your Instagram bio and on your Google listing, and add the website chat widget, which runs the same agent. Place a WhatsApp QR code at reception and on the event hall door. A guest attending someone else's wedding at your venue is a strong prospect for their own.

#### WhatsApp Status

Everyone who messages you is saved as a contact and can see your Status. Schedule posts of the decorated hall after each event.

### What to capture in the chat

The agent should answer first and then collect, one question at a time.

> Hello, I want to make enquiry about your hall for a wedding reception in March. About 300 guests.

> Congratulations on your wedding. Our main hall seats up to 350 guests banquet style. Hall hire starts from ₦1.8 million and includes air conditioning, standby generator, chairs and tables. I can send photos and the full event package now. What date in March are you considering?

The figures above are examples; the agent quotes what you enter. After that reply it sends the hall photos and event brochure from your media library, then collects:

- Event type and date, or travel dates
- Number of guests, and number of rooms if needed
- Whether they need catering, decoration or accommodation for guests
- Budget range
- Name, and for corporate inquiries the company name
- When they can come for a site visit

Then it books the site visit and sends a reminder before the day. People showing buying intent appear in the leads list in the dashboard on every plan, so your sales or events manager starts each morning with a list instead of a scroll.

### Following up without being a nuisance

Event and group leads decide slowly. A couple may view four venues over three weeks.

- On the Pro plan, the agent sends follow-ups automatically to people who went quiet. On other plans, your events manager works through the leads list.
- When a prospect asks for a discount or a custom package, the agent hands the chat to a person.
- Stop after a clear no or two unanswered follow-ups.

### Five mistakes that waste hotel leads

1. **Hiding the price.** "Come for a meeting to discuss" sends planners to the venue that gave a starting figure.
2. **One ad for everything.** A wedding planner and a procurement officer need different messages.
3. **Nobody owns the handoff.** Give your events manager a login and make handed-over chats their first task each day.
4. **Confirming dates the agent cannot check.** It does not see your event calendar unless a developer connects it. Have it say the events team will confirm the date, or type in the dates already taken.
5. **Buying phone number lists.** Dailzero does not message people who have never contacted you. Grow your own list.

It can send documents you have uploaded, such as a standard event package or rate card. A custom quotation should be prepared by your team and sent after handoff.

#### Does it collect deposits for events?

It shares the account details or payment link you provide. It does not process or verify payments, so your accounts staff confirm the deposit.
