A Nigerian real estate agent closes more deals with WhatsApp AI by making sure every serious inquiry gets a full answer within seconds, an inspection gets booked while the client is still interested, and nobody is forgotten afterwards. The AI does not negotiate or collect money. It keeps prospects moving until they reach you ready to talk terms.

The clearest way to see it is to follow one ordinary day.

### 7.15am: the overnight inquiries are already answered

You posted a two-bedroom flat in Ikate last night. By morning, eleven people have messaged. Without help, you would wake to eleven versions of the same question and answer them between calls, losing a few to agents who replied faster.

With an AI agent on your number, each one got a reply at the time they asked.

> Is the 2 bed in Ikate still available? What's the total package?

A good reply says yes if you have marked it available, then breaks down the total package the way Nigerian tenants expect: annual rent, agency fee, legal fee, caution deposit and service charge, with the sum. It sends the photos and the walkthrough video from your media library, and asks when the person would like to inspect.

### 9.30am: inspections are in your diary

The agent books viewings into the slots you allow and collects a name and phone number. If you charge an inspection fee, it states the amount and shares the account details you gave it. It cannot confirm that a transfer has landed, so it passes the chat to you to check your bank alert before the slot is confirmed.

### 1pm: you are in traffic on the Lekki-Epe Expressway

A buyer messages about the terrace duplex you listed in Sangotedo.

> Good afternoon. What title does the property have? And is the price negotiable?

The agent answers the title question from what you entered, for example that the estate has a Governor's Consent or a C of O, and offers to send the brochure and payment plan as a document. On price, it should not bargain. A good reply says the listed price, and that the agent in charge will discuss terms directly, then hands the chat to you.

Negotiation is where your skill earns the commission. The AI flags the chat, you reply from your phone when you park, and it stays quiet on that chat until you switch it back on.

### 4pm: the leads list tells you who to call

Not everyone who messages is equal. Someone who asked for the payment plan, the title and an inspection date is a buyer. Someone who sent "how much" and disappeared is not, yet.

The dashboard lists people showing buying intent as leads, on every plan.

### Friday evening: reminders go out

People who booked Saturday inspections get a reminder. No-shows waste an agent's fuel and a whole morning, especially when the property is in Ibeju-Lekki and you are coming from Yaba. A reminder the evening before, with the meeting point, cuts down on wasted trips.

### The week after: quiet prospects hear from you

A client inspected, said "I'll get back to you", and went silent. On the Pro plan, automated follow-ups message people who went quiet, so you are not relying on memory. When they return a month later asking about a different flat, the agent remembers the earlier conversation and what they were looking for.

### What you must keep doing yourself

- **Keep availability current.** The AI cannot see which unit was taken yesterday. If you do not mark a listing as gone, it will keep offering it.
- **Verify every payment.** Inspection fees, commitment fees and deposits must be confirmed against your own bank alert. The AI does not process or verify payments.
- **Handle documents and due diligence.** It can say what title a property has from your notes. It is not a lawyer and should not advise on a transaction.
- **Negotiate and close.** That is your job, and clients expect a person for it.

### Frequently asked questions

#### Can the AI negotiate rent or sale price with a client?

It should not, and you should instruct it not to. It states the listed price and passes negotiation to you.

#### Can it send property photos and videos?

Yes. It sends photos, videos and documents such as brochures from the media library you upload. An image uses 8 credits and a video 12.

#### What if a property is taken but people keep asking?

Update or remove the listing in the dashboard. The agent can then tell inquirers it is gone and suggest similar properties you still have.
