A clinic or hospital can use WhatsApp AI to answer the non-clinical questions that fill its front desk phone: opening hours, which services and specialists are available, published fees, directions, and booking appointments with reminders. It must not diagnose, recommend drugs or interpret results. Anything clinical goes to your staff.

### What the AI should handle

These questions have one correct answer that your front desk already repeats all day:

- Opening hours, including weekends and public holidays.
- Which services you offer: antenatal, dental, eye clinic, lab tests, scans, immunisation.
- Which days each consultant is in.
- Published fees, such as registration, consultation and common tests.
- Which HMOs you accept, from a list you provide.
- Directions and landmarks, and where to park.
- What to bring: hospital card, HMO ID, previous results.
- Booking, rescheduling and reminding patients of appointments.

### What the AI must never do

- Suggest what an illness might be.
- Recommend, adjust or comment on medication or dosage.
- Interpret a lab result, scan or photo.
- Decide whether something is urgent enough to come in. If in doubt, the answer is always "please call or come in".
- Discuss a patient's record or results.

On Dailzero you write these rules into the agent's instructions in plain English, and it hands the chat to a person when a question crosses the line. Dailzero is not a regulated-advice tool.

### Two chats, two different responses

An administrative question gets a full answer. The fees below are invented for the example; the agent only quotes what you publish.

> Good morning, how much is consultation and is the dentist around on Saturday?

> Good morning. General consultation is ₦10,000 and registration for new patients is ₦5,000. Our dentist is available on Saturdays from 9am to 2pm. Would you like me to book a slot for you?

A clinical question gets a handover, not an answer.

> My baby has had fever since yesterday night, what drug can I give her?

> I am sorry your baby is unwell. I cannot give medical advice on WhatsApp, so I am passing this chat to our nurse now. If she is having difficulty breathing, a convulsion or will not wake properly, please come to the clinic immediately or call our emergency line.

The nurse replies from the dashboard or the clinic phone, and the AI pauses on that chat. Give the agent your real emergency number to share whenever a message sounds urgent.

### Appointments and reminders

The agent can book a slot, collect the details you need, such as the patient's name and whether it is a first visit, and send a reminder before the appointment.

One limit to know: the agent cannot see your hospital management system or your doctor's diary on its own. It books against the hours and rules you gave it. A small clinic can work that way and have the front desk confirm. A hospital that wants live slots from its own system needs a developer to connect it through webhook tools. The [appointment booking page](/solutions/appointment-booking) explains the flow.

### Patient privacy

WhatsApp chats about health are sensitive, and health information is treated as sensitive personal data under Nigerian data protection law. Sensible practice looks like this:

- **Collect only what booking needs.** Name, phone number, preferred time, type of visit. Not a medical history.
- **Keep results off the AI.** Tell the agent never to send or discuss results. A staff member should do that through your normal process.
- **Expect photos and voice notes.** Patients will send a picture of a rash or a lab slip. The agent can understand photos and voice notes, so instruct it clearly not to interpret them and to hand the chat to staff.
- **Limit who sees chats.** Give each staff member their own login instead of sharing one password.
- **Tell patients.** Say in your greeting that an automated assistant handles enquiries and that staff handle medical questions.

This is practical guidance, not legal advice. Ask your own adviser how the rules apply to you.

### Setting it up in a clinic

1. Write the approved answer to the 20 questions your front desk hears most.
2. Upload documents patients ask for, such as a price list or antenatal package brochure.
3. Write the clinical boundary rules and emergency instructions.
4. Link the clinic's WhatsApp number by QR or pairing code.
5. Test with staff phones. Send clinical questions on purpose and check that each one is handed over.

### Frequently asked questions

#### Can the AI tell a patient what is wrong with them?

No, and you should instruct it never to try. It answers administrative questions and passes clinical ones to your staff.

#### Does it work at night when the front desk is closed?

Yes, it replies 24/7. For night-time clinical questions it should give your emergency number, because a handed-over chat waits until a staff member picks it up.

#### Can it check a patient's HMO cover or hospital record?

Not on its own. It has no access to your systems unless a developer connects them. Without that, it shares the list of HMOs you accept and staff confirm cover.

#### Is this suitable for a large hospital?

For enquiries and bookings, yes. A hospital that needs a verified badge or an official Meta integration needs the WhatsApp Business API instead. See the [healthcare page](/solutions/healthcare).
