For most Nigerian online stores the answer is both, with different jobs: Instagram is better for getting discovered, and WhatsApp is better for closing the sale and keeping the customer. One fact to know before you choose a tool: Dailzero's AI works on WhatsApp and a website chat widget only. It does not reply to Instagram DMs.

### How the two channels compare

| What matters | Instagram DMs | WhatsApp |
|---|---|---|
| Being found by new people | Strong. Reels, posts and hashtags bring strangers | Weak. People need your number or link first |
| Customer contact you keep | A handle, inside Instagram | A phone number you can save |
| Reaching past customers | Posts and Stories, to followers | Status and broadcasts, to your contacts |
| Voice notes, address and order details | Possible, less common | The normal way Nigerians do it |
| AI replies with Dailzero | Not supported | Supported, 24/7 |

### Where Instagram wins

Instagram is a shop window. Someone who has never heard of your brand can see a Reel of your ankara two-piece, tap your page, and send "price?" within a minute. WhatsApp has nothing like that. Nobody browses WhatsApp for new stores.

### Where WhatsApp wins

The sale itself tends to go better on WhatsApp, for practical reasons:

- **You get a phone number.** That is the same number the dispatch rider will call, and you keep it even if your Instagram page is restricted or hacked.
- **Order details flow naturally.** Customers send addresses, voice notes, screenshots of transfer receipts and photos of what they want.
- **You can reach them again.** Saved contacts see your Status, and you can broadcast a restock to existing customers.
- **It can be answered by AI at midnight.** With Dailzero, the reply comes in seconds using your prices, sizes and delivery fees.

A message like this is common at 11pm, after someone has seen your post:

> "Hi, I saw the green two-piece on your IG. Do you have size 14 and how much to deliver to Lekki Phase 1?"

On WhatsApp with an AI agent, the reply is immediate:

> "Hello. Yes, the green two-piece is available in size 14 at ₦32,000. Delivery to Lekki Phase 1 is ₦3,000 and arrives the next working day. Would you like to order it?"

### What to do about Instagram DMs

Since Dailzero does not cover DMs, you have three honest options:

1. **Move the conversation.** Put a WhatsApp link in your bio and add "Order on WhatsApp" to captions and Story stickers.
2. **Use Instagram's own basics.** Saved replies help you answer common DM questions faster by hand.
3. **Use an Instagram automation tool alongside.** Several exist. If most of your sales truly close inside DMs and you want them automated, that is the right tool for that channel, and Dailzero is not.

### Connect the two with click-to-WhatsApp ads

If you run Instagram or Facebook ads, you can choose the click-to-WhatsApp format, where the ad button opens a chat with your number. The customer discovers you on Instagram and lands directly where the AI can answer.

Dailzero records which Facebook or Instagram ad each customer came from. Over a few weeks you can see which ads start real buying conversations, not just which get likes.

### Which should you focus on?

**Lean on Instagram DMs if** you get only a handful of enquiries a day, you enjoy chatting with each customer personally, and your buyers resist leaving the app.

**Lean on WhatsApp with AI if** you are missing messages at night or during busy hours, you answer the same price and delivery questions all day, you want a contact list you own, or you are paying for ads and need every click answered.

**Do both if** Instagram is where people find you, which is true for most fashion, beauty and food brands. Keep posting there, and send the buying conversation to WhatsApp.

### What WhatsApp AI will not fix

It will not bring you new followers; that is still content and ads. It does not confirm payments either. The agent shares the account details or Paystack link you gave it, and hands the chat to you to check the alert. And it works from the stock list you typed in, so sold-out sizes need updating by you.

### Frequently asked questions

#### Does Dailzero reply to Instagram DMs?

No. Dailzero works on WhatsApp and on a website chat widget. It has no Instagram DM, Facebook Messenger, SMS or email channel.

#### Should I stop selling on Instagram?

No. Instagram is usually where new customers first see you. Keep it as your shop window and move the ordering conversation to WhatsApp.

#### How do I send Instagram followers to WhatsApp?

Add a WhatsApp link to your bio, mention it in captions and Stories, and use click-to-WhatsApp ads if you advertise.

#### Can I tell which Instagram ad brought a WhatsApp customer?

Yes, for click-to-WhatsApp ads. Dailzero shows which Facebook or Instagram ad a customer came from.
