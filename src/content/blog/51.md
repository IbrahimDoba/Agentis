The return on WhatsApp automation for a hotel, guest house, shortlet or restaurant is the profit from bookings you would otherwise have lost, plus the value of staff time saved, minus what the tool costs. On Dailzero the cost side is fixed and published, from ₦20,000 a month, so the only numbers you need to find are your own.

Nobody can honestly quote you a percentage before seeing those numbers. This guide gives you the formula and shows where to get each figure.

### The formula

**Monthly return = (extra bookings won × profit per booking) + (staff hours saved × what an hour costs you) − monthly cost of the tool**

Work through the three parts in order.

### Step 1: work out the cost

Dailzero charges in credits. A text reply uses about 5 credits and a photo 8. Hospitality chats are photo-heavy, because guests want to see the room before they pay.

Say a guest house in Ikeja gets 20 booking chats a day, and a typical chat needs four text replies and two room photos:

- One chat: (4 × 5) + (2 × 8) = 36 credits
- One month: 20 chats × 36 credits × 30 days = 21,600 credits

That fits the Basic plan, which is ₦20,000 a month for 25,000 credits. Paying yearly charges 10 months for 12, so Basic works out at ₦200,000 a year. There is no setup fee and no per-conversation fee to Meta. The full list is on the [pricing page](/pricing).

### Step 2: count the bookings you are losing now

This is the number most owners guess. Do not guess. For one week, open the WhatsApp on the front desk phone each morning and count:

1. Enquiries that arrived when nobody was on the phone: after 10pm, during check-in rush, during a generator changeover, on Sunday morning.
2. Enquiries that waited more than an hour for a first reply.
3. How many of those people never booked.

A guest who sends this at 11:40pm is usually messaging two or three other places at the same time:

> Good evening, how much is your deluxe room for Friday and Saturday? Is there 24 hours light?

The place that answers first, with a price and a photo, has the advantage. A reply like this can go out in seconds while your night staff are asleep:

> Good evening. The deluxe room is ₦45,000 a night, so ₦90,000 for Friday and Saturday, breakfast included. We have 24-hour power with generator and inverter backup. May I take your name and arrival time so the front desk can confirm the room for you?

Be conservative. If you counted 12 lost enquiries in the week, do not assume the AI wins all 12. Assume it wins a small share and see if the sums still work.

### Step 3: value the staff time

Time how long your receptionist or manager spends each day answering the same questions: rates, location, check-in time, airport pickup, whether the pool is working, whether you allow parties in the shortlet. Multiply those hours by what an hour of that person's time costs you. Use your own payroll figure, not an average from the internet.

### The break-even shortcut

If you only want one number, use this:

**Bookings needed to break even = monthly plan cost ÷ your profit per booking**

Here it is with a hypothetical profit of ₦25,000 per booking. Replace it with yours.

| Plan | Monthly cost | Extra bookings needed to break even |
|---|---|---|
| Basic | ₦20,000 | 1 (20,000 ÷ 25,000 = 0.8) |
| Starter | ₦40,000 | 2 (40,000 ÷ 25,000 = 1.6) |
| Pro | ₦75,000 | 3 (75,000 ÷ 25,000 = 3) |

So the question becomes simple. Does answering every WhatsApp enquiry in seconds, day and night, win you at least that many extra bookings a month? Your count from Step 2 tells you.

### What not to put in the calculation

Honest limits keep the numbers real.

- **It does not see your room availability.** Dailzero has no connection to a hotel or property management system unless a developer builds one through webhook tools. Without that, the AI quotes rates, sends photos, collects the guest's details and passes the booking to your front desk to confirm.
- **It does not take or verify payment.** It shares the account details or Paystack link you give it. A person still confirms the transfer or the caution deposit.
- **It does not answer phone calls.** If most of your bookings come by voice call, the return will be smaller.

### Frequently asked questions

#### Can I test the return without paying?

Yes. The free trial lasts 7 days and includes 1,000 credits, about 200 AI messages. That is enough to see how guests respond on your real number. You can [start a free trial](/signup) today.

#### Does the formula work for a restaurant or lounge?

Yes. Swap "profit per booking" for your profit per table reservation or per delivery order. The cost side is calculated the same way.

#### What if my enquiries are seasonal?

Use pay as you go in quiet months and a monthly plan for December and other festive periods. Packs start at ₦1,000 and the credits last 12 months.
