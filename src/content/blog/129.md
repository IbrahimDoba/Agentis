### Step 1: use the free tools first

Open the WhatsApp Business app, go to Business tools and set up three things:

1. **Greeting message.** Sent automatically when someone messages you for the first time. Put your price list link or catalogue here.
2. **Away message.** Sent when you are closed. Say when you will reply.
3. **Quick replies.** Saved answers you send by typing a shortcut such as `/price` or `/delivery`.

Add your products to the catalogue as well, so customers can browse without asking.

This costs nothing, and for some businesses it is all they need. If you get five to ten chats a day and you can answer within the hour, do not pay for anything yet.

### Where free stops working

Free tools send the same text whatever the customer wrote. Quick replies still need you to be on the phone. You have outgrown them when:

- Customers message at night and have bought elsewhere by morning
- You answer "how much?" and "do you deliver to Abuja?" dozens of times a day
- Chats pile up while you are packing orders or serving people in the shop
- Ads are bringing in more enquiries than you can answer

### Costs people forget

- **Your own time.** Free tools still need you to type. An hour a day answering the same questions is a real cost.
- **Lost sales.** A customer who waits three hours for a price often buys from whoever answered first.
- **Meta fees.** Tools on the official WhatsApp Business Platform carry Meta's message charges on top of the tool's price. Dailzero's default linked-device route has none.
- **Dollar pricing.** A tool billed in dollars costs more Naira when the rate moves.

### Where the free route is the better choice

Be honest with yourself about volume. If you get a few chats a day, sell one or two items and are happy to reply yourself, an AI agent will not pay for itself yet. Set up the three free tools, keep your catalogue current and come back when chats start slipping.
