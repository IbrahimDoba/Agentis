Dailzero is an AI WhatsApp agent for Nigerian businesses. You connect the WhatsApp number your customers already message, and the agent replies in seconds, day and night, using your own prices and product details. It sends product photos, takes orders, books appointments and hands the chat to you when a person is needed. Pricing is in Naira, from ₦20,000 a month or pay as you go from ₦1,000, with a 7-day free trial.

### What Dailzero does

Most Nigerian businesses sell on WhatsApp, and most lose customers the same way: a message arrives, nobody answers for two hours, and the customer buys elsewhere. Dailzero answers those messages for you.

- **Replies on your own number.** Customers keep messaging the number they know. They do not install anything or move to a new chat.
- **Answers from your information.** You add your products, prices, opening hours and policies. The agent answers from those, in the tone you choose.
- **Sends media.** Product photos, videos and documents from a library you upload.
- **Understands what customers send.** It reads images and voice notes as well as text.
- **Takes orders, captures leads and books appointments.** These appear in your dashboard.
- **Remembers returning customers** and what they asked before.
- **Hands over to a person.** When a customer asks for a human, or the agent cannot help, the chat comes to you. You can reply from the dashboard or straight from your phone, and the AI pauses on that chat.

It also covers the marketing side of WhatsApp: paced broadcasts to your existing contacts, scheduled WhatsApp Status posts with Autopilot, saving the contacts who message you so they can see your Status, and follow-ups for customers who went quiet. The full list is on the [features page](/features).

### Who it is for

Dailzero is built for owners and small teams who sell or support customers on WhatsApp: online shops and fashion vendors, restaurants, real estate agents, clinics, logistics companies, schools and online trainers, hotels and service businesses. You do not need a developer. Setup is a few steps in the dashboard.

Developers are also covered. There is an HTTPS API to send WhatsApp messages and run the agent from your own code, documented on the developers page. Agencies can run Dailzero for their own clients under their own brand.

### How it connects to WhatsApp

There are two routes, and you should know which one you are on.

| | Linked device (the default) | Official WhatsApp Business Platform |
|---|---|---|
| How it works | You link your number by QR code or pairing code, like WhatsApp Web | Your number runs on Meta's Cloud API |
| Setup | Minutes, done by you | Arranged with the Dailzero team on request |
| Meta verification | Not needed | Part of the official route |
| Meta message fees | None | Meta's own fees apply |
| WhatsApp Status and contact saving | Available | Not available |
| Approved message templates | Not available | Available |

Most small businesses use the linked-device route. It is not the official API, so Dailzero protects the number with queued, human-paced sending, a warm-up period for new numbers and automatic slow-down when WhatsApp signals a limit. The official route suits businesses that need compliance sign-off or approved templates. More detail is in [how Dailzero connects to WhatsApp](/blog/how-dailzero-connects-to-whatsapp).

### Frequently asked questions

#### Is Dailzero a chatbot?

It is an AI agent, which is different from a keyword chatbot. A keyword bot matches fixed words to fixed replies. Dailzero reads the customer's message, answers from your business information and can take an order or book an appointment.

#### Does Dailzero work with the number I already use?

Yes. On the linked-device route you link your existing WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business number by QR code or pairing code. Your customers keep messaging the same number.

#### Is Dailzero the official WhatsApp API?

The default route is a linked device, not the official API. Dailzero also offers the official WhatsApp Business Platform, set up with the team on request, for businesses that need it.

Monthly plans are ₦20,000, ₦40,000 and ₦75,000. Pay as you go starts from ₦1,000 with no subscription. Every new account starts with a 7-day free trial.
