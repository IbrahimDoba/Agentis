Dailzero stores the conversations between your customers and your AI agent so the agent can reply and you can read them in your dashboard. Its privacy policy says conversation transcripts are kept for up to 12 months and then deleted or anonymised, that data is not sold or used for advertising, and that you can ask for deletion at any time. This page sets out what the privacy policy and security page say, and nothing more.

### What Dailzero collects

The privacy policy lists two groups of data.

**About you, the business:**

- Account information: name, email address, phone number, business name and a hashed password.
- Business information: your description, products and services, FAQs, opening hours and response guidelines.
- Usage data: pages visited, features used, login times, IP address, browser and device.
- Payment information: billing records and transaction history. Card details are processed by the payment partner. Dailzero does not store raw card data.
- Messages you send to Dailzero by email or the contact form.

**About your customers on WhatsApp:**

- Their WhatsApp phone number.
- The text of messages exchanged with the agent.
- Message details such as timestamps and delivery status.
- Media shared in the conversation: voice notes, images, videos or documents.
- AI-generated summaries of conversations.

The policy says this customer data is processed only to provide the service to you.

### How long it is kept

| Data | Kept for |
|---|---|
| Account data | The length of your subscription, plus 90 days after termination |
| Conversation transcripts and message details | Up to 12 months, then deleted or anonymised |
| Payment records | 7 years, to meet Nigerian financial regulations |
| Contact form submissions | 2 years |

Anything WhatsApp or Meta holds about messages follows Meta's own retention rules, which Dailzero does not control.

### Who can see your chats

- **You and your team.** Conversations appear in your dashboard. Team members you add have their own logins.
- **Authorised Dailzero staff.** The policy states that role-based access control limits data access to authorised personnel.
- **Service providers.** Dailzero uses third-party providers for AI processing, hosting, the database and email. They are named in the privacy policy, which says they are bound by data processing agreements. Some process data outside Nigeria.
- **WhatsApp and Meta.** Messages travel through WhatsApp's servers, under Meta's own privacy policy.
- **Authorities, when the law requires it.** The policy allows disclosure in response to a court order or a lawful request.

### What Dailzero says it does not do

- It does not sell your data or your customers' data.
- It does not use conversation data for advertising or marketing profiling.
- It does not share conversation data with Meta or advertising networks for ad targeting.
- It does not use advertising or third-party tracking cookies on its platform.

### How data is protected

The security page and privacy policy list these measures:

- Data in transit is encrypted with TLS 1.3, and HTTPS is enforced.
- Data is stored in databases that are encrypted at rest.
- Passwords are hashed with bcrypt and never stored in plain text.
- Access is role-based, and admin access is restricted by role.
- API keys and service credentials are kept out of source code.
- If a data breach is confirmed, affected users are notified within 72 hours, as the NDPR requires.

The policy also says plainly that no internet transmission is 100% secure.

### How to delete your data

1. Go to the data deletion page at `/data-deletion`, or email support@dailzero.com with the subject "Data Deletion Request".
2. Give your name and the email linked to your account, and choose what you want deleted.
3. Confirm the request. You receive a reference number.
4. Dailzero may contact you to verify your identity.
5. The request is processed within 30 days of verification.

The deletion page says this covers account details, business configuration and agent settings, WhatsApp conversation transcripts and message details, and AI-generated summaries. It also says what may be kept: financial records required by Nigerian law for up to 7 years, anonymised analytics, a record of the request itself, and anything Meta holds independently.

If you close your account without a deletion request, data is kept for up to 90 days after termination and then deleted, unless the law requires longer.

### Your rights, and your customers' rights

Under the Nigerian Data Protection Regulation you can ask for a copy of your data, correct it, have it erased, restrict or object to processing, receive it in a machine-readable format, and withdraw consent. Write to support@dailzero.com. The policy commits to a response within 30 days. You can also complain to NITDA, which supervises NDPR compliance.

A customer who chatted with your agent can ask you, the business, about their data. They can also use the deletion form and give the phone number they messaged from.

### What you are responsible for

The terms make you the data controller for your customers' data, with Dailzero as the processor. In plain words, you are expected to:

- Tell customers they are talking to an AI agent.
- Have your own privacy notice that mentions WhatsApp and AI messaging.
- Message only people who agreed to hear from you, and honour requests to stop.
- Not use chat data for advertising or sell it.

### Frequently asked questions

#### Does Dailzero sell customer data?

No. The privacy policy states that Dailzero does not sell your data or your customers' data, and does not use conversation data for advertising.

#### How long does Dailzero keep my chats?

Conversation transcripts are kept for up to 12 months, after which they are deleted or anonymised. You can ask for earlier deletion.

#### Can Dailzero staff read my conversations?

Access is limited by role to authorised personnel. The policy does not say staff never see conversations, so ask support@dailzero.com if you need the exact position for your business.

#### Is my data stored in Nigeria?

Not all of it. The privacy policy says some service providers may process data outside Nigeria, with data processing agreements in place.
