There is no single best WhatsApp AI tool for every Nigerian online seller. There are four types of tool, and the right one depends on how many chats you get, whether you have a developer, and whether you need the official WhatsApp API. For a seller who runs the business from one WhatsApp number and wants an AI that answers customers without a technical setup, an agent that links to your existing number, such as Dailzero, is usually the quickest to start. For a large team that needs a verified badge and many staff on one number, an official API platform is the better choice.

### The four types of tool

| Type | Examples | Best for | Main limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| WhatsApp Business app features | Quick replies, away messages, catalogue, labels | Sellers just starting, low chat volume | Not AI. You still type most replies |
| Flow or menu chatbots | Tools such as ManyChat | Simple, predictable questions | Customers must follow the menu. Free-typed questions often fail |
| Official API platforms | Tools such as Wati and Respond.io | Larger teams, shared inbox, verified badge | More setup, Meta's own message charges, template approval |
| AI agents on your existing number | Dailzero | Small and mid-sized sellers who want real conversations answered | Not the official API. One number per agent |

Features and prices change often, so check each tool's own website before you decide.

### Flow and menu chatbots

These send a menu ("Reply 1 for prices, 2 for delivery") and follow a script you draw. The weakness shows up in how Nigerian customers actually write. Nobody replies "2". They send this:

> Abeg how much be that black bag for your status and una dey deliver go Benin?

A menu bot has no branch for that. Many of these tools now add AI steps, so test a message like this one before you commit.

### Official WhatsApp API platforms

These are built on Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform. They suit businesses that need a green verified badge, many staff working one number in a shared inbox, or very high volumes. In return, expect a business verification process, approved templates for messages you start, and charges from Meta alongside the platform's subscription.

### Who Dailzero is not the right fit for

- A business that needs the official API: very high volume, a verified badge, or many agents on one number at enterprise scale.
- A store that needs the tool to read live stock or orders from Shopify, WooCommerce, Jumia or Konga with no developer. Dailzero has no native integrations with these. A developer can connect your own system through webhook tools, described on the [developer page](/developers).
- A seller who wants one inbox for Instagram DMs, Messenger and email. Dailzero covers WhatsApp and a website chat widget only.

### Four questions to ask any tool before you pay

1. **Does it use my existing number, or do I need a new one?** Your customers have your current number saved.
2. **What does one month cost in Naira at my chat volume?** Include any Meta charges and setup fees. Ask for the total.
3. **What happens when a customer types something unexpected, in Pidgin, or sends a voice note?** Test it yourself during the trial.
4. **How does a human take over a chat?** If the handover is clumsy, complaints will go badly.

### How to run a fair trial

On Dailzero the free trial is 7 days with 1,000 credits, about 200 AI messages. Send the tool your ten most common customer questions, one in Pidgin, one as a voice note, and one photo of a product with "do you have this?". Ask for a human. Judge it on those answers, not on the sales page.

### Frequently asked questions

#### Is the free WhatsApp Business app enough for a small seller?

Often, yes. If you can reply to every chat within a few minutes during the day and you are not losing night-time enquiries, stay with the app until that changes.

#### Do I need the official WhatsApp API to use AI?

No. Linked-number agents such as Dailzero work on a normal WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business number. You need the official API only if you want the verified badge, enterprise scale or many agents on one number.

#### Is linking an AI tool to my number safe?

Dailzero queues every message and sends at a human pace, warms up new numbers and slows down when WhatsApp signals a limit. No tool can promise a number will never be restricted, so avoid any that does.
