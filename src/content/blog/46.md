An AI concierge is a chat assistant that answers guests' questions and takes their requests during a stay. In a Nigerian hotel the practical place to run one is WhatsApp, because guests already have it and few will download a hotel app for a two-night stay. Before you set one up, there are six things worth knowing.

### 1. It only knows what you tell it

Every answer comes from the information you load. If you never told it the pool closes at 7pm, it cannot tell a guest.

A useful starting list:

- WiFi name and password, and what to do when it drops
- Breakfast time and place, and whether it is included in the rate
- Restaurant and room service hours, with the menu as a document
- Laundry prices and turnaround time
- Pool, gym and bar hours
- Generator or power changeover times, if you have them
- Airport pickup and car hire prices
- Check-out time and the late check-out fee
- Directions to the nearest ATM, pharmacy and supermarket
- Visitor policy

Most hotels can write all of this in one sitting, because the front desk answers these questions every day.

### 2. It answers; your staff still deliver

This is the point hotels most often get wrong. A concierge chat can accept a request for extra towels, but a person still has to carry the towels upstairs.

Dailzero has no connection to housekeeping or maintenance systems. What it does is collect the request, with the room number and the details, and hand the chat to a person. Your staff see it in the dashboard or on the phone the number lives on. Team members can have their own logins, so the duty manager and the front desk can both watch for handovers.

A guest writes:

> Abeg the AC in 204 is not cooling at all.

A good reply:

> Sorry about that. I am passing this to our maintenance team now with your room number, 204, and someone from the front desk will update you shortly. Would you like us to arrange another room in the meantime?

Decide who picks up those handovers on each shift before you go live.

### 3. It is not a telephone

Dailzero does not make or answer voice calls, and it does not work on the room intercom. It covers WhatsApp and a chat widget on your website. Guests who prefer to call the front desk will keep calling, and that is fine.

It does understand voice notes and photos, so a guest can send a voice note asking for a wake-up call or a picture of a leaking tap.

### 4. Guests need a way in

A concierge nobody messages is no use. Make the number easy to reach:

- Print it on the key card sleeve and on a card beside the bed.
- Use the QR code that WhatsApp generates for your number, so a guest can scan and start a chat.
- Have the front desk mention it at check-in: "For anything you need, message this number."
- If the guest booked on WhatsApp, they already have the chat. The agent remembers earlier conversations, so it knows who they are.

### 5. Sensitive matters go to a person

Set clear rules for what the agent must never handle alone: billing disputes, refund requests, lost property, security concerns, medical situations and complaints about staff. For these it should apologise where that is called for, collect the facts, and hand over.

It also cannot check a guest's bill, extend a stay in your booking system, or see which rooms are free, unless a developer has connected your system. Without that, it works from what you typed in.

### 6. The cost depends on how chatty your guests are

In-stay chats are short. Say 25 occupied rooms produce 15 concierge chats a day at about 4 replies each: 15 × 4 × 5 credits × 30 days = 9,000 credits a month. If the same number also handles booking enquiries, add those. Most small hotels would land inside the Basic plan at ₦20,000 for 25,000 credits. ### Frequently asked questions

#### Does an AI concierge replace the front desk?

No. It takes repeated questions off the desk and logs requests. People still deliver the service, handle complaints and answer the phone.

#### Can it book a taxi or a restaurant table outside the hotel?

It can share the prices and contacts you gave it and pass the request to staff. It cannot place a booking with an outside business itself.

#### Can guests message in Pidgin?

Yes. The agent copes with everyday Nigerian English and Pidgin in guests' messages. Replies in several languages are a Pro plan feature.

#### What if the agent gives a wrong answer?

It answers from your information, so wrong answers usually trace back to something missing or out of date. Read the chats weekly and correct what you loaded.
