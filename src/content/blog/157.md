A WhatsApp AI agent helps a school or online trainer by answering the same enquiries every parent and student sends: fees, admission requirements, start dates, class times and how to pay. It replies in seconds from the details you give it, records each enquiry as a lead, books visits or consultations, and passes the chat to a staff member when a person is needed. It does not replace your school portal, your teachers or your accounts office.

### The questions that fill a school's WhatsApp

Most schools and training businesses in Nigeria run admissions on one or two phone numbers. During admission season and before each new cohort, the same messages arrive all day:

- "How much is the school fees for JSS1?"
- "When is the entrance exam?"
- "Is the data analysis class online or physical?"
- "Do you have weekend classes?"
- "Can I pay in two instalments?"
- "Where are you located?"

Each answer is already written down somewhere. The problem is that one admin officer cannot type it fifty times a day and still do the rest of the job. Slow replies matter, because a parent comparing three schools often goes with the one that answered.

### For online trainers and course sellers

If you sell courses through Instagram, TikTok or ads, your WhatsApp is your sales desk. An AI agent suits this well:

- **It answers the first questions fast.** Price, duration, start date, whether there is a certificate, whether classes are recorded.

- **It leaves closing to you if you want.** The agent can pause when someone is ready to pay, so you finish the sale yourself.

### What it should not do

Be clear about the limits before you set one up.

- **It is not your student portal.** It does not show results, attendance or a child's fee balance unless your own system is connected to it through an API, which needs a developer.
- **It should not discuss a named child's welfare, grades or discipline.** Send those chats to a staff member.
- **It should not agree discounts, scholarships or payment plans** unless you have written the exact rules for it.
- **It does not replace class or PTA groups.** On Dailzero the agent does not reply in group chats unless you turn that on.

### How to set it up for a school

1. **Write down the facts.** Fees per class or course, what the fee covers, admission steps, key dates, address, hours and payment details.
2. **Decide the handover rules.** Complaints, anything about a specific child, and special payment requests go to a person.
3. **Link the admissions number.** On Dailzero you link the WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business number you already use, by QR code or pairing code.
4. **Add your files.** Prospectus, fee schedule, course outline, timetable.
5. **Test with real questions.** Ask about a class you do not offer and a fee you did not enter. The agent should say it will check, not guess.
6. **Tell your staff.** Show them how to reply in a chat, which pauses the AI on that chat.
7. **Update it every term.** Old fees are the most common cause of wrong answers.

### Be careful with broadcasts

Schools like to message every parent at once. That is fine when the parents are already your contacts and expect to hear from you. Do not message bought lists or numbers copied from other groups. Reports and blocks are the main reason numbers get restricted.

#### Can the AI tell a parent their child's result or fee balance?

Not by default. It answers from the general information you give it. Looking up one student's record needs your own system connected through an API, and many schools prefer to keep that with staff.

#### Can it send the prospectus as a PDF?

Yes, the agent can send documents from your media library. Check the pricing page for which plan includes document sending.

#### Can several staff members use it?

Yes. Team seats depend on the plan: 1 on Basic, 2 on Starter and 5 on Pro.

#### Is it suitable for a small tutorial centre?

It can be. If you get only a few enquiries a day, the free tools in the WhatsApp Business app may be enough. An AI agent pays off when enquiries are too many to answer quickly by hand.
