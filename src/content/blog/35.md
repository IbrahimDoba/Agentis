A Nigerian hotel can use WhatsApp AI to handle booking inquiries by letting the agent answer rates and room questions, collect the guest's dates and details, share payment instructions, and then pass the chat to the front desk for the one step that needs a person: confirming the room is free and the money has arrived.

That last step matters. An AI agent does not see your room chart unless a developer connects it, so the honest design is "AI gathers, front desk confirms". Here is how the whole inquiry runs.

### A booking inquiry, message by message

Take a 30-room hotel in Port Harcourt. A guest writes on Thursday evening:

> Good evening. Do you have a room for Friday to Sunday? How much?

**Step 1: answer the price.** The agent replies with your room types and rates, not with "please call us".

> Good evening. For Friday to Sunday, that is 2 nights. Our Standard room is ₦35,000 a night and the Deluxe is ₦48,000 a night, both with breakfast, Wi-Fi and 24-hour power. Which would you prefer, and is it for one guest or two?

**Step 2: send proof.** When the guest picks Deluxe, the agent sends photos of that room from your media library. Guests book what they can see.

**Step 3: collect the details.** Full name, number of guests, arrival time, and anything special such as airport pickup or an early check-in.

**Step 4: explain payment.** The agent shares the account details or Paystack link you gave it, and your deposit rule, for example "one night's payment secures the room".

**Step 5: hand over.** The agent tells the guest the front desk will confirm shortly, and passes the chat to your staff with everything already collected. Your receptionist checks the room chart, checks the bank alert, and sends the confirmation.

The rates above are examples. The agent quotes whatever you enter.

### What to give the agent before it goes live

The quality of the replies depends on what you type in during setup. Cover these:

- Every room type with its rate, bed size, and what is included
- Weekend, festive season or long-stay rates if they differ
- Check-in and check-out times, and your early check-in and late checkout charges
- Deposit and cancellation policy
- Payment details: account name, bank, account number, or a payment link
- Facilities: generator and inverter backup, pool, gym, restaurant hours, parking, security
- Location with landmarks, and distance from the airport
- Policies guests ask about: visitors, children, ID at check-in
- Photos of each room type, the lobby and the pool, plus your restaurant menu as a document

### The availability question

"Do you have a room on the 24th?" is the hardest question for any AI agent, because the true answer lives in your booking register or hotel software. Dailzero has no built-in connection to property management or booking systems. You have three workable options:

1. **Confirm by handoff.** The agent collects dates and details, then your front desk confirms. This is the simplest and suits most small hotels and guest houses.
2. **Tell the agent your blackout dates.** If you are fully booked for a wedding weekend or the Christmas week, type that in so it stops taking requests for those dates.
3. **Connect your system.** A developer can link your booking system through webhook tools, so the agent checks live availability mid-chat. This is worth it for larger properties. Details are on the [developers page](/developers).

### When the agent should step aside

Tell it to hand the chat to a person for:

- Rate negotiation and corporate or group rates
- Payment confirmation and refund requests
- Complaints from guests already in the hotel
- Event hall bookings that need a site visit

When your staff reply from the dashboard or from the hotel phone, the AI pauses on that chat. Switch it back on when you are done.

### Frequently asked questions

#### Can the AI confirm a booking without my staff?

It can take the booking request and collect every detail. Because it does not verify payments or see your room chart, a person should send the final confirmation unless a developer has connected your system.

#### Will it work on the number already printed on our signboard?

Yes. You link the WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business number you already use by scanning a QR code or entering a pairing code. The hotel phone stays the main device and should come online regularly.

#### What if a guest sends a voice note or a screenshot of their transfer?

The agent understands voice notes and photos. For a transfer screenshot, it should thank the guest and hand the chat to staff, since only you can confirm the money arrived.

#### Does it recognise a guest who has stayed before?

Yes. It remembers returning customers and earlier chats, so a regular guest does not have to explain everything again.
