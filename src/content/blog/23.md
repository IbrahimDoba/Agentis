You can answer property enquiries around the clock without hiring anyone by linking an AI agent to the WhatsApp number you already use. It replies in seconds at any hour using your listings, prices and photos, and leaves the chats that need a person waiting for you in the morning.

The easiest way to see how this works is to follow one night.

### One night on an agent's WhatsApp

Picture a two-person agency in Port Harcourt that handles rentals in GRA and sales in a new estate off the airport road. Both agents stop checking their phones around 7pm.

**7:40pm.** A banker on her way home sees the agency's Instagram post and messages:

> Pls is the 3 bedroom in GRA Phase 2 still available and what's the rent

The AI answers within seconds:

> Good evening. Yes, the 3 bedroom flat in GRA Phase 2 is still available. Rent is ₦4.5 million a year, and the total package with agency, legal and caution deposit is ₦5.6 million. It has a fitted kitchen and space for two cars. Shall I send photos?

She says yes. It sends six photos from the media library and offers an inspection slot on Saturday. She picks 11am.

**11:15pm.** A man sends a voice note asking, in a mix of English and Pidgin, whether the estate land has a C of O and whether he can pay small small. The AI understands the voice note, answers with the title and the instalment plan the agency loaded, and sends the estate brochure as a document.

**2:30am.** A Nigerian nurse in Houston, awake while Port Harcourt sleeps, asks whether she can buy a plot without travelling home, and whether her brother can inspect on her behalf. The AI explains the process the agency wrote down for buyers abroad, sends the site video, and collects her brother's name and number for the inspection.

**6:05am.** An early riser asks for a discount on the 3 bedroom. The agency told the AI that negotiation belongs to a person, so it says a team member will respond once the office opens and hands the chat over.

By 8am the agents have one booked inspection, two leads in the dashboard with their details collected, and one negotiation waiting. Nobody was ignored for twelve hours.

### What you load so it can answer like that

The night above only works if the AI knows what you know. Before going live, give it:

- Every available property, with price, total package, location, title and key features.
- Photos, videos and brochures in the media library.
- Your inspection days, times and any inspection fee.
- Your payment plans and the account details you want shared.
- Your process for buyers in the diaspora.
- What it should not handle: negotiation, payment confirmation, document disputes.

Setup needs no code. You sign up, describe the business, add the above, link your number by QR code or pairing code, test it by chatting from another phone, and go live. The steps are on the [how it works page](/how-it-works).

### What still waits for a human

Round-the-clock replies do not mean round-the-clock deals. These still need you:

- **Confirming payment.** Dailzero does not process or verify payments. It can share your account details and hand the chat to you to confirm the transfer.
- **Negotiation.** You decide what discount is possible.
- **Availability changes.** The AI cannot see your records. If a flat is taken on Friday evening and you do not update it, the AI will keep offering it. Make updating listings a daily habit.
- **Physical inspections.** Someone still has to hold the keys.

### What the night shift costs

Arithmetic on Dailzero's own prices: a text reply uses about 5 credits and an image 8.

Say you get 15 after-hours enquiries a night, and each one needs 6 text replies and 2 photos. That is 30 credits plus 16 credits, so 46 credits a chat. Fifteen chats is 690 credits a night, or 20,700 credits over 30 nights.

That fits inside the Basic plan, which is ₦20,000 a month for 25,000 credits. If the AI also handles your daytime chats, work out the total the same way and compare it with the plans on the [pricing page](/pricing). To compare with a night staff member, plug in the salary you would actually pay.

There are no overage fees. If credits run out, the AI pauses and messages still arrive in your WhatsApp as usual.

### Frequently asked questions

#### Will the AI reply during the day too, or only at night?

It replies whenever a message arrives. If you take over a chat yourself, from the dashboard or your phone, the AI pauses on that chat until you switch it back on.

#### What if someone asks something the AI does not know?

It hands the chat to a person instead of guessing. You see it when you are back online and can add the missing answer to its knowledge so it handles that question next time.

#### Can it serve buyers in other time zones?

Yes. It replies at any hour, which is the main benefit for diaspora buyers who message during their own evening.
