Do not buy a monthly plan at first. A small Nigerian business affords AI customer service on WhatsApp by using a free trial, then buying credits as it goes from ₦1,000, and pointing the AI at only its most repeated questions. You scale the spending up when the chats prove it is worth it, not before.

This guide is about that starting path. For the full price table and the formula for estimating a monthly bill, see [what WhatsApp AI automation costs in Nigeria](/blog/how-much-does-whatsapp-ai-automation-cost-for-nigerian-businesses).

### Step one: spend nothing for a week

Dailzero's free trial runs for 7 days with 1,000 credits, which is about 200 AI messages. It runs on your real number with your real customers.

To get value from it, prepare before you sign up. Write down your prices, delivery fees, opening hours and return policy first, so the trial days are spent testing with customers and not typing.

By the end of the week you will know how many replies a typical chat needs and which questions the AI handled well.

### Step two: buy credits, not a subscription

After the trial, pay as you go. There is no monthly commitment and credits stay valid for 12 months.

What the small packs get you, at about 5 credits per text reply:

- **₦1,000** buys 600 credits, about 120 replies.
- **₦5,000** buys 3,200 credits, about 640 replies.
- **₦10,000** buys 6,500 credits, about 1,300 replies.

If a typical chat takes six replies, the ₦5,000 pack covers a little over 100 customer conversations. For a tailor in Ibadan or a cake seller in Enugu getting four or five enquiries a day, that can be most of a month.

When credits finish, nothing breaks. The AI pauses, customer messages still arrive in your WhatsApp as usual, and you answer by hand until you top up. There are no overage charges.

### Step three: automate the boring chats first

You do not have to hand everything to the AI. Start with the questions where the answer never changes:

1. Price of your main products or services.
2. Delivery areas and fees, and how long delivery takes.
3. Location and opening hours.
4. How to pay.
5. What sizes, colours or options are available.

Give the agent solid answers to those and tell it to pass anything else to you. A message like this is a perfect first target:

> How much is the 2kg cake and do you deliver to Trans Ekulu?

A good reply gives the price, the delivery fee for that area and the notice you need, then asks for the date.

Leave complaints, refunds, bulk orders and bargaining for yourself at this stage. The agent hands those chats over when it cannot help or when the customer asks for a person,.

### Step four: keep each chat cheap

A few habits stretch your credits:

- **Answer the next question before it is asked.** If the price reply already includes the delivery fee and payment method, the chat needs three replies instead of six.
- **Send photos on request, not by default.** An image costs 8 credits against 5 for text.
- **Hold off on broadcasts.** A text to 500 customers uses 2,500 credits. Wait until you have a real offer to announce.

### Step five: know when a plan becomes cheaper

Pay as you go costs more per credit than a monthly plan. On the smallest pack, one text reply works out at about ₦8. On the Basic plan, ₦20,000 for 25,000 credits, it is ₦4.

So the rule is simple: when your top-ups reach about ₦20,000 in a month, switch to Basic. You get nearly twice the credits for the same money. Unused monthly credits carry into the next month, up to 25% of the plan allowance, and paying yearly charges 10 months for 12.

### Costs people forget to count

Dailzero has no setup fee and nothing is paid to Meta, because it links to your existing number the way WhatsApp Web does. The other costs are:

- **Your time to set it up**, writing good answers and uploading photos.
- **A phone that stays in service.** It remains the main device and should come online regularly.
- **Checking the chats** daily at first, to catch wrong answers.

### Is it worth it for a very small business?

Ask one question: how many buyers do you lose in a week because you replied late? If one recovered sale covers a ₦5,000 pack, the decision is easy. If you get three messages a week and answer them all within minutes, you do not need this yet, and that is fine.

### Frequently asked questions

#### What is the cheapest way to start?

The 7-day free trial, then a ₦1,000 or ₦5,000 pay as you go pack. There is no setup fee.

#### Do pay as you go credits expire?

They are valid for 12 months from purchase, so a quiet season does not waste them.

#### Can I make the AI answer only some questions?

In practice, yes. Give it clear answers for the topics you want covered and instruct it to hand everything else to you.

#### Will I get a surprise bill?

No. There are no overage fees. When credits run out the AI pauses and you reply manually until you top up.
