To hand over from AI to a human on WhatsApp, a person replies in the chat and the AI stops answering that customer. On Dailzero you can reply from the dashboard or straight from WhatsApp on your phone, and the AI pauses on that one chat as soon as you send a message. Other chats keep getting AI replies. You switch the AI back on for that chat when you are done.

### Why handover matters

An AI agent is good at prices, delivery questions and taking orders. It should not be the one to settle a refund, calm an angry customer or agree a special discount. Without a clean handover two things go wrong: the AI keeps talking over you, or nobody answers because each side thinks the other has it.

### The three ways a chat moves to a human

| What starts it | What happens on Dailzero |
|---|---|
| You reply yourself | The AI pauses on that chat. The customer's next message comes to you. |
| The customer needs a person | The AI flags the chat when a customer is frustrated, asks for a human or raises something sensitive such as a refund, a complaint or a custom quote. The chat shows as "Needs human" in the dashboard. |
| The customer is ready to buy | When a customer confirms clear buying intent, the AI marks a lead and can pause so you close the sale yourself. |

All three are settings on the agent and are on by default. You can turn each one off.

### How to take over a chat

1. **Open the chat.** Use the conversations page in the dashboard, or open the chat in WhatsApp on your phone.
2. **Send your reply.** With "Auto-pause AI when I reply manually" on, the AI pauses for that single conversation. This works whether you or a team member sends the message, from the dashboard or from the linked phone.
3. **Carry on as normal.** The customer sees one continuous chat from your number. Nothing tells them the sender changed.
4. **Finish the issue.** While the chat is in Human mode, the AI stays quiet on it.
5. **Hand it back.** In the dashboard, set that chat back to AI in the chat header.

### How to hand the chat back to the AI

Each chat in the dashboard has a selector with three choices:

- **AI.** The agent replies.
- **Human.** You reply. If you have set an auto-resume timer, the AI comes back after that period of inactivity.
- **Always human.** The chat stays with you and the AI never resumes on its own. Use this for a supplier, a relative or a VIP customer.

The auto-resume timer is off unless you set it in the agent settings. It is useful if you often forget to switch the AI back on. If many chats are paused, the conversations page shows a banner that lets you resume the AI on all of them at once.

### Settings worth checking on day one

- **Auto-pause when I reply manually.** Leave it on unless you prefer to switch each chat by hand.
- **Auto-pause when the AI asks for human help.** Leave it on, so the AI does not keep talking to an upset customer.
- **Auto-pause when the AI qualifies a lead.** Keep it on if you like to close sales personally. Turn it off if you want the AI to keep going until the order is taken.
- **Auto-resume timer.** Set one if your team is small and chats get left in Human mode.
- **AI replies.** There is also a master switch that turns AI replies off for the whole agent, for days when you want to answer everything yourself.

These are described on the [features page](/features) under live operator handoff.

### Good handover habits

- **Tell the agent what it must not handle.** Write it in the instructions: refunds, bargaining, anything about money already paid.
- **Check the "Needs human" filter a few times a day.** A flagged chat means a customer is waiting.
- **Reply fast after a handover.** The customer has been told a person is coming.
- **Give staff their own logins.** Team seats depend on your plan: 1 on Basic, 2 on Starter and 5 on Pro. See [pricing](/pricing).
- **Do not leave chats paused for days.** A customer who returns next week will get silence if the chat is still in Human mode and nobody is watching.

### What a customer sees

Nothing changes on the customer's side. The messages come from the same number, in the same chat. If you want the customer to know a person has joined, say so in your first message: "Hello, this is Tunde from the shop. I have seen your message."

### Limits to know

Handover moves the chat to you. It does not find a person for you at 2am. If nobody is available, the customer waits. Set expectations in the agent's instructions, for example that the team replies between 8am and 8pm.

### Frequently asked questions

#### Will the AI and I both reply to the same customer?

Not once you have replied. With auto-pause on, your message pauses the AI on that chat, so the customer's next message goes to you and not the AI.

#### Can I reply from my phone, or must I use the dashboard?

Either. A message sent from WhatsApp on your linked phone pauses the AI the same way a reply from the dashboard does.

#### How do I turn the AI back on for a chat?

Open the chat in the dashboard and set it back to AI. You can also set a timer so the AI resumes after a period of inactivity.

#### Can the AI decide by itself to call a human?

Yes. It flags a chat when a customer is frustrated, asks for a person or raises something sensitive. With the default setting it also pauses, and the chat is marked "Needs human".

#### Does pausing one chat stop the AI for everyone?

No. The pause applies to that single conversation. Every other customer still gets AI replies.
