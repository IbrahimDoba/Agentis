The simplest way to offer WhatsApp AI as a service to your clients is to resell an existing platform under your own name instead of building one. Dailzero has white-label reseller accounts for this: you run AI WhatsApp agents for your clients under your own brand and domain, and your agency does the setup, the training and the monthly care.

### Two routes: resell it, or manage the client's own account

| Question | White-label reseller | Managed client account |
|---|---|---|
| Whose brand the client sees | Yours, on your domain | Dailzero's |
| Who the client pays | You, under your reseller terms | Dailzero for the plan, you for your service |
| Best when | You want a product line with recurring revenue | You have one or two clients and want to test demand |
| How to start | Ask Dailzero for reseller terms | Client signs up, adds you as a team member |

The reseller route suits a digital agency, web developer or consultant who plans to sell this repeatedly. [Contact Dailzero](/contact) for the terms before you price anything, and do not assume the public plans apply.

The managed route needs no agreement. The client opens their own account, pays in Naira through Paystack, and gives you a team login. You charge for your work. It is also the low-risk way to test demand, starting on the 7-day free trial.

### Pick a niche before you pick a price

An agent is only as good as the business information behind it. If you serve one type of business, you build that knowledge once and reuse the structure. Good candidates are businesses that already live on WhatsApp and answer the same questions all day:

- Restaurants and food vendors: menu, delivery areas, order taking.
- Fashion and beauty sellers: sizes, prices, pay on delivery, dispatch.
- Clinics, salons and spas: opening hours and appointment booking.
- Property agents: listings, inspection fees, viewing times.

### What goes into the service package

Clients are not paying you for software. They are paying for the work around it:

1. **Discovery.** Collect the client's price list, FAQs, delivery or booking rules, and the 20 questions customers ask most. Read their real WhatsApp chats if they allow it.
2. **Build.** Enter products, prices, policies and tone. Upload photos, menus or brochures to the media library.
3. **Linking.** The client scans a QR code or enters a pairing code on their own phone, the same way WhatsApp Web is linked. You cannot do this step without them.
4. **Testing.** Message the agent as a customer would, in plain English and in Pidgin, and correct weak answers.
5. **Monthly care.** Update prices, review handed-over chats, add new FAQs, schedule Status posts and broadcasts, and send the client a short report on leads and chats handled.

Step 5 is where the retainer is earned. Prices in Nigeria change often, and an agent quoting last month's price is worse than no agent.

### How to work out what to charge

Start from the client's usage, then add your labour. Say a client restaurant in Surulere gets 50 chats a day at about 6 replies each: 50 × 6 × 5 credits × 30 days = 45,000 credits a month. On the public [Dailzero plans](/pricing), that sits inside Starter at ₦40,000 for 60,000 credits. Your cost as a reseller depends on your terms, so use your real figure.

On top of the platform cost, price three things separately: a one-off setup fee, a monthly management fee, and extras such as broadcasts, which use 5 credits per person for text and 8 for an image.

### What to tell clients honestly before they sign

Overselling loses retainers. Put these in the proposal:

- **It is not the official WhatsApp Business API.** There is no verified green badge and no Meta per-conversation fee. The agent links to the client's existing number as a linked device.
- **Their phone stays the main device** and should come online regularly. One number per agent.
- **It does not take or verify payments.** It shares the account details or payment link the client provides and hands the chat over for confirmation.
- **It does not see their stock, bookings or delivery status by itself.** That requires development work using [webhook tools in the API](/developers), which you can sell as a separate project.
- **Broadcasts go to existing contacts only,** not bought lists, and no tool can promise a number will never be restricted.
- **A brand-new number** should be used normally for about two weeks before it is connected.

### Frequently asked questions

#### Do I need to be a developer to offer this?

No. Setup is done in a dashboard with no code. Developer skills only matter if a client wants the agent connected to their own systems.

#### Can I put my agency's name on it?

Yes, with a white-label reseller account, which runs under your own brand and domain. On the managed route the client sees Dailzero's name.

#### How much do resellers pay?

Reseller terms are agreed directly with Dailzero, so ask through the contact page. Do not build your price list on a guess.
