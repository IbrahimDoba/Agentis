Fashion buyers on WhatsApp want five things answered before they pay: is it available, will it fit, how much, can I see it properly, and will it reach me before my event. A Nigerian fashion brand converts more of them with WhatsApp AI by answering all five in seconds, with photos, at whatever hour the customer is scrolling.

This guide goes through each of the five questions and what the AI needs from you to answer it well.

### Question 1: "Is this still available?"

It usually arrives as a screenshot of your Instagram post with no other words. The agent understands photos customers send, so it can usually tell which of your listed products the customer means and reply with the name, price and sizes. If it is unsure, it asks.

What it needs from you: every product listed with a clear name, its colours and its sizes. One honest limit applies here. Dailzero does not connect to an inventory system, so it knows a dress is sold out only if you update the listing. For fast-moving drops, check your listings at the end of each day.

### Question 2: "What size should I take?"

This is the question that decides a fashion sale, and the one most often answered badly. "We have M and L" does not help a customer who does not know your sizing.

> I'm usually a UK 12 but my hips are big. Will the L fit me?

A reply that converts gives measurements:

> Our L fits bust 38 to 40 inches and hips 42 to 44 inches, and the fabric has a little stretch. If your hips are above 44, the XL will sit better and you can belt the waist. Would you like to send your bust, waist and hip measurements so I can confirm?

What it needs from you: a size chart in inches for each size, plus notes on how each piece fits (true to size, runs small, stretchy, lined). Upload the chart to your media library as an image as well, so the agent can send it.

### Question 3: "How much, and how much is delivery?"

Give the agent the price of each item, any bundle price, and your delivery fee by area: Lagos mainland, the island, Abuja, Port Harcourt, other states by waybill, and international if you ship abroad. If you take a deposit for made-to-order pieces, state the percentage and when the balance is due.

### Question 4: "Can I see more pictures?"

Customers want the back view, the fabric close up, and the outfit on a real person. Put several photos and a short video of each piece in your media library. The agent sends them on request, so you are not digging through your gallery while a customer waits. A photo costs 8 credits to send and a video 12, against 5 for a text reply.

Be honest about colour. Add a line such as "the green is deeper in person than on screen" where it applies. It prevents returns.

### Question 5: "Will it get to me before Saturday?"

Nigerian fashion runs on dates: weddings, owambe, birthdays, Sallah, Christmas, graduation. A customer who is unsure about delivery will not pay.

Give the agent your real timelines: ready-to-wear dispatch time, production time for made-to-order, and your cut-off for weekend delivery. If an order placed on Thursday cannot reach Enugu by Saturday, the agent should say so and offer what can. A lost sale costs less than a ruined event and a public complaint.

### Bespoke and made-to-order: collecting measurements

If you sew to measure, the agent can collect the order details in the chat: name, the style chosen, fabric, bust, waist, hip, shoulder, sleeve and full length, event date and delivery address. It asks for anything left out. You open the chat to find a complete brief instead of six half-answered messages.

Fittings can be booked too. The agent books appointments and sends reminders, which helps if customers visit your studio. The [appointment booking page](/solutions/appointment-booking) shows how.

### After the questions: payment and the handover

When the customer is ready, the agent shares the account details or Paystack link you gave it. It does not verify payment. You confirm the transfer, then book the dispatch rider.

Custom design requests and alterations need you. The agent hands the chat over when the customer asks for a person or when it cannot help.

### Frequently asked questions

#### Can the AI recommend a size from a customer's measurements?

Yes, if you have given it your size chart and fit notes. It compares what the customer sends with your chart. For bespoke pieces it collects the measurements and passes them to you.

#### Can it reply to customers in Instagram DMs?

No. It works on WhatsApp and a website chat widget. Use your bio link and click-to-WhatsApp ads to bring Instagram shoppers into WhatsApp.

#### Can it announce a new collection to past customers?

Yes. You can broadcast to existing customers, at 8 credits per person for an image, and schedule WhatsApp Status posts. Broadcasts go only to people who have messaged you before.
