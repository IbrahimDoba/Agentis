You grow a business with WhatsApp AI through five moves: reply to every enquiry in seconds, send adverts straight to WhatsApp and track which ones bring buyers, bring past customers back with broadcasts, stay visible on Status, and chase the people who went quiet. Each one is a setting or a habit, and each one has a number you can watch.

Below is each move, what to do this week, and what it costs on Dailzero.

### Move 1: stop losing the enquiries you already get

Growth starts with the enquiries you are already paying to attract. Someone who messages three sellers at 10pm tends to buy from the one who answers while they are still holding the phone.

An AI agent replies in seconds, day and night, using the products, prices and policies you gave it.

**This week:** scroll back through last week's chats and count how many waited more than an hour for a first reply. That count is your baseline.

### Move 2: point your adverts at WhatsApp, then measure them

Click-to-WhatsApp adverts on Facebook and Instagram open a chat instead of a website. The problem is that a busy advert can bring 60 chats in an evening, and nobody can answer 60 people properly by hand.

The agent handles the volume. Dailzero also knows which advert each customer came from, so at month end you can compare adverts by the leads and orders they produced, not by likes.

**This week:** run two adverts with different pictures or offers and check which one fills your leads list with people who actually asked to buy.

### Move 3: bring past customers back

Selling again to someone who has already bought from you is usually easier than finding a stranger. A broadcast sends one message to your existing customers, each send paced to protect your number.

Here is the arithmetic. Say a skincare brand in Lekki has 800 past customers.

- A text broadcast to all 800 costs 800 × 5 = 4,000 credits.
- With a product image it costs 800 × 8 = 6,400 credits.
- With a video it costs 800 × 12 = 9,600 credits.

On pay as you go, the ₦10,000 pack gives 6,500 credits, which covers the image broadcast. On the Basic plan, 4,000 credits is 16% of the month's 25,000.

A broadcast that works reads like a message from a shop the customer knows:

> Hello Chioma, the vitamin C serum you asked about is back in stock, ₦14,500. Reply YES and I will reserve one for you.

Broadcasts are for people who have messaged you before. Dailzero does not send to bought lists.

**This week:** send one broadcast with a clear reason, such as new stock, a restock or a payday offer.

### Move 4: show up on Status every day

Dailzero saves the contacts who message you so they can see your Status, and posts Status on a schedule with Autopilot.

Reach per post is up to 2,000 contacts on Basic, 5,000 on Starter and 10,000 on Pro. Unlike a broadcast, nobody feels disturbed by a Status, so it suits daily content: new arrivals, a customer's delivery, today's menu.

**This week:** schedule five Status posts, one for each working day.

### Move 5: follow up with people who went quiet

Plenty of buyers ask the price, say "let me get back to you" and vanish. On the Pro plan, the agent sends automated follow-ups to customers who stopped replying. On other plans, use the leads list in your dashboard and follow up yourself.

**This week:** message the ten most recent people who asked for a price and never ordered.

### A simple 30-day plan

1. **Days 1 to 3.** Start the free trial, add your products, prices, delivery fees and photos, link your number and test it with a friend's phone.
2. **Days 4 to 10.** Let the agent handle live chats. Read a few each evening and fill any gaps in what it knows.
3. **Days 11 to 20.** Turn on one click-to-WhatsApp advert and schedule daily Status posts.
4. **Days 21 to 30.** Send your first broadcast to past customers and review the month's leads by advert.

### What WhatsApp AI cannot do for your growth

It cannot fix a product people do not want. It does not process or verify payments, so you still confirm transfers yourself. It does not know your stock unless you keep it updated or a developer connects your system. And it works on WhatsApp and a website chat widget only, so Instagram DMs still need you.

### Frequently asked questions

#### Which move should I start with if I can only do one?

Move 1. Answering every enquiry quickly costs the least and improves the result of every advert, broadcast and Status post you do afterwards.

#### How much should I budget for a month of this?

Estimate chats per day × replies per chat × 5 credits × 30, then add any broadcasts. The [pricing page](/pricing) lists every plan and pack.

#### Will broadcasting get my number banned?

No tool can promise a number will never be restricted. Dailzero queues every message, sends at a human pace and slows down when WhatsApp signals a limit. Your part is to message only people who know you.
