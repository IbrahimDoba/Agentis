WhatsApp restricts business numbers mainly for three things: people blocking or reporting you, messaging people who never contacted you, and activity that looks automated, such as a new number suddenly sending hundreds of messages. To keep your number safe, message only people who know you, grow your volume slowly, send content people want, and never use modified apps such as GB WhatsApp.

### Why numbers get restricted

WhatsApp does not publish exact limits, so be careful with anyone who quotes a "safe" number of messages a day. What is consistent is the pattern behind most bans:

- **Blocks and reports.** When several recipients block you or tap "Report", your number is flagged quickly. This is the biggest cause.
- **Messaging strangers.** Sending to bought lists, scraped numbers or people from a group who never messaged you.
- **A new number doing too much.** A SIM registered yesterday that messages 300 people today looks like spam, whatever the content.
- **The same message to many people at once**, especially with a link.
- **Modified apps.** GB WhatsApp, WhatsApp Plus and similar break WhatsApp's terms outright.
- **Being added to many groups or adding many people** in a short time.

### Nine habits that keep a number safe

1. **Message people who messaged you first.** Customers who started the chat almost never report you.
2. **Ask customers to save your number.** A saved contact is a strong signal that the conversation is wanted.
3. **Warm up a new number.** Use it normally for about two weeks before any broadcast or automation: chat, reply, post Status.
4. **Grow volume gradually.** Start broadcasts with small batches and increase over days, not hours.
5. **Space your sends.** Seconds apart at least, with pauses, the way a person types.
6. **Vary your messages.** Use the customer's name and avoid sending identical text to everyone.
7. **Give a way out.** "Reply STOP and I will remove you" is better than a block, because a block counts against you.
8. **Keep your business profile complete**: name, category, address, hours and a real photo.
9. **Use the official app.** WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business from the app store, kept up to date.

### Where automation fits, honestly

There are two ways to automate WhatsApp, and they carry different risk.

**The official WhatsApp Business Platform (the API).** This is the route WhatsApp itself provides for automation. You verify your business with Meta, pay Meta's message fees and use approved templates to message people first. If you break the rules you can still be restricted, but automation itself is allowed. It is the safest option and the right one for banks and large senders.

**A linked device, the way WhatsApp Web connects.** Tools such as Dailzero link to the number in your own app and send through it. This costs far less and needs no verification, but WhatsApp does not officially support automating an account this way, so it carries more risk than the official route. How much depends on how the tool behaves.

### If your number is restricted

1. **Stop sending.** Do not try to keep messaging through another tool.
2. **Read the notice.** A temporary restriction shows a timer. Wait it out and send nothing unusual afterwards.
3. **Appeal in the app.** For a ban, tap "Request a review" and explain plainly that this is a business number used to talk to your own customers.
4. **Do not re-register repeatedly** or switch to a modified app. Both make it worse.
5. **When you return, go slower** than before for a few weeks.

### Frequently asked questions

#### How many messages can I send a day without being banned?

WhatsApp does not publish a number, and it depends on your number's age, history and how recipients react. Reports and blocks matter more than the count.

#### Does WhatsApp Business get banned more than normal WhatsApp?

No. The same rules apply to both apps. Business accounts are restricted more often only because they send more messages to more people.

#### Is it safe to connect an AI agent to my main number?

It is lower risk when the agent mostly replies to customers who wrote first and sends at a human pace, as Dailzero does. It is not zero risk. Many owners start on a second number, or on a main number that is at least a few months old.

#### Can a banned number be recovered?

Sometimes. Temporary restrictions lift by themselves. For a ban, use "Request a review" in the app. Nobody outside WhatsApp can restore a number for a fee, so ignore anyone who offers.
