Consultants respond faster to prospects by letting a WhatsApp AI agent send the first reply within seconds while they are with a client, then taking over once the prospect has shared their problem and booked a call. The consultant's own response time does not change. What changes is that the prospect is no longer waiting for it.

A consultant's day is spent in rooms where the phone stays face down. The clearest way to see the difference is to follow one day.

### A Wednesday in Abuja, hour by hour

Picture an HR consultant who works alone. She is running a full-day workshop for a client in Maitama. Her number is on her LinkedIn profile and her slide decks.

**9:00am.** The workshop starts. Phone on silent, face down.

**10:12am.** A message arrives from the operations manager of a logistics company in Port Harcourt:

> Good morning ma. We were referred to you. We need someone to review our staff handbook and salary structure before year end. Do you take on this kind of work?

Without an agent, this sits unread until evening. With one, a reply goes out at 10:12am:

> Good morning, and thank you for reaching out. Yes, handbook reviews and salary structure work are both services we offer. I am the practice's assistant, and I can set up a call with the consultant. To prepare her, roughly how many staff do you have, and is there a date this must be finished by?

**10:15am.** The prospect answers: about 80 staff, board meeting on the 12th of December. The agent offers the call slots the consultant set for the week and the prospect picks Thursday at 4pm.

**1:00pm.** Lunch. She glances at the dashboard: one new lead, call booked. She goes back in.

**2:40pm.** A different message, from a past client:

> Please send me your profile and rate card, my MD wants to see it.

The agent sends both PDFs from the media library. Because it remembers earlier chats, it greets the client as a returning contact.

**4:30pm.** A third prospect asks for a discount on a training programme before anything has been discussed. The agent does not negotiate. It hands the chat over, and the consultant sees it flagged when the workshop ends.

**6:00pm.** In the car, she has two things to deal with: a booked call to prepare for and a discount request to answer personally.

**Thursday 3:00pm.** The agent sends the logistics manager a reminder about the 4pm call.

### What made that day work

Four pieces of setup, done once:

1. **A clear description of services** and who they are for, written the way she explains them in person.
2. **A decision on fees.** She chose to share a rate card for standard training and to keep advisory work as "quoted after a call". The agent follows whichever rule you set.
3. **Call slots.** She gave it the windows she keeps free. The agent works from what you tell it and cannot see your Google or Outlook calendar, so update the slots when your week changes.
4. **Handover topics.** Discounts, anything about a current engagement, and anything the agent is unsure of.

### How much to let the agent say

A consultant sells judgement, so the agent should not perform it. Good boundaries:

- **Yes:** what you do, sectors you serve, how an engagement usually runs, your availability, your documents, published rates.
- **No:** diagnosing the prospect's problem, recommending a solution, estimating the fee for bespoke work, commenting on a client's confidential project.

### When this is the wrong tool

If prospects reach you by email, LinkedIn messages or phone calls, a WhatsApp agent will not see them. Dailzero works on WhatsApp and a website chat widget only, with no voice calls. Set it up only if a real share of first contact reaches you on WhatsApp.

### Frequently asked questions

#### Will a senior prospect be put off by an AI assistant?

Possibly, but long silence puts people off too. Keep the assistant brief and courteous, and let it say you are with a client and will speak to them personally on the booked call.

#### Can I still reply myself when I am free?

Yes. Reply from your phone as usual. The AI pauses on that chat as soon as you do, and you switch it back on when you want it to resume.

#### Do I need a separate business number?

No. It links to the WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business number you already use. If that is also your personal number, consider a dedicated business line first, since the agent replies on the number it is linked to.
