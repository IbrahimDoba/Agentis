Yes, a modern WhatsApp AI agent can usually read Pidgin text and can handle voice notes if the tool turns them into text first. How well it does depends on the tool and on how your customers speak, and no honest vendor can give you one accuracy figure that holds for every accent and every mix of languages. The practical answer is to test it with your own customers' messages before you rely on it.

### Typed Pidgin

Nigerian Pidgin is close to English and is written all over the internet, so the language models behind AI agents generally follow it. A message like "abeg how much be the black one" or "una dey deliver reach Warri?" is not hard for a good agent.

It gets harder when:

- The message is heavy slang or very short, such as "last price?" with no product named
- Pidgin is mixed with Yoruba, Igbo or Hausa in one sentence
- The customer refers to something outside the chat, such as "that one wey my sister buy"

In those cases a good agent asks a short question back instead of guessing.

### Voice notes

An AI agent does not listen to a voice note the way you do. The tool first transcribes the audio into text, then the AI answers the text. So the result depends on the transcription.

Voice notes usually work well when the speaker is clear and the room is quiet. They are less reliable with:

- Loud background noise, such as a market, a generator or a danfo
- Heavy code-switching between languages in one note
- Product names, street names and people's names, which transcription can spell wrongly
- Very long notes that cover several topics

A wrong transcription leads to a wrong answer, so decide what the agent should do when it is unsure. The safe rule is: ask the customer to confirm, or hand over to a person.

### Images

Many customers send a screenshot from your Instagram page and write "how much?". An agent that can read images can use that photo to work out which product the customer means. Check that your tool supports this, and test it with photos of your real products.

### How to test it with your own customers

1. **Collect real messages.** Scroll through last month's chats and copy ten messages written the way your customers really write.
2. **Collect real voice notes.** Pick five, including one recorded somewhere noisy.
3. **Send them from a second phone** to your business number during a trial.
4. **Check three things for each.** Did it understand the question? Was the answer correct? Was the tone right for your brand?
5. **Note where it asked for clarification.** That is good behaviour, not a failure.
6. **Fix your instructions.** If it missed a common phrase, add a line that explains it, such as "customers say 'aso ebi' for group fabric orders".

### When to keep a human on it

Some chats should go to a person however good the agent is:

- Complaints and refund requests
- A voice note the agent could not follow after one try
- Bargaining, where you decide the last price
- Anything about money already paid

### Frequently asked questions

#### Can a WhatsApp AI reply in Pidgin?

It can if you tell it to in its instructions. Many businesses keep replies in simple English with a friendly tone, because every customer understands that. Choose the voice your customers expect from you.

#### Does the AI understand Yoruba, Igbo and Hausa?

Support differs between tools and between text and voice. Dailzero's pricing page lists English on Basic and Starter and English plus any language on Pro. Test with real messages in the language your customers use before you decide.

#### Does a voice note cost more than a text message?

On Dailzero, yes. A voice note costs 3 credits a second with a minimum of 15, while a typical text reply costs about 5 credits.

#### What happens if the AI does not understand a voice note?

A well set up agent asks the customer to type the message or repeats what it understood for the customer to confirm. You can also step in and reply yourself.

#### How accurate is it?

Nobody can give you one honest number. It depends on the accent, the noise and the mix of languages. Dailzero does not publish an accuracy figure. A trial with your own customers' messages will tell you more than any percentage.
