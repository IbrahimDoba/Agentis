A WhatsApp sales funnel for a Nigerian online store has five stages: get the click, reply at once, collect the order, take payment, and bring the customer back. Build each as its own step, count how many people make it through each one, and fix the weakest step first.

The example running through this guide is a sneaker store in Yaba that advertises on Instagram.

### Stage 1: get the click into WhatsApp

Every source of attention should end at the same place, your WhatsApp chat.

- **Click-to-WhatsApp ads** on Facebook and Instagram, where the button opens a chat instead of a website.
- **A WhatsApp link** in your Instagram and TikTok bio.
- **WhatsApp Status**, seen by people who have your number saved and whose numbers you have saved.

If you run more than one advert, you need to know which one sells. Dailzero shows which Facebook or Instagram click-to-WhatsApp ad each customer came from, so you can stop funding the advert that only brings "how much?" and nothing else.

### Stage 2: reply at once

Someone who taps an advert is curious for a few minutes. The first message from an ad click is often short and vague:

> Hello! Can I get more info on this?

They have not said which shoe or what size. A good first reply narrows it down and gives a reason to continue:

> Hello, welcome. That is the white low-top at ₦38,000, available in sizes 40 to 45. Here is a closer look. What size do you wear, and where should we deliver to?

A photo goes with it. An AI agent sends this within seconds, at any hour, from the product details and media you uploaded.

### Stage 3: collect the order

Interest becomes an order when the details are written down. Tell the agent exactly what to collect:

1. Item, size and colour
2. Quantity
3. Full name and a phone number the rider can call
4. Delivery address with a landmark
5. Preferred delivery day

The agent asks for these in conversation, fills gaps, then repeats the order with the total, including the delivery fee for that area.

### Stage 4: take payment

Share one clear way to pay. The agent sends the bank account details or Paystack link you gave it and asks the customer to send proof.

Here the funnel passes to a person, and you should plan for that. Dailzero does not process or verify payments and cannot see your bank. You check the alert, confirm in the chat, and book the rider. The AI pauses on that chat while you reply.

Two things reduce drop-off at this stage:

- **State your pay-on-delivery rule before the customer asks.** If you allow it only in parts of Lagos, or only after a delivery fee is paid upfront, put it in your policies.
- **Confirm payments quickly.** A customer who paid and heard nothing for three hours starts to panic. Set times in the day when someone clears payment chats.

### Stage 5: follow up and bring them back

The funnel does not stop at the sale.

- **Quiet prospects.** On the Pro plan, the agent sends automated follow-ups to people who asked and stopped replying. On other plans, the leads list in the dashboard shows who was interested, so you can message them yourself.
- **Past buyers.** Broadcast new arrivals to existing customers. A text costs 5 credits per person and an image 8, so a photo of a new release to 400 past customers is 400 × 8 = 3,200 credits, the size of a ₦5,000 pay-as-you-go pack.
- **Everyone who has your number.** Schedule Status posts for restocks and new colours. Dailzero saves the contacts who message you so they can see your Status.

Broadcasts go only to people who have messaged you before, never to bought lists.

### Measure the funnel with four counts

Each week, write down:

1. Chats started
2. Chats where the customer gave a size and address
3. Payments confirmed
4. Orders from returning customers

Divide each number by the one above it. The step with the biggest fall is the one to fix. If many people start a chat but few give an address, look at your first reply, your prices and your delivery fees. If many give an address but few pay, look at trust and payment options.

### Frequently asked questions

#### Do I need a website to build a WhatsApp sales funnel?

No. Ads, bio links and Status can all send people straight into a WhatsApp chat, and the order can be completed there.

#### Can the whole funnel run without me?

Stages 2, 3 and 5 can run on the agent. Stage 1 needs your content or adverts, and stage 4 needs a person to confirm payment.

#### How do I know which advert is bringing sales?

For click-to-WhatsApp ads on Facebook and Instagram, Dailzero records which ad a customer came from. Compare that with the chats that ended in payment.
