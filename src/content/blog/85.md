A WhatsApp AI agent is software that reads the messages customers send to your business WhatsApp number and writes replies by itself, using the products, prices, hours and policies you have given it. Unlike an auto-reply, it understands the question and answers that specific question. For a Nigerian business, it helps most by replying in seconds at any hour, so fewer people who ask "how much?" drift off to another seller.

### How it differs from an auto-reply or a chatbot

People use these words loosely, so here is the difference.

| Feature | Auto-reply | Menu chatbot | AI agent |
|---|---|---|---|
| What it sends | One fixed message | Options to pick: "Reply 1 for prices" | A written answer to the actual question |
| Understands free typing | No | Only set keywords | Yes, including informal phrasing |
| Handles follow-up questions | No | Only along the menu | Yes |
| Can take an order | No | In a rigid flow | Yes, by asking for what is missing |

The greeting and away messages in the WhatsApp Business app are auto-replies. They are free and worth switching on, but they cannot answer anything.

### What it looks like in a real chat

Say you sell generators and inverters in Port Harcourt. A customer writes at 9pm:

> Abeg which inverter fit carry fridge, TV and 4 bulbs? And how much?

An AI agent that has your product list replies with the model that suits that load, its price, whether installation is included, and offers to send a photo. If the customer says "send am", it sends the photo from your media library. If they ask about delivery to Bonny, it gives the fee you set, or hands the chat to you if you never told it.

### What it cannot do

- **It does not confirm payments.** It can share your account details or Paystack link, then pass the chat to you to check the transfer.
- **It cannot see your stock, bank or delivery status by itself.** It works from what you typed in. Live lookups need a developer to connect your system.
- **It is WhatsApp only**, plus a chat widget for your website. No Instagram DMs, SMS, email or phone calls.
- **It is not the official WhatsApp Business Platform.** There is no green verified badge and no Meta per-conversation fee.
- **It does not replace judgment.** Refunds, complaints and unusual requests should go to a person.

### How it helps, in plain terms

**You stop losing the fast buyers.** Customers often message more than one seller and buy from whoever answers first with a clear price.

**You get your evenings back.** The questions that arrive after closing get answered, and you read a summary in the morning instead of replying at midnight.

**Your answers become consistent.** Every customer gets the same price and the same delivery terms, not whatever a tired staff member remembers.

**You can see your leads.** Instead of scrolling through chats, you get a list of people who showed interest.

None of this is automatic. An agent given thin or wrong information will give thin or wrong answers.

### Which businesses benefit most

It suits you if customers already message you on WhatsApp, most of their questions repeat, and you have clear prices and policies. Online shops, restaurants, clinics, salons and estate agents commonly fit this pattern.

It is a weaker fit if you get only a few messages a week, if every sale is a long custom negotiation, or if you need the official API for very high volume or a verified badge.

### Frequently asked questions

#### Is a WhatsApp AI agent the same as a chatbot?

Not quite. A traditional chatbot follows a fixed menu or keywords. An AI agent understands free-typed messages and writes its own reply from your business information.

#### Do I need a new phone number?

No. The agent runs on the WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business number you already use. Your phone stays the main device.

#### Will it understand Pidgin?

It copes with everyday Nigerian English and Pidgin phrasing in customer messages and replies in English. Replies in other languages are a Pro plan feature on Dailzero.

#### Can I still reply to customers myself?

Yes. Reply from your phone or the dashboard at any time. The AI pauses on that chat until you switch it back on.
