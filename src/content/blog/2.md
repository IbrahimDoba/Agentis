There is no single best WhatsApp AI agent for every Nigerian financial institution. The right choice depends on one question first: do you need the official WhatsApp Business Platform or not. Commercial banks and large wallets almost always do. Small lenders, cooperatives, microfinance banks and early-stage fintechs often do not, and can start much faster and cheaper without it.

This guide groups the options into three types and says who each one suits.

### The three types of tool

| Type | How it connects | Suits | Main trade-off |
|---|---|---|---|
| Official API platform with an AI layer | WhatsApp Business Platform, through a provider or Meta's Cloud API | Banks, large wallets, high volume | Meta charges per message, templates need approval, setup takes longer |
| Custom bot built by your own engineers | Usually the official API plus your core banking system | Institutions with a strong tech team | Costs engineering time to build and to maintain |
| Linked-device AI agent | Your existing number, linked like WhatsApp Web | Small and mid-sized finance businesses | No verified badge, one number per agent |

### Type 1: official API platforms

Providers such as Infobip, Twilio and Gupshup give you access to the official WhatsApp Business Platform, and tools such as WATI and Respond.io build shared inboxes and chatbot features on top of it. Many now include AI replies.

Choose this route if you need a green verified badge so customers can tell you from impersonators, if many officers must work one number, if you send large volumes of alerts and OTP-style notifications, or if your compliance team insists on an official channel.

### Type 2: a custom bot on your own stack

Several Nigerian banks have run WhatsApp banking for years, where a customer can check a balance or buy airtime inside the chat. That kind of transactional banking needs deep, secure integration with a core banking system, strong customer authentication and a security review. If that is your goal, budget for a team, not a subscription.

### Type 3: linked-device AI agents

This is where Dailzero sits. You scan a QR code or enter a pairing code, and the AI answers customers on the number you already use. There are no Meta per-conversation fees and no template approval.

What it does for a finance business:

- Answers general questions day and night from the fees, limits, requirements and policies you give it.
- Sends documents from a media library, for example a loan requirements sheet or an agent onboarding form.
- Understands voice notes and photos, such as a screenshot of an error.
- Collects the details an officer needs, then hands the chat to a person when the customer asks or when the AI cannot help.
- Lists people showing buying intent as leads in the dashboard on every plan.
- Lets a developer connect up to 50 webhook tools so the agent can query your own backend mid-chat.

What it does not do: it does not process or verify payments, it has no access to customer accounts unless a developer connects them, it makes no voice calls, and it does not give you a verified badge. For loans and banking topics it answers general questions and passes account-specific or fraud cases to a human.

### A quick decision guide

- **You are a commercial bank or a wallet with millions of users.** Go official API, with a platform or your own build. Dailzero is not the right fit.
- **You are a microfinance bank, cooperative, lender, insurance broker or bureau de change with one or two support numbers.** A linked-device agent is usually enough, and you can be live the same day.
- **You are a fintech startup with a developer and a modest support load.** Start with a linked-device agent plus webhook tools, and move to the official API when volume or compliance demands it.
- **You need deep integration and have no developer.** Neither route will give you account lookups. Use the AI for general questions and lead capture only.

### Questions to ask any vendor

1. Is this the official API or a linked device, and what does that mean for my number?
2. What exactly can the AI see about my customers?
3. How does a chat reach a human, and what happens when my credits or quota run out?

### Frequently asked questions

#### Can a Nigerian bank use Dailzero?

A commercial bank that needs a verified badge and enterprise-scale volume should use the official WhatsApp Business Platform instead. Dailzero suits smaller finance businesses and teams within larger ones that run an ordinary WhatsApp line.

#### Do linked-device agents risk my number?

Dailzero queues every message and sends at a human pace, warms up new numbers and slows down when WhatsApp signals a limit. No tool can promise a number will never be restricted.

#### Which option is cheapest to start?

A linked-device agent, because there is no setup fee and no Meta fee. Dailzero has a 7-day free trial with 1,000 credits, described on the [finance solutions page](/solutions/finance).
