For most Nigerian fintechs an AI agent is the better choice for customer questions and sales inquiries, because customers type freely and a menu chatbot only understands the options it was given. A menu chatbot is still the better choice for a small set of fixed, regulated flows where every customer must see exactly the same words. Many teams end up using each for what it does well.

### The difference in one example

Take a single customer message:

> I did transfer to GTB since yesterday, e never enter and dem don debit me. Wetin I go do?

**A menu chatbot** replies: "Welcome. Reply 1 for Account Opening, 2 for Loans, 3 for Cards, 4 to speak to an agent." The customer's actual problem is ignored. They press 4, or they leave angry.

**An AI agent** reads the message, understands it is a failed interbank transfer with a debit, apologises, explains your general reversal policy as you wrote it, asks for the date, amount and a receipt screenshot, and passes the chat to a support officer with those details attached.

### Side by side

| | Menu chatbot | AI agent |
|---|---|---|
| Understands free typing, Pidgin, typos | Poorly | Yes |
| Reads screenshots and voice notes | No | Yes |
| Wording of replies | Identical every time | Varies, within your instructions |
| Handles a question you did not plan for | Falls back to the menu | Tries from what it knows, or hands over |
| Setup | Draw every branch | Describe the business, add FAQs |
| Upkeep when products change | Redraw branches | Edit the information |

### When a menu chatbot is the right tool

It wins when:

- **The flow is fixed and regulated.** Consent wording, disclosures or a complaint-logging sequence that must read the same for every customer.
- **You need structured input.** "Enter your 10-digit account number" followed by a system check is a form, not a conversation.
- **You run on the official WhatsApp Business Platform** and want buttons, lists and approved templates. Those features belong to Meta's official API.
- **Your compliance team will not accept generated wording** for a given topic. That is a legitimate position.

### When an AI agent is the right tool

- Customers ask the same things in a hundred different ways.
- Leads come from ads and need a real answer before they lose interest.
- You want cover at night and weekends without staffing a shift.
- Your products change often and you do not want to maintain a tree.
- Customers send screenshots of error messages and expect you to understand them.

### The risks of an AI agent, and how to limit them

An AI agent composes its replies, so the instructions you give it matter. For a fintech, write these down clearly:

1. It answers general questions only, from the information you supplied.
2. It never asks for a PIN, OTP, password or card details.
3. It never promises a refund, an approval or a timeline that is not in your policy.
4. It hands over anything about a specific account, a dispute or suspected fraud.
5. A named person reviews chats weekly and corrects any answer that drifted.

### A sensible split for a fintech

- **AI agent:** product questions, requirements, fees, how-to steps in the app, lead capture, first response on complaints.
- **Fixed flow or your app:** identity checks, consent, anything that changes an account.
- **Humans:** disputes, fraud, exceptions, credit decisions.

If a developer is available, webhook tools let the agent call your backend mid-chat, for example to fetch an application status from your system, which closes some of the gap between the two approaches. ### Frequently asked questions

#### Is an AI agent just a smarter chatbot?

In everyday speech people use both words loosely. The practical difference is that a rule-based chatbot follows a script you drew, while an AI agent understands free text and composes replies from the information you gave it.

#### Can an AI agent give wrong information?

It can phrase something poorly if your information is incomplete or out of date. Keep its answers limited to what you supplied, set clear handover rules, and review chats regularly.

#### Can I switch from a menu chatbot to an AI agent on the same number?

A WhatsApp number can only be run one way at a time. If your current bot is on the official API, moving that number to a linked-device tool means taking it off the API first, so many teams start the AI agent on a second number.
