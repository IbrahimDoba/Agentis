### Frequently asked questions

#### Which WhatsApp AI agent is cheapest in Nigeria?

It depends on your volume. Neria and Zella list the lowest starting prices we saw in November 2026. Dailzero's pay as you go starts at ₦1,000 with no subscription. Tools on the official platform also pass on Meta's message fees, so compare the total.

#### Which ones work on my existing WhatsApp number?

Dailzero links to the number already in your app, and it keeps working in your phone. Tools on the official platform register your number with Meta. Ask each provider what happens to your app and chats.

#### Do all of these charge in Naira?

No. Dailzero, Neria, ChatRouter, Evolve and Zella price in Naira. WATI and respond.io price in US dollars.

#### Is a linked device or the official API better?

The official API is the safest route and suits regulated or large senders. A linked device is faster and cheaper and keeps your number in your app, with more risk. Many small businesses start on a linked device.
