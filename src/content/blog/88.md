You stop missing WhatsApp inquiries by closing the specific gaps where they get lost: nights, rush hours, ad traffic, Status replies and the days your phone is not in your hand. An AI agent on your number closes most of those gaps because it answers every message in seconds. No tool closes all of them, so the last part of this guide covers the gaps that remain and how to plug them yourself.

### Gap 1: messages that arrive while you sleep

A customer in Abuja browsing at midnight sends "Is this still available?" By 8am she has bought elsewhere.

**Fix:** an agent that replies around the clock with the price and availability you gave it, and asks the next question, such as size or delivery area. The free away message in the WhatsApp Business app only says you are closed.

### Gap 2: the rush you cannot type through

Friday afternoon, a new post goes up and thirty people message in an hour. You answer the first ten properly and find the rest on Monday.

**Fix:** an AI agent answers all the chats at once.

### Gap 3: ad clicks that get no reply

You are paying for click-to-WhatsApp ads on Facebook or Instagram. Each unanswered chat is ad money spent on nothing.

**Fix:** instant replies, plus knowing where the lead came from. Dailzero records which ad a customer clicked, so you can see which ads bring serious buyers. See [capturing leads on WhatsApp](/solutions/lead-generation).

### Gap 4: the chat that started but never finished

Not every missed inquiry is an unread message. Many are chats where you answered, the customer went quiet, and nobody returned to it.

> Ok. Let me check with my husband and get back to you.

That customer may never come back unprompted.

**Fix:** follow up. On Dailzero's Pro plan the agent sends automated follow-ups to customers who went quiet. On other plans, the dashboard lists people who showed buying intent as leads, so you can go down the list and message them yourself.

### Gap 5: half an inquiry

Someone says "I want two", you say "ok", and three hours later you realise you never got the address. The inquiry was not missed, but the order was.

**Fix:** have the agent collect the details every time: name, address, size, quantity, preferred delivery day. It does not forget to ask.

### Gap 6: voice notes and photos you put off

A customer sends a 90-second voice note or a screenshot of a product from someone else's page asking "do you have this?" These are slow to deal with, so they wait.

**Fix:** the agent understands voice notes and photos customers send, so it can respond to them like any other message.

### Gap 7: more than one person replying

Two staff share the phone. Each assumes the other replied.

**Fix:** give team members their own logins and let the agent answer first. When a chat needs a person, it is handed over.

### The gaps an AI agent does not close

"Never" needs honesty. These gaps remain.

- **Your credits run out.** The AI pauses. Messages still arrive in WhatsApp but nobody is answering automatically. Watch your balance before a promo.
- **Your phone stays offline too long.** Dailzero links to your number the way WhatsApp Web does. Your phone is the main device and should come online regularly. A phone that stays dead or out of data for days can drop the link.
- **WhatsApp restricts your number.** Sending is paced to reduce the risk, but no tool can promise it will never happen.
- **The handoff nobody picks up.** When the agent passes a complaint or a payment confirmation to you, the inquiry is now waiting on a human. If nobody checks, it is missed all the same.
- **Inquiries on other channels.** The agent covers WhatsApp and a website chat widget. Instagram DMs, Facebook comments and missed phone calls are outside it.
- **Questions it was never taught.** If you did not tell it your delivery fee to Benin, it cannot invent one. It hands off, which is correct but slower.

### A ten minute daily check

1. Open the dashboard and clear the handed-off chats first.
2. Go through yesterday's leads and message anyone who went quiet.
3. Check your credit balance.
4. Read two full conversations and fix any answer that was vague.
5. Make sure the phone is charged and online.

### How to tell if it is working

Before you start, count how many chats in the past week waited more than an hour for a first reply. Count again after two weeks.

### Frequently asked questions

#### Does the AI reply when my phone is switched off?

It can keep replying while your phone is offline for a while, as with WhatsApp Web, but the phone needs to come online regularly to keep the link active.

#### Can it follow up with people who stopped replying?

Automated follow-ups are part of the Pro plan. On other plans, use the leads list in the dashboard to follow up by hand.

#### Will it catch inquiries from Instagram or Facebook?

Only when the person lands in WhatsApp, for example through a click-to-WhatsApp ad or the link in your bio. It does not read Instagram DMs or Messenger.
