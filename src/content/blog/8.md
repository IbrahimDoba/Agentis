You can automate the first reply to nearly every loan inquiry on WhatsApp, because borrowers ask the same handful of questions: how much, what rate, what do I need, how fast. An AI agent should answer those from your published terms and collect the applicant's details. It should never approve, decline or promise a loan. That decision stays with your credit officer.

### The questions borrowers actually send

Scroll any Nigerian lender's WhatsApp and the pattern is the same.

- How much can I borrow?
- What is your interest?
- Do you need collateral or a guarantor?
- I am not a salary earner, can I still apply?
- How long before the money enters my account?
- What happens if I pay late?

Each has one correct answer in your credit policy. Write those answers down once, in the words a customer would understand, and you have covered most of the inbox.

### What a good automated reply looks like

Say you lend ₦50,000 to ₦500,000 to salary earners in Port Harcourt. These figures are an illustration. Use your own.

A borrower writes:

> Good afternoon. I need 200k urgently for my shop rent. Wetin be una interest and how fast I fit get am?

A weak reply says "Kindly fill our form" and drops a link. A good reply answers both questions, then moves the application forward:

> Good afternoon. For ₦200,000 over 3 months, our interest is X% per month, so you would repay ₦Y in total. Once your documents are complete, approved loans are paid out within 24 hours. To check if you qualify, please tell me: are you a salary earner or self-employed, and how much do you earn in a month?

The X and Y come from the terms you gave the agent. Give it a repayment table for your common amounts and tenors so it quotes exact totals instead of doing the sums loosely.

### What to load into the agent

1. **Your product terms.** Minimum and maximum amount, tenors, interest, every fee, the penalty for late payment, and early repayment rules.
2. **Eligibility.** Who you lend to, states you cover, minimum income, whether you accept traders and business owners.
3. **The document list.** For example a valid ID, recent bank statements, an employment letter, a guarantor form. Upload the forms to the media library so the AI can send them. A document costs 8 credits to send.
4. **The process and timeline.** What happens after applying, and how long each stage normally takes.
5. **Repayment details.** The account or payment link customers should use. Dailzero does not process or verify payments. It shares the details you provide and hands the chat to you to confirm a payment.
6. **The details to collect.** Name, location, employer or business type, monthly income, amount and tenor wanted.

### Rules that keep you out of trouble

Lending is regulated, and a careless chat reply is still a statement from your company. Instruct the agent plainly:

- **No approvals or guarantees.** "You qualify to apply" is fine. "Your loan is approved" is not, unless a human said so.
- **No rates outside your published terms.** No discounts invented to close a deal.
- **No sensitive data in chat.** BVN, card details and bank statements go through your secure application channel. The AI tells the borrower where.
- **No debt pressure.** Repayment reminders should be polite and factual. Anything about default, restructuring or a dispute goes to a person.
- **No advice.** Whether someone should borrow is their decision. The AI states your terms and stops.

### From inquiry to a credit officer's desk

People showing real intent are flagged as leads in the Dailzero dashboard on every plan. Your credit officer starts the day with a list of applicants and the chat where each one stated an amount, income and employer, instead of two hundred unread "how much is your interest" messages.

Borrowers who went quiet after seeing the document list are common. On the Pro plan, automated follow-ups message them again. A short nudge such as "Do you still need the ₦200,000? I can resend the requirements" is enough.

### Frequently asked questions

#### Can an AI agent approve loans on WhatsApp?

No. It answers questions and gathers details. The credit decision belongs to your officers and your scoring process.

#### Can it tell a borrower their outstanding balance?

Only if a developer connects your loan system through webhook tools. Otherwise it collects the borrower's details and passes the chat to your team.

#### Is this suitable for a registered digital lender?

Yes for inquiries and lead capture, as long as your disclosures meet your regulator's rules. The [finance solutions page](/solutions/finance) shows the setup.
