### First, where do your sales really close?

Most Instagram vendors work in one of two ways.

- **Instagram shows, WhatsApp sells.** Your bio link, Story stickers and ads all say "DM us on WhatsApp". The questions, the order and the payment happen in WhatsApp. An AI on WhatsApp covers almost everything.
- **Instagram shows and sells.** Customers ask prices in DMs and comments and often never open WhatsApp. You need a tool that answers inside Instagram.

Be honest about which one you are. Check your last 30 orders and count where each conversation happened.

### A simple setup for Instagram to WhatsApp sellers

1. Put a WhatsApp link in your Instagram bio and on every Story that shows a product.
2. Add your products, prices, sizes and delivery fees to the AI agent.
3. Link your WhatsApp number and test with real questions.
4. In Instagram DMs, send one saved reply that moves the buyer to WhatsApp, or use a DM tool if DM volume is high.
5. Ask every buyer to save your number so they see your Status.

Some vendors use two tools: a DM tool for Instagram and Dailzero for WhatsApp. That works, but it means two bills, so only do it if both channels are busy.

### Frequently asked questions

#### Does Dailzero work with Instagram?

No. Dailzero works on WhatsApp. It recognises customers who arrive from an Instagram or Facebook click-to-WhatsApp ad, but it does not read or answer Instagram DMs or comments.

#### Which tool answers Instagram DMs for Nigerian vendors?

Zella, Sowo, Neria, WATI and respond.io all list Instagram on their own sites as of November 2026. ManyChat is also widely used for this.

#### Should I move customers from Instagram to WhatsApp?

Many Nigerian vendors do, because customers are comfortable paying and sending addresses there, and a saved contact sees your Status. If your buyers prefer DMs, serve them in DMs.

#### Can I use Dailzero and an Instagram DM tool together?

Yes. They work on different apps and do not conflict. You will pay for both.

Dailzero has a 7-day free trial with 1,000 credits, about 200 AI replies. Several tools above list their own trials.
