Dailzero has three monthly plans in Naira: Basic at ₦20,000, Starter at ₦40,000 and Pro at ₦75,000. Each plan gives you a monthly allowance of credits, and a typical AI text reply uses about 5 credits. You can also skip the subscription and pay as you go from ₦1,000. Every new account starts with a 7-day free trial, and there are no overage fees.

### What every paid plan includes

- An AI agent on your own WhatsApp number that answers from your products and prices
- Conversation logs and lead detection
- Customer memory, so the agent remembers returning customers
- Pay as you go top-ups if you need more credits in a month

Starter adds conversation analytics. Pro adds a second agent, five team seats and priority email support. The full list is on the [pricing page](/pricing).

### What each credit costs you

Divide the price by the credits:

- **Basic:** ₦20,000 ÷ 25,000 = ₦0.80 a credit, about ₦4.00 a text reply
- **Starter:** ₦40,000 ÷ 60,000 = about ₦0.67 a credit, about ₦3.33 a text reply
- **Pro:** ₦75,000 ÷ 100,000 = ₦0.75 a credit, about ₦3.75 a text reply

Starter has the lowest cost per credit. Pro costs a little more per credit because you are also paying for the second agent and the extra team seats. If you only need more credits and not a second agent, Starter plus a top-up can be the cheaper mix.

### How to pick a plan

1. Count the customers who message you on a normal day.
2. Multiply by the AI replies a chat needs. Six is a fair average.
3. Multiply by 5 credits, then by 30 days.
4. Choose the plan whose allowance covers that number.

**Example:** 40 chats a day × 6 replies = 240 replies a day. 240 × 5 credits = 1,200 credits a day. 1,200 × 30 days = 36,000 credits a month. That is above Basic and inside Starter, with 24,000 credits spare for images, Status posts and a broadcast.

### What happens when credits finish

Your monthly plan credits are used first. After that, sends draw from any pay as you go balance. When both are empty, the agent pauses until you top up or the plan renews. Nothing is billed for going over.

### Unused credits

On a monthly plan, unused credits carry into the next cycle, up to 25% of your plan's allowance, for one cycle. On Basic that cap is 6,250 credits. On Starter it is 15,000 and on Pro 25,000.

### Paying, switching and refunds

- Payment is in Naira through Paystack, by card or bank transfer.
- Monthly plans renew automatically on the payment method you choose.
- Upgrades take effect immediately. Downgrades apply at the start of your next billing cycle.
- If you are not satisfied within your first 7 days of a paid plan, you get a full refund.

### What is not in the price

Dailzero's default connection links your number like WhatsApp Web, so there are no Meta message fees. If you ask for the official WhatsApp Business Platform route, Meta's own message fees apply on that route.

### Frequently asked questions

#### How much is Dailzero a month?

₦20,000 for Basic, ₦40,000 for Starter and ₦75,000 for Pro. Pay as you go starts from ₦1,000 with no monthly commitment.

#### Does Dailzero charge overage fees?

No. When your plan credits and any pay as you go balance are used up, the agent pauses. You are never billed for going over.

#### Is there a setup fee?

No. You sign up, set up your agent in the dashboard and link your number yourself.

#### Can I change plans later?

Yes. You can move between Basic, Starter and Pro at any time. Upgrades apply at once and downgrades apply from your next billing cycle.

#### Do I need a card for the free trial?

No. The 7-day free trial needs no credit card.
