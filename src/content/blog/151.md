To connect Click-to-WhatsApp ads to an AI agent, you point the ad at a WhatsApp number that has an agent on it. When a customer taps the ad on Facebook or Instagram and sends the first message, WhatsApp attaches the ad's details to that message. Dailzero reads the ad's headline and description, so the agent greets the customer about the product they clicked. There is nothing extra to switch on.

### What a Click-to-WhatsApp ad is

It is a Facebook or Instagram ad whose button opens a WhatsApp chat with your business, with no website in between. The weak point is what happens next. The customer sends a pre-filled message such as "Hello, can I get more info on this?". If nobody answers for an hour, you paid for that click and lost the customer.

### How to run and answer these ads by hand

1. **Connect your WhatsApp number to your Facebook Page.** In WhatsApp Business this is under Business tools, in the Facebook and Instagram section.
2. **Create the ad.** Boost a post from your Page or use Meta Ads Manager, and choose WhatsApp as the place people should message you. The button reads "Send WhatsApp message" or similar.
3. **Write the ad for one product or one offer.** A specific ad brings specific questions.
4. **Set the pre-filled message** so the customer's first line tells you what they want.
5. **Set a greeting message** in WhatsApp Business so every new chat gets an instant first reply.
6. **Answer fast.** When an ad chat arrives, WhatsApp shows a small preview of the ad above the first message, so you can see which ad the customer came from.

This works when someone is at the phone, but ads run round the clock.

### How to connect the ads to a Dailzero agent

Dailzero links to the WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business number you already use, the way WhatsApp Web does. The ad still points at your number. The agent answers on it.

1. **Create your agent** and add your products with names, prices, descriptions and photos. Add delivery fees and payment details to its instructions.
2. **Link your number** in the dashboard by QR code or pairing code. Use the same number your ads send people to.
3. **Run your Click-to-WhatsApp ad as usual** in Meta's tools. You do not change anything in the ad to make this work.
4. **Send a test.** Tap your own ad from another phone and send the first message.
5. **Open Conversations in the dashboard** and select the chat. A banner at the top of the chat reads "From ad" with the ad's headline, its description, the time the customer clicked and a link back to the ad.

### What the agent does with the ad

| Step | What happens |
|---|---|
| First message arrives | The ad's headline, description and source link are captured automatically |
| The greeting | The agent is told how the customer arrived and does not open with a generic "How can I help?" |
| Next few messages | It talks about the product or offer in the ad and moves to the next step: price, photos, order or booking |
| After the opening exchanges | The ad context drops out, so the agent does not keep bringing it up |
| A later ad click | The chat keeps the first ad it came from |

If a customer taps your ad for a ₦45,000 ankara gown and sends "Hello, can I get more info on this?", the agent answers about that gown, not about your whole shop. It works for ads shown on Facebook, Instagram and Messenger placements.

### Write the ad so the agent can use it

The agent reads the ad's headline and description. It does not see the picture or video in the ad. So:

- **Name the product in the headline.** "Red Ankara Gown, ₦45,000" tells the agent what the customer wants. "Shop now, limited offer" does not.
- **Keep the ad and the catalogue in step.** The product in the ad should be in your agent's product list at the same price.
- **One ad, one product or offer.** A carousel of ten items under a vague headline leaves the agent guessing, and it will have to ask.

### What Dailzero does not do here

- It does not create, manage or pay for your ads. You still do that in Meta's tools and pay Meta for them.
- It does not report ad spend or cost per sale. You can see which chats came from which ad in Conversations, and you compare that with Meta's figures yourself.

### Costs

There is no extra charge for ad context. Replies to ad customers are billed like any other AI reply, about 5 credits for a typical text message, and on the linked-device route there are no Meta message fees. Your ad budget is separate and goes to Meta. When credits finish the agent pauses, with no overage fees. See [pricing](/pricing) and [features](/features).

### Frequently asked questions

#### Do I need the WhatsApp Business API to run Click-to-WhatsApp ads?

No. These ads can point at a number on the WhatsApp Business app. Dailzero then links to that same number, so no API setup is needed for the agent to answer.

#### How does the AI know which ad the customer clicked?

WhatsApp attaches the ad's details to the first message a customer sends from the ad. Dailzero captures the headline and description and gives them to the agent for the opening of the chat.

#### Do I have to set anything up in Dailzero for each new ad?

No. Ad details are captured automatically on the first message. Just make sure the product in the ad is in your agent's product list.

#### Can I see which chats came from an ad?

Yes. In Conversations, a chat that started from an ad shows a "From ad" banner with the headline, description and time of the click.

#### Does the agent keep mentioning the ad?

No. It uses the ad for the first few messages, then treats the chat like any other conversation.
