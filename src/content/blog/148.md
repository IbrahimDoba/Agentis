You can take orders on WhatsApp automatically in two ways. The free way is WhatsApp Business: a catalogue, the cart, quick replies and labels, with you still typing the confirmations. The fuller way is an AI agent on your number that answers product questions from your own prices, sends photos, collects the order details and passes the chat to you when a person is needed. Dailzero does the second on the number you already use.

### What "taking an order" really involves

On WhatsApp an order is a conversation: the customer asks what is available, the price and the delivery fee, wants photos, says what they want, gives a name and address, then pays. Everything before payment is the same questions all day, and that is the part worth automating. Payment involves money, and a person should still check it.

### How to do it with WhatsApp Business alone

1. **Build your catalogue.** In WhatsApp Business go to Business tools, then Catalogue, and add each product with a photo, price and description.
2. **Turn on the cart.** Customers can then add items from your catalogue and send you the cart as one message.
3. **Set a greeting message** that tells new customers to browse the catalogue and what details you need to process an order.
4. **Set an away message** for the hours you are closed, saying when you will reply.
5. **Save quick replies** for the answers you type most: delivery fees, payment details, "what is your address?". Type `/` in a chat to use one.
6. **Use labels** such as New order, Pending payment, Paid and Order complete to track each chat.

This is free, and for a shop with a few orders a day it may be all you need. Its limit is that nothing is answered until you pick up the phone. Greeting and away messages do not answer questions, and the cart cannot tell a customer whether size 12 is in stock.

### How to do it automatically with Dailzero

Dailzero links to your WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business number the way WhatsApp Web does, and an AI agent replies on it.

1. **Create your agent** in the dashboard and describe your business: what you sell, opening hours, delivery areas and fees, payment methods and return policy.
2. **Add your products.** In the agent's Product Catalogue, click "Add Product" and enter the name, price and a short description, then upload the product photos. The agent quotes these prices and sends these photos.
3. **Write down how you want orders taken.** In the agent's instructions, list what it must collect before an order counts: item, size or colour, quantity, name, delivery address and phone number. Say when it should send your payment details.
4. **Test it before customers see it.** Chat with the agent in the dashboard as if you were a customer, and correct anything it gets wrong.
5. **Link your number** by QR code or pairing code. Your chats and your app stay as they are.
6. **Let it run, and watch the first day.** The agent answers within seconds, day and night, including the 11pm "how much?" messages.

### What happens when a customer orders

- The agent answers the product questions from your catalogue and sends photos.
- It reads images and voice notes customers send, so a screenshot of an item from your page still gets an answer.
- It collects the details you listed and repeats the order back to the customer.
- Conversations with clear buying intent are flagged as leads in the dashboard, so you can see who is ready to pay.
- The order itself lives in the conversation, where you can read every detail.
- If the customer asks for a person, is upset, or asks for something sensitive such as a refund, the agent hands the chat over and pauses on that chat.

### Confirming payment and dispatch

Be clear about where the automation stops. Checking that a transfer has landed in your account is still a job for you or a staff member. The simplest arrangement is this: the agent gathers the order and sends the payment details, the customer sends proof, and you confirm and dispatch.

You can reply from the dashboard or straight from your phone. When you send a message yourself, the AI pauses on that chat so the customer is not answered twice, and you can switch it back to "AI replies" when you are done. ### Frequently asked questions

#### Can WhatsApp Business take orders automatically without any other tool?

Partly. The catalogue and cart let a customer send you a list of items, and greeting and away messages reply once. Nothing in the app answers questions or collects delivery details for you.

#### Will the AI give customers the wrong price?

It answers from the products and prices you entered, so keep them up to date. Test it with your own questions before you link your number, and update the catalogue whenever a price changes.

#### Can the AI confirm that a customer has paid?

Treat payment confirmation as a human step. The agent can send your payment details and collect proof of payment in the chat, and you confirm before dispatch.

#### Do I need a new number or the WhatsApp API?

No. Dailzero links to the number you already use, with no Meta verification. An official WhatsApp Business Platform route is also available on request for businesses that need it.

#### What happens when my credits finish?

The agent pauses. There are no overage fees, and it resumes when your plan renews or you top up.
