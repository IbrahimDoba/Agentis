You can set up a WhatsApp AI agent in one day if you use a tool that links to the number you already have. The work is about two to three hours in total: create an account, link your number, check the products and prices the agent will use, test it from a second phone, then switch it on. The slow route is the official WhatsApp API, which usually needs business verification with Meta first and is rarely a same-day job.

### What to have ready before you start

- The phone with your business WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business number, charged and online
- Your price list, with delivery fees for Lagos and outside Lagos
- Your opening hours, address and payment details
- Your policies: returns, deposits, how long delivery takes
- Product photos you normally send customers
- A second phone, or a friend's phone, for testing

Writing these down first is the step most people skip. The agent can only answer from what you give it.

### A one-day plan

This is how the day looks with Dailzero on the default linked-device route. The steps come from the Dailzero dashboard as it works today.

1. **Morning: create your account.** Sign up on the Dailzero website and confirm your email with the code sent to you. The 7-day free trial starts with 1,000 credits, about 200 AI replies.
2. **Link your number.** On the connect screen, either scan the QR code or choose the phone number option and enter a pairing code. On your phone, open WhatsApp, go to Linked Devices and tap Link a device. It is the same thing you do for WhatsApp Web.
3. **Let it build a first draft.** After you link, Dailzero reads your recent WhatsApp chats and drafts an agent from them: how you talk, the products and prices it found, and the questions customers ask most. The screen says this usually takes about 5 minutes. The AI stays off until the draft is ready.
4. **Review the draft.** This is the most important step of the day. Read the instructions, the product list and the FAQs. Fix every price. Remove anything out of date. If the draft missed products, add them. You can also skip the draft and set everything up by hand.
5. **Midday: add what is missing.** Upload documents such as a price list or returns policy to the knowledge base. Add product photos to the media library so the agent can send them.
6. **Set your handover rules.** In the agent settings, check that the AI pauses when you reply to a chat yourself. This is on by default. See [how to hand over from AI to a human](/blog/how-to-hand-over-from-ai-to-a-human-on-whatsapp).
7. **Afternoon: test from a second phone.** Message your business number the way a customer would. Ask for prices, delivery to Ibadan, a product you do not sell, and send a voice note. Use the script in [how to test a WhatsApp AI agent](/blog/how-to-test-a-whatsapp-ai-agent-before-customers-see-it).
8. **Fix and retest.** Every wrong answer points to something missing in your instructions or products. Correct it and ask again.
9. **Evening: go live.** Leave the agent on and watch the first real chats in the dashboard. Step in when you want to. The AI pauses on that chat.

### How long each part takes

| Step | Time for most small businesses |
|---|---|
| Sign up and confirm email | A few minutes |
| Link the number | A few minutes |
| First draft from your chats | About 5 minutes |
| Review prices, products and FAQs | 30 to 60 minutes |
| Testing and fixing | About an hour |

The review and the testing are where your time should go. Linking is the easy part.

### What can slow you down

- **A brand new number.** A SIM registered this week should be used normally for about two weeks before you connect any automation. New numbers also start on a warm-up period with lower sending limits. Read [how to stop WhatsApp banning your business number](/blog/how-to-stop-whatsapp-banning-your-business-number).
- **No price list.** If your prices live in your head, write them down first.
- **The official API.** If you need the official WhatsApp Business Platform, Dailzero sets that route up with its team on request. Plan for more than a day, and for Meta's own message fees.
- **Too many numbers.** Each agent runs one number. Start with one and add the next later.

### After day one

Read the conversations every day for the first week. You will see questions you did not plan for. Add the answers. An agent gets good in its first week of real chats, not on the day you set it up.

No tool can promise a number will never be restricted. Keep the agent on replies to customers who wrote first while the number is still warming up, and leave broadcasts for later.

### Frequently asked questions

#### Can I really be live in one day?

Yes, on the linked-device route. Linking takes minutes and most of the day is checking prices and testing. On the official WhatsApp API it usually takes longer because of Meta's checks.

#### Do I need a developer?

No. Signing up, linking the number and editing products are all done in the dashboard.

#### Do I need WhatsApp Business, or does normal WhatsApp work?

Either works. Dailzero links the WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business number you already use.

#### Will customers notice anything has changed?

They message the same number and see the same chat. Replies arrive faster. Your phone keeps working as normal and you can still reply yourself.

#### What does the first day cost?

Nothing. The free trial runs for 7 days with 1,000 credits. After that, plans start at ₦20,000 a month, or you can pay as you go from ₦1,000.
