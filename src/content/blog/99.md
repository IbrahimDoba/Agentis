WhatsApp AI is software that reads the messages customers send to your business number and writes back a proper answer by itself, using the prices, products and policies you taught it. You do not need a new number, a website or any technical skill to start. If you can fill a form and scan a QR code, you can set one up.

### The words you will hear, in plain English

- **AI agent.** The software that chats with your customers. It understands a full sentence, including everyday Nigerian English and Pidgin phrasing, and replies in its own words.
- **Chatbot.** Often the older kind that says "Press 1 for prices, press 2 for location". An AI agent does not work with menus. The customer just types as they would to you.
- **Linked device.** The way WhatsApp Web connects a laptop to your phone. Dailzero connects to your number the same way.
- **Credits.** The units you pay with. One AI text reply costs about 5 credits.
- **Handoff.** When the AI steps back and passes a chat to you or your staff.
- **Broadcast.** One message sent to many of your existing customers.

### What it can do on day one

Say you bake cakes in Enugu. Once you have given the agent your details, it can:

- Reply in seconds at any hour.
- Quote your prices and explain your sizes, flavours and delivery areas.
- Send photos, videos and documents such as your price list from a media library you upload.
- Understand a photo or voice note a customer sends, such as a picture of a cake design they want.
- Take the order details: name, date, address, quantity.
- Remember a returning customer and what you discussed before.
- List people who look ready to buy in your dashboard.
- Pass the chat to you when the customer asks for a person or when it does not know.

### What it cannot do

- It does not confirm payment. It can share your account details or payment link, and you confirm the transfer.
- It does not know your stock or your diary unless you tell it, or a developer connects your system.
- It does not make calls, and it does not answer Instagram DMs, Facebook messages, SMS or email.
- It is not the official WhatsApp Business API, so there is no verified badge.
- It only knows what you taught it.

### What you need before you start

1. A WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business number your customers already use. If the number is brand new, use it normally for about two weeks first.
2. The phone that number is on, with data. It stays your main device and should come online regularly.
3. Your information written down: products, prices, opening hours, delivery fees, payment details, return policy, and the ten questions customers ask most.
4. Photos of your products.

Number three is the real work. The agent is only as good as what you give it.

### Your first hour, step by step

1. [Create a free account](/signup). The trial lasts 7 days and includes 1,000 credits, about 200 AI replies.
2. Describe your business in a few sentences, the way you would explain it to a new staff member.
3. Add your products and prices, then your common questions and answers.
4. Upload photos and your price list.
5. Link your number by scanning the QR code or entering the pairing code.
6. Test it from a friend's or relative's phone before real customers meet it.

### A test script to run

From the second phone, send messages the way your customers really write. For example:

> Hw much is ur chocolate cake 8 inches

A good answer gives the price, mentions flavours or delivery, and asks a helpful next question. Then try something it should not know:

> Can you do me a 5 tier wedding cake for 300 guests this Saturday?

A good answer does not guess a price. It hands the chat to you. Also send a voice note, a photo and "I want to talk to a human". Whenever an answer is wrong or thin, add the missing information and test again.

### Five beginner mistakes

- **Going live without testing.** Ten minutes with a second phone catches most problems.
- **Never reading the chats.** Read a few every day in the first week and fix what you find.
- **Forgetting the AI is paused.** When you reply to a chat yourself, the AI pauses on that chat. Switch it back on when you are done.
- **Broadcasting to strangers.** Broadcasts are for people who have messaged you, not bought lists.

### Frequently asked questions

#### Do I need to know anything about computers?

No. Setup is filling in information about your business and scanning a code. There is no coding.

#### Will I lose my chats or my number?

No. Your number stays on your phone and every chat still appears there. The AI works alongside you.

#### What happens after the free trial?

You choose a monthly plan from ₦20,000 or buy credits as you go from ₦1,000. If credits run out, the AI pauses and messages still reach your phone. See [pricing](/pricing).
