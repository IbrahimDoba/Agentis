Use WhatsApp AI for the first conversation with a prospect, not for live client work. An agent can explain your services, give starting prices, send your portfolio or rate card, gather a short brief and book a discovery call at any hour. It cannot see your project board, so it should never guess whether a client's website or campaign is on schedule.

### Two kinds of message arrive on an agency's number

**From prospects:** "How much is a website?", "Do you run Facebook ads?", "Can you manage our Instagram?", "Send your profile." These are repetitive and arrive at odd hours.

**From existing clients:** "When will the first draft be ready?", "Please change the banner", "Why did leads drop this week?" These need context only your team has.

An AI agent is well suited to the first group and should hand over the second.

### What to give the agent

- **Your services, described plainly.** Web design, social media management, paid ads, SEO, branding, whatever you actually sell.
- **Starting prices or ranges.** "Business websites start from ₦350,000" is enough to filter. Use your own figures. If you never quote without a call, tell the agent to say that and book the call.
- **Your process and timelines.** Deposit terms, typical turnaround, number of revisions.
- **Documents in the media library.** A company profile, rate card or portfolio PDF the agent can send on request. A document costs 8 credits to send.
- **Your call slots.** The days and hours you take discovery calls.

### The brief the agent should collect

Before a prospect reaches a human, have the agent ask four things:

1. What does the business do, and where is it based?
2. Which service do they need?
3. When do they want to start, and is there a deadline such as a launch date?
4. Do they have a budget range in mind?

In practice it looks like this. A prospect writes on a Saturday night:

> "Hello, we run a logistics company in Abuja and need a new website plus someone to handle our ads. What do you charge?"

A good first reply:

> "Hello, and thank you for reaching out. Business websites start from ₦350,000 and ad management from ₦100,000 a month, depending on scope. Do you have a launch date in mind? I can also book a 20-minute call with our team this week to go through the details."

By Monday morning you have a name, a need, a timeline and a call booked. The agent books appointments and sends reminders. It works from the slots you gave it and does not read your calendar app, so check for clashes when a booking comes in.

### What to keep with your team

- **Custom quotes.** The agent gives starting prices. A proposal for a specific scope is your job.
- **Project status.** Instruct the agent to hand the chat to the account manager when an existing client asks about their work. It remembers returning contacts and earlier chats, but it does not know what your designers finished today.
- **Unhappy clients.** Always a human.

When any of your team replies, from the dashboard or from the phone, the AI pauses on that chat until you switch it back on. Team members can have their own logins.

### When this is not the right fit

If your agency lives on referrals and gets two enquiries a week, you do not need automation; answer them yourself. And if you need the official WhatsApp Business Platform, with a verified badge or approved templates, Dailzero is not that. It links to your number like WhatsApp Web.

### Frequently asked questions

#### Will prospects know they are talking to an AI?

You set the tone and wording. Being upfront that an assistant handles first enquiries, and that a team member joins for the call, is the safer choice for an agency whose product is trust.

#### Can the AI send our portfolio?

Yes. Upload the PDF, images or a short video to the media library and the agent sends them when a prospect asks.

#### Can it give clients updates on their projects?

Not on its own. It has no access to your project management tool, so set it to hand those chats to the account manager. A developer could connect your own system through webhook tools.
