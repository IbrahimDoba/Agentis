The best time to offer a room upgrade is while the guest is still deciding, in the same WhatsApp chat where they asked for the rate. A WhatsApp AI agent helps a hotel upsell by making that offer every time, with the price difference and a photo, instead of only when a staff member remembers.

### Four moments to make an offer

#### When the guest asks for a price

Quote what they asked for first, then one step up. Never lead with the expensive room.

> How much is your room per night? I am coming with my wife this Saturday.

A reply that sells without pushing:

> Our Standard room is ₦40,000 a night. Since you are coming as a couple, the Deluxe at ₦55,000 has a king bed, a balcony and breakfast for two included. I can send photos of both if you like. Which date should I note?

The guest's own words, "with my wife", gave the agent a reason for the suggestion.

#### After the booking is agreed

Once dates are settled, the guest is thinking about logistics. This is the moment for airport pickup, early check-in or a late check-out, each with a price.

#### Just before arrival

When the agent sends a reminder before the stay, it can add one relevant extra: a dinner reservation at your restaurant, a cake and room decoration for an anniversary, or a car for the weekend.

#### During the stay

A guest who asks what time check-out is can be told about the late check-out fee. In-stay offers should answer the question asked, not interrupt.

### What you can realistically upsell

- A higher room category
- Breakfast, if it is not in the rate
- Airport pickup and drop-off
- Early check-in and late check-out
- An extra bed or a connecting room for families
- Celebration packages: cake, decoration, a bottle
- The hall or meeting room, for a guest who mentions an event

Pick three or four that you can always deliver.

### How to set the agent up for it

The agent uses the products, prices and tone you give it. For upselling, be specific.

1. List each room type with its rate, what is different about it, and the exact price difference from the one below.
2. List each add-on with a price and any condition, such as "late check-out until 3pm, ₦10,000, subject to availability".
3. Upload photos and a short video of each room type to the media library, so the agent can show the upgrade.
4. Write the rules: answer the question first, mention one upgrade once, and drop it if the guest says no. An agent told to always push the suite will annoy people.
5. Tell it who gets what. Couples hear about the deluxe, families about the connecting room, business travellers about the pickup and early check-in.

The agent remembers returning guests and earlier chats, so someone who took the pickup last time can be asked if they want it again.

### A worked example

Use your own figures. As an illustration, say the gap between your standard and deluxe rooms is ₦15,000 and ten guests a month accept the upgrade for an average of two nights. That is 10 × 2 × ₦15,000 = ₦300,000 in extra room revenue. Add five airport pickups at ₦12,000 and you have another ₦60,000.

The cost of making the offer is small. A text reply is about 5 credits and each photo sent is 8, so showing a guest two room photos adds 16 credits to the chat. On the Basic plan, ₦20,000 buys 25,000 credits. How many guests say yes is something you only learn by running it for a month.

### Where a person still has to step in

- **Availability.** The agent cannot see your booking system unless a developer has connected it. If the deluxe rooms are sold out on Saturday, it will not know. Either update the agent when a category is full, or have it say the upgrade is subject to confirmation by the front desk.
- **Payment.** It does not charge the guest or verify a transfer. It shares your account details or Paystack link, and staff confirm.
- **Discounts.** A guest who asks for the deluxe at the standard price is negotiating. Hand that to a manager.
- **Delivery.** Make sure add-ons agreed in chat reach the people who fulfil them.

### Frequently asked questions

#### Can the AI offer upgrades only when the better room is free?

Not on its own. It works from the information you give it. Keep it updated, or have it offer upgrades subject to confirmation, unless a developer links it to your booking system.

#### Can it send photos of the upgraded room?

Yes. Upload room photos and videos to the media library and the agent sends them in the chat. An image costs 8 credits and a video 12.

#### Will it message guests with offers they did not ask for?

No. Within a chat it makes offers as part of replying. A broadcast to past guests is a separate action you choose to send.
