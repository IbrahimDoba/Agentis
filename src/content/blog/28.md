You automate property viewing scheduling on WhatsApp by giving an AI agent your fixed inspection slots, the details it must collect from each person, and your rules on fees and meeting points. The agent then offers a slot, books it in the chat, and sends a reminder. Most of the work is deciding your own rules, not configuring software.

Below are the steps, using Dailzero as the example tool.

### Step 1: decide your inspection windows first

The AI cannot see your diary, your Google Calendar or where your field agents are. It works from the availability you type in. So the first job is yours: fix your windows.

Open-ended scheduling ("when are you free?") is what causes the endless back-and-forth. Fixed windows end it. For example:

- Lekki and Ajah listings: Tuesdays and Thursdays 10am to 4pm, Saturdays 9am to 2pm
- Mainland listings: Wednesdays and Saturdays, 10am to 3pm
- Estate site tours in Ibeju-Lekki: Saturdays only, bus leaves the office at 9am

### Step 2: write your booking rules

Put these into the agent's instructions in plain English:

- How long a viewing takes and how many people can come at the same time
- The meeting point for each property, with a landmark
- Your inspection fee, if any, and whether it is paid before or at the viewing
- How much notice you need (for instance, no same-day bookings after 12 noon)
- What to do if nobody is free in the offered slots: hand the chat to a person

If you charge an inspection fee, give the agent the account details to share. Dailzero does not process or verify payments, so tell it to hand the chat to you once the person says they have paid, and you confirm the transfer.

### Step 3: tell it what to collect

A booking is only useful if your field agent knows who is coming. Have the AI collect:

1. Full name
2. Which property or properties they want to see
3. Preferred day and time from your windows
4. Number of people coming
5. A phone number for calls, if different from the WhatsApp number

Keep it to what you will actually use. Each extra question is a chance for the person to drop off.

### Step 4: test the conversation

Message your number from another phone and try to book. A good exchange looks like this:

> I want to check the 2 bedroom at Sangotedo this weekend

> Sure. Viewings for Sangotedo hold on Saturday between 9am and 2pm. Is 11am okay for you? The meeting point is the estate gate, opposite the filling station. The inspection fee is ₦5,000, paid by transfer before the viewing.

> 11 is fine. Send account

The agent shares your account details, takes the person's name and the number of people coming, and confirms the booking. When they say they have paid, it hands the chat to you to check the alert.

### Step 5: go live and manage the day

Once live, the agent books appointments and sends reminders ahead of the viewing. Your part each evening:

- Check tomorrow's bookings and pass them to whoever is doing the showings.
- Remove any property that has been taken so nobody is booked to view it.
- Change your windows in the agent's instructions if a field agent is away.

One caution on clashes. The agent does not know about a viewing you arranged by phone, and you should not depend on it to police a strict one-person-per-slot diary. Give it windows wide enough to take several visitors, keep phone-arranged viewings outside those windows, and review the bookings each evening.

### How to cut no-shows

No-shows are the real cost in viewings. A few rules help:

- **Reminders.** The agent sends one before the appointment.
- **An inspection fee.** Even a small fee filters out people who are not serious. Whether to charge is your call and depends on your market.
- **Exact meeting points.** "Opposite the filling station at the estate gate" beats "Sangotedo".
- **Watch for silence.** Someone who does not answer the reminder is the likeliest no-show. Call them before your field agent sets out.

### What this setup does not do

There is no sync with Google Calendar or any booking software built in. If you want bookings written into your own system automatically, a developer can connect it using webhook tools in the [developer API](/developers). Without that, the dashboard and your WhatsApp chats are the record.

The AI also does not attend the viewing or hold the keys. It gets the right person to the right gate at the right time.

### Frequently asked questions

#### Can people reschedule through the AI?

Yes. They message to change the time, and the agent offers another slot from your windows. Anything outside your rules goes to a person.

#### What does this cost?

A booking chat is mostly text replies at about 5 credits each. The free trial gives 1,000 credits to test with, and plans start at ₦20,000 a month. See the [pricing page](/pricing).

#### Does it work for short-let and commercial viewings too?

Yes. The method is the same: fixed windows, clear rules, and a short list of details to collect. More on the feature is on the [appointment booking page](/solutions/appointment-booking).
