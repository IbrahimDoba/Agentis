To qualify property buyers on WhatsApp with AI, give the agent a short list of questions to ask every new enquiry (what they want, where, budget, how they will pay, and when), then read the answers in your dashboard and call the serious ones first. The AI does the asking. You do the judging.

Here is how to set that up so it feels like a helpful conversation and not an interrogation.

### What "qualified" means for a Nigerian property buyer

A qualified buyer is someone whose budget matches what you sell, who can pay in a way you accept, and who intends to act soon. Everything else is detail. That gives you five things to find out:

1. **Purpose.** Are they buying to live in, to invest, or for a relative? A buyer in the UK purchasing for parents in Ibadan needs different handling from a couple moving next month.
2. **Location.** Lekki Phase 1 and Ibeju-Lekki are different conversations at very different prices.
3. **Budget.** The real number, or at least a range.
4. **Payment method.** Outright, instalments over 6 or 12 months, or a mortgage.
5. **Timeline.** This month, this quarter, or "just checking".

### The order matters

Ask about budget first and people go quiet. Start with what they are looking for, give them something useful, then ask about money.

### What it looks like in a chat

Say you sell terraces and land in Lagos. A new message arrives from a Facebook ad:

> Hello, I saw your advert for the 4 bed terrace in Ajah. How much?

The agent replies with the price you loaded, then moves one step:

> Hello, thanks for reaching out. The 4 bedroom terrace in Ajah is ₦95 million, with a 12 month payment plan available. Are you buying to live in or as an investment?

Over the next few messages it learns the buyer wants to move in before December, has about ₦90 million, and prefers to spread payment over six months. Nobody filled a form. Those answers now sit in the chat, and the person appears in your leads list because they showed buying intent.

### How to brief the agent

In Dailzero you describe your business and how the agent should behave in plain English. For qualification, include instructions like these:

- "Before offering an inspection, find out the buyer's preferred location, budget range and whether they are paying outright or by instalment."
- "Ask one question at a time. Never ask for budget in your first reply."
- "If the buyer refuses to share a budget, do not push. Send the price list and offer an inspection."
- "Price negotiation and payment confirmation are handled by our sales team. Hand those chats to a person."

Then load the facts it needs to answer well: every property with its price, title, payment plans, location and photos. An agent that cannot answer "what is the title?" will lose the buyer before it qualifies them.

### Sorting buyers after the AI has asked

Dailzero lists people who show buying intent as leads on every plan. It does not put a score on them for you, so do a quick sort yourself each morning:

- **Call today.** Budget fits, payment method works, wants to inspect within two weeks.
- **Nurture.** Budget fits but timing is months away, or they are waiting on funds from abroad. On the Pro plan, automated follow-ups can check back in with people who went quiet.
- **Redirect.** Budget is far below your cheapest unit. Point them to something that fits if you have it, or thank them and move on.

Ten minutes with the leads list beats two hours scrolling through every chat.

### Mistakes that spoil qualification

- **Asking everything at once.** A wall of six questions gets no answer.
- **Hiding the price until they qualify.** Nigerian buyers have seen that trick. Give the price, then ask.
- **No handover.** When a buyer says "I am ready to pay", a person should take over quickly. The agent hands the chat to you, and it pauses on that chat when you reply.

### What the AI cannot do here

It cannot verify that someone really has the money, check a mortgage approval, or confirm a payment. It works from what the buyer types. Proof of funds, KYC and receipts stay with your team.

### Frequently asked questions

#### How many questions should the AI ask before handing over?

Three to five, spread across the conversation. If the buyer asks for a person earlier, the agent hands over straight away.

#### Does this work for rentals as well as sales?

Yes. Swap the questions: move-in date, number of occupants, budget for the total package, and whether they can pay a full year upfront.

#### Can the agent understand voice notes from buyers?

Yes. Dailzero understands voice notes and photos customers send, so a buyer who prefers to talk can still be qualified.
