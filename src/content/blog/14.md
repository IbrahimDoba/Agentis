The return on WhatsApp automation for a financial services business comes from two places: staff hours no longer spent on repeat questions, and inquiries that used to go unanswered now turning into customers. There is no honest universal percentage. You can work out your own figure in about ten minutes with five numbers, and this article walks through the sum.

### The formula

Monthly return = (hours saved × what an hour of staff time costs you) + (extra customers won × what a customer is worth to you) − what the automation costs.

ROI is that result divided by the cost.

### The five numbers you need

1. **Chats per day** on your business WhatsApp.
2. **Share of chats that are routine.** Requirements, rates, fees, opening hours, "how do I apply". Tag fifty recent chats by hand to find out.
3. **Minutes a person spends per routine chat**, including the time lost switching between tasks.
4. **Cost of an hour of that person's time.** Monthly pay plus what you spend on data, power and supervision, divided by hours worked.
5. **Value of one new customer.** For a lender, the average margin on a first loan. For a savings or investment product, your earnings on an average account over its first year.

### The savings side, as a worksheet

Carry on with the same hypothetical bank. Suppose its own tagging showed two thirds of chats are routine. That is 40 chats a day the AI can finish alone.

- 40 chats × your minutes per chat = minutes saved a day.
- Multiply by your working days in a month, divide by 60 for hours.
- Multiply by your cost per hour.

If the answer is above ₦40,000, the automation has paid for itself on time alone. If it is below, the case rests on the revenue side, and you should know that before you buy.

Saved hours only become saved money if you put them to use, for example on recoveries or faster processing.

### The revenue side, as a break-even

Ask: how many extra customers a month would cover the cost?

Break-even customers = monthly cost ÷ value of one customer.

To illustrate with made-up figures: if a new borrower is worth ₦10,000 to you, ₦40,000 ÷ ₦10,000 = 4. You need four extra funded loans a month. Now look at your chat history and count the inquiries from last month that came in at night or on Sunday and never got a proper reply.

> Good evening. Please what is the interest on 500k for 6 months and what do I need to bring?

Sent at 9.40pm, that message normally waits until morning. An AI agent answers it at once with your rate and requirements, asks what the loan is for and whether the customer earns a salary or runs a business, and lists the person as a lead in the dashboard for your loan officer.

### Costs people leave out

- **Upkeep.** Rates and requirements change. An agent quoting last quarter's rate costs you trust.
- **Human cover.** Complaints, failed transactions and fraud reports still go to staff.

### When the return is poor

The numbers will not work well if:

- You get fewer than about ten chats a day. Pay as you go from ₦1,000 may still make sense, but a subscription will not.
- Most of your chats are account-specific, such as transaction disputes. The AI cannot see accounts unless a developer connects your system, so it would hand most chats over.
- You need the official WhatsApp API for OTPs, bulk alerts or a verified badge. That is a different product with a different cost structure.

### Frequently asked questions

#### What is a typical ROI for WhatsApp automation in financial services?

There is no reliable typical figure, because it depends on your staff costs, chat volume and customer value. Use the formula above with your own numbers.

#### Does the AI replace a customer service officer?

Not fully. It takes the repeat questions and the out-of-hours first replies. Disputes, exceptions and anything involving a customer's account still need a person.

#### Does it count leads for me?

Yes. People showing buying intent are listed in the dashboard on every plan, which gives you the lead count for the revenue side.
