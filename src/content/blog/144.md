Putting your WhatsApp Status on autopilot means a tool posts from a library of your own photos, videos and text at times you have approved, so your Status stays active without you posting each one. WhatsApp has no such feature. In Dailzero you fill a gallery, tag what is in it, choose or describe a plan, check the week it produces and turn Autopilot on. It posts at most 6 times a day, at least 2 hours apart.

### The manual version

You can get most of the benefit with discipline and no tool.

1. **Build a bank of posts.** Keep 20 to 30 product photos, short videos, price lists and reviews in one album on your phone.
2. **Write a weekly rota.** For example: new arrivals in the morning, prices at lunch, a customer review in the evening.
3. **Set three daily alarms** and post from the album when each one goes.
4. **Rotate.** Tick off what you posted so the same picture does not come back two days later.

It works until you get busy. Autopilot is the same rota, kept by software, and it only ever follows a plan you have confirmed.

### How to set it up in Dailzero

1. **Fill your gallery.** Open **Status** and go to the **Gallery** tab. Everything you post from the Posts tab shows up here. To add something without posting it, build it in **Posts** and click "Save to gallery".
2. **Tag your posts.** In the Gallery, click "Suggest tags" and then "Add these tags", or use "Add tags" on a single post and type your own, such as `ankara` or `offer`. Each post can carry up to 5 tags. Tags are what let you say "prices on Friday".
3. **Choose what Autopilot may use.** Each gallery post has a tick box, "Autopilot can post this". Untick anything that is out of stock or out of season.
4. **Open the Autopilot tab and pick a starting point.** You have three choices: "Suggested for you", which is sized to your gallery, with a "Use this" button; a ready-made plan; or "Say how you want it to work".
5. **If you use your own words**, type something like "Post my ankara videos every morning, prices on Friday evening, nothing on Sunday" and click "Make my plan". Dailzero turns the sentence into rules and shows them to you. Nothing is saved until you confirm.
6. **Check your plan.** On the "Check your plan" screen you can change each time, the days, the post type, which tags it draws from and the order. "Add a time" adds another slot, and "Don't repeat a post within" sets how many days must pass before the same post returns.
7. **Read "Next 7 days".** This is the real week Autopilot would post. If a slot says "Nothing fits, skipped", tag more posts for it.
8. **Click "Turn on Autopilot"**, or "Save, don't start yet" if you want to come back later.

### The ready-made plans

| Plan | What it does |
|---|---|
| Daily seller | 3 posts a day, Monday to Saturday, taking turns through your gallery |
| Gentle | 1 post every evening |
| New arrivals first | 2 posts a day, your newest posts first |
| Weekend push | 1 post each evening, and 3 a day from Friday to Sunday |

Each slot can pick posts in one of three orders: taking turns, best results first, or newest first.

### The limits, and why they exist

- **At most 6 Autopilot posts a day.** More than that reads as spam to your contacts.
- **At least 2 hours between posts.** If a plan breaks either rule, the later time is dropped and you are told which one.
- **Up to 12 posting times in a plan.**
- **Posts go out a few minutes after the set time**, not on the dot, so the pattern does not look automated.
- **Autopilot pauses itself if 2 posts in a row fail**, for example when your number is disconnected.

### What it costs

Each Autopilot post costs the same as a post you make yourself: 5 credits for text, 8 for an image, 12 for a video, with no charge per viewer. The Autopilot tab shows the estimate for your plan in credits per week and per month before you turn it on.

Daily seller is 18 posts a week. If all are images, that is 144 credits a week. See the [pricing page](/pricing). When credits finish there is no overage fee. Posting stops until you renew or top up.

### Keeping control after it is on

- **Swap** replaces one planned post with another from your gallery.
- **Skip** drops one planned post, and "Undo skip" brings it back.
- **Change it in your own words**, for example "no posts this Sunday".
- **The switch at the top** turns Autopilot off. Nothing more is posted.

### Before you turn it on

Autopilot is only as good as the audience and the gallery behind it. A Status reaches only people saved in your phone, so [save your contacts first](/blog/how-to-save-all-your-whatsapp-contacts-automatically). And a gallery of five pictures will repeat quickly, so start with a gentle plan and add to the gallery every week. If you prefer to choose each post and time yourself, use [scheduled posts](/blog/how-to-schedule-whatsapp-status-posts) instead.

Autopilot works on the linked-device connection. It is not available on the official WhatsApp Business Platform route.

### Frequently asked questions

#### Will Autopilot post something I have not approved?

No. It only posts items in your gallery that have "Autopilot can post this" ticked, on the plan you confirmed. You can see and change the next 7 days at any time.

#### How many times a day can Autopilot post?

Up to 6 times a day, with at least 2 hours between posts. Most businesses do well on 1 to 3.

#### Does Autopilot write my Status for me?

No. It posts your own photos, videos and text from the gallery. It can suggest tags and turn your instructions into a schedule, but the content is yours.

#### What happens when people reply to an Autopilot post?

The reply arrives as a normal chat and the AI answers it, using the note you saved with that post. See [how to make AI answer replies to your WhatsApp Status](/blog/how-to-make-ai-answer-replies-to-your-whatsapp-status).

#### What if my gallery is small?

Autopilot warns you when too few posts fit a slot, and the suggested plan posts less often for a small gallery. Add more posts or post less often.
