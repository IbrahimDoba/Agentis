Before you pay for a WhatsApp AI agent, ask the vendor 12 questions about how it connects to WhatsApp, what it costs in total, where its answers come from, how a person takes over, and what happens to your number and your data. A good vendor answers each one plainly and in writing. If you get vague replies on cost, number safety or data, treat that as your answer.

### The 12 questions, and what a good answer sounds like

| # | Question | A good answer sounds like |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | How do you connect to WhatsApp? | "We use the official API", "we link to your number like WhatsApp Web", or "both". Then the trade-offs of that route. |
| 2 | What is my total monthly cost at my volume? | One figure in Naira, with every extra fee named |
| 3 | Do I pay Meta message fees as well? | A clear yes or no, and who bills you |
| 4 | What happens when I pass my limit? | "It pauses" or "we bill overage at this rate". Not "we will sort it out". |
| 5 | Where do the AI's answers come from? | "From the products, prices and documents you add" |
| 6 | What does it do when it does not know? | "It says it will check and hands over." Not "it always knows". |
| 7 | How do I take over a chat? | "Reply from your phone or the dashboard and the AI stops on that chat" |
| 8 | Does it handle voice notes and images? | Yes or no, with the cost of each |
| 9 | How do you protect my number? | Specific measures, with no promise that a ban can never happen |
| 10 | Where is my customer data kept, and can I delete it? | A written privacy policy and a deletion process |
| 11 | Can I try it first, and is there a refund? | A trial period and a refund rule in writing |
| 12 | What can it not do? | A real list. Every tool has one. |

### Warning signs

- Prices only in dollars, with no Naira total
- "Unlimited messages" with no fair use rule you can read
- No trial and a long contract
- A promise that your number will never be banned
- No privacy policy on the website

### When another tool is the better buy

If you need a verified badge, a large shared inbox for a support team, or ready-made links to a store platform or CRM, an official API provider built for teams will fit better. If you get only a few chats a day, the free WhatsApp Business app may be all you need.

### Frequently asked questions

#### What is the single most important question?

"What is my total monthly cost at my volume?" Plan prices hide message fees, extra users and overage. The total is the only number you can compare.

#### Should I only buy a tool that uses the official WhatsApp API?

Not always. The official API is the right route for banks, large senders and anyone who needs approved templates. A linked-device tool is faster and cheaper for a small business that mostly replies to customers who wrote first, with more risk to the number.

#### What should I get in writing?

The price, what happens at your limit, the refund rule and how to delete your data.

#### Do I pay Meta when I use Dailzero?

Not on the default linked-device route. If you choose the official WhatsApp Business Platform route, Meta's own message fees apply.
