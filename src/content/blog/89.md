Your business needs a WhatsApp AI agent when replying to customers has become the thing that limits your sales: you answer the same questions all day, messages wait hours for a reply, and you can no longer tell who was a serious buyer. If three or more of the seven signs below describe your week, it is time to test one.

### 1. You have typed the same answer five times today

Open your WhatsApp and look at what you sent this morning. If it is some version of "It's ₦18,500, delivery within Lagos is ₦3,000, we deliver in 24 to 48 hours" again and again, you are doing work a machine does well.

### 2. You wake up to unread messages, and some never reply again

Customers do not shop on your schedule. Messages come in after work, late at night and on Sunday afternoon. If your morning starts with apologies for late replies, and several of those people never answer, that is the sign.

### 3. You are running ads and cannot keep up with the clicks

Click-to-WhatsApp ads on Facebook and Instagram can send a burst of chats in an hour. If some sit unanswered until evening, you are paying for leads and then ignoring them.

An agent replies to each click in seconds. Dailzero also records which ad the customer came from, so you can see which ad brings buyers and which brings window shoppers.

### 4. "How much?" is where most of your chats end

You know the pattern:

> How much is the wig?

You reply with the price. Silence. Some of those people were interested but needed a photo or one more answer.

A well set up agent does not stop at the price. It replies with the price, the length and colour options, sends a photo and asks which one they want. On the Pro plan it can also follow up automatically with people who went quiet.

### 5. Your phone is the business

If sales stop when you are driving, in a meeting, in church, at the market in Balogun or when your battery dies during a power cut, the business depends on one device.

An agent keeps answering while you are busy. Your phone stays the main device and should come online regularly, as with WhatsApp Web, but you no longer have to be the one typing.

### 6. Customers get different answers depending on who replies

One staff member quotes last month's price. Another forgets the delivery fee for the mainland. A third promises next-day delivery to Kano that you cannot do.

An AI agent gives every customer the same prices and policies, the ones you entered. When they change, you update them in one place.

### 7. You cannot say who your hot leads were yesterday

Could you name the five people closest to buying this week without scrolling for twenty minutes? If not, follow-up is happening by luck.

Dailzero flags people who show buying intent and lists them in the dashboard on every plan, so follow-up starts from a list.

### Signs you do not need one yet

Not every business should buy this. Hold off if:

- **You get only a handful of chats a week** and reply to them all within minutes. Use the free quick replies and away message in the WhatsApp Business app.
- **Every sale is a long, custom negotiation.** If no two chats are alike, the agent will hand most of them to you anyway.
- **Your customers mostly call or use Instagram DMs.** Dailzero covers WhatsApp and a website chat widget only, with no voice calls.
- **You need the official WhatsApp Business API**, a green verified badge or many agents on one number at very high volume. Dailzero is not that product.
- **Answers depend on live stock or order tracking** and you have no developer. The agent works from what you type in unless your system is connected through webhook tools.

### What to do if the signs fit

Do not jump to the biggest plan. Do this instead:

1. Write out your ten common questions and the correct answers.
2. Start the 7-day free trial, which includes 1,000 credits, about 200 AI messages.
3. Link your existing number by QR code and test it from a friend's phone.
4. Watch the real chats for a week. Note which ones were handed to you and why.
5. Then choose: pay as you go if volume is light, or a monthly plan from ₦20,000 if it is steady.

If your number is brand new, use it normally for about two weeks before connecting it.

### Frequently asked questions

#### How many chats a day make an AI agent worth it?

There is no fixed number. A useful guide is whether replying takes more than an hour of your day or whether messages regularly wait more than an hour for an answer.

#### I already have a staff member replying. Do I still need it?

Possibly. If that person spends most of the day typing prices, the agent can take those chats and leave them the complaints, payment checks and follow-ups.
