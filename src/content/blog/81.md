A Nigerian accounting firm can automate the routine half of its WhatsApp client support: fee and service questions, document checklists, appointment booking, deadline reminders and the first reply to new enquiries. What it should not automate is advice. Anything that depends on a client's own books, their tax position or a letter from the tax office belongs with an accountant.

### Sort your client messages into three piles

Scroll back through a normal week of your firm's WhatsApp and sort each message.

**Pile one: the answer is the same for everybody.** What services you offer, what a company registration or annual returns filing costs at your firm, where your office is, your opening hours, which bank account to pay into, what documents a new client must bring. An AI agent can answer all of these from the information you type in.

**Pile two: you need details before anyone can help.** A new enquiry about bookkeeping, a request for a tax clearance certificate, someone who wants audited accounts for a loan application. The agent can collect the business name, the type of business, the service needed and the deadline, then pass a tidy summary to you.

**Pile three: it needs professional judgment.** How much tax a client owes, whether an expense is allowable, how to respond to a query or penalty notice, anything about a specific filing already in progress. The agent should hand these to a person immediately and say so.

### What a good automated reply looks like

A pile one message:

> Good morning. How much do you charge to file annual returns for a small company and what do I need to send?

A good reply states the fee you have set, sends your document checklist as a PDF, and asks for the company name so the request is logged. With Dailzero, the checklist comes from a media library you upload, so every client gets the same current version.

A pile three message:

> We just received a letter from the tax office saying we underpaid last year. What do we do?

A good reply does not attempt an answer. It says something like: "I am passing this to one of our accountants now. Please send a clear photo of the letter so they can read it before they call you." Then the chat is handed over. You can reply from the dashboard or your phone, and the AI stays paused on that chat until you switch it back on.

### Use it hardest in deadline season

Accounting work comes in waves. Before a filing deadline, clients send documents late and ask the same "is it too late?" question. Three things help:

- **Broadcast reminders to existing clients.** A text broadcast costs 5 credits per person, so reminding 150 clients costs 750 credits. Sends are paced to protect your number.
- **Appointment booking and reminders.** Clients can book a consultation in the chat and get a reminder before it.
- **Returning-client memory.** The agent remembers earlier chats, so a client does not have to reintroduce their company each time.

Broadcasts go only to people who have already messaged you. They are not a tool for cold marketing to a bought list.

### Limits you should plan around

- **It cannot see your accounting software or your filing status.** If a client asks "have you filed my VAT?", the agent only knows what you typed in. Out of the box, that question should be a handoff. A developer can connect your own practice system through [webhook tools](/developers) if you want live status answers.
- **It does not confirm payments.** It can share your account details or a payment link you provide, then hand the chat to you to confirm the transfer.
- **Client documents arrive in WhatsApp as normal.** The agent understands photos clients send, but your firm still needs its own process for filing and storing them.

On confidentiality, give each staff member their own login and keep the linked phone with a partner or office manager.

### A sensible first week

1. List your ten most repeated questions and write the firm's official answer to each.
2. Upload your fee schedule, engagement checklist and service brochure as documents.
3. Write the handoff rule: any question about a specific client's figures, filings or tax notices goes to a person.
4. Link the firm's WhatsApp number by QR code and test it from another phone with questions from all three piles.
5. Go live and read the handed-off chats daily to tune the answers.

### Frequently asked questions

#### Can the AI give clients tax advice?

No, and it should not be set up to try. It can explain what your firm does and what you need from the client, then hand anything account-specific to an accountant.

#### Can it tell a client whether their returns have been filed?

Not by itself, because it cannot see your records. Either treat status questions as a handoff or have a developer connect your system through webhook tools.
