A fintech should use AI on WhatsApp for the first response to a fraud inquiry, not for the investigation. The AI's job is to reply within seconds, tell the customer how to secure the account, collect the facts your fraud desk needs, and hand the chat to a trained person. It should never decide whether fraud happened, promise a refund, or ask for a PIN or OTP.

### What the AI can and cannot do in a fraud case

**It can:**

- Reply at once, at any hour, so the customer knows the report has landed.
- Give the safety steps you have approved: how to block a card or freeze the account in your app, and your official emergency line.
- Ask for the facts: date, time, amount, the recipient's name if shown, and a screenshot. It can read screenshots and voice notes.
- Mark the chat for a human and stop replying once your officer takes over.

**It cannot:**

- See the customer's account, transactions or card status. Dailzero has no access to your core system unless a developer connects it.
- Block an account, reverse a transfer or raise a dispute with another bank.
- Confirm that a debit was fraudulent or say whose fault it was.
- Verify identity in any way that would satisfy your own KYC rules.

### A first-response script that works

Here is the shape of a good exchange.

> Somebody just remove 85,000 from my account!! I no do any transfer. Please help

A good reply does four things in order:

1. **Acknowledges and calms.** "I'm sorry about this. I'm passing it to our fraud team now."
2. **Secures.** Gives the exact steps to freeze the account or block the card in your app, plus your emergency phone line.
3. **Collects.** Asks for the time of the debit, the amount, and a screenshot of the alert or transaction page.
4. **Sets expectations.** Says a member of the team will take over the chat, using the response time you have actually committed to, and reminds the customer never to share a PIN, OTP or password with anyone, including support.

Then it hands over, and your officer opens the chat with the details already there.

The second common case is the customer who is the target, not yet the victim.

> Someone called me saying he is from your office and I should read out the code sent to my phone. Is it true?

Good reply: no, your staff never ask for codes; do not share it; here is the only official number and the only official WhatsApp line; if you already shared the code, freeze your account now using these steps and tell us. This one the AI can finish alone, because the answer is the same for everyone.

### Rules to write into the agent's instructions

- Never request a PIN, OTP, password, full card number or CVV. If a customer sends one anyway, tell them to change it immediately.
- Never state a cause, a liability decision or a refund timeline that your policy does not guarantee.
- Never say "your account is now blocked" unless your system has confirmed it.
- Always hand over when a message mentions an unauthorised debit, a stolen phone, a compromised account or a scam.

### How to set it up

1. Write your fraud first-response wording with your compliance and fraud leads. Keep it short.
2. Add it to the agent alongside your general FAQs, and state the handover triggers explicitly.
3. Give your fraud officers their own logins so handed-over chats reach the right people, and agree who covers nights and weekends. An instant AI reply followed by twelve hours of silence makes things worse.

The general setup process is on the [how it works page](/how-it-works), and round-the-clock cover is discussed in [how to set up 24/7 customer support for a fintech on WhatsApp](/blog/how-to-set-up-247-customer-support-for-your-nigerian-fintech-on-whatsapp).

### If you want the AI to do more

With a developer, you can connect your backend through webhook tools so the agent can, for example, log a fraud ticket in your system and return its reference number. Whether an AI may trigger something as serious as an account freeze is a decision for your risk team. See the [developer documentation](/developers).

### Where this approach does not fit

Dailzero links to your number the way WhatsApp Web does. It has no verified badge, which larger institutions often want because of impersonation risk, and it is not built for a bank with millions of customers. Those organisations should look at the official WhatsApp Business Platform.

### Frequently asked questions

#### Can AI detect fraud from a WhatsApp conversation?

No. It can recognise that a customer is reporting fraud and respond correctly. Detecting fraud in transactions is a job for your monitoring systems and fraud team.

#### What if the report comes in at night and no officer is online?

The AI still replies, gives the securing steps and collects details. You need an on-call arrangement or a clear, honest response time for the human follow-up.

#### Will the AI keep replying after my officer joins the chat?

No. It pauses on that chat when a person replies from the dashboard or the phone, and you switch it back on when the case is done.
