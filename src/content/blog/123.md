### Why Nigerian businesses look beyond WATI

WATI is an established platform for business conversations on the official WhatsApp Business API, with a shared inbox, AI agents, broadcasts and over 100 integrations. For a larger team it is a strong choice. As of November 2026 its pricing page lists plans at $39, $79 and $199 a month when billed yearly, with extra users, extra numbers and add-ons priced separately, and Meta's template message charges on top. Check wati.io for current prices.

For a small Nigerian business, three things usually cause the search:

- **The bill is in dollars** and moves with the exchange rate.
- **Meta's message fees and add-ons** make the monthly total hard to predict.
- **The official API changes how the number works**, when the owner only wanted customers answered on the number they already use.

### 1. Dailzero: for owners who want to keep their number

Dailzero links to your WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business number by QR code or pairing code, the way WhatsApp Web does. The AI agent answers from your own prices and products, sends photos, takes orders, books appointments and hands over to you when a person is needed. On this route there are no Meta fees and no overage. When credits finish, the agent pauses.

It also does things the official API cannot: schedule WhatsApp Status, save the contacts who message you and answer Status replies. And if you later need the official route, Dailzero's app is approved by Meta and the team sets it up on request.

**Where WATI is still better:** shared inbox for a large team, a flow builder, a long list of integrations and a verified badge through Meta. The full comparison is in [Dailzero vs WATI](/blog/dailzero-vs-wati).

### 2. Neria: official platform, priced per chat

Neria says it connects your WhatsApp Business number through Meta's official platform and sells packs of chats in Naira, with the first 10 chats free and no setup fee. It also offers AI phone call agents. It suits a business that wants the official route without a dollar subscription. See [Dailzero vs Neria](/blog/dailzero-vs-neria).

### 3. Zella: low-cost AI selling with Instagram

Zella lists AI replies on WhatsApp and Instagram DMs, automatic receipts and broadcasts. It suits a small vendor with low volume who sells on both apps.

### 4. ChatRouter: the nearest like-for-like on the official API

ChatRouter describes a bot builder, flow builder, intelligent routing, team inbox, template manager and bulk messaging on the official WhatsApp Cloud API. If you liked WATI's structure but want a Nigerian provider billing in Naira, start here.

### 5. Evolve: set up for you

Evolve builds your flows, trains an AI assistant on your material and, on its top plan, adds a CRM and pipeline with team seats. It runs on the official Cloud API, and Meta bills its message fees to you directly.

### 6. respond.io: when you have outgrown WATI

Respond.io unifies WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger, TikTok, email and calls in one inbox. It costs more than WATI's entry plan, so it is an alternative for bigger teams, not a cheaper one. See [Dailzero vs respond.io](/blog/dailzero-vs-respond-io).

### How to choose

1. **Decide on the connection first.** If compliance, approved templates or a verified badge matter, stay on the official API with ChatRouter, Evolve, Neria or respond.io. If you want your existing number answered with no Meta fees, choose a linked-device tool such as Dailzero.
2. **Decide who replies.** A team of agents needs a shared inbox. An owner who wants the AI to do the selling needs an agent.
3. **Add up the full monthly cost**: plan, extra users, add-ons and Meta fees, in the currency you earn.
4. **Test with real customers.** Dailzero's 7-day trial includes 1,000 credits, about 200 AI replies. Plans are on the [pricing page](/pricing).

One honest note: a linked-device connection is not officially supported by WhatsApp for automation and carries more risk than the official API. Dailzero paces every send and warms up new numbers, but no tool can promise a number will never be restricted.

### Frequently asked questions

#### What is the best WATI alternative in Nigeria?

It depends on why you are switching. For Naira pricing and an AI agent on your existing number, Dailzero fits most small businesses. For the official API in Naira, look at ChatRouter, Evolve or Neria.

#### Is there a WATI alternative with no Meta fees?

Dailzero's linked-device route has no Meta message fees, because it links to the number in your own app. Any tool on the official WhatsApp Business Platform comes with Meta's fees.

#### Which WATI alternatives use the official WhatsApp API?

ChatRouter, Evolve, Neria and respond.io describe an official API connection on their sites. Dailzero offers it on request alongside its default linked-device route.

#### Can I move my number from WATI to another tool?

Usually yes, but the steps depend on where you are going. A number registered on the official platform has to be set up for normal app use before a linked-device tool can connect to it. Ask both providers before you start.
