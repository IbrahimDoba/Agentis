The best WhatsApp AI setup for a solo freelancer in Nigeria is the smallest one that works: one work number, pay as you go credits instead of a subscription, a short brief covering your services and starting rates, and a strict rule that the AI hands the chat to you the moment a lead is serious. You do not need a monthly plan until enquiries are steady.

Here is each part of that setup and why it is chosen.

### The number: one you use for work

The agent replies on the number you link, so link the number clients use, not the one your family and friends message. 

If you buy a new SIM for work, use it normally for about two weeks before connecting it. A brand-new number that starts sending automated replies on day one is more likely to be restricted by WhatsApp.

Linking is done by scanning a QR code or entering a pairing code, the same way you link WhatsApp Web. Your phone stays the main device and should come online regularly.

### The plan: free trial, then pay as you go

A freelancer's enquiries are irregular: fifteen one week, three the next. A subscription makes little sense for that pattern.

Start with the 7-day free trial, which gives 1,000 credits, about 200 AI messages. After that, buy credits in packs. They stay valid for 12 months, so a slow month does not waste money.

A rough estimate: say you get 5 enquiries a day and each needs about 8 replies. That is 5 × 8 × 5 credits × 30 days = 6,000 credits a month. The ₦10,000 pack gives 6,500 credits, which covers it. At 2 enquiries a day the same sum gives 2,400 credits, inside the ₦5,000 pack of 3,200.

### The brief: what to tell the agent

Keep it short and honest. The agent only knows what you give it.

- What you do, and what you do not do. "Logo and brand identity design. No printing."
- Starting prices or ranges in Naira, if you are comfortable sharing them. "Logos start from" a figure you choose is enough.
- Your process: deposit before work starts, number of revisions, typical turnaround.
- How payment works: bank transfer details or a Paystack link you provide.
- A rate card or portfolio PDF and a few sample images, uploaded to the media library so the agent can send them when asked.

### The three questions it should ask every lead

Tell the agent to collect the same three things before it hands over:

1. What is the project?
2. When do you need it?
3. What budget are you working with?

That turns a vague opener into something you can quote on. For example:

> Hello, I saw your page. I need a website for my school. How much?

A good reply: "Thanks for reaching out. School websites start from the price on our rate card, depending on the number of pages. To give you a proper quote, how many pages do you have in mind, and when do you need it live?" The agent sends the rate card and, once the lead answers, passes the chat to you.

### The handoff rule: you close, the AI filters

The client is hiring you. The AI's job is to answer quickly and collect the brief. It should not negotiate, agree a discount or promise a delivery date.

Set it to hand over when:

- The lead has answered the three questions.
- Anyone asks for a discount or a custom price.
- An existing client messages about work in progress.
- The customer asks to speak to you.

When you reply from your phone or the dashboard, the AI pauses on that chat so you are not talking over each other. Switch it back on when you are done.

### What to leave out

- **Broadcasts.** They are useful once you have a few hundred past contacts. At the start, they just burn credits.
- **A monthly plan.** Consider Basic at ₦20,000 only when your top-ups pass that figure in a month.
- **Automated follow-ups.** These are on the Pro plan. For a solo freelancer, checking the leads list in the dashboard each morning and following up by hand is enough.

Maintenance is five minutes each morning: check the flagged leads, reply to anything handed off overnight and read one full chat to see whether an answer needs fixing.

### Frequently asked questions

#### Can I use my personal WhatsApp number?

Technically yes, but it is better to link a number you use only for work, so the agent is not answering personal chats. If you must share one number, put a WhatsApp label on your personal chats and set that label to AI off; the agent stays silent on any chat carrying it.

#### How much should a solo freelancer budget?

At 2 to 5 enquiries a day, the formula above lands on a ₦5,000 or ₦10,000 pay as you go pack a month. Use your own chat count.

#### Will the AI quote prices I have not approved?

It works from the prices and ranges you give it. Tell it plainly that custom quotes come from you, and it will hand those chats over.

#### Do I need a website or any coding?

No. Setup is done in the dashboard without code. If you do have a website, the same agent can run as a chat widget on it.
