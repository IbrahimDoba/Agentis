Hotel no-shows fall when three things happen on WhatsApp before arrival day: the guest pays something to hold the room, receives a reminder with a question they have to answer, and has an easy way to say they are no longer coming. Automation helps with all three, though it cannot take the deposit for you.

### Work out what a no-show costs you

Use your own numbers. Say your standard room is ₦45,000 a night and four reservations a month never arrive. That is ₦180,000 of rooms held for nobody, and on a busy weekend you probably turned away a walk-in guest for each one.

Count your own no-shows for last month before you change anything. The figure tells you how strict your policy needs to be.

### Why guests do not turn up, and the fix for each

#### They booked two or three hotels to keep their options open

A reservation that costs nothing to make costs nothing to abandon. The fix is a deposit: one night, or a fixed percentage, paid by transfer or Paystack link to confirm the room. Have the AI agent state the policy in the same message as the rate, so it never comes as a surprise later.

#### Their plans changed and telling you felt awkward

A WhatsApp message asking a simple question is easier to answer honestly than a phone call. Give them the words: reply CHANGE or CANCEL.

#### They forgot the details

A guest who booked three weeks ago for a wedding in Port Harcourt may not remember which hotel or which date. A reminder with the hotel name, the date, the room type and a location pin fixes that.

#### They arrived late and the room was gone

If your policy releases unpaid rooms at 6pm, a guest whose flight was delayed becomes a no-show through no fault of theirs. Ask for an arrival time when the booking is made, and again in the reminder.

### The message sequence that does the work

1. **At booking.** The agent quotes the rate, explains the deposit and the release time, and collects the guest's name, dates and expected arrival time.
2. **When the deposit is sent.** The guest sends a receipt. The agent hands the chat to your reservations staff, who check the bank alert and confirm.
3. **Before arrival.** A reminder goes out with the booking details and a question.
4. **On the day.** If the guest has not replied or arrived by your cut-off, staff call once, then release the room.

A reminder that gets answered looks like this:

> Good afternoon Mrs Bello. This is a reminder of your Deluxe room at our Ikeja hotel tomorrow, Friday, for 2 nights. What time should we expect you? If your plans have changed, reply CHANGE or CANCEL and we will sort it out.

And when the guest replies that their flight now lands at 9pm, the agent answers:

> Thank you for letting us know. I have noted a 9pm arrival and passed it to the front desk, so your room will be held. Safe trip.

### Write a deposit policy guests accept

A policy only works if it is short enough to fit in a chat. For example: a one-night deposit confirms the room, free date changes up to 48 hours before arrival, and unpaid reservations are released at 6pm.

A decision to waive the deposit for a returning guest or corporate client should stay with a manager.

After a month, count your no-shows again. If they are down but so are bookings, your deposit is too heavy for your market.

### Frequently asked questions

#### Can the AI collect the deposit itself?

No. It shares the bank details or Paystack link you provide and passes the chat to you to confirm that the money arrived.

#### Will reminders annoy guests?

One clear reminder with the booking details is useful. Several messages in a day is what annoys people.

#### What if the guest never replies to the reminder?

Have staff call once on arrival day. If there is still no answer and no deposit, release the room at the cut-off time you stated at booking.
