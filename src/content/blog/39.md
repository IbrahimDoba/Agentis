You can handle guest inquiries around the clock without hiring a night receptionist for the phone by linking an AI agent to your hotel's WhatsApp number. It answers in seconds at any hour using your rates and policies, and hands a person only the few chats that need one. For a small hotel this typically fits a ₦20,000 a month plan.

The clearest way to see it is to walk through one night.

### One night at a 20-room hotel in Abuja

**9:50pm.** Your receptionist is checking in a late arrival. A message comes in from someone planning a trip next week, asking for rates and whether you are close to the Central Business District. The agent replies with both room rates, your address in Wuse 2 with landmarks, and room photos. The receptionist never touches the phone.

**12:40am.** A traveller whose flight was delayed writes:

> I just landed in Abuja, flight was delayed. Do you have a room for tonight? I can come now.

This is the message that used to be lost. The agent replies at once:

> Welcome to Abuja. Our Standard room is ₦32,000 for the night. I am checking with the night desk that a room is ready for you right now. Please send your name and how soon you will arrive.

It collects the name and arrival time and hands the chat to the night duty staff, who confirm the room is free. The agent cannot see your room chart by itself, so this one does need a person, but only for a single yes, not the whole conversation. The rate is an example; the agent quotes what you enter.

**2:15am.** Someone in the UK planning a December visit asks about a two-week stay and long-stay discounts. The agent gives the standard rate, notes the dates, and tells the guest the manager will send a long-stay quote in the morning. The inquiry is waiting in the leads list at 8am.

**5:30am.** A guest asks for a 6am airport drop-off. The agent gives your shuttle price and hands the chat to the duty staff to arrange the driver.

Four chats. Two needed nobody. Two needed a person for one decision each.

### What can run unattended and what cannot

Fully handled by the agent:

- Rates, room photos and what is included
- Directions, check-in times, breakfast hours and facilities
- Collecting booking details for future dates
- Sharing your bank details or payment link

Needs a person, even at night:

- Confirming a room for tonight
- Confirming a payment has landed
- Anything physical: a faulty AC, extra towels, a locked-out guest
- Complaints
- Emergencies

### The night handoff rule

"Without extra staff" does not mean without anyone. Most hotels already have somebody on site overnight, a night porter, security supervisor or duty manager. Give that person one job with the phone: respond to handed-over chats.

Set it up like this:

1. Tell the agent which situations to hand over at night and which can wait until morning. A long-stay quote waits. A same-night arrival does not.
2. Give the duty person their own login, or keep the hotel phone with them. When they reply, the AI pauses on that chat.
3. For emergencies, tell the agent to give the duty phone line immediately. The agent does not make voice calls.
4. In the morning, the front desk opens the leads list and the handed-over chats before anything else.

If you truly have nobody awake, say so in the agent's instructions. It is better to tell a midnight arrival that the desk reopens at 6am than to promise a room no one can open.

### What it costs

A worked example: 25 chats a day across guests and inquiries, about 6 replies each, at 5 credits per text reply.

25 × 6 × 5 × 30 days = 22,500 credits a month.

That fits the Basic plan: ₦20,000 a month for 25,000 credits. If a quarter of those replies are photos at 8 credits each, the total rises to about 25,900 credits, slightly over, so you would add a small pay-as-you-go top-up or step up to Starter at ₦40,000 if you are growing. The hotel phone is the main device for the linked number and should come online regularly, like WhatsApp Web. Keep it charging on inverter power and connected to Wi-Fi with data as backup, and check your credit balance before public holidays and December. ### Frequently asked questions

#### Can it take a booking for tonight with no staff involved?

It collects everything, but a person should confirm a same-night room, because the agent does not see live availability unless a developer has connected your booking system.

#### What if a guest calls instead of chatting?

The agent handles WhatsApp messages only, not voice calls. Calls still ring on the hotel phone.
