The near future of PropTech in Nigeria is less about new apps and more about the chat: property search, inquiry, inspection booking and follow-up are moving into WhatsApp, with AI doing the first reply. What will not change soon is the part that needs a human and a file: verifying title, inspecting the site and signing documents.

This is a reasoned view, not a forecast with numbers. Nobody has reliable figures for where Nigerian property technology will be in five years.

### Where things stand now

Most Nigerian property deals already start the same way. A buyer or tenant sees a listing on a portal, Instagram or a WhatsApp Status, then sends a message. From that point, the "technology" is a salesperson's phone.

That phone is the weak point. Inquiries arrive at night and on Sundays, the same ten questions get typed again and again, and good leads sit beside time wasters in one long chat list. The first wave of useful PropTech here is not virtual reality tours. It is fixing that phone.

### Three shifts that are already underway

#### 1. First reply by AI, on the number you already use

An AI agent linked to your WhatsApp number can answer price and title questions in seconds, send the brochure, and book an inspection, at 11pm as easily as 11am. This is available now, at prices a two-person agency can pay. On Dailzero, monthly plans start at ₦20,000, and there is a 7-day free trial.

> Is the 3 bed in Gwarinpa still available? What is the total package?

The reply that used to wait until morning now goes out in seconds, with the rent, fees and caution deposit listed and Saturday inspection slots offered.

#### 2. Ads that open a chat instead of a website

Click-to-WhatsApp ads on Facebook and Instagram skip the landing page. The buyer taps and is in your chat. Tools can now tell you which ad each chat came from, so you can compare ads by serious inquiries, not just clicks.

#### 3. Your contact list becomes your marketing channel

Every person who messages you is a contact who can see your Status. Firms that post new listings to Status daily, and broadcast price changes to existing contacts, depend less on paying for portal placement every time. Scheduled Status posting makes this routine instead of a task someone forgets.

### What is likely next

These are directions, not promises.

- **Agents connected to your own records.** Today most AI agents work from what you typed in. The next step is the agent checking your own system mid-chat: is plot 14 still unsold, how much has this subscriber paid on their instalment plan. Dailzero already supports this through webhook tools, but it needs a developer and a system worth connecting to. Many firms still keep allocations in a spreadsheet or a notebook, so the real work is tidying your records first.
- **Voice notes as a normal input.** Many buyers prefer to talk. AI that understands a voice note in everyday Nigerian English or Pidgin removes a barrier that text-only bots had.

### What technology will not fix soon

It would be dishonest to write about the future without this part.

- **Title verification.** No chat tool can tell a buyer that a C of O is genuine or that land is free of government acquisition. That still takes a search at the land registry and a lawyer.
- **Physical inspection.** Photos and videos help buyers shortlist. People still want to stand on the land before paying.
- **Payment confirmation.** An AI agent can share your account details or a payment link. It does not see your bank. A person confirms the money arrived.
- **Trust.** Buyers have been burned by fake agents and double-sold plots. Fast replies help, but your documents, your office and your track record do the convincing.

Any vendor telling you AI will close property sales with no humans involved is selling something that does not exist.

### What a property firm should do this year

1. **Write down your answers.** Prices, payment plans, title status per estate, fees, inspection days. This document is useful for staff even if you never use AI.
2. **Fix first response before anything else.** Put an AI agent on your main WhatsApp line and measure how many inquiries now get an answer within a minute. The [how it works page](/how-it-works) shows the setup.
3. **Clean your records.** A single, current list of what is available and what is sold. Every later automation depends on it.
4. **Build your contact list on purpose.** Save everyone who messages you and post to Status consistently.

### Frequently asked questions

#### Will AI replace estate agents in Nigeria?

No. It takes over repetitive first replies and scheduling. Inspections, negotiation, documentation and building trust remain human work, and agents who respond faster with AI will likely take business from those who do not.

#### Do I need a developer to start?

Not for answering inquiries, sending brochures and booking inspections. You need a developer only when you want the agent to read from your own database, through the [developer API](/developers).

#### What is the cheapest sensible first step?

Write your standard answers, then run a free trial of an AI agent on your existing number for a week and see how many inquiries it handles without you.
