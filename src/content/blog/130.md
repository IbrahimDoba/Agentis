Pay as you go WhatsApp AI means you buy credits when you need them and pay no monthly subscription. On Dailzero, packs run from ₦1,000 for 600 credits to ₦200,000 for 140,000 credits, and the credits stay valid for 12 months. A typical AI text reply uses about 5 credits. It suits businesses with low, seasonal or unpredictable chat volume. If your volume is steady, a monthly plan costs less per reply.

The reply counts divide the credits by 5. Replies that send an image cost 8 credits and a video 12, so real numbers are a little lower if your agent sends many photos. You pick one of these packs. Custom amounts are not offered.

### How it works

1. Create a Dailzero account and set up your agent.
2. Link your WhatsApp number by QR code or pairing code.
3. Buy a pack in the dashboard. Payment is in Naira through Paystack, by card or bank transfer.
4. The agent replies and the balance goes down as it works.
5. When the balance reaches zero, the agent pauses. Top up and it resumes.

There is no overage fee and no automatic charge. You are never billed for more than the pack you bought.

### The 12 month validity

Credits stay valid for 12 months. Each new top-up starts a fresh 12 months for your balance, so a business that tops up at least once a year does not lose credits. Credits only lapse if you buy nothing for a full year.

Pay as you go credits also work across all your agents.

### What you can spend them on

The same things as plan credits:

- AI text replies, about 5 credits each
- Images and documents at 8 credits, videos at 12
- Broadcasts: 5 credits per person for text, 8 for an image, 12 for a video
- WhatsApp Status posts: 5 for text, 8 for an image, 12 for a video, with no per-recipient charge
- Saving contacts, 1 credit each
- Developer API calls, which are billed against the same credits

### Worked example: a seasonal business

A cake baker gets about 5 chats a day in a normal month and 25 a day in December. Each chat needs about 6 AI replies.

- Normal month: 5 × 6 = 30 replies a day. 30 × 5 = 150 credits a day. 150 × 30 = 4,500 credits.
- December: 25 × 6 = 150 replies a day. 150 × 5 = 750 credits a day. 750 × 31 = 23,250 credits.
- Year: 11 normal months × 4,500 = 49,500 credits. 49,500 + 23,250 = 72,750 credits.

One ₦100,000 pack gives 68,000 credits and one ₦5,000 pack gives 3,200. Together that is 71,200 credits for ₦105,000. A further ₦5,000 pack takes it to 74,400 credits for ₦110,000, which covers the year.

The Basic plan for 12 months would cost ₦20,000 × 12 = ₦240,000. For this baker, pay as you go is less than half the price, because most months are quiet.

### When a monthly plan is cheaper

Compare what ₦20,000 buys:

- Pay as you go: 13,000 credits
- Basic plan: 25,000 credits a month

So if you use more than about 13,000 credits every month, Basic costs less. That is roughly 14 chats a day at 6 replies each, since 14 × 6 × 5 × 30 = 12,600 credits. On a plan, each credit costs ₦0.80 on Basic and about ₦0.67 on Starter, against ₦1.43 to ₦1.67 on pay as you go.

You can also mix the two. Plan credits are used first, then your pay as you go balance. Many businesses keep a small balance as a cushion for a busy week. See [Dailzero pricing explained](/blog/dailzero-pricing-explained).

### Who pay as you go suits

- **New businesses** still learning how many chats they get
- **Seasonal sellers**: bakers, event vendors, school suppliers, Christmas and Sallah sellers
- **Side businesses** with a few enquiries a day
- **Developers** testing the API before building on it
- **Anyone who dislikes subscriptions** and wants to see the money go only when work is done

### Who it does not suit

- **Steady, busy numbers.** Above about 13,000 credits a month you are overpaying.
- **Teams that need two agents and several logins.** The Pro plan is built for that.
- **Businesses with a few chats a day who reply quickly themselves.** The free tools in the WhatsApp Business app may be enough. See [the cheapest way to automate WhatsApp replies in Nigeria](/blog/cheapest-way-to-automate-whatsapp-replies-in-nigeria).

### Frequently asked questions

#### What is the smallest amount I can pay?

₦1,000, which buys 600 credits, about 120 AI text replies.

#### Do pay as you go credits expire?

They stay valid for 12 months. Each top-up starts a new 12 months, so regular users do not lose credits.

#### Is there a monthly fee on pay as you go?

No. There is no subscription. You pay only when you buy a pack.

#### What happens when the credits finish?

The agent pauses and customers' messages still arrive in your WhatsApp as normal. Nothing is charged until you choose to top up.

#### Can I move from pay as you go to a plan later?

Yes. You can choose Basic, Starter or Pro at any time on the [pricing page](/pricing). Plan credits are used first and your pay as you go balance stays as a backup.
