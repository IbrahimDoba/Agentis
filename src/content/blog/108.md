Not much. Every tool here is online, so a shop in Ikeja and one in Ibadan get the same product. What matters in Lagos is volume and speed. ### Frequently asked questions

#### How fast can a Lagos business start automating WhatsApp?

With a linked-device tool and an established number, the same day. With a tool built for you on the official platform, plan for one to three weeks.

#### Do I need a new SIM?

No. Dailzero links the WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business number you already use. Some owners choose a second number for automation to protect their main line.

#### Do I need a registered company or CAC documents?

Not for Dailzero's linked-device route, which needs no Meta verification. For the official platform, ask the provider what Meta requires for your business.

#### What does it cost to try?

Dailzero has a 7-day free trial with 1,000 credits, about 200 AI replies. Most of the other tools here also list a free trial on their sites.

#### Can I switch tools later?

Yes, but linked-device and official tools use your number differently. Ask the provider how the switch works for your number before you start.
