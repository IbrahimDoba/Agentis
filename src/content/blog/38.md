The best AI tools for a Nigerian hotel or guest house fall into four groups: a WhatsApp AI agent to answer guests, a general AI assistant for writing, a design tool with AI features for photos and flyers, and, for larger properties, hotel management software with its own smart features. Most guest houses need only the first two to start.

This guide is organised by the job you need done, because "best" depends on your size. It does not quote other companies' prices, which change often.

### Job 1: answering guest messages on WhatsApp

For most Nigerian hotels this is the highest-value job, because WhatsApp is where guests ask for rates and where slow replies lose bookings.

There are two kinds of tool:

- **Linked-device AI agents**, such as Dailzero. You connect the WhatsApp number you already use by QR code or pairing code. There are no Meta per-conversation fees and no template approval.- **Official WhatsApp Business Platform tools.** These run on Meta's official API. They suit very high volume, a verified green tick and many staff on one number, and they come with Meta's own fees and template rules.

What Dailzero does for a hotel: replies in seconds, 24/7, with your rates and policies; sends room photos, menus and price lists; understands voice notes and photos; takes booking details; books appointments and sends reminders; lists likely bookers as leads; and hands the chat to staff when needed. Plans start at ₦20,000 a month, with a 7-day free trial and pay as you go from ₦1,000. See the [features page](/features).

What it does not do: it does not see your room availability or connect to hotel software out of the box, it does not process payments, and it does not make calls or handle Instagram DMs or email.

### Job 2: writing and replying

General AI assistants such as ChatGPT, Gemini and Claude are useful to a hotel manager for work that is not live chat:

- Drafting a calm reply to a harsh online review
- Writing room descriptions for your website and listings
- Turning your scattered policies into a clean guest information sheet
- Drafting a corporate rate proposal for a company in Victoria Island

Always read and edit before sending. These tools write well but do not know your hotel, and they can state things that are wrong.

### Job 3: photos, flyers and Status content

Guests choose rooms by photo. Design tools with AI features, such as Canva, help a small team produce a Sallah weekend flyer or a clean rate card without a designer.

One caution: do not use AI to make a room look like something it is not. Brighten and straighten, do not invent.

Once you have the content, schedule it. Dailzero posts WhatsApp Status on a schedule to the contacts who have messaged you, up to 2,000 contacts per post on Basic, 5,000 on Starter and 10,000 on Pro.

### Job 4: running rooms, rates and bookings

A hotel with 40 rooms and listings on several booking sites needs hotel management software and usually a channel manager, so that a room sold on one site closes on the others.

Ask for a demo, and ask how it behaves when the internet or power drops. A 10-room guest house running on a booking register may not need it yet.

Dailzero has no native integration with hotel management systems. If yours has an API, a developer can connect it through webhook tools so the WhatsApp agent can check availability. See the [developers page](/developers).

### Three questions to ask before paying for any AI tool

1. **Can I pay in Naira?** Dollar billing on a Naira income is a moving cost. Dailzero bills in Naira through Paystack, by card or bank transfer.
2. **What happens when it does not know the answer?** It should hand the guest to a person, not guess.
3. **What does it cost at my volume?** Ask for the price at your real number of chats, not the entry price.

### Who Dailzero is not the right fit for

If you need a verified green tick, dozens of agents on one number, or live room availability with no developer to connect your system, a linked-device agent is not the full answer. The official WhatsApp Business Platform, or your hotel software's own messaging module, will suit you better.

### Frequently asked questions

#### What is the single most useful AI tool for a small guest house?

A WhatsApp AI agent on your existing number, because unanswered rate inquiries are usually the biggest leak. Add a general AI assistant for writing.

#### Are free AI tools good enough?

For writing and editing, free tiers of general assistants go a long way. For live guest chat you need a tool built for WhatsApp; Dailzero's trial is free for 7 days with 1,000 credits.
