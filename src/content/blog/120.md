Dailzero and ChatRouter both automate WhatsApp for Nigerian businesses, but they start from different places. ChatRouter is a messaging platform built on the official WhatsApp Cloud API, with a bot builder, visual flows, message routing to departments and a team inbox. Dailzero is an AI agent that links to the number you already use and holds the conversation without flows to build. Choose ChatRouter if you want the official API with structured flows and routing. Choose Dailzero if you want an AI answering and selling on your own number the same day.

### Where ChatRouter is the better choice

- **You need the official API.** ChatRouter says it is built on the official WhatsApp Cloud API. For a business that must use Meta's own route, that is the starting requirement.
- **You have departments.** Its routing sends a message to sales, support or billing by intent, keyword or customer type. This suits a company where different teams own different questions.
- **You want to design exact flows.** A visual flow builder gives you control over every step, which matters when the process is fixed, such as a form or a payment step.
- **You send template campaigns.** A template manager, scheduling and bulk messaging fit a business that sends approved notices to large lists.
- **You also send SMS.** ChatRouter lists bulk SMS alongside WhatsApp.
- **You want to plug in your own AI workflows.** It lists OpenAI and Flowise integrations for teams that build their own logic.

### Where Dailzero is the better choice

- **You do not want to build flows.** You tell Dailzero about your business and add your products and prices. The agent then answers whatever the customer asks, in your chosen tone, without a flow chart behind it.
- **You want to keep your number in your app.** Dailzero links like WhatsApp Web. Your chats, your app and your customers' saved contact stay as they are.
- **You want no Meta fees.** On the linked-device route you pay only for Dailzero credits. When credits finish, the agent pauses. There are no overage fees.
- **You sell through Status.** Dailzero schedules Status posts, runs Autopilot from a gallery, saves the contacts who message you and answers Status replies.
- **You are an owner or a small team.** There is no verification, no template approval and no bot to maintain. Most businesses are live the same day.
- **Your volume is irregular.** Pay as you go starts at ₦1,000 with no subscription, and those credits last 12 months.

### What you give up with Dailzero

Dailzero does not have a visual flow builder or department routing. The AI decides how to answer from what you have told it, and it hands the chat to a person when it cannot help. If you need every step fixed in advance, a flow builder gives more control.

On the default route Dailzero is a linked device, not the official API. That means no approved templates, no verified badge and no uploading a list of numbers to message. WhatsApp does not officially support automating an account this way, so the risk is higher than on the official platform. Dailzero queues every message, sends at a human pace, warms up new numbers and slows down when WhatsApp signals a limit, but no tool can promise a number will never be restricted.

If you need the official route, Dailzero's app is approved by Meta and the team sets it up on request. Meta's fees apply there, and Status and contact saving are not available. Read [official WhatsApp API vs linked device](/blog/official-whatsapp-api-vs-linked-device-which-should-you-use).

### A quick way to decide

1. Does compliance require the official API, or do you send approved templates to large lists? Choose ChatRouter or another official provider.
2. Do several departments need messages routed to them automatically? Choose ChatRouter.
3. Are you an owner who wants customers answered and orders taken on your existing number, without building anything? Choose Dailzero.
4. Do you want Status scheduling and contact saving in the same tool? Choose Dailzero.

### Frequently asked questions

#### Is Dailzero a ChatRouter alternative?

For a small or medium business that wants an AI agent on its existing number, yes. For a business that needs flow building, department routing and template campaigns on the official API, ChatRouter is the closer fit.

#### Does ChatRouter use the official WhatsApp API?

Yes. Its website says it is built on the official WhatsApp Cloud API.

#### How much does ChatRouter cost?

We could not read plan prices on its pricing page in November 2026, so we do not quote any. Its page says plans are billed in Naira, monthly or yearly. Check with ChatRouter directly.

#### Do I need to build a chatbot flow on Dailzero?

No. You describe your business and add your products, prices and policies. The agent answers from that. See [WhatsApp AI agent vs keyword chatbot](/blog/whatsapp-ai-agent-vs-keyword-chatbot) for the difference.

#### Which one is faster to set up?

Dailzero on the linked-device route, because there is no Meta verification or template approval. An official API setup takes longer with any provider.
