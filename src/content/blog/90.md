Yes, you can set up a WhatsApp AI agent for your business in 7 days, and linking the number is only a QR scan. The week is for the real work: writing down what the agent should know, testing it with awkward questions and letting it loose on customers gradually. One exception: if your WhatsApp number is brand new, use it normally for about two weeks first, which makes it a three week job.

This plan uses Dailzero, where no code is needed.

### Before day 1: check your number

The agent links to the WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business number you already use, as a linked device, the same way WhatsApp Web connects.

- **If you have used the number with customers for months,** go straight to day 1.
- **If the SIM is new,** spend two weeks using it like a normal person would. Reply to customers by hand, save contacts, post Status. WhatsApp is more likely to restrict a fresh number that suddenly starts sending fast, automated replies. Do day 1 during those two weeks, so you lose no time.

### Day 1: collect what the agent needs to know

Do this on paper or in a notes app, before you even sign up, so your free trial days are not spent typing.

- Scroll through your last 50 chats and write down the questions that repeat.
- Write your official answer to each: prices in Naira, delivery fees by area, opening hours, payment methods, return or refund policy.
- Decide your tone: "Good afternoon ma" or "Hi there"?

### Day 2: sign up and enter your business details

[Create your account](/signup). The 7-day free trial gives 1,000 credits, about 200 AI messages, with no payment needed.

Describe the business, then add your products or services with prices, and paste in the questions and answers from day 1. Be specific. "Delivery to Lekki is ₦2,500 and takes 1 to 2 days" beats "we deliver nationwide".

### Day 3: upload photos and documents

Add product photos, short videos, your price list, menu or brochure to the media library. The agent sends these when customers ask.

Images use 8 credits and videos 12, against 5 for a text reply, so upload what customers really ask to see.

### Day 4: link your number and try to break it

Link the number by scanning the QR code or entering the pairing code. Then test from a different phone. Try the hard questions:

- A question in Pidgin: "Una dey deliver go Ibadan? How much e go cost?"
- A voice note asking for a price.
- A photo of a product with "do you have this one?"
- Something you never told it, like a discount for bulk orders.
- "I want to speak to a person."

The last two should end in a handoff, not a guess. When an answer is wrong or vague, fix the information behind it and ask again.

### Day 5: set the handoff rules and your team

Decide what the agent must never handle alone. For most businesses that list is:

1. Complaints and refund requests.
2. Payment confirmation. The agent can share your account details or Paystack link, but it cannot verify that money arrived. That chat comes to you.
3. Bargaining and custom quotes.
4. Order tracking, unless a developer has connected your system. The agent cannot see a rider's location or your stock by itself.

Give team members their own logins, and agree who checks handed-off chats and when. When any of you replies to a chat from the dashboard or the phone, the AI pauses on that chat until it is switched back on.

### Day 6: go live quietly

Let the agent answer real customers, and check the dashboard several times during the day.

Look for:

- Chats where the customer had to ask twice.
- Handoffs that happened because information was missing.
- Replies that are correct but too long.

Do not send a broadcast or run a promo today.

### Day 7: review and choose how to pay

Read ten full conversations from day 6. Fix what needs fixing. Then look at how many credits one day used and multiply by 30.

- Light or irregular volume: pay as you go packs start at ₦1,000 and credits last 12 months.
- Steady volume: Basic is ₦20,000 a month for 25,000 credits, about 5,000 AI messages.

### After the first week

- Keep your phone coming online regularly. It remains the main device.
- Update prices the same day they change. An agent quoting last month's price is worse than no agent.
- Check the leads list in the dashboard each morning and follow up.

### Frequently asked questions

#### Can I set it up in one day?

Technically yes, if your information is ready. The 7-day plan exists because testing and a quiet first live day prevent embarrassing wrong answers.

#### Why wait two weeks with a new number?

WhatsApp treats new numbers with more suspicion. Normal use first, then a gradual start, lowers the risk of restriction. No tool can promise a number will never be restricted.

#### Do I need a developer?

No. Setup needs no code. A developer is only needed if you want the agent to look things up in your own system, such as order status.
