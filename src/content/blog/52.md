WhatsApp AI raises an online store's sales in three ways: it answers every buyer within seconds, including the ones who message at midnight, it sends the product photo and price without waiting for you, and it follows up with people who asked and then went quiet. It does not create demand. It stops you losing the demand you already paid for with ads, content and referrals.

The easiest way to see this is to follow one ordinary day. Say you run a skincare store from Wuse, Abuja, selling on Instagram and WhatsApp, and you have linked your business number to an AI agent.

### One day in a store with the agent switched on

#### 6:50am, before you wake up

A customer who saw your post last night sends a voice note asking if you have something for dark spots and how much it costs. The agent understands voice notes, so it replies in text with the two products you listed for hyperpigmentation, the prices, and a photo of each from your media library. By the time you pick up your phone, she has already chosen one and sent her address in Gwarinpa.

#### 11:15am, while you are at the market

You are restocking and cannot type. Nine chats come in within an hour. The questions are the usual ones: price, whether it suits oily skin, delivery fee to Kubwa, whether you deliver to Lagos.

> How much is the vitamin C serum and do you deliver to Lokoja?

A good reply answers both parts and moves the sale forward:

> The vitamin C serum is ₦12,500. Yes, we send to Lokoja by park waybill, delivery is ₦3,000 and it arrives in 1 to 2 days. Would you like one bottle or the set of two at ₦23,000?

All nine get an answer in seconds, at the same time.

#### 2:30pm, a customer wants to pay

A buyer is ready and asks for the account number. The agent sends the bank details or Paystack link you gave it and tells her to send proof of payment. This is where you come in. Dailzero does not verify payments, so the chat is passed to you to confirm the transfer. You check your bank app, reply from your own phone, and the AI pauses on that chat while you do.

#### 5:00pm, yesterday's quiet customers

Three people asked for prices yesterday and never replied. On the Pro plan, the agent sends each an automated follow-up. On other plans, they still appear in the leads list in your dashboard, so you can message them yourself in two minutes instead of scrolling through chats trying to remember who was serious.

#### 9:40pm, a returning customer

Someone who bought a cleanser last month messages again. The agent remembers earlier chats, so she does not have to explain her skin type from scratch. It suggests the moisturiser that goes with what she bought. That is a second sale you did not have to chase.

#### 11:55pm, the late enquiry

A click from your Instagram ad lands in WhatsApp just before midnight. The agent answers, and the dashboard shows which ad the customer came from, so you know which advert is bringing buyers and which is only bringing likes.

### Where the extra sales actually come from

Looking back over that day, the gains came from four places:

- **Speed.** Buyers comparing vendors tend to go with whoever replies first with a clear price.
- **Coverage.** Early morning, market hours and late night were all answered.
- **Follow-up.** Quiet customers were not forgotten.
- **Repeat buyers.** Returning customers were recognised and offered the next product.

### What you still do yourself

- **Confirm payment.** The AI shares your details. You confirm the money arrived.
- **Keep stock information current.** Dailzero has no native link to Shopify, WooCommerce or an inventory system. If the serum is finished, update the product so the agent stops offering it.
- **Handle complaints and refunds.** The agent hands these to a person.

### Frequently asked questions

#### Will customers know they are talking to an AI?

The replies come from your own WhatsApp number in the tone you set. Whether you tell customers it is an assistant is your choice. Anyone who asks for a person is handed to you.

#### Does it work if I sell on Instagram?

It answers on WhatsApp, not in Instagram DMs. Put your WhatsApp link in your bio and use click-to-WhatsApp ads so the conversation happens where the agent is.

#### How do I know it is working?

Count orders and unanswered chats for a week before you start and for a week after. The 7-day free trial with 1,000 credits is enough to [test it on your own number](/signup).
