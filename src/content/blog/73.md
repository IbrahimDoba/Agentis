You scale an agency without hiring more customer service staff by taking the repeat messages off your people: an AI agent answers enquiries and routine client questions on WhatsApp, and your account managers spend their hours on the work clients pay for. A worked example below shows that doubling a mid-sized agency's chat volume moves a Dailzero bill from ₦40,000 to ₦75,000 a month, with no new seat to fill.

### Why message volume is what stops agencies growing

When an agency goes from 10 clients to 25, the creative work grows in a way you can plan for. The messages do not. Every new client brings "has the post gone up?", "please resend the invoice", "can we shift the meeting?", and every new campaign you run for yourself brings more prospects asking for prices. The usual fix is another client service executive, then another.

The alternative is to separate messages that need judgement from messages that need an answer you have already written down.

### The worked calculation

Take a hypothetical digital agency in Ikeja with 12 retainer clients.

**Today:**

- 20 new enquiries a day from ads and referrals.
- 25 chats a day from existing clients with routine questions.

90 chats × 6 replies × 5 credits × 30 days = 81,000 credits a month. That fits the Pro plan: 100,000 credits for ₦75,000 a month.

### What the agent takes off your team

- **Price and package questions**, answered from the packages you typed in, with your rate card sent as a PDF.
- **Qualifying new prospects:** business type, budget range, timeline, and which of your ads they clicked.
- **Booking and reminding** people about discovery calls and monthly reviews.
- **Standing answers for clients:** report dates, revision limits, office hours, payment details.
- **Chasing quiet prospects.** On Pro, automated follow-ups message people who stopped replying after receiving a quote.

A client message like this never needs to reach a person:

> Good morning, abeg when is the cut-off to send content for next week's posts?

> Good morning. Content for the following week should reach us by 12 noon on Thursday. Anything sent after that goes into the week after. Would you like the content brief template again?

### What you still need people for

An AI agent does not make an account manager unnecessary. It makes one account manager able to carry more accounts. Keep humans on:

- Strategy calls and campaign reviews.
- Complaints, and any client who sounds upset.
- Custom quotes, discounts and contract changes.
- Confirming payments. The agent can share your bank details but cannot see whether a transfer arrived.
- Anything that depends on live project status, unless a developer has connected your project system through webhook tools.

The agent hands the chat over when the client asks for a person or when it cannot help, and it pauses on that chat while you reply.

### Frequently asked questions

#### How many clients can one account manager handle with an AI agent?

There is no honest fixed number, because it depends on the service. Measure it: track how many hours a week each manager spends on routine messages before and after, and add accounts as those hours fall.

#### What happens if we run out of credits mid-month?

The AI pauses and messages still arrive in WhatsApp as normal, so your team can reply by hand. There are no overage fees. Top up with a pay-as-you-go pack or wait for renewal.

#### Do unused credits carry over in a quiet month?

Yes, up to 25% of your plan's monthly allowance carries into the next month.
