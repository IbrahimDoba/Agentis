### What WASBOT does, so you know what to replace

According to its own website in November 2026, WASBOT is a cloud WhatsApp marketing tool that connects as a linked device. It auto-saves contacts, schedules Status and group posts, sends paced broadcasts, runs keyword-triggered sequences and offers group admin tools across several numbers. Its plans were listed at ₦8,000, ₦20,000 and ₦50,000 a month, with a 7-day free trial. Check wasbot.app for current plans.

People usually look for an alternative for one of three reasons: they want customers answered by AI and not by keywords, they need the official API, or they also sell on Instagram.

### 1. Dailzero: the closest match, with AI added

Dailzero covers the WASBOT features most sellers use every day:

- **Status scheduling.** Post text, image or video Status now or later. Autopilot posts from a gallery on a plan you approve, up to 6 a day and at least 2 hours apart.
- **Contact saving.** It bulk-saves the people who message you so they can see your Status, at 1 credit per contact.
- **Status analytics.** Delivered, viewed, liked and replied. Views undercount, because people with read receipts off do not show.
- **Paced broadcasts** to contacts already on your WhatsApp.

Then it adds what a keyword tool cannot: an AI agent that answers from your own prices and products, sends photos, takes orders, books appointments and hands over to a person. It also answers people who reply to your Status. See [Dailzero vs WASBOT](/blog/dailzero-vs-wasbot) for the full comparison.

**Where it falls short of WASBOT:** no group management tools, one number per agent, no contact list uploads, and lower Status reach per post (2,000 on Basic, 5,000 on Starter, 10,000 on Pro). It is priced in credits, not flat limits.

### 2. Zella: AI selling on WhatsApp and Instagram

Zella describes itself as an AI sales assistant built in Lagos. Its site lists AI replies on WhatsApp, Instagram DMs, automatic receipts, voice note transcription, broadcasts and sales analytics. It is a fit if Instagram DMs matter as much as WhatsApp. It does not list Status scheduling or contact saving, so it replaces the reply side of WASBOT, not the Status side.

### 3. Neria: AI agent on the official platform

Neria says it connects your WhatsApp Business number through Meta's official platform and sells packs of chats, with the first 10 chats free. It also offers phone call agents, website chat, Instagram and Facebook. Choose it if you want the official route and pay per conversation. WhatsApp Status is not part of the official platform, so it is not a Status tool.

### 4. ChatRouter: bots and bulk messaging on the official API

ChatRouter says it is built on the official WhatsApp Cloud API, with a bot builder, flow builder, message routing, team inbox, template manager and bulk messaging. It suits a team that wants structured flows and approved templates.

### 5. Evolve: someone sets it up for you

Evolve lists three monthly plans covering automation, an AI assistant and a WhatsApp CRM, with setup done by its team over one to three weeks. It runs on the official Cloud API, so Meta's message fees are billed separately.

### 6. WATI: for bigger teams

WATI is an established official-API platform with a shared inbox, AI agents and over 100 integrations. It is billed in US dollars. See [WATI alternatives in Nigeria](/blog/wati-alternatives-in-nigeria).

### How to choose

1. If you want Status, contact saving and broadcasts on your own number and want the chats answered too, try Dailzero.
2. If groups and several numbers are your main work, WASBOT is still the stronger tool. Stay.
3. If compliance or approved templates matter, pick an official-API option: ChatRouter, Evolve, Neria or WATI. Dailzero can also set up the official route on request.
4. If Instagram DMs are a big sales channel, look at Zella.

### Frequently asked questions

#### What is the best WASBOT alternative in Nigeria?

It depends on what you need. For Status scheduling and contact saving with an AI agent on the same number, Dailzero is the closest match. For the official API, look at ChatRouter, Evolve, Neria or WATI.

#### Is there a WASBOT alternative with AI replies?

Yes. Dailzero, Zella, Neria and Evolve all describe AI replies on their own sites. Of these, Dailzero also covers Status scheduling and contact saving.

#### Which alternatives also schedule WhatsApp Status?

Of the tools verified here, Dailzero does. WhatsApp Status is not available through the official WhatsApp Business Platform, so official-API tools do not offer it.

#### Will switching tools affect my number?

Unlink the old tool before linking a new one, and keep sending volume low for the first days. No tool can promise a number will never be restricted. See [how to stop WhatsApp banning your business number](/blog/how-to-stop-whatsapp-banning-your-business-number).
