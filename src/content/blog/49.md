For an event centre, a WhatsApp AI agent does one job very well: it turns "how much is your hall?" into a complete enquiry with a date, a guest count and an event type, sends the rate card and photos, and books the inspection visit. It does this while your team is on the floor running someone else's wedding.

### The seven things every hall enquiry needs

A price for a hall means nothing without context. Before anyone can quote properly, you need:

1. The date, and whether it is flexible
2. The type of event: wedding reception, conference, birthday, burial reception, church programme
3. The number of guests
4. Which hall or space, if you have more than one
5. The hours needed, including setup time for the decorator
6. What they expect to be included
7. A name and whether they are the client or the planner

Give the agent this list and it will ask for what is missing.

A first message usually looks like this:

> Good morning, pls how much is your hall for 300 guests? Wedding reception, 12th of April.

A good reply:

> Good morning and congratulations. Our Grand Hall seats up to 400 and costs ₦1,800,000 for a Saturday, which covers the hall for 8 hours, air conditioning, standby generator, chairs and tables, and parking. A refundable caution deposit of ₦200,000 applies. I will send the rate card and some photos now. Would you like to come for an inspection this week, and should I check 12th April with our bookings team?

### What to load into the agent

The questions event clients ask are very consistent. Write an answer for each:

- Hall capacities, seated banquet style and theatre style
- Rates by day of the week and by season
- What the fee covers and what costs extra: diesel beyond set hours, extra chairs, the stage, the projector
- Caution deposit, booking deposit and payment schedule
- Whether outside caterers and decorators are allowed, and any vendor fee
- Closing time and the overtime charge
- Parking, security, and inspection days and hours

Then upload a rate card or brochure, photos of the hall empty and decorated, and a walkthrough video to the media library. A document or image costs 8 credits to send and a video 12.

### What your team still does

One double-booked Saturday is a disaster for a venue, so be clear about the boundaries:

- **Date availability.** Dailzero cannot see your booking diary on its own. Have it collect the date and pass it to staff, or update it with the dates already taken. The guide on [automating availability responses](/blog/how-to-automate-room-availability-responses-on-whatsapp-for-nigerian-hotels) explains the options, and they apply to halls as much as rooms.
- **Payment.** It shares your account details or Paystack link. It does not verify that a deposit arrived.
- **Negotiation and contracts.** Discounts and the hire agreement belong with a manager.
- **Calls.** It does not make or receive voice calls.

### Frequently asked questions

#### Can the agent confirm that my hall is free on a date?

Not by itself. It collects the date and hands over, works from a list of booked dates you keep updated, or checks a booking system a developer has connected.

#### Can it send our brochure and hall photos?

Yes. Upload them to the media library once and the agent sends them when a client asks.

#### Can it book inspection visits?

Yes. It agrees a day and time within the hours you set and sends a reminder beforehand.
