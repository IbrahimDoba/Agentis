### What a restaurant should expect from a chatbot

A restaurant's WhatsApp gets the same messages every day: "Send your menu", "Do you deliver to Lekki Phase 1", "How much is the family platter", "Table for six on Saturday". A good tool should:

- Answer menu, price and opening-hour questions straight away.
- Take the order with the items, address and phone number.
- Take a table booking with date, time and number of guests.
- Send the menu as a photo or PDF.
- Hand over to a person for complaints, large orders and special requests.

### Frequently asked questions

#### Can a WhatsApp chatbot take food orders by itself?

Yes. Dailzero's agent collects the items, address and contact details in the chat, and you can read the conversation in your dashboard. Staff still cook, confirm payment and dispatch.

#### What happens when an item finishes?

The agent answers from the details you give it, so update the menu when something is sold out. Tell it to offer an alternative.

#### Can it handle orders at midnight?

It replies at any hour. Set your opening hours so it tells late customers when the kitchen opens and takes the order for then if you allow it.

#### Does Dailzero collect payment?

The agent can share your payment details and take the order. If you want payment links and receipts generated inside the chat, Zella and Sowo list that as a core feature.
