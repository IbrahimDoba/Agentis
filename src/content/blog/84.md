On WhatsApp, automate the handing out of PR materials and never the speaking. For a Nigerian PR agency that means three safe jobs for an AI agent: sending approved materials (press kits, bios, photos, releases), answering new business enquiries, and booking calls and events with reminders. Keep it away from statements, crisis responses or anything a journalist could quote as the client's position. In PR, the wrong automated sentence is a headline.

### Three audiences on one number

A PR agency's WhatsApp carries very different conversations:

- **Journalists and editors** asking for materials, usually on deadline.
- **Clients** asking about coverage, approvals and timelines.
- **Prospects** asking what you do and what a retainer costs.

Each needs its own rule.

### Who handles what

| Message | AI agent | Human |
|---|---|---|
| Request for press kit, bio, logo or event photos | Sends the approved files | Sees who asked |
| "Can I get a comment on the allegations?" | Acknowledges, hands off | Responds |
| New business enquiry | Answers, collects the brief | Follows up, pitches |
| Client asks "has the story been published?" | Hands off | Responds |
| Booking a briefing or call | Books and reminds | Attends |

### Journalists: fast materials, zero opinions

Journalists on deadline want one thing, quickly. A typical message:

> Hi, I'm filing tonight on the product launch. Please can I get the MD's bio and a clear photo from the event?

A good automated reply sends the approved bio as a document and two event photos from your media library, then asks which publication the journalist writes for so your team knows who to follow up with. Those files are ones you upload in advance, so only cleared material goes out.

The dangerous version is a message like this:

> Is it true the company is laying off staff? I need a response before 6pm.

The correct reply is short: "Thank you. I have passed this to the account lead, who will respond to you directly." Then the chat is handed to a person. Write this rule into the agent's instructions: it must not confirm, deny, speculate or describe a client's position. If an approved document does not answer the question, it hands off.

### Clients: acknowledge, record, escalate

Clients message at all hours with edits, approvals and "any update?" questions. The agent cannot see your coverage tracker or your email, so it cannot say whether a story has run. What it can do is make sure nothing gets lost:

- Acknowledge the message immediately, so the client knows it landed.
- Understand a voice note or a photo of marked-up copy and restate the request in text.
- Hand the chat to the account manager.

Team members can have their own logins. When a person replies, the AI pauses on that chat until it is switched back on.

### Prospects: the part most worth automating

New business enquiries are where slow replies cost you. A founder in Abuja asking about media relations on a Saturday should not wait until Monday. Give the agent your services, your sectors and whichever price guidance you are happy to share. Have it ask:

1. What is the company and what does it do?
2. What do you want to achieve: a launch, ongoing visibility, or handling an issue?
3. When do you want to start?

It can then book a discovery call and send a reminder before it.

### Events and announcements

For a press conference or launch, the agent can answer the repeated questions about venue, time, parking and accreditation, and send the invitation as an image or document.

You can also broadcast a short update to contacts who have already messaged you, for example telling your media list a release is available. A text broadcast costs 5 credits per person and an image 8, so a text to 300 contacts is 1,500 credits. Broadcasts only go to existing contacts. This is not a way to pitch journalists who have never messaged your number.

### What it does not replace

- **Email.** Most formal pitching still happens by email, and Dailzero covers WhatsApp and a website chat widget only.
- **Media monitoring and reporting.** It does not track coverage or build reports.
- **Crisis work.** Every part of it stays human.

### Frequently asked questions

#### Could the AI say something on a client's behalf by mistake?

It works from the information and rules you give it. Keep client positions out of its knowledge entirely, load only approved documents, and instruct it to hand off any question about a client's views or conduct.

#### Can it send our press releases to a media list?

It can broadcast to contacts who have already messaged your number. It does not send to a bought or scraped list.

#### Is it suitable during a crisis?

Only as a receptionist. It can acknowledge messages and route them, but every reply of substance should come from a person.
