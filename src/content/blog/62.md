To upsell and cross-sell on WhatsApp with AI, you write your pairings, bundle prices and upgrade options into the agent's instructions, and it makes one relevant offer at the point where the customer has already decided to buy. The AI does not invent deals. It works from the products, prices and rules you give it.

### Upsell and cross-sell are different offers

An **upsell** is a bigger or better version of what the customer already wants: the 100ml perfume in place of the 50ml, the 128GB phone over the 64GB.

A **cross-sell** is a related item that goes with it: a screen guard with a phone case, a charger with a power bank, a bonnet with a wig.

On WhatsApp both work best as a single sentence with a price in it, placed after you have answered the customer's question.

### Four rules to write into your agent

When you set up the agent you describe your business and add products and prices. Add these four things as plain instructions:

1. **Pairings.** List what goes with what. "When someone orders a phone case, mention the tempered glass screen guard once."
2. **Bundle prices.** Give the exact combined price. "Case plus screen guard is ₦8,500." If you only say "give a discount", you have not told it how much, so always state the figure.
3. **Upgrades.** State the next size up and the difference. "100ml is ₦28,000, only ₦10,000 more than the 50ml."
4. **A limit.** "Make one offer per order. If the customer says no, continue with the order and do not offer again."

### What a good offer looks like in the chat

A customer writes:

> "How much is the clear case for iPhone 13? I'm in Surulere."

A reply that answers first and offers second:

> "The clear case is ₦6,000 and delivery to Surulere is ₦2,000. Many customers add the tempered glass screen guard at ₦3,500, and both together are ₦8,500. Should I add it or send just the case?"

### The best moments to make the offer

- **Right after the customer picks an item, before you collect the address.** They are in buying mode and the total is not yet fixed.
- **When the delivery fee is the same either way.** In Lagos a dispatch rider charges per trip, not per item. "Delivery to Ajah is ₦3,500 whether you take one or two" is an honest reason to add something.
- **When a returning customer comes back.** The agent remembers earlier chats, so a customer who bought a 50ml perfume before can be offered the 100ml or a matching body spray.
- **After delivery.** On the Pro plan, automated follow-ups can check in with customers who went quiet. On any plan you can broadcast a restock or bundle offer to existing customers at 5 credits a person for text, or 8 with an image. See [WhatsApp broadcasts with Dailzero](/solutions/broadcasts).

### Mistakes that lose the first sale

- **Offering before answering.** If someone asks the price and gets a pitch for three other things, they leave.
- **Offering what is out of stock.** The AI cannot see your inventory. If the screen guards finish, remove the pairing or it will keep offering them.
- **Hiding the new total.** Always state the full amount including delivery, so the transfer the customer makes matches what you expect.
- **Stacking offers.** One add-on per order. Two or more reads as pressure.

### Where the pairings should come from

Dailzero does not analyse your sales and work out what is "frequently bought together". You decide the pairings. The quickest way is to scroll through your last 50 orders and note which items people asked for together without being prompted. Those are your first pairings.

### What the AI does not do

It does not take payment. After the customer accepts, it collects the order details, shares the bank account or Paystack link you provided, and hands the chat to you to confirm the transfer. It also will not check stock or a supplier's price unless a developer has connected your own system through webhook tools.

### Frequently asked questions

#### Will the AI offer discounts I did not approve?

It works from the prices you give it, so write the exact bundle price for every offer and state that no other discounts apply. Test it with a few haggling messages before you go live.

#### How many add-ons should I offer per order?

One. A single relevant offer with a clear price is easy to accept or decline; several in a row make the chat feel like a sales script.

#### Does upselling use more credits?

Slightly. An offer usually adds one or two text replies at about 5 credits each, or 8 if the agent sends a photo of the add-on.

#### Can it upsell customers who write in Pidgin?

Yes. The agent copes with everyday Nigerian English and Pidgin in customer messages and replies in English. Multi-language replies are a Pro feature.
