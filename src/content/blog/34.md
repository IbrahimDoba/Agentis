WhatsApp AI reduces the no-response rate on property listings by replying to every inquiry within seconds, at any hour, so no message sits unanswered while you are on site, driving or asleep. It fixes the unanswered first message. It does not fix a prospect who goes silent after your reply, though it can help with that too.

"No-response" means two different things in property, and they need different fixes.

### Two kinds of no-response

- **You did not respond.** A prospect messaged about your listing and got nothing, or got a reply six hours later after they had already booked an inspection with another agent.
- **They did not respond.** You replied, and the prospect vanished.

Most agents complain about the second and underestimate the first. Check yours before you decide what to fix.

### Measure your own rate first

There are no trustworthy published figures for this in Nigeria, so use your own. It takes fifteen minutes.

1. Open WhatsApp and go back seven days.
2. Count every first message from a new prospect. Call this A.
3. Count how many got no reply at all, or a reply more than one hour later. Call this B.
4. B divided by A is your no-response rate.

If you had 60 new inquiries and 21 waited over an hour, your rate is 35%. Now you have a number to beat. Count again after two weeks with an AI agent on the line.

### Why listing inquiries go unanswered

Look at the 21 and you will usually find the same causes.

#### They came in outside working hours

People browse listings after work and on Sunday afternoons. A message at 10:15pm waits until you wake up.

#### You were physically busy

Showing a duplex in Ikoyi, stuck on Third Mainland Bridge, in a meeting with a landlord. Property work happens away from the phone.

#### The message looked low value

> Is this still available?

Agents skip this one because it feels like a time waster. But it is simply how people open a conversation on a listing, and some of them are ready to pay.

#### The answer needed digging

"What is the total package for the mini flat in Ogba?" means finding the landlord's breakdown, so you leave it for later, and later does not come.

### What the AI does about each cause

| Cause | What the agent does |
|---|---|
| After hours | Replies in seconds, 24/7 |
| You are busy | Handles the chat alone and hands over only when a person is needed |
| "Is this still available?" | Answers and asks which listing and what they need |
| Answer needs digging | Already has the breakdown you entered, and sends photos and documents from the media library |

Here is the "low value" message handled properly:

> Yes, the 2 bedroom flat on Adeniran Ogunsanya is available. Rent is ₦3.2 million a year. Total first payment with agency, legal and caution is ₦4.1 million. Would you like the video, or should I book an inspection for you this week?

One reply gives the price, the total, and two next steps. The figures here are examples; the agent uses what you enter for each listing.

### Reducing the other kind: prospects who go quiet

Fast replies already help here, because a prospect who gets a full answer in a minute is still holding their phone and still interested. Beyond that:

- **End every reply with one easy question.** "Saturday or Sunday for inspection?" gets more answers than "Let me know."
- **Send proof early.** A walkthrough video of the actual flat keeps people in the chat.
- **Follow up once or twice, then stop.** On the Pro plan, Dailzero sends automated follow-ups to people who went quiet. On other plans, work through the leads list yourself.
- **Keep them watching your Status.** Contacts who messaged you are saved and can see the next listing you post.

Some silence is a real no. No tool changes that, and chasing harder only gets your number reported.

### Keep your listings true

An agent that replies instantly with a flat that was let last week creates a new problem. Two habits prevent it:

1. Mark a unit as taken the same day it goes.
2. For fast-moving listings, tell the agent to say "let me confirm it is still open" and hand the chat to you before booking an inspection.

The AI cannot see availability by itself. It works from what you typed in, unless a developer connects your own listing system through webhook tools.

Only when the prospect contacts you on WhatsApp. It does not read messages inside a portal's own inbox, email or SMS.
