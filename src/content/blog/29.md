A WhatsApp drip campaign for real estate is a planned series of messages, spread over weeks, that keeps you in front of people who enquired but have not bought yet. On WhatsApp you build it from three parts: follow-ups to people who went quiet, broadcasts to your existing contacts, and regular Status posts. It only works on people who have already messaged you. Dripping on a bought list is the fastest way to get a number restricted.

### Why property needs a drip at all

Nobody buys a plot the day they first ask the price. A buyer might be waiting for a contribution to mature, a bonus in December, money from a sibling abroad, or simply for trust to build. That gap can be three weeks or eight months.

### The three parts, and what each is for

**Follow-ups** are one-to-one. They go to a single person who stopped replying, and they pick up from that person's own conversation. On Dailzero, automated follow-ups for customers who went quiet are part of the Pro plan.

**Broadcasts** are one-to-many. You send one message to a group of your existing contacts, and each person receives it as a private chat. Dailzero paces every send to protect your number. A text costs 5 credits per person, an image 8 and a video 12.

**Status posts** are passive. Contacts see them when they open their Status tab, with no notification and no sense of being chased. Dailzero saves the contacts who message you so they can see your Status, and its Autopilot posts on a schedule. Reach per post is up to 2,000 contacts on Basic, 5,000 on Starter and 10,000 on Pro.

One honest note: this is not an email-style sequence builder where every contact automatically receives message 1 on day 1 and message 2 on day 4. You plan the calendar and schedule the pieces. For property, that is usually enough.

### A six week plan you can copy

Assume someone asked about your estate in Mowe and went quiet after seeing the price.

- **Days 2 to 5:** a follow-up that adds something new. Not "are you still interested?" but the answer to a question they did not ask.
- **Week 2:** a broadcast with a short site video showing the current state of the road and the perimeter fence.
- **Week 3:** nothing direct. Status posts carry it: a photo from Saturday's inspection, a picture of a buyer's allocation day (with their permission).
- **Week 4:** a broadcast explaining the payment plan in plain numbers, including the smallest deposit that secures a plot.
- **Week 5:** Status only again. Document sample, survey plan, a short clip of the site.
- **Week 6:** a broadcast with a real deadline, if you truly have one, such as a price review date. Then stop direct messages unless they reply.

### What good drip messages look like

A weak follow-up:

> Hello, are you still interested in the land?

A stronger one gives a reason to reply:

> Good afternoon Mrs Adebayo. You asked about the 600 sqm plots at Mowe last week. Two things since then: the access road has been graded, and we now accept a 30% deposit with the balance over 9 months. Would a short video of the site be helpful?

When someone replies to any drip message, the AI agent answers at once, whatever the hour, and hands the chat to you when they want to negotiate or pay.

### Rules that keep your number safe

- **Only message people who messaged you first.** Dailzero does not send to bought lists, and neither should you by any other means.
- **Stop when asked.** If someone says "please stop", remove them that day.

Messages are queued and sent at a human pace, and sending slows automatically if WhatsApp signals a limit. That lowers the risk. No tool can remove it.

### Frequently asked questions

#### How many messages should a property drip contain?

Three or four direct messages over six weeks is plenty, with Status in between. After that, message only when you have real news, such as a new phase or a price change.

#### Can I send drip messages to numbers I collected at an event or bought online?

Not through Dailzero, and you should not anyway. Broadcasts are for contacts who have already messaged you. Invite event contacts to message your number first, for example with a QR code on your banner.

#### Do I need message templates approved by Meta?

No. Dailzero is not the official WhatsApp Business API, so there is no template approval and no Meta conversation fee.
