To qualify real estate buyers automatically on WhatsApp, give an AI agent five questions to ask every new inquiry (purpose, location, budget, how they will pay, and timeline), then sort the answers into three groups: call today, nurture, and send the brochure. The agent asks the questions inside a normal chat, so the buyer never feels like they are filling a form.

Here is the full script, the sorting rule and the limits.

### The five questions, in the order that works

Order matters. Start with the easy questions and leave money for the middle, after you have given something useful.

1. **Purpose.** "Are you buying to live in, to build later, or as an investment?" This decides which properties you show.
2. **Location.** "Which area are you looking at? Lekki, Ibeju-Lekki, Epe, the mainland, or somewhere else?"
3. **Budget.** "What range are you working with, so I only send what fits?" Asking for a range gets more honest answers than asking for a figure.
4. **Payment method.** "Would that be outright payment or an instalment plan?" A buyer who asks about a 12-month plan is still a buyer, but your sales team needs to know.
5. **Timeline.** "When are you hoping to pay or come for inspection?"

Add a sixth for buyers abroad: "Are you in Nigeria at the moment, or will someone inspect for you?" Diaspora buyers often send a relative, and that changes how you book the inspection.

### What the chat looks like

A buyer clicks your Instagram ad at 9:40pm and sends:

> Good evening, how much is the land at Ibeju and is it C of O?

A good reply answers first, then asks one question:

> Good evening. Plots in our Ibeju-Lekki estate start from ₦8 million for 300sqm, and the title is a registered survey with C of O in process. Are you buying to build or as an investment?

The price is given straight away, because hiding it is the fastest way to lose a serious buyer. The title is described exactly as you typed it in, so write it carefully: "C of O in process" must never become "C of O". The figures here are examples; the agent uses whatever you enter for each property.

### Sorting buyers into three groups

Tell the agent, in plain English, what each group looks like and what to do with it.

| Group | What the buyer said | What happens next |
|---|---|---|
| Call today | Budget fits a property you have, paying within 30 days, asks for inspection or account details | Agent books the inspection and hands the chat to a salesperson |
| Nurture | Budget fits but timeline is "later this year", or needs an instalment plan you offer | Agent sends the brochure and payment plan, then follows up |
| Brochure only | Budget far below your lowest price, or "just checking" | Agent sends the price list politely and leaves the door open |

Do not tell the agent to ignore the third group. A polite brochure costs you about 8 credits, and today's "just checking" may refer a relative next month.

### What automatic qualifying cannot do

Be clear about the limits before you rely on it.

- **It cannot verify what a buyer says.** A stated budget of ₦50 million is a claim, not proof of funds. Your team still confirms seriousness at inspection.
- **It does not confirm payments.** The agent can share the account details you give it, then hand the chat to you. You check your bank and confirm.
- **It does not replace your CRM.** There is no built-in sync with a CRM or Google Sheets. A developer can connect your own system through webhook tools; otherwise the leads list in the dashboard is where you work from.
- **It does not negotiate price** unless you tell it your limits. Most firms tell the agent to hand discount requests to a person.

### Frequently asked questions

#### Will buyers be annoyed by questions from an AI?

Not if each question follows a useful answer. One question per message, after you have given the price or location, feels like a good salesperson. Five questions in one message feels like a form.

#### Can the agent book inspections after qualifying?

Yes. It books appointments on the days you set and sends reminders. See [how to automate property viewing scheduling](/blog/how-to-automate-property-viewing-scheduling-with-whatsapp-ai-in-nigeria) for the details.

#### Does follow-up happen automatically?

Automated follow-ups for buyers who went quiet are on the Pro plan. On other plans, use the leads list and message the nurture group yourself. Plans are on the [pricing page](/pricing).
