### Frequently asked questions

#### Can I schedule WhatsApp Status on a normal number?

Yes. Dailzero and WASBOT both link to a normal WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business number and post Status at the time you set.

#### Does my phone need to be on when the Status posts?

You do not need to be holding the phone at the scheduled time. Your phone remains the main device and should come online regularly to keep the link active.

#### Can the official WhatsApp Business API post Status?

Not with Dailzero. Status, contact saving and the other linked-device features are not available on its official API route.

#### How many Status posts a day is safe?

WhatsApp publishes no number. Two to four posts a day keeps most businesses visible without customers muting them. Dailzero caps Autopilot at 6 a day.

#### Why are my Status views lower than my contact count?

A customer usually sees your Status only when both of you have saved each other. Viewers with read receipts off are also not counted.
