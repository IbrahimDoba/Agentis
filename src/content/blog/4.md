WhatsApp lead generation for a fintech means getting a prospect to send the first message, answering within seconds, and capturing enough detail for a salesperson to follow up. You cannot message strangers from a bought list, so the whole game is creating reasons for people to message you.

Think of it as four doors into your WhatsApp, and one desk behind them.

### Door 1: click-to-WhatsApp ads

A Facebook or Instagram ad with a "Send message" button opens a chat with your number. For a fintech this works well for products that need a conversation before sign-up: POS terminals for merchants, business accounts, device financing, target savings, payroll.

Run separate ads for separate products. Dailzero knows which Facebook or Instagram click-to-WhatsApp ad a customer came from, so the reply can match the ad. Someone who tapped a POS terminal ad should not be asked "How can we help you today?"

### Door 2: your own app, site and receipts

Put a WhatsApp link wherever a prospect hesitates: the pricing page, the merchant sign-up form, the footer of transaction receipts, the "talk to sales" button. If you have a website, Dailzero's chat widget runs the same agent there, so web visitors get the same answers.

### Door 3: QR codes in the physical world

A lot of Nigerian fintech is sold on the ground. Print a QR code that opens your WhatsApp on agent banners, POS stickers, flyers at Computer Village or Wuse Market, and the forms your field team carries.

### Door 4: WhatsApp Status

Every person who messages you can be saved as a contact, and saved contacts see your Status. Dailzero saves the contacts who message you and can post Status on a schedule, reaching up to 2,000 contacts per post on Basic, 5,000 on Starter and 10,000 on Pro. Use it for things people act on: a new feature, a fee change, a referral offer. A reply to a Status is a warm lead.

### The desk: what happens in the first two minutes

Leads go cold quickly on WhatsApp. The person who asked about a POS terminal at 9pm has usually messaged two other providers by morning. An AI agent answers at once and asks the questions your sales team would ask.

A typical first message:

> Good evening. I saw your advert. How much is the POS machine and how I go take get am? I get shop for Mile 12.

A good reply answers the question first, then asks one thing:

> Good evening. Our terminal costs ₦X as a one-time fee, with delivery to Mile 12 in two working days. To get one you need your BVN, a valid ID and a utility bill. What do you sell, and roughly how many transfers or card payments do you take in a day?

The ₦X is your real price. The AI replies from the prices, requirements and policies you give it. It then collects what you told it to collect, such as name, business type, location and expected volume.

Dailzero marks people showing buying intent as leads and lists them in the dashboard on every plan. Your salesperson opens the list in the morning, reads the chat and calls the ones worth calling.

### What the AI should not do with a finance lead

- **Promise approval, returns or rates you have not published.** It explains your stated terms and hands the rest to an officer.
- **Collect sensitive data in chat.** A BVN or an ID photo belongs in your app or KYC flow. The AI should tell the prospect where to submit it.
- **Take payment.** Dailzero does not process or verify payments. It can share the payment link or account details you give it and hand the chat to you to confirm.
- **Message people who never contacted you.** Broadcasts go to your existing contacts only.

### Waking up leads who went quiet

Automated follow-ups, on the Pro plan, message people who stopped replying. And a broadcast reaches your existing contacts: sending a text to 2,000 past enquirers costs 10,000 credits, and an image version costs 16,000. Each send is paced to protect your number.

### How to tell if it is working

Track three numbers per ad or door each week: chats started, leads flagged, and accounts actually opened or terminals actually delivered. The third number is the only one that pays salaries. If an ad brings many chats and no sign-ups, read ten of those chats before you change the budget.
