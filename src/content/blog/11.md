To automate FAQ responses for a fintech app on WhatsApp, pull the questions customers already send you, write one approved answer for each, load those answers into an AI agent linked to your support number, and set a firm rule that anything about a specific account goes to a person. Most teams can do this in a few days without a developer.

Here is the order to do it in.

### Step 1: collect the real questions, not the ones you imagine

Open your support WhatsApp and scroll back through the last two or three weeks. Copy every distinct question into a sheet. Do not start from your website FAQ page, because customers rarely phrase things the way your product team does.

A lending or savings app will usually find the list looks something like this:

- How do I upgrade my account limit?
- What documents do I need to sign up?
- What are your transfer charges?
- How long does a loan take to be approved?
- Why do you need my BVN or NIN?
- How do I reset my PIN?
- Which number or email do I use to report a problem?

### Step 2: sort every question into one of three piles

**Pile A, general questions.** The answer is the same for every customer: fees, limits, requirements, opening hours, how a feature works. These are safe to automate fully.

**Pile B, how-to questions.** The answer is a set of steps inside your app. Also safe to automate, as long as the steps are current.

**Pile C, account questions.** The answer depends on one person's account: a failed transfer, a debit without value, a pending loan, a blocked card. An AI agent cannot see your ledger on its own, so these must go to your team.

### Step 3: write short answers a compliance person would sign off

For each question in piles A and B, write an answer of two to four sentences. State the fact, give the steps, and stop. Have whoever owns compliance or product read the list once before it goes live.

### Step 4: load it into the agent and set the handover rule

On Dailzero you describe the business, paste in the answers, upload any documents worth sending (a fee schedule or a requirements checklist as a PDF), and link your support number by QR code or pairing code. The agent then replies in seconds, day and night, on the number customers already use.

Write the handover rule in plain words in the agent's instructions: any message about a specific transaction, balance, loan decision, chargeback or suspected fraud is collected and passed to a human. The agent can still do useful work first, such as asking for the date, amount and a screenshot of the receipt, so your officer does not have to ask again. It understands photos and voice notes, which matters because many customers send a screenshot with no text at all.

> I transferred 15k since morning, receiver never see am

Good reply: acknowledge it, ask for the transaction date, amount and receipt screenshot, tell the customer a support officer will check and respond, and hand the chat over. No guessing at the cause.

### Step 5: test with ugly messages, then go live

Before launch, send the agent the worst-phrased versions of each question: typos, Pidgin, a voice note, two questions in one message. Fix any answer that comes out vague. Then watch the first week of real chats from the dashboard and correct anything that surprises you.

### What it costs to run

A text reply uses about 5 credits. Say your app gets 40 FAQ chats a day and each takes 4 replies: 40 × 4 × 5 × 30 days = 24,000 credits a month. That fits the Basic plan at ₦20,000 a month, which includes 25,000 credits. You can check other volumes on the [pricing page](/pricing).

### When you need a developer

If you want the agent to answer pile C itself, for example "has my transfer of ₦15,000 on Tuesday reversed?", a developer has to connect your backend through webhook tools so the agent can look the transaction up in your system. The [developer documentation](/developers) covers how that works. Without it, pile C stays with your team, and that is a sensible place for it to stay.

### Frequently asked questions

#### Can the AI answer questions about a customer's balance or transaction?

Not by default. It has no access to customer accounts unless a developer connects your system through webhook tools, so those chats are handed to your team.

#### Will it understand Pidgin and badly typed messages?

It copes with everyday Nigerian English and Pidgin phrasing in customer messages and replies in English. Replying in several languages is a Pro plan feature.

#### What happens to FAQs when our fees or limits change?

You edit the answer in the dashboard and the agent uses the new version. Put one person in charge of this, because an outdated fee answer is worse than a slow one.
