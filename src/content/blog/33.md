Nigerian property firms use WhatsApp AI for four jobs: answering listing questions instantly, sending brochures and price lists, booking inspections, and keeping a list of serious prospects for the sales team. How those jobs look depends on the kind of firm, so this article goes through them one type at a time.

The examples below are illustrations of what each type of business can set up, not reports about any named company.

### Estate developers selling land and off-plan homes

A developer running ads for an estate in Epe or along the Lekki-Epe corridor gets a flood of near-identical messages: price per plot, plot sizes, title, payment plan, and "when is the next inspection?"

The agent is given each estate's details once and then answers all of it. It sends the brochure PDF and the subscription form from the media library, explains the 6 and 12 month plans as you wrote them, and books the buyer onto the next free inspection bus.

> Pls send me the price list for the Epe estate and payment plan

> Here is the current price list. A 500sqm plot is ₦6.5 million outright, or ₦7.2 million spread over 12 months with a ₦1 million first payment. Inspections are every Saturday from our Ajah office. Should I reserve a seat for you this Saturday?

### Letting and sales agents

For an agent with twenty flats to let in Yaba, Surulere and Ikeja, the pressure is different. Prospects ask about one specific unit, and they ask the full cost.

The agent can state the total package the way tenants want it: rent, agency fee, legal fee, caution deposit, service charge, and whether power is prepaid meter. It can send the photos and video of that exact unit, say which floor it is on and whether the compound has water, then offer inspection times and mention your inspection fee before the prospect travels.

One limit: the agent does not know a flat has been taken unless you update it, so mark units as let the same day.

### Shortlet and serviced apartment operators

Shortlet guests want speed. They are comparing three apartments at once and book the one that replies.

Here the agent shares nightly rates, the refundable caution fee, check-in time, house rules and whether there is 24-hour power. It collects dates and number of guests, then hands the chat to you to confirm the dates are free and that payment has landed.

It cannot check your calendar on a booking platform by itself. Treat it as the person who collects every detail so you only need to say yes or no.

### Facility and estate managers

Firms that manage estates field resident questions more than sales questions: service charge amount and due date, how to get a gate pass for a visitor, who to call when the borehole pump fails.

The agent answers the routine ones and takes fault reports with a flat number and a photo, then hands the chat to the facility officer. It does not know who has paid their service charge unless a developer connects your records.

### What all four have in common

Whatever the firm type, the setup has the same parts:

- **One number per agent.** The agent runs on the WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business number the firm already advertises, linked by QR code or pairing code.
- **A media library.** Brochures, floor plans, price lists, site videos, uploaded once.
- **A handoff rule.** Price negotiation, payment confirmation, document questions and angry customers go to a person. When a staff member replies, the AI pauses on that chat until it is switched back on.
- **Team logins.** Sales staff get their own logins to the dashboard.
- **A leads list.** People showing buying intent are listed on every plan.

### What property firms should not expect

- **No payment verification.** The agent shares the account details or payment link you provide. Your accounts person confirms receipts.
- **No title verification.** It repeats the title status you entered. It cannot check a C of O or Governor's Consent.
- **No built-in link to property management software or CRMs.** Connections to your own system need a developer and the [developer API](/developers).
- **No cold messaging.** Broadcasts go to people who have already messaged you, not to a bought list of numbers.

A large developer that needs a verified green tick, or dozens of staff sharing one number at enterprise scale, should look at the official WhatsApp Business Platform instead.

### Frequently asked questions

#### Can our sales team still reply from their phones?

Yes. Anyone can reply from the phone or the dashboard. The AI pauses on that chat when a person steps in.

#### Does the agent confirm that a buyer has paid?

No. It shares your account details and hands the chat to you. Only your team can confirm a payment.
