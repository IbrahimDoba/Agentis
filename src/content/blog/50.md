A spa or wellness centre can hand the whole booking chat to a WhatsApp AI agent: it sends the price list, answers treatment questions, agrees a day and time, takes the client's name, and sends a reminder before the appointment. Your therapists keep their hands on clients instead of on a phone. Payment confirmation and anything medical stay with a person.

Here is one ordinary day.

### 7.15am: the message that arrives before you open

Clients plan self-care outside working hours, on the way to work or late at night. With nobody at reception, this would sit unread:

> Hi, how much is deep tissue massage? And do you have space this Saturday afternoon?

The agent answers straight away:

> Good morning. Deep tissue massage is ₦25,000 for 60 minutes and ₦35,000 for 90 minutes. We open 9am to 7pm on Saturdays. Would 2pm or 4pm suit you, and which length would you like? I can also send our full price list.

It then takes her name, books the slot she picks and tells her your deposit rule, if you have one.

### 10am: the client who does not know what she wants

A first-time client asks for "a facial" without knowing you offer six. The agent asks what she wants from it, describes the options that fit, and sends the treatment menu as a document from your media library.

When a client sends a photo of her skin and asks what will clear it, or mentions she is pregnant and wants a massage, the agent should not advise. It is not a medical tool. Set it to give general information about the treatment and hand the chat to your aesthetician or manager. The agent can understand a photo, but judging a skin condition is a professional's job.

### 1pm: the couple's package

A man sends a voice note asking about a couple's massage for his wife's birthday. The agent understands voice notes, quotes the package and books it. When he asks if the room can be decorated, it collects the details and passes the chat to you.

### 4pm: reminders go out

An empty slot cannot be sold later. The agent sends a reminder before each booking. A client who cannot make it will usually reply to say so, and you get the chance to offer the slot to someone else.

If you take a booking deposit by bank transfer or Paystack link, the agent shares the details you gave it. It does not verify the payment. When the client sends a receipt, the chat comes to you to check your bank alert.

### 6pm: a returning client

A regular messages to book "the usual". The agent remembers returning clients and earlier chats, so it can offer the Swedish massage and pedicure she had last month.

### Tuesday morning: filling the quiet days

Most spas are packed on Saturday and slow midweek. Two tools help:

- **Broadcasts.** Send a midweek offer to clients who have messaged you before. With an image it costs 8 credits per person, so 400 clients cost 3,200 credits. See how [WhatsApp broadcasts](/solutions/broadcasts) are paced to protect your number.
- **Status.** Post open slots or a new treatment on a schedule. The agent saves the contacts who message you so they can see your Status.

Broadcasts go to existing contacts only, not to bought lists.

### One limit to plan around

Dailzero books appointments and sends reminders, but it cannot see a separate booking system, a salon software calendar or a paper diary. If reception also takes walk-ins and phone bookings in another place, someone must keep the two in step, or tell the agent which slots are gone. A developer can connect an outside system through webhook tools. Without one, treat the agent's bookings as your main record or check them against your diary each morning.

It also books by the hours and rules you give it. If only one therapist does hot stone massage and she is off on Mondays, say so.

### Frequently asked questions

#### Can clients choose a specific therapist?

Yes, if you list your therapists and their working days. The agent books from that information, so keep it current when rosters change.

#### Does it stop double bookings?

It books within the hours and rules you set, but it cannot see bookings made elsewhere, such as walk-ins written in a diary. Keep one record or reconcile daily.

#### Can it take the booking deposit?

No. It shares your account details or payment link and hands the chat to you to confirm the money arrived.

#### Will it answer questions about skin conditions or injuries?

It should give general treatment information only and pass health-specific questions to a qualified member of your team. Set that rule when you configure it.
