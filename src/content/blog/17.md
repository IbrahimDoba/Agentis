A Nigerian fintech cuts support costs with WhatsApp AI by letting the AI close the repeat questions that need no account access, so paid staff spend their hours only on the tickets that do. On Dailzero an AI reply costs roughly ₦3 to ₦4, and the savings come from four specific places in a support operation, not from replacing the team.

### Where support money actually goes

In most app-based fintechs it is these four things.

#### 1. Repeat questions with the same answer

"How do I upgrade my account?" "What is the charge for transfers?" "Why was my card request declined?"

**What AI changes:** these are answered in seconds from the information you supply. Your officer never opens the chat.

#### 2. Night and weekend cover

Customers transact at all hours, so questions arrive at all hours. Covering nights means a shift allowance, a second team, or an outsourced desk.

**What AI changes:** the agent replies around the clock. Out of hours it answers what it can and collects details on what it cannot, so the morning queue is shorter and each ticket already has the date, amount and screenshot attached.

#### 3. Spikes

Month end and salary week. A switch or bank network having a bad day. A new app version with a confusing screen. Volume triples for a day and your fixed team cannot stretch.

**What AI changes:** it handles every chat at once. During an incident you add one line to its instructions, for example that transfers to a named bank are delayed and reversals are automatic, and every customer who asks gets that answer immediately.

> Why is my transfer still pending? This is the third time today

A good reply during an incident: apologise, state plainly that there is a delay affecting transfers and that your team is working on it, explain what happens to the money as your policy defines it, and offer to pass the customer to an officer if the transfer is urgent.

#### 4. Back-and-forth to gather facts

A surprising share of handling time is an officer asking "what date?", "how much?", "please send the receipt" and waiting for each answer.

**What AI changes:** the agent asks for all of it before handover.

### What not to cut

Some tickets should always reach a person, and trying to automate them costs more than it saves:

- Failed or disputed transactions on a specific account.
- Fraud and scam reports.
- Loan decisions, limits and anything that needs judgement.
- An upset customer who asks for a human.

The AI has no view of customer accounts by default. With a developer, you can connect your backend through webhook tools so it can look up, say, a transaction status in your system. That moves more tickets into the automatic pile, but it is an engineering project, and your risk team should decide what the agent may read. See the [developer documentation](/developers).

### A four-week rollout that keeps risk low

1. **Week 1.** Export or scroll through a month of chats. List the 20 most frequent questions and mark which need account access.
2. **Week 2.** Write approved answers for the ones that do not. Load them, link a test number, and try to break it.
3. **Week 3.** Go live out of hours only. Read every conversation the next morning and fix weak answers.
4. **Week 4.** Go live all day. Track how many chats close without a human and how long handed-over tickets now take.

### Who this does not suit

Dailzero links to your existing number the way WhatsApp Web does, with one number per agent. That suits small and mid-sized fintechs. A digital bank with millions of users and a large support floor on one number needs the official WhatsApp Business Platform, which offers a verified badge, approved templates and enterprise-scale concurrency. Dailzero does not provide those. For that route, read the [guide to the WhatsApp Business API for payment companies](/blog/whatsapp-business-api-for-nigerian-payment-companies-a-complete-guide).

### Frequently asked questions

#### How much of a fintech's support volume can AI handle alone?

It depends on your ticket mix. Count how many of your last 100 chats needed account access; the rest are candidates for full automation.

#### What happens if the AI's credits finish mid-month?

The AI pauses and customer messages still arrive in WhatsApp as normal for your team to answer. You can top up with a pay-as-you-go pack from ₦1,000.

#### Can several support officers work alongside the AI?

Yes. Team members can have their own logins, reply to handed-over chats from the dashboard, and switch the AI back on for a chat when they are done.
