You can automate most guest communication for a short-let by linking the WhatsApp number you host with to an AI agent. It answers price and amenity questions, sends your house guide and directions, and passes anything unusual to you. What it cannot touch is the Airbnb inbox: Dailzero works on WhatsApp and a website chat widget only, so it never reads or replies to messages inside the Airbnb app.

That limit matters less than it sounds, because a lot of a Nigerian host's conversations already happen on WhatsApp.

### Where WhatsApp fits if you list on Airbnb

Most hosts in Lagos, Abuja and Port Harcourt fill their calendar from more than one place. Some guests come through Airbnb. Others see the apartment on Instagram, get your number from a friend, or stayed before and message you directly. Booked guests also tend to move to WhatsApp for directions and gate access once they have your number.

The agent covers all of those WhatsApp chats. For guests who found you on Airbnb, check Airbnb's own rules on off-platform contact and payment, and stay inside them.

### The five stages of a stay, and what to hand to the agent

#### 1. The enquiry

This is the stage where slow replies lose bookings, because the guest is messaging three other hosts at the same time. A typical first message:

> Is the 2 bed in Lekki Phase 1 free from Friday to Sunday? How much, and is there 24 hour light?

A good reply from the agent:

> The 2 bedroom is ₦85,000 a night, so ₦170,000 for Friday to Sunday, plus a ₦50,000 refundable caution deposit. Power is 24 hours with inverter and generator backup. I will confirm those dates with the host and come back to you. How many guests are coming?

Notice what the agent does not do: it does not promise the dates. It cannot see your Airbnb calendar or any other booking system, so tell it to quote, collect the dates and guest count, and hand over to you for the final yes.

#### 2. Payment and confirmation

The agent can share the account details or Paystack link you give it and explain your caution deposit and cancellation terms. It does not process or verify payments. When a guest sends a transfer receipt, the chat comes to you, you check your bank app, and you confirm.

#### 3. Arrival day

Load your check-in guide once: the estate gate procedure, the landmark the driver should ask for, where to park, check-in time, and who hands over the key. Upload a short video of the route from the gate to the door and photos of the building front to the media library, and the agent sends them when a guest says they are on the way.

#### 4. During the stay

Guests ask the same things in every booking: the WiFi password, how to switch to the inverter, how the water heater works, whether visitors are allowed. The agent answers these at any hour, and it understands photos and voice notes too.

Real problems are different. Tell the agent that a leak, a lockout, no power or a security issue goes straight to you or your caretaker.

#### 5. Checkout

The agent can state your checkout time, your late checkout fee if you have one, and where to leave the key. The caution deposit refund stays with you, because someone has to inspect the apartment first.

### What it costs for one or two apartments

Hosting volume is usually low and uneven, so pay as you go tends to suit it better than a subscription. Say you get 5 chats a day and each needs about 8 replies: 5 × 8 × 5 credits × 30 days = 6,000 credits a month. A ₦10,000 pack gives 6,500 credits, valid for 12 months, so quiet months cost you nothing extra. Sending a photo costs 8 credits and a video 12. Full prices are on the [pricing page](/pricing).

### Frequently asked questions

#### Can the agent reply to messages in the Airbnb app?

No. Dailzero works on WhatsApp and a website chat widget. Messages inside Airbnb still need you or Airbnb's own saved replies.

#### Can it check my calendar and confirm dates on its own?

Not by itself. It has no connection to Airbnb or any booking calendar, so set it to collect the dates and pass them to you for confirmation.

#### Do I need a separate number for each apartment?

No. One agent works on one WhatsApp number and can hold the details of several apartments, as long as you describe each one clearly.

#### What happens if a guest has an emergency at 2am?

The agent hands the chat to a person when it cannot help or when the guest asks. Give guests a phone number to call for urgent issues as well, because the agent does not make or answer voice calls.
