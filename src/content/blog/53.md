Order inquiries in a Nigerian online store fall into three groups: questions before the order (price, availability, delivery fee), the order itself (details and payment), and questions after it (where is my package). A WhatsApp AI agent can handle the first two almost completely. The third it can only answer from information you have given it, unless a developer connects your order system.

Knowing which group a message belongs to tells you how much of it you can automate.

### What can be automated, at a glance

| Inquiry | Can the AI handle it alone? | What it needs from you |
|---|---|---|
| Price, colours, specifications | Yes | Your product list and photos |
| Delivery fee and timeline | Yes | A fee for each area you serve |
| Placing the order | Yes | The details you want collected |
| Payment | Partly | Your account details or Paystack link; you confirm the money |
| Where is my order? | Only general answers | A developer connection for live status |
| Change or cancel an order | Collects the request | A person to approve it |

### Before the order: the questions you answer fifty times a day

Say you sell phone accessories from Ikeja: power banks, chargers, earbuds. Most of your inbox looks like this:

> Do you have 20000mah power bank? How much, and is it original?

The agent answers from the product information you typed in, and sends the photo from your media library:

> Yes, we have the 20,000mAh power bank at ₦18,500. It is original with a 6-month warranty, and it can charge most phones about four times. Delivery within Lagos is ₦2,000 to ₦3,500 depending on your area. Where should we deliver to?

To make these answers reliable, write your delivery information as a list the AI can read: mainland fee, island fee, outskirts such as Ikorodu and Badagry, interstate by park or courier, and how long each takes. Also state your pay-on-delivery rule clearly. If you only allow it within Lagos, or only after a commitment fee, say so in your policies and the agent will repeat it the same way every time.

### During the order: collecting details without the back and forth

Once the customer says yes, the agent collects what you need: full name, phone number for the rider, delivery address with a landmark, item, colour and quantity. It asks for anything missing instead of leaving you to chase it later.

Then payment. Dailzero does not process or verify payments. The agent shares the bank account or Paystack link you gave it and asks for proof of payment. The chat is then yours to confirm. Check your bank alert, reply from the dashboard or your phone, and book the dispatch rider. The AI pauses on that chat while you are in it, and you switch it back on when you are done.

This matters because fake transfer receipts are a real problem. Do not let any tool release an order on a screenshot alone.

### After the order: the honest answer on tracking

This is the message every store dreads:

> Good afternoon, I paid since yesterday, I never see my order o

The agent understands this, including the Pidgin phrasing. What it cannot do is look up the rider's location or your order sheet. It has no view of delivery status on its own, and there are no native integrations with Shopify, WooCommerce, Jumia, Konga or Google Sheets.

You have three workable options:

1. **Set expectations and hand over.** Tell the agent your normal timeline, for example "Lagos orders are delivered the next working day, and the rider calls before arriving". It gives that answer, and if the customer is still unhappy or the order is late, it hands the chat to a person.
2. **Reduce the question at source.** When you confirm payment, tell the customer the delivery day and that the rider will call. Fewer people ask where an order is when they were told when to expect it.
3. **Connect your system.** If you have a developer and an order database, a webhook tool lets the agent look up a specific order mid-chat and give the real status. The [developer page](/developers) explains how. Up to 50 webhook tools can be connected.

For most small stores, the first two are enough. Setup needs no code, and the steps are on the [how it works page](/how-it-works).

### Frequently asked questions

#### Can the AI tell a customer where their package is?

Not by itself. It can give your standard delivery timeline and pass the chat to you. Live status needs a developer to connect your order system through a webhook tool.

#### Can it confirm that a transfer has landed?

No. It shares your payment details and asks for proof, then a person confirms the alert. Dailzero has no access to your bank.

#### How many credits does an order chat use?

A text reply is about 5 credits and a photo 8. An order chat with six text replies and one photo uses about 38 credits. See the [pricing page](/pricing) for what each plan includes.
