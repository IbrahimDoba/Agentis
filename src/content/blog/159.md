Test a WhatsApp AI agent by messaging your business number from a second phone and asking it 20 questions a real customer would ask, then scoring each answer. Cover six types: price, availability, off-topic, an angry customer, a voice note and a request for a human. Any wrong price or invented policy is a fail on its own. Fix what you find and run the script again before you leave the agent on.

### How to run the test

1. **Use a second phone.** A friend's or a staff member's number works. The agent replies to messages that come in to your business number, so you cannot test by messaging yourself.
2. **Write like a customer.** Use short forms, typos and Pidgin if your customers do.
3. **Keep the dashboard open.** Watch the conversation appear and note anything the agent flags.
4. **Write down each result** as you go.

### The 20 question script

Replace the words in brackets with your own products and places.

#### Price

1. "How much is [your best-selling product]?"
2. "hw much for d [product], last price?"
3. "How much is [a product you do not sell]?"
4. "If I buy 5 of [product], is there a discount?"
5. "How much is delivery to [a town outside your state]?"

#### Availability

6. "Do you have [product] in [size or colour]?"
7. "Are you open on Sunday?"
8. "Where is your shop? Can I come and pick up?"
9. "Can I get it today if I pay now?"

#### Off-topic

10. "Who won the match yesterday?"
11. "Can you help me write my CV?"
12. "Are you a robot?"
13. "Do you know where I can buy [something a competitor sells]?"

#### Angry customer

14. "I paid since yesterday and nobody has sent my order. This is rubbish."
15. "The one you sent me is fake. I want my money back."
16. "I will report this page if I don't hear from you today."

#### Voice note

17. Record a clear voice note asking the price of one product.
18. Record a voice note in a noisy place, asking two things at once.

#### Asks for a human

19. "Please let me talk to a real person."
20. "Give me the owner's number."

### What a good answer looks like

| Type | Pass | Fail |
|---|---|---|
| Price | The exact price you entered. For question 3, it says you do not sell it or that it will check. | Any price you did not enter |
| Availability | Matches your real hours, address and stock notes | Promises same-day delivery you do not offer |
| Off-topic | Declines politely and returns to your business | Writes the CV or argues about football |
| Angry customer | Apologises, does not argue, and hands over to a person | Sends the price list or account number again |
| Voice note | Answers what was said, or asks the customer to confirm | Answers a different question with confidence |
| Asks for a human | Says a team member will respond, and the chat is flagged | Keeps selling |

### How to score it

Give each question one of three marks:

- **2 points:** correct and in the right tone
- **1 point:** safe but weak, for example it handed over when it could have answered
- **0 points:** wrong, invented or rude

That makes 40 points in total.

| Score | What it means |
|---|---|
| 36 to 40 | Ready. Leave it on and read the first real chats closely. |
| 28 to 35 | Close. Fix the weak answers and retest those questions. |
| Below 28 | Not ready. Your instructions or product list need more work. |

These bands are a rule of thumb, not an industry standard. Two rules override the total:

- **A made-up price or policy is an automatic fail.** Fix it before going live, whatever the score.
- **A failed handover is an automatic fail.** If questions 14 to 16 or 19 do not reach a person, do not launch.

### How to fix what failed

- **Wrong price:** the product is missing, duplicated or out of date in your product list. Correct it.
- **Invented answer:** add a clear rule to the instructions, such as "never state a price or policy that is not written here. Say you will check with the team."
- **Poor tone:** describe the voice you want and give one or two sample replies.
- **Missed handover:** check the handover settings on the agent, and list the situations that must go to a person.
- **Voice note misheard:** tell the agent to confirm what it understood before quoting a price from a voice note.

### Also test your own side

The agent is half of the system. You are the other half.

- Reply to one test chat from your phone and confirm the AI goes quiet on it. Read [how to hand over from AI to a human on WhatsApp](/blog/how-to-hand-over-from-ai-to-a-human-on-whatsapp).
- Switch that chat back to AI and confirm it answers again.
- Check that a flagged chat is easy to find in the dashboard.

### Frequently asked questions

#### Can I test the agent without customers seeing it?

Yes. Test early in the day from a second phone. Customers only see their own chats, not your test conversation. If a real customer writes in while you test, the agent will answer them, so check the main facts are right before you link.

#### Does Dailzero have a test chat or simulator in the dashboard?

Not as a separate screen. You test by messaging your linked number from another phone, which shows you exactly what a customer will see.

#### How often should I retest?

Whenever you change prices, add products or edit the instructions. A short retest of the price and handover questions takes a few minutes.

#### What if the agent keeps failing the same question?

The information it needs is usually missing or unclear. Rewrite that part in plain words. If it still fails, set that topic to go to a person.
