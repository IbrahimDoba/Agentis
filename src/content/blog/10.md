The fastest way to qualify fintech leads on WhatsApp is to ask three or four deciding questions inside the first chat, automatically, so that your sales team only calls people who fit. Qualification is sorting, not selling: the aim is to know within a few minutes whether this person can use the product, is allowed to, and wants it now.

Here are six methods that work on WhatsApp, in the order a lead meets them.

### 1. Let the ad do the first sort

A lead who tapped an ad for invoice financing is already a different lead from one who tapped an ad for a kids' savings plan. Run one click-to-WhatsApp ad per product and per audience. Dailzero knows which Facebook or Instagram ad a customer came from, so the opening reply can skip "what are you interested in?" and go straight to the questions for that product.

### 2. Ask the knockout questions first

Every product has two or three facts that rule a lead in or out. Ask those before anything else. Some examples:

- **Working capital loan for traders:** How long has the business been running? What is your average monthly sales? Which state are you in?
- **Merchant payment terminal:** Do you have a physical shop? Roughly how many payments do you take in a day?

Keep it to one question per message. A wall of five questions gets no reply.

In practice it looks like this. A lead writes:

> Hi, I run a provisions store in Garki. I saw your ad about loans for business owners. I need about 1.5m to stock for December.

A good qualifying reply:

> Thanks for reaching out. We lend ₦500,000 to ₦5 million to businesses that have traded for at least 12 months. How long has your store been open, and what are your average sales in a month?

The amounts in that reply are placeholders for your own criteria. The AI works from the eligibility rules you type in.

### 3. Say no early and kindly

Fast qualification includes fast disqualification. If you only lend in Lagos and Ogun and the lead is in Maiduguri, tell them in the second message, not after they have sent six documents. An honest "we do not cover your state yet" protects your reputation and your team's time.

Write your exclusions into the agent's instructions: states you do not serve, minimum amounts, business types you cannot take.

### 4. Send the document checklist before the call

Many deals stall because the lead learns about the requirements only on the phone with sales. Have the agent send the checklist as soon as the lead passes the knockout questions. Upload it to the media library as a PDF and the AI sends it in the chat for 8 credits.

One caution: the checklist can be shared in chat, but the documents themselves should not be collected there. BVN, ID photos and bank statements belong in your secure application flow.

### 5. Work from the leads list, not the inbox

Scrolling WhatsApp to find serious prospects among the "how far" messages wastes a morning. Dailzero spots people showing buying intent and lists them as leads in the dashboard on every plan. Sales opens the list, reads the chat where the lead already stated business age, turnover and amount, and decides whether to call. When a salesperson replies, the AI pauses on that chat.

### 6. Follow up once, on a timer

Leads go quiet for ordinary reasons: a customer walked in, data finished, NEPA took light. On the Pro plan, automated follow-ups message those who stopped replying. One or two polite nudges are enough. After that, leave them. They are saved contacts and will see your WhatsApp Status.

### A simple scoring rule for your team

Agree what "qualified" means before you switch anything on, or sales and marketing will argue about it forever. A workable rule has three parts:

1. **Fit:** passes every knockout question.
2. **Readiness:** has the documents or can get them this week.
3. **Intent:** named an amount, a date or a next step.

Three out of three gets a call today. Two gets a follow-up. One or none gets Status updates only.

### What automation cannot judge

An AI agent sorts by the answers a lead gives. It cannot check whether those answers are true. It has no view of a credit bureau, a bank statement or your core system unless a developer connects them through [webhook tools](/developers), and it must not promise approval, rates or returns. Verification and the final decision remain your officers' job. For how the pieces fit, see [how Dailzero works](/how-it-works).

### Frequently asked questions

#### How many qualifying questions should I ask on WhatsApp?

Three or four, one per message. Ask only what would change your decision to call the lead.

#### Can the AI score leads automatically?

Dailzero flags people showing buying intent and lists them as leads. A points-based score of your own design is something your team applies when reading the chat, or a developer builds through the API.

#### Is qualifying different from generating leads?

Yes. Generation is getting people to message you, and qualification is deciding which of them to spend sales time on. Our guide to [WhatsApp lead generation for fintech companies](/blog/whatsapp-lead-generation-for-fintech-companies-in-nigeria) covers the first half.
