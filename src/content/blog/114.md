To message customers already in your WhatsApp from your own number, Dailzero and WASBOT both send paced broadcasts through a linked device. To message large lists, or people who have not chatted with you recently, use a tool on Meta's official platform such as ChatRouter, WATI or respond.io, with approved templates and Meta's fees. We make Dailzero, so weigh this accordingly.

### Two kinds of broadcast tool

**Linked-device tools** send through the WhatsApp account on your phone. There is no Meta verification and no Meta message fee. WhatsApp does not officially support automating an account this way, so pacing matters a great deal.

**Official API tools** send through the WhatsApp Business Platform. This is the route WhatsApp provides for businesses that message customers first. You use message templates approved by Meta and pay Meta per message. It is the safer route for big sends, and it costs more.

### The ban risk, honestly

WhatsApp restricts numbers mainly when recipients block or report them, when they message strangers, and when sending looks automated. Pacing helps with the third. It does not help if people did not want the message. No tool, including Dailzero, can promise a number will never be restricted.

A safer broadcast, on any tool:

1. Send only to people who have chatted with you or asked to hear from you.
2. Start with a small group and grow over days.
3. Use the customer's name and write like a person.
4. Offer a way out, such as "Reply STOP and I will remove you".
5. Stop at once if several people block you or WhatsApp shows a warning.

### Frequently asked questions

#### How many people can I broadcast to in a day?

WhatsApp publishes no safe number. It depends on your number's age, history and how recipients react. Dailzero applies its own daily limits that rise as a number warms up.

#### Can I upload a list of phone numbers to Dailzero?

No. Dailzero broadcasts go to contacts already on your WhatsApp, picked in the dashboard. That is deliberate, because messaging strangers is the quickest way to lose a number.

#### Are official API broadcasts free of ban risk?

No. Automation is allowed there, but Meta can still limit or restrict a business that sends unwanted messages or gets many blocks.

#### What does a broadcast cost on Dailzero?

5 credits per person for text, 8 for an image and 12 for a video. On an official API tool, add Meta's per-message fee to the tool's plan.

#### Is WhatsApp's own broadcast list enough?

For a small business sending by hand, it can be. Only contacts who saved your number receive it, and you answer every reply yourself.
