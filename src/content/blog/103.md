ManyChat is a flow builder that started on Facebook Messenger and Instagram and added WhatsApp through Meta's official platform. Dailzero is an AI agent that works on the WhatsApp number you already use. If most of your customers come through Instagram comments and DMs and you like building step-by-step flows, choose ManyChat. If your sales happen in WhatsApp chats and you want an AI to answer in plain conversation, paid for in Naira, choose Dailzero.

### Side by side

| | Dailzero | ManyChat |
|---|---|---|
| Strongest channel | WhatsApp | Instagram and Facebook Messenger |
| WhatsApp connection | Your existing number, as a linked device | Meta's official WhatsApp Business Platform |
| How replies work | AI answers freely from your prices and policies | Flows you design, with AI features on some plans |
| Pricing | Naira, from ₦20,000 a month or pay as you go | US dollars, rising with your number of contacts |
| Meta message fees on WhatsApp | None | Yes, on top of the plan |
| Instagram comment and DM automation | No | Yes, its best-known feature |
| Setup | Describe your business and scan a QR code | Build flows, connect accounts, verify for WhatsApp |

ManyChat changed its plans in 2026 and third-party sources disagree on the figures, so check its own pricing page.

### Where ManyChat is the better choice

- **Your leads start on Instagram.** "Comment LINK and I will DM you" campaigns, story replies and DM funnels are what ManyChat does best. Dailzero does not work on Instagram or Messenger at all.
- **You want exact control of every step.** A flow always follows the path you drew. That suits giveaways, lead magnets and quizzes.
- **You run campaigns across Instagram, Messenger and WhatsApp** and want them in one tool.
- **You need the official WhatsApp API**, for example for approved templates.

### Where Dailzero is the better choice

- **Your customers ask open questions.** "How much is the blue one, do you deliver to Ibadan, and can I pay on delivery?" does not fit a menu. The AI reads the whole message and answers all three parts.
- **You do not want to build flows.** You give the agent your products, prices and rules, not a diagram.
- **You want to keep your number in your own app** and still reply by hand when you choose.
- **You want Naira pricing with no Meta fees.** Your cost depends on messages sent, not on how many contacts you have stored.
- **You want Status and broadcasts handled** on the same number.

### The two approaches on one real message

Say a customer sends this to a shoe seller:

> Pls do you have size 44 in the brown loafers and how much to send to Benin

A flow-based bot usually replies with a menu: 1 for prices, 2 for delivery, 3 to talk to an agent. The customer has to pick, twice.

An AI agent replies with the answer:

> Yes, the brown loafers are available in size 44 at ₦38,000. Delivery to Benin is ₦4,500 and takes 2 to 3 working days. Shall I take your name and address to book it?

The price and delivery fee there are examples. The agent uses the figures you load.

### What you give up with Dailzero

Dailzero is not the official API, so there is no verified badge and no approved templates. It covers WhatsApp and a website chat widget only. Your phone stays the main device and should come online regularly.

Sending is paced to protect your number, and no tool can promise a number will never be restricted. See [how to stop WhatsApp banning your business number](/blog/how-to-stop-whatsapp-banning-your-business-number).

### Can you use both?

Yes, and many sellers should. Use ManyChat to turn Instagram comments into conversations and send people to your WhatsApp link. Let Dailzero take over once they message your number. The two tools then cover discovery and closing. Keep them on separate WhatsApp numbers if you also connect ManyChat to WhatsApp.

There is more on the two channels in [WhatsApp AI vs Instagram DMs](/blog/whatsapp-ai-vs-instagram-dms-which-is-better-for-nigerian-e-commerce-sales).

### Frequently asked questions

#### Is Dailzero a ManyChat alternative?

For WhatsApp conversations, yes. For Instagram and Messenger automation, no. Dailzero does not support those channels.

#### Does ManyChat work on WhatsApp in Nigeria?

Yes, through Meta's official platform, which means business verification and Meta's message fees on top of the ManyChat plan.

#### Which is easier to set up?

Dailzero, for a WhatsApp-only business: there are no flows to build. ManyChat takes longer because you design each flow, but you get precise control in return.

#### Does Dailzero charge per contact?

No. You pay for credits, which are used when messages are sent. A text reply costs about 5 credits. See [pricing](/pricing).
