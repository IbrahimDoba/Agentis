The Dailzero free trial lasts 7 days and includes 1,000 credits, 1 AI agent and the ability to save up to 200 contacts in total. That is about 200 AI text replies on your own WhatsApp number, with real customers. No credit card is needed. When the trial ends or the credits finish, the agent stops replying until you choose a plan or buy credits. You are not charged automatically.

The trial is on Dailzero's default connection. You link the WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business number you already use by QR code or pairing code, the same way you link WhatsApp Web.

### When the 7 days start

The clock starts when you first connect your WhatsApp number, not when you sign up. So you can create the account, describe your business and add your products and prices first. Link the number when the agent is ready to talk to customers.

### How far 1,000 credits go

A typical AI text reply uses about 5 credits, so 1,000 ÷ 5 = about 200 replies. At about 6 replies a chat, that is roughly 33 full conversations.

Other actions draw from the same 1,000:

| Action | Credits |
|---|---|
| AI text reply | About 5 |
| Image or document sent | 8 |
| Video sent | 12 |
| Text Status post | 5 |
| Image Status post | 8 |
| Video Status post | 12 |
| Saving a contact | 1 |

**Worked example:** in your trial week the agent sends 120 text replies and 20 product photos, you post 3 image Status updates and save 100 contacts.

- Text replies: 120 × 5 = 600 credits
- Photos: 20 × 8 = 160 credits
- Status: 3 × 8 = 24 credits
- Contacts: 100 × 1 = 100 credits
- Total: 600 + 160 + 24 + 100 = 884 credits

That leaves 116 credits, about 23 more text replies.

A busy number can finish 1,000 credits before day 7. A shop with 40 chats a day at 6 replies each uses 40 × 6 × 5 = 1,200 credits a day, so the trial credits would last less than one day. If that is you, treat the trial as a short proof that the agent answers correctly, then move to a plan.

### How to get the most from 7 days

1. **Prepare before you link.** Add your products, prices, delivery fees and opening hours first.
2. **Test it yourself.** Message the number from another phone and ask what real customers ask.
3. **Fix what it gets wrong.** Update the details in the dashboard and ask again.
4. **Let it answer real customers.** Read the conversations each evening.
5. **Try a takeover.** Reply to one chat from your phone and watch the AI pause on that chat.
6. **Post one Status and save some contacts.** See how the Status tools fit your business.
7. **Count your chats.** By day 7 you will know your daily volume, which tells you which plan fits.

A fuller walkthrough is in [how to test a WhatsApp AI agent before customers see it](/blog/how-to-test-a-whatsapp-ai-agent-before-customers-see-it).

### What happens when the trial ends

- **The agent stops replying.** This happens after 7 days, or earlier if the 1,000 credits finish.
- **Your WhatsApp keeps working.** Customers' messages still arrive in your app as normal and you can reply yourself.
- **The dashboard asks you to choose a plan.** Sending from the dashboard, broadcasts and Status Autopilot wait until you do.
- **Nothing is charged.** There is no card on file and no automatic upgrade.

To carry on, you have two routes:

- **Pay as you go.** Buy a pack from ₦1,000 for 600 credits, valid for 12 months. A pay as you go balance keeps the agent working after the trial without a subscription.
- **A monthly plan.** Basic at ₦20,000 for 25,000 credits, Starter at ₦40,000 for 60,000 or Pro at ₦75,000 for 100,000.

The [pricing page](/pricing) has both, and [Dailzero pricing explained](/blog/dailzero-pricing-explained) goes through each plan.

### Limits to know about

- **One agent.** A second agent needs the Pro plan.
- **200 saved contacts in total.** Paid accounts save contacts at 1 credit each without this cap.
- **No team seats.** Team members come with paid plans: 1 on Basic, 2 on Starter and 5 on Pro.
- **Number safety still applies.** Dailzero queues messages and sends at a human pace, and new numbers go through a warm-up period. No tool can promise a number will never be restricted. A brand new number should be used normally for about two weeks before you connect it.

### Is the trial enough to decide?

For most small businesses, yes. A week of real chats shows whether the agent answers correctly from your prices and how many chats you get. For a high-volume business the credits run out quickly, so if you need a longer or larger test, talk to the team through the [contact page](/contact).
