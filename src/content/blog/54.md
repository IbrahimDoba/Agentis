You can automate the first half of a complaint on WhatsApp, not the whole of it. An AI agent can reply within seconds, acknowledge the problem, collect the order details and photo evidence, and explain your return policy. The decision that follows (refund, replacement, redelivery) should stay with a person, and a good setup hands the chat over at that point.

Trying to automate the decision as well is how stores end up with a customer arguing with a bot at midnight and posting the screenshots.

### Why the first reply is the part worth automating

An angry customer who waits six hours for any response becomes angrier. The same customer who gets a calm reply in ten seconds, asking the right questions, is far easier to settle. Speed and acknowledgement are what an AI agent does well. Judgment is what you do well.

So split every complaint into two jobs: intake, then resolution.

### Sort complaints into three kinds

#### Kind 1: questions that only look like complaints

"I have not received my order" on the same day it was paid for. "Why is delivery ₦4,000?" "You people did not tell me it needs assembling." These are answered by information you already have: your delivery timeline, your fee list, your product description. The agent can close these on its own if you have written that information down.

#### Kind 2: real problems that need facts first

Wrong size, wrong colour, damaged item, missing piece, rider collected extra money. Here the agent's job is to collect the facts so that when you open the chat, everything is there.

Say you sell shoes and bags from Port Harcourt and this arrives:

> I ordered size 42 and your rider brought 40. I am not happy at all. I need this for Saturday.

A good automated first reply looks like this:

> I am sorry about that, and I understand you need it before Saturday. Please send a photo of the shoe and the size label, and confirm the name the order was placed under. I am passing this to our team now so we can arrange the correct size.

The agent can read the photo the customer sends back, and the name and order details stay in the chat for whoever picks it up.

#### Kind 3: hand over at once

Refund demands, a customer who says they were debited twice, threats to report you, anything involving a chargeback, and anyone who simply asks for a human. The agent should not negotiate these. Dailzero hands the chat to a person when the customer asks or when it cannot help. You reply from the dashboard or straight from your phone, the AI pauses on that chat, and you switch it back on afterwards.

### What to write into the agent before you go live

The agent only knows what you tell it. A vague policy produces vague answers. Write these out in plain sentences:

- **Return window.** For example, "Returns are accepted within 3 days of delivery if the item is unused and in its box."
- **Who pays return delivery** when the mistake is yours and when the customer changed their mind.
- **What you do not accept back**, such as worn items or sale items.
- **How long a refund takes** after you approve it, and by what method.
- **What evidence you need**: photo of the item, the label, the package.
- **What it must never promise.** State clearly that refunds and replacements are approved by a team member, so it never commits to one.
- **Your working hours for complaints**, so a customer at 11pm is told a person will respond in the morning.

### Mistakes to avoid

1. **Letting the AI argue.** If a customer rejects the policy answer, the next step is a person, not a second explanation.
2. **Hiding the human.** Do not make customers hunt for a way to reach someone. If they ask, they get handed over.
3. **Expecting it to check things it cannot see.** Dailzero does not see your bank, your stock or the rider's location on its own. It cannot confirm that a refund has been sent or where a parcel is unless a developer has connected your system through webhook tools.
4. **Leaving complaints unread after handover.** Check the dashboard at set times each day. Team members can have their own logins, so one person can own complaints.

### Frequently asked questions

#### Can the AI issue a refund?

No. Dailzero does not process payments or move money. It explains your refund policy and passes the chat to a person, who sends the refund from the bank or payment provider.

#### Does it understand complaints written in Pidgin or sent as voice notes?

Yes. It copes with everyday Nigerian English and Pidgin phrasing, and it understands voice notes and photos that customers send.

#### What happens to complaints that come in overnight?

The agent acknowledges them, collects the details and tells the customer when your team will respond. The chat is waiting for you in the morning with the evidence already in it.
