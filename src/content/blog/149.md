To book appointments on WhatsApp automatically, you need something that can agree a date and time with the customer, record it and remind both sides. WhatsApp Business cannot do that alone, though quick replies and labels make the manual way faster. With Dailzero you switch on "AI auto-booking" for your agent, and it books the appointment in the chat once the customer has confirmed an exact date and time. The booking then appears on your Appointments page.

### How to do it by hand in WhatsApp Business

1. **Put your hours on your business profile**, with your address.
2. **Set a greeting message** that tells new customers how to book: "Send the service you want and your preferred day and time."
3. **Set an away message** for closed hours, saying when you will confirm.
4. **Save quick replies** for the messages you repeat: your available days, your price list, your location and a confirmation template such as "You are booked for [day] at [time]".
5. **Keep one calendar.** The moment you confirm in chat, enter the booking in your phone calendar with the customer's name and number, and set an alert.
6. **Label the chat**, for example "Booked", so you can find it.
7. **Send a reminder by hand** the day before.

This is free and it works. It fails in three predictable places: enquiries that arrive while you are with another client, bookings agreed in chat that never reach the calendar, and reminders nobody sent.

### How to do it automatically with Dailzero

Dailzero links to the WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business number you already use, and an AI agent replies on it.

1. **Set up your agent** with your services, prices, location and opening days and hours. Say plainly when you do and do not take bookings, and what the customer should bring.
2. **Open Appointments in the sidebar.**
3. **Open "AI auto-booking"** and switch it on for the agent that should book on its own. If you have more than one agent, each has its own switch.
4. **Set "Default reminders".** Choose a first reminder and, if you want one, a second. The options run from 15 minutes before to 1 week before. Click Save.
5. **Link your number** by QR code or pairing code if you have not already.
6. **Test it.** Message the agent as a customer, ask for a slot and check that the booking appears under Upcoming.

### What the agent does in the chat

The agent does not book on a vague "sometime next week". It books only when two things are true: the customer has clearly agreed to come, and a specific date and time have been settled. Before booking, it repeats the exact date and time and asks the customer to confirm. Then it books and sends a short confirmation.

A typical exchange looks like this:

- Customer: "Can I come for braids tomorrow?"
- Agent: gives the price, asks what time suits, and offers times inside your opening hours.
- Customer: "2pm."
- Agent: "To confirm, tomorrow at 2:00 PM for knotless braids?"
- Customer: "Yes."
- Agent: books it and confirms.

| Past and All | Earlier appointments and the full list |

You and your team members get an email when the AI books an appointment and again at each reminder time. For an appointment booked in a WhatsApp chat, the customer also gets a reminder message in that same chat, written to fit the conversation.

### Getting good results

- **Give real hours.** The agent offers times from what you told it. If you close at 6pm, say so.
- **State the rules**: deposit, lateness, cancellation and what to bring.
- **Say what needs a person.** Home service, group bookings or a custom quote can be passed to you. The agent hands over when a customer asks for a human or asks something sensitive, and it pauses on that chat. See [how to hand over from AI to a human on WhatsApp](/blog/how-to-hand-over-from-ai-to-a-human-on-whatsapp).
- **Use one source of truth.** Add walk-in and phone bookings with "+ New appointment" so everything sits in one list.
- **Check Upcoming each morning** and reschedule anything that clashes.

### Where this is not the right tool

If you need customers to pick from a live grid of free slots, take online payment at the moment of booking, or manage many staff calendars with their own availability, a dedicated booking system is the better choice, and you can share its link in your WhatsApp replies. Dailzero suits businesses whose bookings already happen inside a WhatsApp conversation.

### Frequently asked questions

#### Can WhatsApp Business book appointments automatically?

No. It can send a greeting or away message and store quick replies, but it cannot agree a time with a customer or record a booking. You do that by hand or with a tool.

#### Will the AI book a time when I am closed?

It works from the opening days and hours you give it, so write them clearly. Every booking shows on your Appointments page, where you can reschedule or cancel.

#### Do customers get a reminder?

Yes, for appointments booked in a WhatsApp chat. The customer gets a reminder in the same chat, and you and your team get reminders by email, at the times you set.

#### Can I add appointments myself?

Yes. Click "+ New appointment", choose the agent, and enter what it is, when it is and the customer's details. Manual booking always works, even with AI auto-booking off.

#### What if a customer wants to change the time?

They can ask in the chat and you can move it with Reschedule on the Appointments page. If the request is unusual, the agent hands the chat to you.
