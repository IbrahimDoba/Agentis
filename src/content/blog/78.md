To automate client onboarding with WhatsApp AI, give the agent your onboarding sequence: the welcome message, the questions for the brief, the documents to send, the list of assets you need from the client, and your kickoff slots. It then runs that sequence in the chat as soon as a client says yes, at whatever hour they say it. Contract signing and payment confirmation stay manual.

### Why onboarding stalls

The proposal is accepted on Monday. A week later you still have no logo files, no page access and no kickoff date. The account manager sent one long message with nine requests in it, the client answered two, and the rest were buried.

An agent fixes this by asking for one thing at a time and by not forgetting.

### The onboarding sequence, from yes to kickoff

**Stage 1: the welcome.** The moment a client confirms, the agent sends a short welcome and your onboarding pack as a PDF from the media library: who their account manager is, working hours, how approvals and revisions work, and when reports arrive.

**Stage 2: the brief.** The agent asks your intake questions one by one and collects the answers. For a social media retainer these could be the target customer, the three competitors they watch, their best-selling product, the tone they want and anything they never want posted.

**Stage 3: payment details.** The agent shares your invoice instructions and bank details, or the payment link you gave it. When the client sends a transfer receipt, the agent hands the chat to you, because it cannot check your bank.

**Stage 4: assets.** It works through your asset list: logo in high resolution, brand colours, product photos, page access, ad account access.

**Stage 5: kickoff.** It offers your available slots, books the call and sends a reminder beforehand.

Here is how stage 4 can look in a real chat:

> I don send the logo. Na the product pictures remain, my photographer never finish.

> Thank you, the logo has come through. No problem on the product photos. When do you expect them from your photographer, so I can note it for your account manager? In the meantime, could you add our page as an admin on your Instagram? I can send the steps.

The agent understood the Pidgin, acknowledged the image, recorded the blocker and moved to the next item instead of stopping.

### What to prepare before you switch it on

- An onboarding pack PDF, kept to two or three pages.
- Your intake questions, written for each service you sell. A website project and an ads retainer need different briefs.
- An asset checklist per service, in the order you need things.
- Payment instructions: account name, bank, account number, and what the client should send as proof.
- Your kickoff availability, for example Tuesdays and Thursdays between 10am and 2pm.
- A handover rule: who takes over once the kickoff is booked.

Enter these in the dashboard, then test by onboarding yourself from a second phone. Setup needs no code. The [features page](/features) lists what the agent can send and collect.

### Keeping clients moving when they go quiet

On the Pro plan, automated follow-ups message a client who stopped replying partway through. On Basic and Starter there are no automatic follow-ups, so check the dashboard for stalled chats and nudge by hand.

Kickoff reminders are part of appointment booking and work on every plan.

### Where the automation stops

Three limits matter for onboarding:

- **No e-signatures.** The agent can send your contract as a document. Signing happens however you do it today.
- **No payment verification.** It shares details and hands over. A person confirms the money.
- **No automatic entry into your tools.** Answers stay in the chat history and dashboard. Dailzero has no built-in connection to CRMs, Google Sheets or project boards. A developer can push the collected brief into your system with [webhook tools](/developers); otherwise someone copies it across.

Also treat passwords carefully. Ask clients to grant page or ad account access through the platform's own role settings, not to type logins into a chat.

### The cost of onboarding a client this way

An onboarding conversation is longer than a sales enquiry. Say it takes 30 AI text replies and 2 documents: 30 × 5 + 2 × 8 = 166 credits per client. Even with ten new clients in a month that is 1,660 credits, which a ₦5,000 pay-as-you-go pack (3,200 credits) covers. ### Frequently asked questions

#### Can the agent onboard clients for different services differently?

Yes. Describe each service's questions and asset list separately, and the agent follows the one that matches what the client bought.

#### Does the client need to install anything?

No. Everything happens in the WhatsApp chat they already have with your agency number.

#### Can my account manager jump in during onboarding?

Yes. They reply from the dashboard or the phone, the AI pauses on that chat, and they switch it back on when they are done.

#### Will it collect files the client sends?

Files and photos arrive in the WhatsApp chat as normal, and the agent understands photos. Your team still downloads and stores the files wherever you keep client assets.
