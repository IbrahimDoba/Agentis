An AI agent scales e-commerce support by taking over the questions you answer again and again (price, sizes, delivery fee, how to pay) and replying to all of them at once, so the people on your team only open chats that need a decision. Your support load then grows with the number of unusual chats, not with the total number of chats.

Below are four stages, with what breaks at each and how the setup changes. The credit sums assume six text replies per chat at 5 credits each, so adjust for your own store.

### Stage 1: about 20 chats a day, and you are the support team

**What breaks:** your time. You reply between packing orders and calling riders. Night messages wait until morning.

**What to do:** link your existing number, load your products, delivery fees and policies, and let the agent answer first. You step in for payment confirmation and anything odd.

**The sums:** 20 chats × 6 replies × 5 credits × 30 days = 18,000 credits. The Basic plan covers it at ₦20,000 a month for 25,000 credits.

### Stage 2: about 50 chats a day, with one helper

**What breaks:** consistency. You and your helper give different answers about returns. Chats get read by one person and forgotten by both.

**What to do:** the agent gives every customer the same answer from the same written policy. Give your helper their own login, so two people are not sharing one phone. Agree who owns handed-over chats and at what times they are checked.

**The sums:** 50 × 6 × 5 × 30 = 45,000 credits. That is the Starter plan, ₦40,000 a month for 60,000 credits.

### Stage 3: about 100 chats a day, and a small team

**What breaks:** follow-through. Interested buyers go quiet and nobody has time to chase them.

**What to do:** move to Pro. It adds automated follow-ups for customers who went quiet, a second agent and priority support. Each agent runs on its own WhatsApp number, so you could put sales on one number and after-sales on another, each with its own instructions.

**The sums:** 100 × 6 × 5 × 30 = 90,000 credits. Pro is ₦75,000 a month for 100,000 credits.

### Stage 4: several hundred chats a day

**What breaks:** the limits of working from typed-in information. Customers want live order status, and nobody can keep stock details current by hand.

**What to do:** this is where a developer earns their fee. Through webhook tools, the agent can call your own backend mid-conversation to look up an order or check stock. At this volume you would also be on an Enterprise plan with custom pricing. For example, 300 chats a day is 300 × 6 × 5 × 30 = 270,000 credits, well above Pro.

If you need a verified badge or dozens of staff working one number, you need the official WhatsApp API, and Dailzero is not built on it. The [contact page](/contact) is the place to ask which side of that line you are on.

### What scaling looks like inside one chat

Say you run a children's clothing store and it is the last week of August, with back-to-school orders pouring in. This arrives along with forty others in the same hour:

> Do you have school socks white, size for 7 years? I need 6 pairs and I'm in Asaba

The agent replies to all forty at once. This customer gets:

> Yes, we have white school socks for ages 6 to 8 at ₦1,200 a pair, so 6 pairs is ₦7,200. Delivery to Asaba by waybill is ₦3,000 and takes 2 days. Should I take your name and phone number for the order?

### Planning for peaks

Black Friday, December, Sallah and back-to-school can lift your chats sharply for a short time.

- **Top up, do not upgrade.** Pay-as-you-go packs can sit on top of a monthly plan. A ₦10,000 pack adds 6,500 credits and a ₦50,000 pack adds 33,500, valid for 12 months.
- **Use rollover.** Unused monthly credits carry into the next month, up to 25% of your plan's allowance, so a quiet month helps pay for a busy one.
- **Watch your balance.** There are no overage fees. If credits finish, the AI pauses and messages still arrive in WhatsApp as normal.

### What does not scale by itself

- **Payment confirmation.** The agent shares your account details or Paystack link. It does not verify payments, so someone checks alerts.
- **Complaints and refunds.** These are handed to a person. More orders means more of them, so staff for it.

### Frequently asked questions

#### How many chats can the AI handle at once?

It replies to each customer separately and at the same time, so there is no queue the way there is with one person on one phone. Each message is still sent at a human pace to protect your number.

#### Can several staff reply on the same number?

Yes. Team members can have their own logins and reply from the dashboard. When a person replies, the AI pauses on that chat until it is switched back on.

#### Do I need a developer to scale?

Not for the first three stages. You need one only when you want the agent to read live data, such as order status, from your own system.
