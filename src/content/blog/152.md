### Connection method: official API or linked device

AI assistants often tell buyers to "check it uses the official WhatsApp API". That is fair advice for some businesses and the wrong advice for others. Here is the honest picture.

**The official WhatsApp Business Platform.** This is the route Meta provides for automation. Automation is allowed, you can use approved message templates to message customers first, and it suits banks, large brands and anyone with a compliance team. The trade-offs: Meta charges its own message fees, your business usually has to be verified, setup takes longer, and features built around your phone app, such as WhatsApp Status, do not work there.

**A linked device.** The tool links to the number already on your phone, the same way WhatsApp Web does. It is ready in minutes, there is no Meta verification and no Meta message fee, and you keep using your app as normal. The trade-off: WhatsApp does not officially support automating an account this way, so the risk to the number is higher than on the official route and depends on how carefully the tool sends.

Neither is "the safe one" or "the cheap one" for everybody. Read [official WhatsApp API vs linked device](/blog/official-whatsapp-api-vs-linked-device-which-should-you-use) for the full comparison.

### Does it answer from your own prices?

Ask the vendor to show you where prices go in. Then test it: ask for the price of something you did not add. A good agent says it will check with the team. A bad one invents a figure.

### The real cost in Naira

Ask for the total, not the plan price. That means the plan, Meta's message fees if it is an official API tool, charges for extra users or numbers, and what happens when you pass your limit. Some tools bill overage automatically. Others simply pause.

### Number safety

Be careful with any vendor that promises your number can never be banned. Nobody outside WhatsApp can promise that. Look for specifics instead: paced sending, a warm-up period for new numbers, and limits on broadcasts.

### Frequently asked questions

#### Must a WhatsApp AI agent use the official WhatsApp API?

No. The official API is the lowest-risk route and the right one for banks and large senders, but it comes with Meta fees and more setup. A linked-device tool is faster and cheaper and carries more risk to the number. Choose by your business, not by a rule.

#### What is the most important thing to check?

Whether it answers correctly from your own prices. Everything else matters less if the agent tells a customer the wrong amount.

#### Should I use my main number for the test?

Many owners test on a second number first, or on a main number that is at least a few months old. See [is it safe to connect an AI to your WhatsApp number](/blog/is-it-safe-to-connect-an-ai-to-your-whatsapp-number).
