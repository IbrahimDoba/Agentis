You can handle hundreds of property inquiries a day on WhatsApp by letting an AI agent answer the standard questions for every chat at once, and routing only the serious or unusual ones to your team. At that volume the planning matters more than the tool: you need to budget credits properly, keep availability accurate, and decide who picks up handed-over chats.

### First, work out what "hundreds" costs

On Dailzero a text reply uses about 5 credits. Take a hypothetical agency receiving 200 inquiries a day, each needing 6 replies:

200 × 6 × 5 credits × 30 days = 180,000 credits a month.

The Pro plan includes 100,000 credits for ₦75,000. That leaves 80,000 to cover. Pay-as-you-go packs of ₦100,000 (68,000 credits) and ₦20,000 (13,000 credits) add 81,000, bringing the month to ₦195,000. At that level you should also ask about an Enterprise plan through the [contact page](/contact), since it is custom priced.

Photos and videos raise the number. An image is 8 credits and a video 12, so a reply that sends six photos uses 48 credits by itself.

Measure your real average during the free trial before you commit to a plan. Current packs and plans are on the [pricing page](/pricing).

### Design the first reply to do most of the work

At high volume, every extra message is multiplied by hundreds. Aim for a first reply that answers what almost everyone asks next.

> Hi, is this still available? Mini flat Yaba

A good reply confirms availability, gives the rent and the total package with agency, legal and caution broken out, states the two or three facts people always ask (floor, prepaid meter, water, whether the landlord accepts students or pets), sends two strong photos instead of the whole album, and offers the next inspection slot. It offers more photos or the video on request.

### Sort inquiries into three lanes

1. **Self-serve.** Price, availability, location, features, requirements. The AI finishes these alone.
2. **Book.** The person wants to inspect. The AI books a slot, collects name and number, and sends a reminder.
3. **Human.** Negotiation, corporate lets, a buyer asking about title documents in detail, a complaint from an existing tenant. The AI collects the basics and hands over.

### Staff the handover properly

- Give each team member their own login, and decide who covers which property type or area.
- Reply from the dashboard or the phone. The AI pauses on a chat when a person replies and you switch it back on afterwards.
- Work the leads list in the dashboard first, where people showing buying intent are listed.

### Keep availability accurate, or volume works against you

The AI cannot see your property management system or know that a flat was let this morning. It works from what you entered. With hundreds of people asking, one stale listing means hundreds of people told that a taken flat is available, and a morning of apologies.

Make one person responsible for updating listings the moment a unit is taken, a price changes or a landlord withdraws. If you have a developer and a listings database, webhook tools can let the agent check availability in your own system during a chat.

### Split by number if one line is overloaded

Each agent runs on one WhatsApp number. The Pro plan includes two agents, so a common setup is one number for rentals and one for sales, or one for Lagos and one for Abuja, each with its own listings and instructions.

### Protect the number

A number receiving and replying to hundreds of chats a day needs care.

- Dailzero queues every message and sends at a human pace, and slows down automatically if WhatsApp signals a limit.
- A brand-new SIM should be used normally for about two weeks before you connect it, and then goes through a warm-up period.
- Keep the phone charged and online regularly. It remains the main device, as with WhatsApp Web. Use a dedicated office phone on a charger, with a power bank for when NEPA takes light.
- No tool can promise a number will never be restricted.

### When this is not the right setup

If you need a verified badge, a very large sales floor sharing one number, or volumes well beyond a few hundred chats a day, the official WhatsApp Business Platform is the better fit. Dailzero is a linked-device tool with no built-in connection to property management software.

### Frequently asked questions

#### Can one AI agent really reply to hundreds of people at once?

Yes. It handles chats in parallel, with each message queued and sent at a safe pace, so customers get replies in seconds instead of waiting in line for a person.

#### How do I stop it offering properties that are already taken?

Update or remove the listing as soon as it goes. The agent only knows what you have told it, unless a developer connects your listings system through webhook tools.

#### What happens if credits run out during a busy day?

The AI pauses and messages keep arriving in WhatsApp as normal. Top up with a pay-as-you-go pack and it resumes; there are no overage fees.
