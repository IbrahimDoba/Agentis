There is no review button in Dailzero, and no tool can post a Google review on a guest's behalf. What you can automate is the asking: a WhatsApp message with your review link that goes out after a stay, an AI agent that replies when the guest answers, and a handover to you when the feedback is bad. This guide covers three ways to do it, from simplest to most automatic, and what each one cannot do.

### First, get your review link

Decide where you want reviews to land. For most Nigerian hotels and short-lets that is the Google Business Profile, because it shows when people search the hotel name or look on Maps. Copy the short review link from your profile and keep it somewhere handy. Every method below sends that link.

### Method 1: let the agent ask when the guest messages

This costs nothing extra and takes ten minutes. Add an instruction to your agent along these lines: when a guest says they have checked out, says thank you after a stay, or asks for their receipt, thank them, ask how the stay was, and share the review link if they were happy.

A guest writes:

> We don check out o. Thanks for everything, the room was nice.

The agent replies:

> Thank you for staying with us, we are glad you enjoyed the room. If you have a minute, a short Google review would really help a small hotel like ours. Here is the link. Safe journey home.

The limit is obvious: it only works for guests who send a message at checkout. Many do not.

### Method 2: a broadcast to recent guests

Dailzero can send a [WhatsApp broadcast](/solutions/broadcasts) to your existing contacts, with each message paced to protect your number. Once a week, send a short review request to guests who stayed.

A text broadcast costs 5 credits per person. If 300 guests receive it, that is 300 × 5 = 1,500 credits, which a ₦5,000 pay-as-you-go pack (3,200 credits) covers with room left for the replies.

Two cautions. Broadcasts are for people who have messaged your number, not bought lists. And keep track of who has already been asked, so no guest gets the same request twice.

### Method 3: trigger it from your own system

This is the only version that is automatic in the full sense. Dailzero cannot see your booking or property management system, so by itself it does not know that Mr Okafor checked out of room 12 this morning. A developer can close that gap: when your system marks a checkout, it calls the Dailzero API to send a WhatsApp text to that guest's number with the review link. The [developer documentation](/developers) covers sending messages from your own code.

If you have no developer and no system, a simple routine works: the front desk sends the request by hand at checkout, or you run Method 2 every Monday.

### Write a request people answer

- Send it soon after checkout, while the stay is fresh.
- Keep it to two or three sentences and ask one thing only.
- Put the link on its own line so it is easy to tap.
- Ask every guest, not only the ones you think were happy. Filtering out unhappy guests can break a review site's rules, and so can paying for reviews. Check the rules of the site you use.

### What happens when the answer is a complaint

This is where an AI agent earns its place. Some guests reply to the request with the real story:

> Honestly the generator went off twice at night and nobody picked the intercom.

Set the agent to apologise, avoid arguing or making promises, and hand the chat to a manager. You reply from the dashboard or your phone, and the agent stays paused on that chat until you switch it back on. A complaint settled privately in WhatsApp is one that may never become a public one-star review.

### The limits, in one place

- Dailzero has no dedicated review feature and no checkout trigger of its own.
- It cannot confirm whether a guest actually posted a review, and it cannot read your Google reviews.
- Automated follow-ups on the Pro plan are built for customers who went quiet in a chat. They are useful for unanswered enquiries, but they are not a post-stay review tool.
- It reaches guests on WhatsApp only. A guest who booked by phone call and never messaged you will not be in your contacts.

### Frequently asked questions

#### Can Dailzero send a review request automatically at checkout?

Not on its own, because it cannot see your booking system. A developer can trigger the message through the API when your system records a checkout. Otherwise use a weekly broadcast.

#### How much does it cost to ask 100 guests for a review?

A text broadcast is 5 credits per person, so 100 guests cost 500 credits. The ₦1,000 pay-as-you-go pack holds 600 credits.

#### Should I offer a discount in exchange for a review?

Check the rules of the review site first. Several do not allow rewards for reviews, and a plain, polite request works without one.
